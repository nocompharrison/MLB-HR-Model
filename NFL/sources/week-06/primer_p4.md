**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Matthew Golden (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/matthew-golden.php)**
[Matthew Golden](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/matthew-golden.php) has been absolutely fantastic this season as the WR16 in fantasy points per game. Golden has a 22.7% target share with 2.48 YPRR, eight red zone targets, ten deep targets, and a 26.1% first-read share. He has been crushing. Golden should feast again this week. Over the last two weeks, Dallas has utilized single high at the second-highest rate (68.2%). Against single high, Golden leads the team with a 26% target-per-route run rate with 1.78 YPRR. Dallas has allowed the sixth-most yards per target and the sixth-most fantasy points per opportunity to wide receivers.
**[Tucker Kraft (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tucker-kraft.php) **
[Tucker Kraft](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tucker-kraft.php) is warming up. Over the last two weeks, he has been the TE8 in fantasy points per game. In those games, Kraft has had a 68.8% route share with a 19.7% target share, 2.41 YPRR, four red zone targets, one deep target, and a 25.5% first-read share. Kraft is due for a big week. This could be it. Dallas has allowed the sixth-most yards per target and fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends.
**Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[MarShawn Lloyd (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/marshawn-lloyd.php)**
Last week. [MarShawn Lloyd](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/marshawn-lloyd.php) played 44.1% of the snaps (led the team) among the backs with 38.5% of the rushing share and a 26.5% route share. The blowout nature of that game probably tilts some of the usage stats more in the direction of [Pierre Strong](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/pierre-strong-jr.php). Lloyd also led the team with two red zone rushing attempts (Strong, one). As a rusher, Lloyd has only 1.32 yards after contact per attempt (WOOF), but he has managed a 17% missed tackle rate. Last week, with his two scores, Lloyd was the RB14 for the week while finishing with 11 touches and 37 total yards. Lloyd could have another strong RB2/flex week against Dallas. The Cowboys have allowed the seventh-highest explosive run rate, the 10th-highest rushing success rate, and the third-most yards before contact per attempt.
**[George Pickens (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/george-pickens.php)**
Last week was the monster performance that everyone had been waiting for with [George Pickens](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/george-pickens.php). [CeeDee Lamb](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/ceedee-lamb.php) getting banged up and a ton of single-high coverage helped facilitate that, though. Pickens has only one other game this season with more than 11 PPR points. Overall, he has a 20.8% target share with 2.06 YPRR, four red zone targets, seven deep targets, and a 24.4% first-read share. The big issue for Pickens this week is that he’ll see another secondary that loves to utilize two-high coverage. Green Bay has the fifth-highest rate of two high in the NFL (62.6%). Against two high, Pickens’ target per route run rate drops to 15% and his YPRR dips to 1.59. Those aren’t great numbers at all. The Packers haven’t been a shutdown unit against perimeter wide receivers, so it’s possible that Pickens gets a touchdown this week and saves a middling stat line. Green Bay is 16th in yards per target allowed to perimeter wide receivers while also giving up the 11th-most fantasy points per opportunity.
**Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**
_Players in consideration for lineups, but temper your expectations this week._
**[Jake Ferguson (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jake-ferguson.php)**
[Jake Ferguson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jake-ferguson.php) is a touchdown-or-bust tight end option. Ferguson is the TE23 in fantasy points per game with a 10.9% target share, 0.73 YPRR, four red zone targets, zero deep targets, and a 9.9% first-read share. Sit Ferguson again this week. Green Bay has limited tight ends to the fifth-fewest yards per target and the 13th-fewest fantasy points per opportunity.

**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Terry McLaurin (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/terry-mclaurin.php) **
Last week, [Terry McLaurin](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/terry-mclaurin.php) was still dealing with a hammy issue, but he had a 70.3% route share, a 16% target share, 62 receiving yards (2.38 YPRR), and a 22.2% first-read share. As long as he didn’t pick up any new injuries or aggravate his hammy last week, I expect his playing time to increase this week. Overall, McLaurin is the WR33 in fantasy points per game with a 20.8% target share, 1.61 YPRR, three red zone targets, four deep targets, and a 27.9% first-read share. McLaurin should see plenty of target volume this week, as I expect the Commanders to be trailing. The 49ers are 17th in receiving fantasy points per game allowed to perimeter wide receivers while also sitting at 16th in fantasy points per game given up to the position.
**Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Rachaad White (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rachaad-white.php)**
With Jacory Croskey-Merritt out this week, Rachaad White should be their lead back. Last week, Rachaad White returned and had a 60% snap rate, a 32.4% route share, a 4% target share, eight touches, and 20 total yards. White hasn’t been very effective on early downs behind Washington’s patchwork offensive line with 1.79 yards after contact per attempt and only a 10% missed tackle rate, but he should see plenty of volume and some passing game work. Washington will likely be playing catch-up mode in this game, which could mean a lot of White checkdowns. White has an 8% target share, a 16% target per route run rate, and 0.85 YPRR this season. The 49ers have quietly allowed the most receptions to running backs and the second-most receiving fantasy points per game to the position. They have also allowed the lowest explosive run rate, the fifth-fewest yards after contact per attempt, and the eighth-lowest missed tackle rate.
**[Stefon Diggs (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/stefon-diggs.php)**
Stefon Diggs missed last week’s game with a hamstring injury. Diggs managed limited practice sessions on Friday and Saturday of this week and has been listed as questionable for this week. I’m expecting him to suit up for Week 6. Diggs is the WR23 in fantasy points per game with a 21.9% target share, 1.65 YPRR, four red zone targets, four deep targets, and a 26.9% first-read share. Diggs is a solid flex this week against a 49ers secondary that has allowed the tenth-most yards per target to slot receivers while ranking 15th in fantasy points per opportunity allowed to the position.
**Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**
_Players in consideration for lineups, but temper your expectations this week._
**[Jayden Daniels (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jayden-daniels.php)**
[Jayden Daniels](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jayden-daniels.php) triumphantly returned to the huddle last week as the QB2 for the week. That was on the strength of nine rushing attempts, 103 rushing yards, and two rushing scores. I don’t think we can count on a hundo on the ground weekly from Daniels, but it was nice to see him running despite the brace. Daniels hasn’t been good as a passer this season. Among 30 qualifying passers, Daniels has the fifth-fewest yards per attempt, the ninth-lowest passing success rate, the fourth-lowest catchable target rate, and the third-lowest hero throw rate. He’ll have to get a ton of his fantasy production from his legs again this week against the 49ers. The 49ers pass defense has been a shutdown unit this season, allowing the third-fewest yards per attempt, the eighth-lowest passing success rate, and the seventh-lowest CPOE. Daniels could easily be a QB1 again this week with his rushing production alone, but also Washington will be forced to attempt to keep pace with the 49ers’ high-powered offense.
