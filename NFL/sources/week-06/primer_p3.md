**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[DK Metcalf (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dk-metcalf.php)**
[DK Metcalf](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dk-metcalf.php) is the WR40 in fantasy points per game, with only one game this season with more than 15 PPR points. Metcalf has a 19.9% target share with 1.18 YPRR, one red zone target, ten deep targets, and a 28.2% first-read share. This could be a decent bounce-back spot for Metcalf. Tampa Bay’s love of single high should help him. Tampa Bay has the second-highest single-high rate in the NFL (65.2%). Since 2025, against single-high, Metcalf’s target-per-route run rate has increased from 18% to 25%, and his YPRR has jumped from 1.48 to 1.98. Tampa Bay is 15th in yards per target allowed to perimeter wide receivers, while they have given up the 13th-most fantasy points per opportunity to the position.
**[Darnell Washington (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/darnell-washington.php)**
Yes, I know you were expecting to see [Pat Freiermuth](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/pat-freiermuth.php)‘s name here, but I lean toward [Darnell Washington](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/darnell-washington.php) having the busier day in Week 6. This goes back to Tampa Bay’s love of single high and how Pittsburgh has deployed these tight ends. Tampa Bay has the second-highest rate of single-high in the NFL (65.2%). Against single-high, Washington leads the team with a 25% target per route run rate with 2.0 YPRR. Freiermuth has been one of their go-tos against two-high and usually non-existent vs. single-high. Against two high, Friermuth has a 29% target per route run rate and 2.12 YPRR, but against single high he has only a 12% target per route run rate and 0.80 YPRR. Overall, Washington has a 65.3% snap rate and only a 35.8% route share with an 8.6% target share (22% TPRR), 1.79 YPRR, three red zone targets, one deep target, and a 10.3% first-read share. With the Steelers short on wide receivers and the coverage matchup, I could see Washington having a surprising game as a wonderful streaming option. Tampa Bay has allowed the 11th-most receiving fantasy points per game and the 14th-most fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends. Washington was a TE1 in Week 4 (TE12). He could be a TE1 again this week.

**[Aaron Rodgers (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aaron-rodgers.php)**
After two weeks of fun as Jameis Rodgers, Ole Aaron turned back into a low aDOT machine last week. Against Indy last week, Rodgers had a 4.29 aDOT with only a 3.2% deep ball rate. We’ll see which version we get of Rodgers this week, but that was disappointing to see. Rodgers still had a nice fantasy day as the QB11 for the week. Rodgers has had at least 22 fantasy points in each of his last three games, as he has been the QB4 in fantasy points per game since Week 3. How successful the Bucs’ blitz plan is this week will go a long way to determining how Rodgers plays. The Bucs have allowed the 14th-most passing yards while ranking 15th in CPOE and 16th in EPA per dropback. The Bucs continue to blitz, though, despite it not helping their pressure rate. Tampa Bay has the fourth-highest blitz rate, but they also have the lowest pressure rate in the NFL. Against the blitz, Rodgers has the sixth-fewest yards per attempt and the fifth-lowest passing success rate. Rodgers could be a QB1 this week again, or he could fall flat. There’s a wide range of outcomes for the cave dweller this week.

Among 34 qualifying quarterbacks, Daniels is 20th in passing success rate, 33rd in aDOT, 26th in CPOE, and seventh in catchable target rate. He has the sixth-highest first-read rate and the highest rate of passing yardage coming from yards after the catch (74.5%). The Bucs plan with him has been to get the ball out to his playmakers and hit his first reads. Daniels should be able to post solid QB2 numbers again this week against a Pittsburgh pass defense that has allowed the 11th-most yards per attempt, the eighth-highest passing success rate, and the 14th-highest EPA per dropback.

Kincaid could feast this week.
