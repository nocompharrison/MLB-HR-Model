## **_Matchups Analysis - Sunday 9_** _:30 EST Game (London)_
## **Houston Texans at Jacksonville Jaguars**
**Matchups We Love:**
**WR Parker Washington**
After a big Week 5 performance in which he finished as the overall WR3, Washington could have another great fantasy day in Week 6. The Houston Texans have allowed the second-most fantasy points and seventh-most fantasy points per game to opposing slot WRs. That makes Washington a must-start WR2 this weekend.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**RB[Woody Marks](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/25942/Woody+Marks) **
[David Montgomery](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/20882/David+Montgomery) will miss the next few weeks with a groin injury. That will make Woody Marks the lead back for the Texans offense moving forward. However, Marks will be a riskier RB2 play this week in a tough matchup. The Jaguars have allowed the third-fewest fantasy points and fewest rushing yards per game to opposing RBs. Start with caution in Week 6.
**RB[Bhayshul Tuten](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/25898/Bhayshul+Tuten) **
Bhayshul Tuten has been a consistent RB2 option for fantasy managers to begin the season. He has scored above 11 PPR fantasy points in four straight games and is coming off a season-high 22 carries with a 68% snap share. While Tuten will be a low-end RB2 this week, expectations should be tempered in this matchup. Houston has allowed just one RB to score above 12 PPR fantasy points this season.
**TE[Brenton Strange](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24223/Brenton+Strange)**
Brenton Strange has been more involved in the passing game over the past two weeks. He caught seven passes for 95 yards against the Bengals in Week 4 and seven passes for 68 yards against the Eagles in Week 5. For this week, though, Strange will be hard to trust as a TE1. The Texans have allowed the sixth-fewest fantasy points and haven't given up a touchdown to the TE position this year.
**TE[Dalton Schultz](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/19920/Dalton+Schultz)**
Dalton Schultz should only be started in desperate situations on Sunday. He has scored under seven PPR fantasy points in three straight games and hasn't seen more than four targets in a game since Week 2. To make matters worse for Schultz, the Jaguars have allowed the seventh-fewest fantasy points, fourth-fewest receptions, and fifth-fewest receiving yards to opposing TEs.
**Other Matchups:**
**QB[C.J. Stroud](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23239/C.J.+Stroud)**
C.J. Stroud has been a better-than-expected fantasy option this season. He has scored above 20 fantasy points in back-to-back games and now gets his college teammate ([Marvin Harrison Jr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24974/Marvin+Harrison+Jr.)) in this offense.
