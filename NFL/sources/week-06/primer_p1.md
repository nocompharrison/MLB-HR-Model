**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Emanuel Wilson (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/emanuel-wilson.php)**
Over the last two games, [Emanuel Wilson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/emanuel-wilson.php) has been Seattle’s clear workhorse back. He has averaged 23 touches and 96 total yards with RB6 and RB20 weekly finishes. In those two games, he has averaged 60.2% of the snaps with 69.5% of the rushing attempts, a 36.1% route share, and an 11.8% target share. Among 42 qualifying backs, Wilson is 24th in yards after contact per attempt and 33rd in missed tackle rate. Wilson should have another productive day in Week 6. Denver has allowed the sixth-highest explosive run rate, the most yards after contact per attempt, and the highest missed tackle rate.

**[RJ Harvey (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rj-harvey.php)**

Dobbins remains a touchdown-dependent flex. He’s a bust if he doesn’t get into the endzone again this week. I don’t see him maximizing his touches in this horrible matchup. Seattle has limited rushers to the seventh-lowest explosive run rate, the tenth-fewest yards before contact per attempt, and the sixth-lowest missed tackle rate.
