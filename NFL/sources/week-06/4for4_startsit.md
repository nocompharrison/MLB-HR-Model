Welcome to another edition of Hoopes There It Is! We’re previewing every game in Week 6, highlighting advanced stats like EPA, success rate, pass rate over expected (PROE), and completion percentage over expected (CPOE) to help make the best decisions for your fantasy teams. I’ll provide a quick start/sit section at the top that stems from the longer write-ups. And if you’re interested in more, which I hope you are, you can jump to any of the [14 games this week](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it#links). Let’s get started!
## Start ‘Em, Sit ‘Em
[Cam Skattebo](https://www.4for4.com/player/cam-skattebo) (**Start**): UPDATE: The Giants' coaching staff is insane and starting [Najee Harris](https://www.4for4.com/player/najee-harris) this week. You can bench Skattebo.
Skattebo’s role didn’t change in Week 5 as he takes most of the early-down snaps and splits the passing work with [Tyrone Tracy](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyrone-tracy). So, he ultimately ran about half of the team’s routes and out-carried [Najee Harris](https://www.4for4.com/player/najee-harris), and Tracy combined 13 to eight. But he’s got a fantastic matchup this week. The Saints are bottom-five in rushing efficiency, success rate, and explosive run rate allowed. Skattebo hasn’t scored a TD since Week 1, meaning he could be cheap to get via trade. And the benefit of that idea is he gets to play the Cowboys’ atrocious defense during fantasy championship week.
All Broncos (**Sit**): While much was made of Sean Payton taking back play calling duties last week, the results didn’t improve. The Broncos hit 15th percentile passing efficiency with a brutal 32.4% passing success rate. And the team only had 20 targets for the day as they ran about 5% more than expected. Meanwhile, there’s no upside in this offense if the Broncos continue to be dead-last in air yards per attempt. This week, they play a Seahawks pass defense that is top four in efficiency, success rate, and explosive play rate allowed. I’m looking elsewhere.
[Germie Bernard](https://www.4for4.com/player/germie-bernard) (**Start**): The Steelers hit +5.7% PROE last week and are surprisingly up to 2nd in the league. And it was [Germie Bernard](https://www.4for4.com/player/germie-bernard) who paced the team with targets in Week 5, earning eight compared to [DK Metcalf](https://www.4for4.com/player/dk-metcalf)’s four. That was not the expectation going into the game, as [Roman Wilson](https://www.4for4.com/player/roman-wilson) was expected to fill the void left by [Michael Pittman](https://www.4for4.com/player/michael-pittman)’s injury, but Wilson hurt his finger during the game and is already ruled out for Week 6. And the underlying usage for Bernard was strong, as he only ran one fewer route than Metcalf. I think he’s a totally fine play this week.
All Chargers If [Ladd McConkey](https://www.4for4.com/player/ladd-mcconkey) Doesn’t Play (**Sit**): The Chargers have easily the lowest implied point total of the week, lower than both the Browns and the Titans. Last week, the Chargers opted for a pre-historic -18.4% PROE, but that only got them 12th percentile rushing efficiency and one of the worst rushing success rates I can remember at 18.2%. Their passing game was even worse, finishing the day at 5th percentile efficiency. Meanwhile, the RB split is disgusting, with [Kimani Vidal](https://www.4for4.com/player/kimani-vidal) taking 28 snaps compared to 23 for [Keaton Mitchell](https://www.4for4.com/player/keaton-mitchell) and 20 for [Omarion Hampton](https://www.4for4.com/player/omarion-hampton). What an abysmal runout here for this entire team.
Tertiary Rams Players (**Start**): The Rams are projected for 32.0 points this week, the highest I can remember seeing for quite a while. You’re obviously starting [Puka Nacua](https://www.4for4.com/player/puka-nacua), [Davante Adams](https://www.4for4.com/player/davante-adams), and [Kyren Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyren-williams). But I’d also be willing to start [Blake Corum](https://www.4for4.com/player/blake-corum) and [Tyler Higbee](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-higbee) depending on my other options. It’s still Williams’ backfield despite a nice game from Corum, as Williams out-snapped Corum 60 to 22, ran 26 more routes, and took 11 more carries. Corum did see four targets, but that was on only eight routes, and he won’t see a 50% TPRR likely for the rest of the season. But as 12.5-point favorites, maybe we see a bit more Corum in this one. And then Higbee also earned six targets last week, and all we need is a TD, with the Rams’ massive implied point total improving the probability of that outcome.

##  Seahawks at Broncos (O/U 40.5, SEA -3.0)
### Seahawks (21.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Putting the most generous spin on this, the Seahawks’ offense has been below average to start the year.
  * They’re 22nd in success rate and plays per game and 26th in efficiency and pass rate over expected (PROE). 
  * They were even worse in Week 5, hitting -7.2% PROE, 13th-percentile passing efficiency, and awful 2nd-percentile rushing efficiency, albeit in a very tough matchup with the 49ers.
  * Unfortunately, it’s not like the Broncos’ defense is much easier, especially against the pass where they’re top eight in efficiency, explosive pass rate, and CPOE allowed.
  * [Jaxon Smith-Njigba](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaxon-smith-njigba) is obviously a must-start, though he’s only seen 14 total targets in the last two weeks after seeing at least 11 in each of his first three games.
  * JSN is the only pass catcher I’d be interested in starting.
  * At RB, the split was predictable in Week 5 with [Emanuel Wilson](https://www.4for4.com/player/emanuel-wilson) as the early-down back and [George Holani](https://www.4for4.com/player/george-holani) as the receiving back.
  * The routes were almost even between the two, but Wilson took 20 carries to Holani’s four.
  * I don’t know [Zach Charbonnet](https://www.4for4.com/player/zach-charbonnet)’s status at the time I’m writing, but my assumption is that he won’t be activated on a short week.
  * With consecutive games of 20+ carries and a TD, you could certainly do worse than Wilson, especially as the Broncos’ run defense is much weaker than their pass defense, with teams opting to run on them at the 10th-highest rate in the league versus expectation.


### Broncos (18.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Putting the most generous spin on this, the Broncos’ offense has been below average to start the year.
  * And while much was made of Sean Payton taking back play-calling duties last week, the results weren’t any better.
  * The Broncos hit 15th percentile passing efficiency with a brutal 32.4% passing success rate.
  * And the team only had 20 targets for the day as they ran about 5% more than expected.
  * [Evan Engram](https://www.4for4.com/player/evan-engram) actually led the team with four targets, but he ran only 18 of the team’s 29 routes, followed by [Jaylen Waddle](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylen-waddle) and [Courtland Sutton](https://www.4for4.com/player/courtland-sutton), who earned three targets each.
  * I’m not especially excited to start any Broncos’ pass catcher this week given the implied points and the Seahawks’ pass defense that is top four in efficiency, success rate, and explosive play rate allowed.
  * There’s not a lot of upside in this offense if the Broncos continue to be dead-last in air yards per attempt.
  * The RB rotation for the Broncos is similar to last season as JK Dobbins led [RJ Harvey](https://www.4for4.com/player/rj-harvey) in carries, 18 to three, but Harvey ran 13 routes to Dobbins’ five.
  * One thing in Harvey’s favor is that [Tyler Badie](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-badie) is mostly out of our lives this season, with Harvey taking over the two-minute drill snaps that Badie had last year, according to PFF.


##  Texans at Jaguars (O/U 42.5, JAX -3.0)
### Texans (19.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The big news here is that the Texans acquired [Marvin Harrison Jr.](https://www.4for4.com/player/marvin-harrison-jr) from the Cardinals.
  * I think that just dings the prospects of the secondary WRs, mostly personal favorite [Jaylin Noel](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylin-noel), rather than changing how we’re playing this passing offense.
  * Noel only ran 20 of the team’s 37 routes last week, and now there is additional competition here, so the only WR you want to play is [Nico Collins](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/collin1).
  * The more impactful fantasy news is [David Montgomery](https://www.4for4.com/player/david-montgomery)’s groin injury, as he’s considered week-to-week and unlikely to play in Week 6.
  * Montgomery only played nine snaps last week, and then [Woody Marks](https://www.4for4.com/player/woody-marks) took nearly every other snap, crucially running 30 of the team’s 37 routes.
  * No one is running on the Jaguars recently, with the Jags’ run defense top-three in efficiency, success rate, and explosive run rate allowed.
  * They’ve completely shut down the Bengals and Eagles rushing attacks in consecutive weeks, and the weakness of this Texans offense is their running game.
  * So, if Marks gets there in fantasy, it should be through the air, with his 81% route rate last week really encouraging.


### Jaguars (22.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Jalen Carter looked like the best player on the field last week and helped limit an otherwise good Jaguars’ rushing attack to only 11th percentile efficiency and a nasty 26.9% success rate.
  * It doesn’t get much easier this week against a Texans’ defense that has allowed explosive passes but otherwise is average to elite in every other metric.
  * [Brenton Strange](https://www.4for4.com/player/brenton-strange) continues to be more involved than you’d think, tying [Parker Washington](https://www.4for4.com/player/parker-washington) for the team lead with eight targets last week.
  * Strange is up to TE14 in expected fantasy points per game and outperforming his expectation by 1.5 points per game, so you could certainly do worse.
  * But Washington is the guy you want here, especially after his 6/124/2 line last week.
  * [Brian Thomas Jr.](https://www.4for4.com/player/brian-thomas-jr) can be cut if you haven’t already done so, as he only ran 26 of the team’s 44 routes last week and didn’t catch any of his three targets.
  * And [Bhayshul Tuten](https://www.4for4.com/player/bhayshul-tuten) continues to *almost* explode for fantasy.
  * Tuten took 22 of the 24 RB carries last week and out-snapped the combination of [LeQuint Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/lequint-allen) and [Chris Rodriguez](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-rodriguez) 49 to 22.
  * According to PFF, Tuten took 100% of the snaps in the Jags’ first two drives for the first time this season.
  * It’s a tough matchup, and it’s annoying that he’s splitting short-yardage and goal-line work, but the nuclear Tuten game is coming.


##  Jets at Patriots (O/U 43.5, NE -7.5)
### Jets (18.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Jets won the game, but it was still a brutal day for the passing attack as they finished with 8th percentile efficiency in Week 5.
  * But this is an incredibly easy offense for our purposes, as [Garrett Wilson](https://www.4for4.com/player/garrett-wilson) earned a massive 40.7% target share and 32.4% targets per route run on his way to a 7/104/1 line.
  * Besides Wilson, the only other player you’re starting is [Breece Hall](https://www.4for4.com/player/breece-hall) if he’s healthy or [Braelon Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/braelon-allen) if he’s not.
  * With Hall sidelined, Allen took 24 of the 26 RB carries, had an 86% snap rate, and ran 28 of the team’s 35 routes.
  * The Patriots’ defense is slightly above average season-to-date, but they’ve allowed rushing efficiency against them.
  * We’ll just need the Jets to keep the game close to get volume from Allen.


### Patriots (25.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * We’ve gotten last year’s Patriots in consecutive games now, with the Pats hitting 98th percentile passing efficiency and a massive 62.2% passing success rate last week.
  * And that’s with [Efton Chism](https://www.4for4.com/player/efton-chism) pacing the team with six targets.
  * AJ Brown is eligible to return from IR this week, but I don’t know if that will happen at the time I’m writing.
  * I’d be willing to start Brown right away if he plays, but if he doesn’t, I’m going to be honest: I have no idea if Chism or Pop Douglas or [Romeo Doubs](https://www.4for4.com/player/romeo-doubs) will be the guy in Week 6.
  * Doubs ran eight more routes than Chism and 15 more than Douglas, so he’d still be my bet, but how confident are you feeling after he caught one pass last week?
  * Personal favorite [Eli Raridon](https://www.4for4.com/player/eli-raridon) is starting to eat into [Hunter Henry](https://www.4for4.com/player/hunter-henry)’s workload a bit, as Henry ran 26 of the team’s 38 routes, while Raridon was close with 20.
  * Raridon actually out-targeted Henry five to four last week.
  * Things are easier to parse at RB as [TreVeyon Henderson](https://www.4for4.com/player/treyveon-henderson) suffered an ankle injury in Week 5 and is expected to miss multiple games.
  * [Rhamondre Stevenson](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/stever1) led [Corey Kiner](https://www.4for4.com/player/corey-kiner) in snaps, 53 to 15 last week, and you want to start Stevenson this week with Henderson on the sideline and the Pats 7.5-point home favorites.


##  Ravens at Browns (O/U 41.5, BAL -4.5)
### Ravens (23.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Ravens only hit 25th percentile passing efficiency last week, but that was without [Lamar Jackson](https://www.4for4.com/player/lamar-jackson), who missed the game with an ankle injury.
  * My assumption is that Jackson plays this week, but that’s not a given.
  * [Zay Flowers](https://www.4for4.com/player/zay-flowers) is the only pass catcher that I really want to play, and he paced the team with nine targets last week.
  * [Mark Andrews](https://www.4for4.com/player/mark-andrews) tied for 2nd on the team with six targets, but he only managed a 4/28/0 line despite a 71% route rate.
  * Part of that higher route rate was because Matt Hibner hurt his hand in the 1st half, though he was able to return. 
  * Generally, though, he’s losing snaps to both Matt Hibner and [Josh Cuevas](https://www.4for4.com/player/josh-cuevas).
  * And then you’re playing [Derrick Henry](https://www.4for4.com/player/derrick-henry), though it’s annoying that he only ran 12 of the team’s 38 routes, with [Justice Hill](https://www.4for4.com/player/justice-hill) running nearly twice as many routes on the day.


### Browns (18.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The floor fell out on this Browns offense last week, hitting 20th percentile passing efficiency and only 7th percentile rushing efficiency.
  * [Isaiah Bond](https://www.4for4.com/player/isaiah-bond) actually led the team with seven targets, partially because [KC Concepcion](https://www.4for4.com/player/kc-concepcion) only played 11 snaps due to a head injury.
  * [Denzel Boston](https://www.4for4.com/player/denzel-boston) is still my preferred WR here given the air yards, though the Ravens’ defense has limited opposing QBs to the 6th-lowest aDoT on the season.
  * But with only 18.5 implied points, it’s completely reasonable to look for other options.
  * And it’s a mess at RB because [Quinshon Judkins](https://www.4for4.com/player/quinshon-judkins) was seen in a walking boot after the game and [Raheim Sanders](https://www.4for4.com/player/raheim-sanders) left in the 4th quarter with a hip injury.
  * I guess that leaves [Jaleel McLaughlin](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaleel-mclaughlin) as the guy, but [Dylan Sampson](https://www.4for4.com/player/dylan-sampson) is eligible to return from IR this week.
  * Sampson would certainly be my preferred option between the two, but I think my real preferred option is an RB on another team.


##  Titans at Colts (O/U 44.5, IND -7.0)
### Titans (18.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * [Jahmyr Gibbs](https://www.4for4.com/player/jahmyr-gibbs) has nine touchdowns this season, while the Titans have…six.
  * The Titans only hit 7th percentile passing and 15th percentile rushing efficiency last week.
  * [Tyjae Spears](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyjae-spears) actually led the team with nine targets, followed by seven for Wan’Dale Robinson, and only four for [Carnell Tate](https://www.4for4.com/player/carnell-tate).
  * Last week was the first time this season that Tate’s target share dropped below 20%, which…isn’t ideal as someone who touted him in last week’s article.
  * Tate is still my preferred option in the offense, and the matchup is fine this week against a Colts pass defense that is very good in success rate but allows air yards and gives up explosives.
  * The Spears usage is interesting, as he played about a third of early-down snaps and over 50% of the total snaps, according to PFF, which are both improvements over recent weeks.
  * This Colts’ run defense is bottom-two in the league in efficiency and success rate allowed, with Spears a good play if [Tony Pollard](https://www.4for4.com/player/tony-pollard) is traded before the game.


### Colts (25.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * [Keenan Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/keenan-allen) and [Tyler Warren](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-warren) paced the Colts with nine targets each last week.
  * Warren is up to TE5 in expected fantasy points per game, so he’s a clear start, but his average target is 7% less valuable than the average receiver.
  * The issue with Warren’s targets is his aDoT, as he’s only seen one target with 20+ air yards this season and it was incomplete.

  * But it’s a brutal matchup against a 49ers’ pass defense that is 1st in the league in explosive pass rate allowed, partially because they limit the aDoT of opposing QBs.
  * At RB, [Rachaad White](https://www.4for4.com/player/rachaad-white) saw 15 more snaps than [Jacory Croskey-Merritt](https://www.4for4.com/player/jacory-croskey-merritt), though JCM was given four more carries.
  * JCM got home in fantasy because he scored a TD, but I wouldn’t bank on that this week against a 49ers’ run defense that is 4th in efficiency and 1st in explosive run rate allowed.
  * I know it’s a tough matchup for Daniels, but no QB is averaging more scrambles per game than him this season, so you at least have a reasonable floor with the hope for more play volume than we saw in Week 5.


### 49ers (29.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w6/fantasy-football-week-6-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The 49ers’ efficiency machine sputtered in Week 5, hitting 25th percentile passing and 27th percentile rushing efficiency.
  * Turnovers were the big problem, with [Brock Purdy](https://www.4for4.com/player/brock-purdy) throwing an interception and the team losing two fumbles.
  * [Mike Evans](https://www.4for4.com/player/mike-evans) looks awesome, though, as he paced the team with nine targets and finished the day with a 7/113/1 line.
  * Evans is a must-start against a bad Commanders’ pass defense, with opponents passing on them at the 2nd-highest rate in the league versus expectation.
  * And I know [George Kittle](https://www.4for4.com/player/george-kittle) had a rough box score with a 5/24/0 line, but he’s TE9 in expected fantasy points per game and outperforming that expectation by over four points a game because, well, he’s [George Kittle](https://www.4for4.com/player/george-kittle).
  * There also aren’t any other healthy pass catchers, as [Deebo Samuel](https://www.4for4.com/player/deebo-samuel) suffered a hamstring injury in Week 5 and is likely to miss a few weeks, potentially returning in Week 9 after the team’s bye.
  * And then you’re obviously starting [Christian McCaffrey](https://www.4for4.com/player/christian-mccaffrey) every week, especially since the concern about [Kaelon Black](https://www.4for4.com/player/kaelon-black) is long past us, with CMC taking 49 snaps compared to Black’s 11 in Week 5. 
  * Despite the efficiency being dragged down by turnovers, the 49ers’ 52.4% rushing success rate in Week 5 was awesome.
  * That was the highest rush success rate for any team against a Mike Macdonald defense.
