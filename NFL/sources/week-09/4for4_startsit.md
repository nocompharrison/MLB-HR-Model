Welcome to another edition of Hoopes There It Is! We’re previewing every game in Week 9, highlighting advanced stats like EPA, success rate, pass rate over expected (PROE), and completion percentage over expected (CPOE) to help make the best decisions for your fantasy teams. I’ll provide a quick start/sit section at the top that stems from the longer write-ups. And if you’re interested in more, which I hope you are, you can [jump to any of the 15 games this week](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it#links). ## Start ‘Em, Sit ‘Em
[Jonah Coleman](https://www.4for4.com/player/jonah-coleman) (**Start**): With [RJ Harvey](https://www.4for4.com/player/rj-harvey) on IR, the Denver backfield consolidated to just JK Dobbins and [Jonah Coleman](https://www.4for4.com/player/jonah-coleman) last week. The plan going into the game seemed to favor Dobbins as he played three more snaps than Coleman in the 1st quarter, according to PFF. But seemingly after a big loss on a Dobbins stretch run, Sean Payton pushed more and more work onto Coleman, including 19 of the team’s 21 2nd-half snaps. Payton’s notorious for changing up his player usage on a dime, but two things are in Coleman’s favor this week. The first is [Bo Nix](https://www.4for4.com/player/bo-nix)’s allergy to air yards, which should benefit Coleman’s receiving work. And the second is the Panthers’ run defense, which is 25th in efficiency and 22nd in explosive run rate allowed.
[Quinshon Judkins](https://www.4for4.com/player/quinshon-judkins) (**Start**): [Quinshon Judkins](https://www.4for4.com/player/quinshon-judkins) saw 12 fewer snaps than [Dylan Sampson](https://www.4for4.com/player/dylan-sampson) last week. But that was only because of the game script. Sampson plays a lot more when the Browns are in catch-up mode, and that was the case for the entire game against the Steelers. But the sportsbooks think this game against the Saints will be close, meaning we’ll see more Judkins. Additionally, this Saints’ run defense is awful, ranking dead-last in efficiency and success rate allowed. And the pace of the Saints’ offense tends to create more plays for their opponents.
[Malik Willis](https://www.4for4.com/player/malik-willis) (**Start**): The Dolphins are implied for a solid 22 points this week, which is a testament to a Lions’ pass defense that is bottom-five in passing efficiency, success rate, and explosive play rate allowed. While the Dolphins’ pass game wasn’t as efficient as their fantastic Week 7 performance, they still were slightly above average in Week 8. Willis now has two passing TDs in consecutive games, 6+ carries in four of his seven games this season, and a fantastic matchup this week. He’s a low-end QB1 to me as the way the Dolphins want to attack, via deep passes, is exactly how Lions’ opponents have attacked them this season. We’ll need some passing explosives and rushing from Willis for this to hit.
Commanders’ RBs (**Sit**): The backfield split has cleaned up a bit here as [Jeremy McNichols](https://www.4for4.com/player/jeremy-mcnichols) has unfortunately landed on IR. He took 12 snaps for the Commanders before leaving last week. [Rachaad White](https://www.4for4.com/player/rachaad-white) took 28 snaps, while [Austin Ekeler](https://www.4for4.com/player/austin-ekeler) saw 20, according to PFF. If we only had one of these guys in our lives, I think the sell would be a lot easier. But it’s still a timeshare against a Rams’ run defense that is top 10 in efficiency, success rate, and explosive play rate allowed.

##  Jaguars at Ravens (O/U 42.5, JAX -9.5)
### Jaguars (26.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Jaguars were the most consistent team in pass rate over expected (PROE)…until Week 8 when they hit -15.4%.
  * To be fair, that was in a 42-7 demolition of the lowly Colts, but my fear for the pass catchers is we could see a similar situation this week against the Ravens with someone named [Joe Fagnano](https://www.4for4.com/player/joe-fagnano) at QB.
  * The Jags were efficient in the rare occasions they passed the ball last week, but that only resulted in 16 team targets, with no pass catcher seeing more than three.
  * This is perhaps the central theme of this series: individual player talent is important, but so is team-level volume.
  * I don’t think it’s insane to sit basically any non-RB here given the projected low number of targets and the lack of concentration in those targets last week.
  * [Bhayshul Tuten](https://www.4for4.com/player/bhayshul-tuten) is the only must-start player on this offense, though we’ll need to monitor his injury status as he was listed as a non-participant for Monday’s walkthrough and Tuesday’s practice.
  * In Week 8, Tuten led [Chris Rodriguez](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-rodriguez) in snaps, 27 to 12, and carries, 16 to six, while splitting the routes. 
  * The Ravens’ defense has been great at limiting explosive runs but otherwise has been below average in efficiency and success rate allowed.


### Ravens (16.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * What an absolutely terrible runout for this entire team, but specifically [Zay Flowers](https://www.4for4.com/player/zay-flowers).
  * Going into last week, Flowers had 7.8 yards per route run with [Lamar Jackson](https://www.4for4.com/player/lamar-jackson) at QB, but it was on a frustratingly small 45 routes.
  * Well, it sounds like we won’t see this combination for the rest of the 2026 season with Jackson suffering a season-ending thumb injury in Week 8.
  * With only 16.5 implied points coming off a game where they hit 1st percentile passing efficiency (somehow not the worst mark on the week), I’d be comfortable sitting all Ravens.
  * The run game at least hit 38th percentile efficiency last week, but [Derrick Henry](https://www.4for4.com/player/derrick-henry) hasn’t had a 20+ yard run in five weeks and hasn’t hit 60+ rushing yards in three weeks, and now plays one of the best run defenses in the league.
  * One potential positive for Henry, and negative for the player and the Jaguars, is that star DT DaVon Hamilton is potentially done for the season, meaning this matchup is not as tough as it looks on paper.


##  Bengals at Falcons (O/U 52.5, CIN -3.0)
### Bengals (27.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Bengals passed at a rate 13.8% above expectation last week; it’s rare to see a team this committed to the pass in the modern NFL.
  * That plus overtime gave the Bengals 47 team targets in Week 8, about triple the amount the Jaguars had, for example.
  * They were fantastic passing once again, hitting 83rd percentile efficiency and a 64.9% success rate; they’re up to 4th and 1st in those metrics this season, respectively.
  * The targets were heavily concentrated with Ja’Marr Chase earning 16, [Tee Higgins](https://www.4for4.com/player/tee-higgins) 11, [Chase Brown](https://www.4for4.com/player/chase-brown) nine, and [Mike Gesicki](https://www.4for4.com/player/mike-gesicki) six.
  * You’re starting Chase and Higgins every week against any opponent.
  * [Chase Brown](https://www.4for4.com/player/chase-brown) easily led the backfield with 51 snaps compared to [Samaje Perine](https://www.4for4.com/player/samaje-perine)’s 26, with Brown seeing 21 more routes, eight more targets, and eight more carries than Perine.
  * Brown *still* hasn’t scored a TD since Week 1, but there’s not much to do here besides start him against a Falcons defense that just got obliterated by the Buccaneers’ run game.
  * [Mike Gesicki](https://www.4for4.com/player/mike-gesicki) has 17 receptions and six TDs; he’s the poster boy for regression candidates.
  * The tricky part is he might keep getting away with it because of team injuries at the position; both [Tanner Hudson](https://www.4for4.com/player/tanner-hudson) and [Erick All](https://www.4for4.com/player/erick-all) were placed on IR in recent weeks.
  * And because of those injuries, Gesicki hit a season-high 67.6% snap rate, according to PFF.
  * We might have to start taking Gesicki seriously as a cheap way to get exposure to the Bengals’ passing attack.


### Falcons (24.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * This is a high implied point number for a team coming off zero points.
  * The Falcons passed at a rate 17.4% below expectation and hit 0th percentile passing efficiency and an awful 26.9% success rate.
  * Sportsbooks are taking last week as a fluke given the weather and the strength of the Buccaneers’ defense.
  * And I think we should largely do the same; you’re still starting [Bijan Robinson](https://www.4for4.com/player/bijan-robinson-0) and [Drake London](https://www.4for4.com/player/drake-london) every week with the Bengals’ defense a much easier task than the Buccaneers’.
  * Probably the most mad I’ve made people in this series was suggesting to bench [Kyle Pitts](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/pittsk1) last week, but I hope you did given his two-catch, six-yard performance.
  * But I think Pitts is viable this week given he ran 19 of the team’s 25 routes last week; he was 3rd in targets, and the Bengals have been very soft against opposing TEs for a while now.


##  Jets at Chiefs (O/U 46.5, KC -9.5)
### Jets (18.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * My initial thought is that I’m surprised by the nearly 10-point spread.
  * The Jets’ offense was great last week, hitting 80th percentile passing and 85th percentile rushing efficiency.
  * The box score still wasn’t great for [Garrett Wilson](https://www.4for4.com/player/garrett-wilson), but the 12 targets were really promising, and I’d continue to start him.
  * And I was just multiple weeks too early on [Adonai Mitchell](https://www.4for4.com/player/adonai-mitchell), who looked left for dead after Omar Cooper’s Week 7 performance.
  * But last week Mitchell earned nine targets to Cooper’s zero on the exact same number of routes; it looks like this is going to be tough to parse going forward.
  * I think Mitchell is very talented, but Cooper has also flashed and gets the benefit of playing in 12 personnel, with Mitchell only seeing 23% of the 12 personnel snaps, according to PFF.
  * The Chiefs’ pass defense is strong and has limited opponents to the lowest air yards per attempt in the league, so my preference would be to sit both Cooper and Mitchell this week given the team’s low implied point total.
  * It’s also going to be tricky to parse the RB situation with [Breece Hall](https://www.4for4.com/player/breece-hall) returning this week.
  * In Hall’s absence, [Braelon Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/braelon-allen) dominated the RB work and looked good in the process, so I’d assume he eats into Hall’s workload going forward.
  * And now the Jets get a Chiefs’ run defense that is top nine in efficiency, success rate, and explosive rate allowed.


### Chiefs (28.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * This is a great implied points number for the Chiefs, who had a nice game offensively despite a frustrating loss to the Broncos in Week 8.
  * They passed at a rate 6.8% above expectation and hit 74th percentile passing and 66th percentile rushing efficiency.
  * [Rashee Rice](https://www.4for4.com/player/rashee-rice) and [Travis Kelce](https://www.4for4.com/player/travis-kelce) paced the team with eight and seven targets, respectively, and both are starts against a Jets pass defense that is 28th in efficiency and 24th in success rate allowed.
  * And I know [Kenneth Walker](https://www.4for4.com/player/kenneth-walker) had a tough box score game, but you’re starting him every week against any opponent; he’s RB3 overall in expected fantasy points per game.
  * [Emmett Johnson](https://www.4for4.com/player/emmett-johnson) had a fantastic Week 8, earning 128 receiving yards, 121 of which came after the catch.
  * But he still needs to be on your bench because he only saw 12 snaps compared to Walker’s 56 and only had one carry; that’s just not nearly enough volume to be flex-worthy.


##  Lions at Dolphins (O/U 50.5, DET -7.0)
### Lions (28.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Lions’ PROE dropped sharply last week, as they passed at a rate 15.5% below expectation.
  * But I think that was game-plan specific against a Vikings’ defense that opponents ran on at the highest rate in the league to avoid their blitzes.
  * With that low PROE, the Lions only had 24 team targets, with [Amon-Ra St. Brown](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/stbra1) earning eight, [Jameson Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/jameson-williams) six, and [Sam LaPorta](https://www.4for4.com/player/sam-laporta) five. 
  * You’re starting St. Brown and [Jahmyr Gibbs](https://www.4for4.com/player/jahmyr-gibbs) every week against any opponent.
  * And you know what you’re getting with the tertiary pieces, which are volatile performances on one of the best offenses in the league.
  * Williams has been cooking recently, as he scored his fifth TD in three games against the Vikings; I’d fire him up again this week.
  * LaPorta has struggled over his last two games, but he’s still TE6 in expected fantasy points per game, so I’d keep starting him, especially against a Dolphins’ pass defense that is dead-last in efficiency, success rate, and CPOE allowed.
  * Gibbs only earned three targets last week, but he did have 23 carries and was the engine of the Lions’ victory, with the team hitting 92nd percentile rushing efficiency in the 2nd half of Week 8.


### Dolphins (21.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * This is a really nice implied point total for the Dolphins, which is a testament to a Lions’ pass defense that is bottom-five in passing efficiency, success rate, and explosive play rate allowed.
  * While the Dolphins’ pass game wasn’t as efficient as their fantastic Week 7 performance, they still were slightly above average in Week 8.
  * They’ve yet to pass at a rate above expectation this season, partially explaining why they’re 30th in plays per game.
  * That makes volume a concern for the pass catchers, along with how spread out the targets have been; four players saw between five and seven targets last week.
  * My preferred plays on this team are [Malik Willis](https://www.4for4.com/player/malik-willis) and [Ollie Gordon](https://www.4for4.com/player/ollie-gordon).
  * Willis has two passing TDs in consecutive games, has had 6+ carries in four of his seven games this season, and has a fantastic matchup this week; he’s a low-end QB1 to me.
  * And Gordon continues to dominate the RB work, taking 53 of the team’s 65 snaps last week, including running 25 of the team’s 36 routes.
  * It’s a rare week when the Dolphins are not near the bottom of the league in implied points, which is fantastic for both Willis and Gordon.


##  Rams at Commanders (O/U 47.5, LAR -5.5)
### Rams (26.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * It didn’t really matter because they were playing the Chargers, but the Rams didn’t pass the ball very efficiently in Week 8.
  * And it didn’t matter for [Puka Nacua](https://www.4for4.com/player/puka-nacua) either because he took half of the team’s 24 targets and over half of the team’s receiving yards.
  * You’re starting Nacua every week against any opponent.
  * And I don’t know [Terrance Ferguson](https://www.4for4.com/player/terrance-ferguson)’s status at the time I’m writing, but if he’s activated from IR and the coach speak seems positive, I’d be willing to start him.
  * It’s been messy at TE for the Rams, as [Tyler Higbee](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-higbee) has maintained the clear route lead in the room but hasn’t done anything with it.
  * Ferguson was fantastic in Week 2 with Nacua sidelined, and I’d be willing to risk starting him against a bad Commanders’ pass defense.
  * The RB split was the same as usual for the Rams, with [Kyren Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyren-williams) and [Blake Corum](https://www.4for4.com/player/blake-corum) rotating by drive but Williams playing the two-minute drill snaps and playing a lot more in close games. 
  * Williams is a must-start, and Corum has been outperforming his workload recently, mostly because he’s scored a TD in four consecutive games.
  * I prefer to play Corum in games the Rams are expected to win by a decent margin, and that is the expectation here against the Commanders.


### Commanders (21.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Like the Rams, the Commanders were below average in passing efficiency last week, but it didn’t matter because they were playing the Eagles (sad go birds).
  * [](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/willia3)[Antonio Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/antonio-williams) led the team with six targets and 29 routes (with a season-high snap rate) and turned that into a 5/54/0 line; I think he’s flex-viable depending on your league type.
  * After Williams, [Treylon Burks](https://www.4for4.com/player/treylon-burks) and [Stefon Diggs](https://www.4for4.com/player/stefon-diggs) each earned five targets, while Chig Okonkwo earned four.
  * Okonkwo was actually 2nd on the team in routes, and while I wouldn’t start him this week against a very good Rams’ pass defense, he should be on your watchlist.
  * It’s messy at RB, with [Rachaad White](https://www.4for4.com/player/rachaad-white) seeing 28 snaps, [Austin Ekeler](https://www.4for4.com/player/austin-ekeler) 20, and [Jeremy McNichols](https://www.4for4.com/player/jeremy-mcnichols) 12.
  * McNichols hurt his foot in the 3rd quarter and didn’t return to the game; he just landed on IR, so that makes it cleaner for White and Ekeler.
  * But I’d prefer not to start either against a Rams’ run defense that is top 10 in efficiency, success rate, and explosive play rate allowed.


##  Broncos at Panthers (O/U 43.5, DEN -2.5)
### Broncos (23.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w9/fantasy-football-week-9-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * A battle against two teams that used to generate air yards; [Bo Nix](https://www.4for4.com/player/bo-nix) and [Bryce Young](https://www.4for4.com/player/bryce-young) were both bottom-three in air yards per attempt in Week 8.
  * The Broncos were at least efficient in the process, hitting 78th percentile passing efficiency and getting a TD to the breakout article cover boy [Jaylen Waddle](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylen-waddle).
