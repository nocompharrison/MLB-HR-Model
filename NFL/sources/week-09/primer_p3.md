**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Jonah Coleman (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jonah-coleman.php)**
In Week 7, [Jonah Coleman](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jonah-coleman.php) led the backfield in snaps (50%), rushing attempts (nine, 34.6%), and was second in route share (21.9%), running one less route than [RJ Harvey](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rj-harvey.php). Coleman finished with 11 touches and 69 total yards. Last week, his role grew further with [RJ Harvey](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rj-harvey.php) out. Coleman finished with 15 touches and 104 total yards (RB12 for the week) with a 67.9% snap rate, a 47.8% rushing share, and a 50% route share (12.5% target share). This season, among 58 qualifying rushers, Coleman is first in explosive run rate and 19th in missed tackle rate. Coleman is a wonderful fantasy option this week, and he could continue to crush. Over their last five games, Carolina has allowed the tenth-highest explosive run rate, the ninth-highest yards before contact per attempt, and the fourth-highest missed tackle rate.
**[Jaylen Waddle (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jaylen-waddle.php)**
[Jaylen Waddle](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jaylen-waddle.php) is the WR42 in fantasy points per game, with only three games this season with 14 or more PPR points. [Bo Nix](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/bo-nix.php) has crushed him. Waddle has an 18.8% target share with 1.62 YPRR, four red zone targets, eight deep targets, and a 28.6% first-read share. This week, he faces a pass defense that has the fifth-highest single high rate (59.3%). Against single high, Waddle has had a 31% TPRR (leads the team) with 1.56 YPRR. Waddle could post a strong stat line this week if Nix can get him the ball. Over their last five games, Carolina has allowed the tenth-most yards per target and receiving fantasy points per game to perimeter wide receivers.
