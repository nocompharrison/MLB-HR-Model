##  **_Matchups Analysis - Sunday 9_** _:30 AM EST Game (Madrid)_
## **Cincinnati Bengals at Atlanta Falcons**
**Matchups We Love:**
**RB[Chase Brown](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24374/Chase+Brown)**
Chase Brown's usage in the passing game has kept him as a top-20 running back this season. Brown ranks second among all running backs in receptions per game and just totaled eight catches for 46 yards in last week's win. The good news for the Bengals playmaker is that the Falcons have given up the fourth-most receptions and third-most receiving touchdowns to RBs this season. Brown will be a solid RB2 play.
**TE[Kyle Pitts Sr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22508/Kyle+Pitts+Sr.) **
[Kyle Pitts](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22508/Kyle+Pitts) has been a much better fantasy option in recent weeks. Although he scored only 2.6 PPR fantasy points in a rain game last week, Pitts has finished as a top-12 fantasy TE in three of his last four games. This week, the Falcons TE will be a low-end TE1 in all formats. The Bengals have given up the second-most receptions and second-most receiving yards to opposing TEs in 2026.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**None**
**Other Matchups:**
**QB[Michael Penix Jr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23242/Michael+Penix+Jr.) **
Michael Penix Jr. will come in as a middle-of-the-pack QB2 in Superflex formats in Week 9. He has finished outside the top 16 at the QB position in four of his six starts and threw four interceptions against the Buccaneers last week. There is some potential for Penix in this game, though, as the Bengals rank 27th in pass yards allowed per game this season.
**QB[Joe Burrow](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21693/Joe+Burrow) **
Joe Burrow is on a roll right now. He has scored above 22 fantasy points in five straight games and has finished as the overall QB1 in back-to-back weeks. Last week, Burrow threw for 462 yards, five touchdowns, and one rushing touchdown en route to a 42.5 fantasy-point day. You're starting the Bengals signal-caller in all formats.
**RB[Bijan Robinson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23189/Bijan+Robinson) **
Bijan Robinson has been a bit quiet in recent weeks. He has scored under eight PPR fantasy points in two of the last three weeks and hasn't scored a touchdown since Week 4 against the Saints. Still, Robinson is a must-start RB1.
**Bengals WRs**
Both [Ja'Marr Chase](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22564/Ja'Marr+Chase) and [Tee Higgins](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21690/Tee+Higgins) should be in all lineups this week. Chase is always a must-start regardless of matchup. He has scored over 17 PPR fantasy points in five of his last six weeks. Higgins will come in as a must-start WR1 after back-to-back elite performances. He has finished as the WR3 and WR1 in consecutive weeks. Both are WR1s this weekend.
**WR[Drake London](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23151/Drake+London)**
Drake London didn't do much in the rain last week, catching only one pass for eight yards. However, London should get back on track in this matchup against the Bengals. The Falcons playmaker has over a 32% target share in six games with Penix under center, and Cincinnati let both Wan'Dale Robinson and [Chimere Dike](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/25987/Chimere+Dike) finish inside the top-16 at the position last week.
**TE[Mike Gesicki](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/19853/Mike+Gesicki) **
Mike Gesicki has been a touchdown machine for the Bengals this season. He has scored a touchdown in five straight games and is coming off a four-catch, 76-yard, one-touchdown performance against the Titans last week. Although his 9.2% target share is a bit worrisome, Gesicki's touchdown upside makes him a potential TE1 start in deeper leagues. Tennessee has given up four TE touchdowns this season.
**Injuries:**
None
##  **_Matchups Analysis - Sunday_** _1:00 EST Games_
## **Cleveland Browns at New Orleans Saints**
**Matchups We Love:**
**RB[Quinshon Judkins](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/25946/Quinshon+Judkins) **
Quinshon Judkins could be in for a big fantasy game in Week 9. Judkins will go up against a Saints defense that has given up the most fantasy points, second-most rushing yards, and most rushing touchdowns to opposing RBs this season. After both [Jaylen Warren](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23479/Jaylen+Warren) and [Rico Dowdle](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21952/Rico+Dowdle) finished with above 14 PPR fantasy points against this defense back in Week 7, Judkins is an RB2 play this weekend.
**TE[Harold Fannin Jr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/25870/Harold+Fannin+Jr.) **
[Harold Fannin](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/25870/Harold+Fannin) Jr. had his best fantasy game of the season a week ago. He caught 10 passes for 154 yards while finishing as the overall TE2 that week. Fannin will now get another plus matchup against the Saints secondary. New Orleans has given up the fifth-most fantasy points, receptions, and receiving yards to opposing TEs this season. Fannin will be a strong TE1 option.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**QB[Tyler Shough](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23187/Tyler+Shough)**
Tyler Shough has been an up-and-down fantasy option recently. He did finish as the overall QB2 in Week 6, but also scored 1.8 fantasy points against the Vikings in Week 5 and 14.8 fantasy points in Week 7. With another tough matchup on deck against a Browns defense that has allowed just one top-16 fantasy QB since Week 2, Shough is a riskier QB1 option.
**Other Matchups:**
