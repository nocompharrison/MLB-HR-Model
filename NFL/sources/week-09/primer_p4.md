**[Drew Lock (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/drew-lock.php)**
With [Sam Darnold](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/sam-darnold.php) sidelined with a quad injury, [Drew Lock](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/drew-lock.php) will make a start for Seattle. Funny enough, his last start of the season was against the Cardinals. In that game, he completed 73.1% of his passes with 235 yards through the air and three passing scores. Lock was the QB9 in weekly scoring that week. This season, among 40 qualifying passers, Lock is third in yards per attempt, seventh in highly accurate throw rate, 22nd in catchable target rate, and 11th in hero throw rate. Lock could have another QB1-level stat line this week. Arizona’s pass defense has been horrible. Over their last five games, the Cards have allowed the ninth-most yards per attempt, the fifth-highest passing touchdown rate, the sixth-most EPA per dropback, and the second-most fantasy points per dropback.

Over their last five games, they have allowed the sixth-lowest explosive run rate, the sixth-lowest rushing success rate, and the seventh-fewest yards after contact per attempt.

Gainwell won’t have an easy road this week, but if he gets 10-15 touches, he can still be flex-worthy.

**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Khalil Shakir (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/khalil-shakir.php)**
Since Week 4, Khalil Shakir has been a dependable, low-ceiling weekly PPR flex as the WR32 in fantasy points per game. In each of those games, he scored between 11 and 13.4 PPR points. Since Week 4, he has had an 18.5% target share with 2.55 YPRR, three red zone targets, one deep target, and a 21% first-read share. This week, he’ll face a Vikings secondary that has the fourth-highest two-high rate in the NFL (64%). Against two high, Shakir has a 23% TPRR and 2.14 YPRR. Josh Allen could lean on Shakir this week. Minnesota has been more giving to the slot. Over their last five games, they have allowed the 11th-most yards per target while ranking 15th in fantasy points per opportunity allowed to the position. Another feather in Shakir’s cap is how much his production has spiked when Josh Allen has been blitzed. Minnesota leads the NFL with a 74.2% blitz rate. Against the blitz, Shakir has seen his TPRR jump from 19% to 29% and his YPRR sky rocket from 1.37 to 3.63 (WOW!). Shakir could cook this week.
**[T.J. Hockenson (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tj-hockenson.php)**
[T.J. Hockenson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tj-hockenson.php) is the TE10 in fantasy points per game. Hockenson has an 18.7% target share with 2.22 YPRR, seven red zone targets, and a 20.6% first-read share. He could easily be a TE1 again this week against a defense that, over its last five games, has allowed the fifth-most yards per target to tight ends.
**Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Carson Wentz (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/carson-wentz.php)**
Carson Wentz will draw the start for Minnesota this week. In Wentz’s two games of action this season, he was the QB12 and QB30 in weekly scoring. Among 42 qualifying passers this season, Wentz is 20th in yards per attempt, 37th in passing success rate, 40th in highly accurate throw rate, and 37th in catchable target rate. Wentz likely isn’t the savior for this offense and passing attack. His accuracy issues have been substantial. The thing working in Wentz’s favor is the fact that the Bills’ pass defense hasn’t been a unit to be scared of this season. Over their last five games, Buffalo has allowed the 13th-highest passing touchdown rate, the 12th-highest passing success rate, the 13th-most EPA per dropback, and the 15th-most fantasy points per dropback. Wentz can post serviceable QB2 numbers this week.
**[Jordan Mason (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-mason.php)**
Over the last two games, [Jordan Mason](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-mason.php) has been the Vikings’ lead back, averaging only 12.5 touches and 44 total yards. Mason has had a 46.2% snap rate, 48.9% of the rushing work, and a 24.2% route share. He has led the Minny backfield in each of those categories over the last two games. Mason has posted a disappointing 1.89 yards after contact per attempt and an 8% missed tackle rate this season. With the limited volume and effectiveness, Mason is a touchdown-dependent flex this week. Over their last five games, the Bills have allowed the 12th-highest explosive run rate and rushing success rate, and the sixth-highest missed tackle rate.
**[Aaron Jones (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aaron-jones.php)**
Over the last two weeks, [Aaron Jones](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aaron-jones.php) has been a committee back with [Jordan Mason](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-mason.php), averaging eight touches and 33 total yards. In those games, he has had a 32.1% snap rate, 27.7% of the rushing attempts, and a 21% route share. Mason has bested him in each of those categories as Minny’s lead back. Jones has shown his age this season with only 1.97 yards after contact per attempt and a 12% missed tackle rate. He’s a touchdown-dependent flex this week. Over their last five games, the Bills have allowed the 12th-highest explosive run rate and rushing success rate and the sixth-highest missed tackle rate.
**[Jordan Addison (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-addison.php)**
Since Week 5, with [Justin Jefferson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/justin-jefferson.php) in the lineup and [Kyler Murray](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kyler-murray.php) under center, [Jordan Addison](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-addison.php) has been the WR56 in fantasy points per game. During that stretch, he has had a 20.8% target share with 1.54 YPRR, zero red zone targets, five deep targets, and a 25.6% first-read share. In those two games, Addison surpassed 50 receiving yards once. Addison is a decent flex this week with a good matchup. Over their last five games, Buffalo has allowed the third-most yards per target and the fifth-most fantasy points per opportunity to perimeter wide receivers.
**Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**

Over their last five games, Minnesota has held perimeter wide receivers to the fewest yards per target and the third-fewest fantasy points per opportunity. Moore will need a healthy dose of volume and a touchdown to overcome the rough matchup this week.
**[Dalton Kincaid (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dalton-kincaid.php)**

Overall, he has a 61.6% route share, a 17.1% target share, 2.75 YPRR, six red zone targets, five deep targets, and a 21.4% first-read share. This feels like another down week incoming for Kincaid. This week, he’ll face a Vikings secondary that has the fourth-highest two-high rate in the NFL (64%). Against two-high, Kincaid has a 20% TPRR with 1.44 YPRR. Over their last five games, Minnesota has allowed the fourth-fewest yards per target and the fewest fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends.
