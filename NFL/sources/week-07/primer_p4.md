**Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**
_Players in consideration for lineups, but temper your expectations this week._
**[Patrick Mahomes II (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/patrick-mahomes.php)**
[Patrick Mahomes](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/patrick-mahomes.php) is the QB7 in fantasy points per game with at least 21.4 fantasy points in three games this season. Among 32 qualifying passers, Mahomes is fifth in yards per attempt, third in passing success rate, seventh in hero throw rate, and eighth in EPA per dropback. Mahomes can still put up QB1 numbers this week, but I’m not expecting a high-end QB1 showing. Seattle has allowed the fewest yards per attempt, the second-lowest passing success rate, and the fifth-lowest passer rating.
**[Sam Darnold (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/sam-darnold.php)**
[Sam Darnold](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/sam-darnold.php) is the QB26 in fantasy points per game, but his game against Washington is still carrying all of the water there. In his two starts since that game, he hasn’t had more than 14.3 fantasy points in either outing. Among 32 qualifying passers, Darnold is 17th in yards per attempt, 26th in passer rating, 23rd in passing success rate, and he has the highest turnover-worthy pass rate. Sit Darnold again this week with a tough matchup incoming. Kansas City has allowed the fifth-fewest yards per attempt, the third-lowest passing success rate, the ninth-lowest CPOE, and the second-fewest fantasy points per dropback.
**[Emanuel Wilson (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/emanuel-wilson.php)**
I have zero confidence in starting anyone from the Seattle backfield right now. Last week, [Emanuel Wilson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/emanuel-wilson.php) lost his lead back role as he played only 32.9% of the snaps while having 53.8% of the rushing attempts and only a 13.8% route share (one target). If Seattle is playing from behind in this game, Wilson could see his role eaten up by [George Holani](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/george-holani.php), who had a 56.1% snap rate last week. Last week, Wilson finished with 14 touches and 52 scoreless rushing yards. Among 38 qualifying backs, Wilson is 26th in explosive run rate and 25th in missed tackle rate. Kansas City has allowed the sixth-lowest explosive run rate, the seventh-lowest rushing success rate, and the third-lowest missed tackle rate.
**[Rashee Rice (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rashee-rice.php)**
[Rashee Rice](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rashee-rice.php) opened with a DNP (hamstring) this week. I’ll update his outlook on Friday.
**[Xavier Worthy (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/xavier-worthy.php)**
[Rashee Rice](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rashee-rice.php) opened the week with a DNP (hamstring). I’ll update [Xavier Worthy](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/xavier-worthy.php)‘s outlook on Friday. It will vary massively depending on whether Rice is active or out.
**[Travis Kelce (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/travis-kelce.php)**
[Travis Kelce](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/travis-kelce.php) is still motoring along as the TE7 in fantasy points per game. Among 38 qualifying tight ends, Kelce is 13th in target share (16.5%), third in YPRR (2.41), fourth in FD/RR (0.121), and tenth in first-read share (19.1%). Kelce has three red zone targets and two deep targets this season. Kelce won’t have it easy this week, but he could still post a TE1 week as he presents an easier outlet for Mahomes than attacking Seattle’s talented corners. Seattle has allowed the third-fewest yards per target and the seventh-fewest fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends.

**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[DeVonta Smith (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/devonta-smith.php)**
[DeVonta Smith](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/devonta-smith.php) returned with a fury last week as the WR9. Overall, Smith is the WR10 in fantasy points per game. Last week, he had a 26.7% target share, 2.21 YPRR, one red zone target, three deep targets, and a 35.3% first-read share. Those are all alpha numbers. This week, he’ll face a tougher matchup, but Smith is talented and seeing enough volume to overcome it. Dallas has held perimeter wide receivers to the sixth-fewest yards per target and the 13th-fewest receiving fantasy points per game.
**[George Pickens (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/george-pickens.php)**
[George Pickens](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/george-pickens.php) is the WR23 in fantasy points per game, drawing a 20.3% target share with 1.96 YPRR, five red zone targets, seven deep targets, and a 22.4% first-read share. The matchup isn’t easy this week, but if Philly continues their recent trend of single high usage, it could be a good coverage structure for his volume. Since Week 5, Philly has had the eighth-highest rate of single high (60.5%). This season, Pickens’ splits against single high and two high have been dramatic. Against single high, his TPRR rises from 17% to 35%, and his YPRR jumps from 1.58 to 2.74. Philly has allowed the 13th-most yards per target to perimeter wide receivers, but only one touchdown. Pickens could pile up some yardage this week, but his touchdown prospects are dimmer.
**[Dallas Goedert (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dallas-goedert.php)**
Last week, [Dallas Goedert](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dallas-goedert.php) was the TE14 for the week with a 17.8% target share, 61 receiving yards (1.65 YPRR), and a 23.5% first-read share. Those are all solid if not damn good numbers. Goedert should be competing for the second spot in the target pecking order this week for Philly. He could easily be a TE1 this week. Dallas has allowed the 13th-most yards per target and the seventh-most fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends.
**Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Dak Prescott (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dak-prescott.php)**
[Dak Prescott](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dak-prescott.php) has been STRUGGLING this season as the QB17 in fantasy points per game. Over the last two games, Prescott hasn’t eclipsed 14.6 fantasy points in either game. A lot of this is on Prescott, but the play designs for Dallas have been bad. Dallas was 11th in play action usage and 13th in motion rate last year. This season, Brian Schottenheimer has made everything even harder on his offense and quarterback, as Dallas has the 12th-lowest play action usage and the third-lowest motion rate. It’s wild. I don’t understand what Schotty is doing, but hopefully he comes to his senses soon. When you pop open the hood, Prescott hasn’t been nearly as bad as I thought, and this could be correctable if Dallas gets back to their offensive bells and whistles. Among 33 qualifying quarterbacks, Prescott is 19th in yards per attempt and 17th in hero throw rate, but he is also 13th in passing success rate, first in highly accurate throw rate, and 11th in catchable target rate. Philly has still fielded a tough pass defense, allowing the sixth-lowest passing success rate, the 13th-lowest passer rating, and the 14th-lowest CPOE. Prescott should have time in the pocket, though. Philly has the fifth-lowest pressure rate and the third-lowest quick pressure rate. Prescott most likely posts low-end QB1/high-end QB2 numbers this week.
**[Tanner McKee (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tanner-mckee.php)**
[Tanner McKee](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tanner-mckee.php) limped to a QB22 finish last week. Among 29 passers, he was 26th in yards per attempt, 21st in passing success rate, 16th in highly accurate throw rate, and 22nd in catchable target rate. Ok, not great. He also doesn’t run, so we won’t be getting production on the ground from McKee. McKee could post better numbers this week against a hapless Dallas secondary that has allowed the fourth-most yards per attempt, the third-highest passing success rate, the fifth-highest CPOE, and the highest EPA per dropback. Dallas is 14th in pressure rate, so that’s a concern, but they are only 17th in quick pressure. McKee could have a nice game in Week 7.
**[Makai Lemon (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/makai-lemon.php)**
[Makai Lemon](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/makai-lemon.php) is someone who could pop off with a randomly strong game this week. So far this season, Lemon hasn’t scored a touchdown and hasn’t had more than 43 receiving yards in any game. That could change this week. Last week, Lemon had an 89.6% route share with an 11.1% target share, 6.20 aDOT, 19 receiving yards (0.44 YPRR), and a 14.7% first-read share. Is any of that outside of his route share good? Nope. Dallas has been having ISSUES with stopping slot receivers, though. Dallas has allowed the most yards per target, the most receiving yards, and the third-most fantasy points per opportunity to slot receivers this season. Lemon is an intriguing flex this week.

_Players in consideration for lineups, but temper your expectations this week._
**[Dontayvion Wicks (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dontayvion-wicks.php)**
Last week, [Dontayvion Wicks](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dontayvion-wicks.php) disappeared from the passing offense, with [DeVonta Smith](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/devonta-smith.php) and [Dallas Goedert](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dallas-goedert.php) both leading the way. We have to see how many weapons [Tanner McKee](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tanner-mckee.php) can support before believing in a tertiary receiver like Wicks.
**[Jake Ferguson (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jake-ferguson.php)**
[Jake Ferguson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jake-ferguson.php)‘s fantasy value has tanked this season as the TE25 in fantasy points per game. Ferguson has only a 9.8% target share, 0.69 YPRR, four red zone targets, zero deep targets, and an 8.4% first-read share. The matchup isn’t good enough for Ferguson to sniff the random streamer radar. Philly is 15th in yards per target allowed to tight ends, but they are also holding the position to the 12th-fewest fantasy points per opportunity (only two receiving scores). Sit Ferguson.
