Welcome to another edition of Hoopes There It Is! We’re previewing every game in Week 7, highlighting advanced stats like EPA, success rate, pass rate over expected (PROE), and completion percentage over expected (CPOE) to help make the best decisions for your fantasy teams. I’ll provide a quick start/sit section at the top that stems from the longer write-ups. And if you’re interested in more, which I hope you are, you can [jump to any of the 14 games this week](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it#links). Let’s get started!
## Start ‘Em, Sit ‘Em
[Tyler Higbee](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-higbee) (**Start**): [Tyler Higbee](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-higbee) suddenly became very important at work because [Davante Adams](https://www.4for4.com/player/davante-adams)’ hamstring injury is expected to sideline him for six to 10 weeks. [Terrance Ferguson](https://www.4for4.com/player/terrance-ferguson) is eligible to return from IR in Week 8, but recent reporting suggests he won’t be activated for a few weeks after that. That makes Higbee the 2nd receiving weapon for the Rams in my opinion. [Colby Parkinson](https://www.4for4.com/player/colby-parkinson) was 2nd on the team in targets last week with six, but he ran only 19 of the team’s 36 routes, compared to 29 for Higbee. The Rams have a high 26 implied points, and the Raiders’ pass defense is bad, ranking in the bottom eight of both efficiency and success rate allowed.
[Jake Ferguson](https://www.4for4.com/player/jake-ferguson) (**Sit**): The Cowboys trailed throughout Week 6 and passed at a rate about 5% above expectation, leading to a massive 52 targets for the team. Well, [Jake Ferguson](https://www.4for4.com/player/jake-ferguson) only earned three of them and finished the day with a 2/13/0 line. He was out-targeted by fellow TE [Brevyn Spann-Ford](https://www.4for4.com/player/brevyn-spann-ford), even though Spann-Ford only ran 16 of the team’s 56 routes. But Spann-Ford took over as the primary TE in 11 personnel on early downs, according to PFF, and that was enough to limit Ferguson to only 29 routes or about a 52% route share. Ferguson has exceeded 23 receiving yards once this season, and he’s TE28 in expected fantasy points per game.
[A.J. Brown](https://www.4for4.com/player/aj-brown) (**Start**): This one should be obvious, but I thought it needed to be said. [A.J. Brown](https://www.4for4.com/player/aj-brown) returned from his ankle injury in Week 6 and paced the team with nine targets. But the problem was he only caught three of them as he struggled with drops. This is your reminder that drops do not matter at all unless they start changing coaching decisions. That’s not going to be an issue in Week 7. And Brown now gets a Bears pass defense that is dead-last in explosive play rate allowed. Follow the targets, ignore the drops.
All Giants RBs (**Sit**): I hope you caught that I updated the article last week when the reporting came out that [Najee Harris](https://www.4for4.com/player/najee-harris) would start at RB for the Giants. I called [Cam Skattebo](https://www.4for4.com/player/cam-skattebo) a start and then felt the need to update that call given the Giants’ coaching staff lost their minds. Well, Harris is already off the team, but the Giants are still expected to play three RBs with an unknown split. And the team might be starting JJ McCarthy with about 16 implied points. This is a situation to avoid entirely.
[Germie Bernard](https://www.4for4.com/player/germie-bernard) (**Start**): [Germie Bernard](https://www.4for4.com/player/germie-bernard) is still only started in 40% of Yahoo fantasy leagues, and I think that should be much higher. The Steelers are now 2nd in PROE, and they’ve only passed at a rate below expectation once this season. So, we’re getting a lot of team targets that have been heavily concentrated on just two players: [DK Metcalf](https://www.4for4.com/player/dk-metcalf) and Bernard. Now the Steelers get a Saints pass defense that is bottom-five in success rate allowed and typically see a lot of plays against them because of how fast their offense plays. The only other thing we’d like to see is [Aaron Rodgers](https://www.4for4.com/player/aaron-rodgers) bring back some air yards in the offense.

##  Patriots at Bears (O/U 50.5, CHI -3.5)
### Patriots (23.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Week 6 was another efficient passing day for [Drake Maye](https://www.4for4.com/player/drake-maye), as the Pats hit 71st percentile passing EPA per play but threw the ball at a rate 4.1% below expectation.
  * [A.J. Brown](https://www.4for4.com/player/aj-brown) paced the team with nine targets but only caught three of them as he struggled with drop issues.
  * But for our purposes, drops do not matter at all unless they start changing coaching decisions, and I think we’re a long way off from that.
  * The matchup for Brown isn’t amazing this week as the Bears’ pass defense is 7th in efficiency and 3rd in success rate allowed, but somehow, they’re dead-last in explosive play rate allowed.
  * Fire up Brown with confidence, and then someone like [Demario Douglas](https://www.4for4.com/player/demario-douglas) could be an option as [Romeo Doubs](https://www.4for4.com/player/romeo-doubs) just landed on IR due to a shoulder injury he suffered in Week 6.
  * At RB, [Rhamondre Stevenson](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/stever1) remains the back you want as he out-snapped [TreVeyon Henderson](https://www.4for4.com/player/treyveon-henderson) 41 to 16 in Week 6 and out-carried him 14 to seven.
  * But [Corey Kiner](https://www.4for4.com/player/corey-kiner) seemingly won’t go away, as he only played 12 snaps but got 11 carries on those snaps and was much more efficient than Stevenson on that work.
  * The Bears are bottom eight in the league in rushing efficiency and explosive run rate allowed, but this is becoming an even tougher backfield to parse.


### Bears (27.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * [Caleb Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/caleb-williams) returned in Week 6, and the Bears opted to push the pass volume, hitting +7.4% PROE, which was a new season high.
  * But they also cut out the air yards, with Williams tying [Aaron Rodgers](https://www.4for4.com/player/aaron-rodgers) for the lowest air yards per attempt in Week 6 at only 3.6. 
  * They still managed 65th percentile passing efficiency in the process, and Williams was 2nd behind only [C.J. Stroud](https://www.4for4.com/player/cj-stroud) in completion percentage over expectation. 
  * That style of play should benefit [Luther Burden](https://www.4for4.com/player/luther-burden) and [Colston Loveland](https://www.4for4.com/player/colston-loveland) the most, and they easily paced the team with targets, earning eight and seven, respectively.
  * The weird thing about Burden was he hit a season low in snaps despite earning a new season high in targets.
  * But I think that was mostly game script, as the Bears had a massive lead against the Falcons for most of the game, with the Falcons only getting the score close at the end due to a 16-point 4th quarter.
  * And the Bears use a lot more heavy-TE sets with a lead, with Burden essentially not playing in 13 personnel and hitting only a 16% snap rate in 12 personnel over his last two games, according to PFF.
  * If we expect this game to stay close, then we should see more Burden.
  * At RB, [Kyle Monangai](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyle-monangai) was listed as a practice non-participant both Monday and Tuesday, increasing the odds he sits once again in Week 7.
  * With Monangai sidelined in Week 6, D’Andre Swift led [Roschon Johnson](https://www.4for4.com/player/roschon-johnson) in snaps 45 to 31, routes 25 to 14, targets four to two, and carries 16 to 13. 
  * Swift is a must-start given the implied points and the generally below-average state of the Patriots’ run defense.


##  Steelers at Saints (O/U 44.5, PIT -3.5)
### Steelers (24.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Steelers had about the same high PROE and low aDoT as the Bears last week; the difference is the Steelers only hit 19th percentile efficiency.
  * But the Steelers are now 2nd in PROE in the league, passing at a rate below expectation only once this season.
  * That led to a high 43 team targets, which were heavily concentrated on [DK Metcalf](https://www.4for4.com/player/dk-metcalf) and [Germie Bernard](https://www.4for4.com/player/germie-bernard), with each player seeing 12+ targets.
  * This Saints’ pass defense is generally bad, as they’re bottom-five in success rate allowed, and they see a lot of plays against them because the Saints’ offense plays at a fast pace.
  * Metcalf and Bernard are clear starts given the target concentration, state of the Saints’ pass defense, and the potential for a high number of plays, but it sure would be nice if Rodgers decided to attempt deeper passes again.
  * At RB, [Rico Dowdle](https://www.4for4.com/player/rico-dowdle) returned from injury in Week 6.
  * And the Steelers actually gave [Jaylen Warren](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylen-warren) most of the work, as Warren led Dowdle in snaps 59 to 21, routes 31 to 11, and carries 14 to seven.
  * The snaps were actually close through three quarters, according to PFF, but Warren took 31 of 37 snaps in the 4th quarter and overtime.
  * Warren is a clear start against the Saints’ league-worst run defense.


### Saints (20.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Saints passed at a rate slightly above expectation in each of their first four games, and then have sharply reversed course, going really run-heavy over their last two games.
  * And that’s annoying because their fast pace and high pass rate were letting the skill players crush despite poor efficiency from [Tyler Shough](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-shough-ore).
  * [Chris Olave](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-olave) and [Devaughn Vele](https://www.4for4.com/player/devaughn-vele) paced the team in targets once again, but it was only seven and five, respectively, compared to the double digits we saw through four weeks.
  * Olave is the only must-start against a strong Steelers’ pass defense that is at least susceptible to explosive plays.
  * It’s still a mess at RB, as [Kendre Miller](https://www.4for4.com/player/kendre-miller), C.J. Donaldson, and [Alvin Kamara](https://www.4for4.com/player/alvin-kamara) all saw at least 19 snaps.
  * If you’re going to start one, Miller is the clear pick as he saw 20 carries on his 32 snaps last week.
  * The issue is that Miller isn’t very good, as only 2% of his carries have gone for 10+ yards this season; only [David Montgomery](https://www.4for4.com/player/david-montgomery) has been worse.


##  Giants at Texans (O/U 42.5, HOU -10.0)
### Giants (16.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The market must be assuming that [J.J. McCarthy](https://www.4for4.com/player/jj-mccarthy) is starting this week because nearly 16 implied points are brutal.
  * We’re also looking at a new starting RB for the Giants, as they decided to cut [Najee Harris](https://www.4for4.com/player/najee-harris) after naming him the starter, giving him four carries, and quickly seeing the error of their ways.
  * Even if it’s McCarthy at the helm, you’re starting [Malik Nabers](https://www.4for4.com/player/malik-nabers) and [Isaiah Likely](https://www.4for4.com/player/isaiah-likely) as they’ve dominated the passing volume to date, including 10+ targets for each of them in Week 6.
  * Likely actually led the team with 51 routes, quickly followed by Nabers’ 49.
  * But you can’t possibly start a Giants RB until we see some stability in the split between the three remaining backs, let alone the very low team implied points.


### Texans (26.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * [C.J. Stroud](https://www.4for4.com/player/cj-stroud) was awesome last week, with the Texans hitting 89th percentile passing efficiency and leaning into the pass with a +3.6% PROE.
  * The offensive line is still bad, as Stroud was pressured on 40% of his dropbacks and faced a quick pressure on 26% of his dropbacks, according to Sumer Sports.
  * But it didn’t matter because Stroud went 11/13 for 147 yards and 2 TDs when facing pressure.
  * It was weird that [Nico Collins](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/collin1) only earned four targets, though you’re starting Collins every week.
  * [Jaylin Noel](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylin-noel) and [Marvin Harrison Jr.](https://www.4for4.com/player/marvin-harrison-jr) actually tied Collins with four targets each, though neither played much.
  * Noel only ran 22 of the team’s 36 routes, and Harrison was really eased in with only nine routes.
  * And now [Tank Dell](https://www.4for4.com/player/tank-dell) is going to play this week, so I’d hold off on starting any WR besides Collins until we see how the team splits up the routes between Noel, Harrison, and Dell.
  * At RB, my assumption is that [David Montgomery](https://www.4for4.com/player/david-montgomery) sits once again because he didn’t practice at all last week and the Texans have their bye in Week 8.
  * In Montgomery’s absence, [Woody Marks](https://www.4for4.com/player/woody-marks) dominated the work, running 25 routes to [Jawhar Jordan](https://www.4for4.com/player/jawhar-jordan)’s four and taking 15 carries to Jordan’s six.
  * I know that Jordan scored a rushing TD, but six carries and one target just aren’t enough for sustainable production, with Marks a reasonable start against a below-average Giants’ run defense.


##  Buccaneers at Panthers (O/U 44.5, CAR -3.5)
### Buccaneers (20.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The spread being this close is a testament to the Buccaneers’ defense rather than anything the offense is doing.
  * The Bucs’ offense hit just 11th percentile passing efficiency in Week 6 and a brutal 32.1% success rate.
  * But [Emeka Egbuka](https://www.4for4.com/player/emeka-egbuka) earned 16 (!) of the team’s 40 targets last week and is a must-start given that massive amount of volume.
  * And the only other Buccaneer you’re starting is [Bucky Irving](https://www.4for4.com/player/bucky-irving), who dominated the RB work in Week 6, seeing 63 snaps to Kenny Gainwell’s 11.
  * Week 6 was a new career-high in snap rate for Irving, according to PFF.
  * And we could see volume for the offense here, as the Panthers’ offense is top-three in PROE and plays per game this season, with the Bucs offense potentially needing to keep pace.


### Panthers (24.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Panthers’ offense is up to 8th in efficiency and is average in success rate; both of those are much better than we expected coming into the season.
  * And they’re giving fantasy managers a lot of volume to go along with that efficiency, as they’re playing fast and passing a lot.
  * [Jalen Coker](https://www.4for4.com/player/jalen-coker) and [Tetairoa McMillan](https://www.4for4.com/player/tetairoa-mcmillan) are must-starts as they are consolidating the passing volume, taking 19 of the team’s 31 targets last week.
  * And then [Chuba Hubbard](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/hubbac1) is also a must-start as he saw an insane 74 of the team’s 79 snaps in Week 6.
  * That included 28 of the 29 RB carries, though he only earned one target on 35 routes given his limitations as a receiver.
  * Tough matchups all around against a very good Bucs defense, but we should see a lot of passing attempts as the Panthers are 3rd in PROE and opponents pass on the Bucs at the 4th-highest rate in the league.


##  Colts at Vikings (O/U 41.5, MIN -6.5)
### Colts (17.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Colts’ passing attack has been bad this season, and they were bad once again in Week 6, hitting only 35th percentile efficiency and passing at a rate 5.3% below expectation.
  * [Keenan Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/keenan-allen) and [Josh Downs](https://www.4for4.com/player/josh-downs) paced the team with 12 and seven targets, respectively, but both are top-seven in the league in the difference between their expected fantasy points and their actual fantasy points.
  * That’s really a systemic problem with this Colts’ passing attack, and the team now faces a Vikings’ pass defense that is 1st in efficiency, success rate, and CPOE allowed.
  * I’d expected a very run-heavy approach from the Colts, as opponents of the Vikings run on them at the 2nd-highest rate in the league vs. expectation, and the Colts are already 24th in PROE.
  * I’d be willing to sit all my Colts’ pass catchers given the matchup, depending on my other options.
  * You’re obviously starting [Jonathan Taylor](https://www.4for4.com/player/jonathan-taylor), but the Vikings’ defense is also 1st in rushing efficiency and success rate allowed.
  * Temper your expectations across the board in this one.


### Vikings (24.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The undefeated Vikings are coming off their bye week.
  * In Week 5, [Justin Jefferson](https://www.4for4.com/player/justin-jefferson) paced the team with seven targets and is obviously a must-start every week.
  * And then [Aaron Jones](https://www.4for4.com/player/aaron-jones) took all 25 of the team’s RB carries and ran 27 of the team’s 40 routes.
  * But we’ll get [Jordan Mason](https://www.4for4.com/player/jordan-mason) back in our lives this week, and my expectation is that they’ll be keen to give him the bulk of the carries.
  * It’s a good matchup as well, as the Colts’ run defense is bottom-three in both efficiency and success rate allowed.
  * It’s hard to recommend Mason as a start until we hear more coach speak about his role or whether he’ll be limited in his first game back, but I’d lean toward starting him.


##  Bengals at Ravens (O/U 51.5, BAL -3.0)
### Bengals (24.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w7/fantasy-football-week-7-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Bengals are coming off their bye and a win against the Dolphins before that.
  * In that Week 5 win, Ja’Marr Chase earned 10 of the team’s 30 targets, followed by only four for [Tee Higgins](https://www.4for4.com/player/tee-higgins); both are must-starts every week.
  * It’s messier at RB as [Chase Brown](https://www.4for4.com/player/chase-brown) and [Samaje Perine](https://www.4for4.com/player/samaje-perine) essentially split the snaps and carries in Week 5; Brown did run 23 routes compared to Perine’s 12.
  * Brown continues to pop in my RB buy-low model because he’s been getting a lot of work but has been inefficient on his rushing attempts.
