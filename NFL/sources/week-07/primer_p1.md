**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Rhamondre Stevenson (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rhamondre-stevenson.php)**
[Rhamondre Stevenson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rhamondre-stevenson.php) remained New England’s lead back last week, but [TreVeyon Henderson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/treveyon-henderson.php) and [Corey Kiner](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/corey-kiner.php) cut into his early-down workload. Stevenson played 60.3% of the snaps with a 42.9% route share, but he had only 38.9% of the rushing work. Stevenson finished with 14 touches and 36 total yards. Overall, he has averaged 14.3 touches and 62.3 total yards this season. Among 38 qualifying rushers, Stevenson is 17th in yards after contact per attempt and 11th in missed tackle rate. Stevenson should return RB2 value this week. Chicago has allowed the 11th-highest explosive run rate, the seventh-highest rushing success rate, and the third-most yards before contact per attempt.
**[Luther Burden (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/luther-burden-iii.php)**
[Luther Burden](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/luther-burden-iii.php) is the WR26 in fantasy points per game after finishing as the WR13 in weekly scoring last week. Burden has a 20.7% target share with 2.31 YPRR, nine red zone targets, three deep targets, and a 26.2% first-read share. It won’t be a cakewalk for Burden this week, but he could see an elevated target share with [Rome Odunze](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rome-odunze.php) likely dealing with a [Christian Gonzalez](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/christian-gonzalez.php) shadow. Burden has the talent and a smart playcaller to overcome a tough matchup. New England has limited slot receivers to the ninth-fewest yards per target and the 11th-fewest fantasy points per opportunity.
**[A.J. Brown (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aj-brown.php)**
[AJ Brown](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aj-brown.php) returned to the huddle last week. He played his usual role as New England’s clear alpha, but he had a rough day at the office with four drops. Brown finished with only three receptions and 27 receiving yards, but his usage was fantastic. Brown had a 28.1% target share (94.3% route share), three red zone targets, one deep target, and a 42.9% first-read share. This is a wonderful bounce-back spot for Brown. Chicago has the ninth-highest rate of single-high (56.1%). Against single high, Brown has had a 29% target-per-route run rate this season. Yes, he has produced only 1.0 YPRR against single high, but that is small sample variance. In 2023-2025, against single high, Brown had a 35% target-per-route run rate and 3.53 YPRR against single high. He has historically shredded single high and could do so again this week against a secondary that has allowed the most yards per target and fantasy points per opportunity to perimeter wide receivers.
**[Colston Loveland (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/colston-loveland.php) **
[Colston Loveland](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/colston-loveland.php) has started to resemble the player that we thought he’d be for 2026 over the last three weeks. Yes, during this stretch he has only been the TE14 in fantasy points per game, but in those three games his usage has trended upward. Since Week 4, Loveland has had a 19.1% target share (70.2% route share) with 1.62 YPRR, six red zone targets, and a 26.2% first-read share. Loveland could be a TE1 again this week with targets being pushed in his direction, with [Rome Odunze](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rome-odunze.php) facing a [Christian Gonzalez](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/christian-gonzalez.php) shadow. New England is 17th in yards per target while allowing the fifth-fewest fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends. New England held [Dalton Kincaid](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dalton-kincaid.php), [Pat Freiermuth](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/pat-freiermuth.php), and [Brenton Strange](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/brenton-strange.php) to quiet days, but [Brock Bowers](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/brock-bowers.php) destroyed them with eight grabs, 110 receiving yards, and a score. I don’t project a blow-up game for Loveland, but he could still be a low-end TE1 this week.
**Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Roschon Johnson (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/roschon-johnson.php)**
[Roschon Johnson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/roschon-johnson.php) has been solid over the last two weeks. Over the last two games, he has had a 39.7% snap rate, a 31.6% rushing share, and a 34.1% route share (11.8% target share). He has averaged 12.5 touches and 62.5 total yards with RB31 and RB15 finishes in weekly scoring. Johnson hasn’t been impressive on a per-touch basis with only 1.82 yards after contact per attempt and a 14% missed tackle rate. New England could allow him to flirt with strong flex value again this week. New England has allowed the 11th-highest rushing success rate, the sixth-most yards after contact per attempt, and the 17th-highest missed tackle rate.
**[TreVeyon Henderson (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/treveyon-henderson.php)**
Last week, [TreVeyon Henderson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/treveyon-henderson.php) had only a 23.5% snap share, a 19.4% rushing share, and a 20% route share (3.1% target share). Henderson finished with eight touches and 40 total yards. [Corey Kiner](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/corey-kiner.php) cut into his early-down load. I expect Henderson to flirt with 8-10 touches again this week as a middling flex with a good matchup. Among 38 qualifying backs, Henderson is 28th in yards after contact per attempt and 31st in missed tackle rate. Henderson likely needs a score to pay off this week, but if he rips off a big run, he could get it. Chicago has allowed the 11th-highest explosive run rate, the seventh-highest rushing success rate, and the third-most yards before contact per attempt.
**[Efton Chism (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/efton-chismi.php)**
[Efton Chism](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/efton-chismi.php) is an interesting deep-league dart throw this week. Over the last two games, Chism has had snap shares of 80.3% and 61.8% with route shares of 65.8% and 25.7%. His target share in these two games has also been all over the map with 17.6% and 3.1%. What gives me hope for Chism is his usage against single-high this season. Chicago has the ninth-highest rate of single-high (56.1%). Against single-high, Chism leads the team with a 32% TPRR and 2.42 YPRR. With the Pats always deploying matchup-specific game plans weekly, it wouldn’t shock me at all if Chism is heavily utilized this week. If that’s the case, Chism could have a strong performance. Chicago has allowed the most yards per target and fantasy points per opportunity to perimeter wide receivers.
**[DeMario Douglas (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/demario-douglas.php)**
Last week, [DeMario Douglas](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/demario-douglas.php) had a 58.8% snap rate, a 74.3% route share, a 25% target share, 86 receiving yards (3.31 YPRR), and a 19% first-read share. It was the third time this season that Douglas has had a route share north of 70%, but he also has two games where his route share dipped below 60%. His usage has been up and down over the last two seasons, which makes me skeptical that it will hold in Week 7. Overall, Douglas has a 65.2% route share, a 14.8% target share, 1.62 YPRR, three red zone targets, eight deep targets, and a 16.9% first-read share. Chicago has the ninth-highest rate of single-high (56.1%). Against single-high, Douglas has a 23% TPRR, but his YPRR has fallen from 1.85 to 1.40. Douglas is flex-viable this week, but there’s a ton of volatility with his projection. Chicago has allowed the 14th-fewest yards per target and the 15th-fewest fantasy points per opportunity to slot receivers. It’s essentially a neutral matchup for Douglas.
**[Hunter Henry (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/hunter-henry.php)**
[Hunter Henry](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/hunter-henry.php) is the TE18 in fantasy points per game, with TE1 finishes in weekly scoring in each of the last two games (TE8, TE6). Henry has a 12.2% target share, 1.63 YPRR, five red zone targets, one deep target, and a 13.6% first-read share. Henry is on the streaming radar this week against a defense that has allowed the tenth-most yards per target and the 14th-most fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends this season.

I’m worried [Rome Odunze](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rome-odunze.php) will see [Christian Gonzalez](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/christian-gonzalez.php) all day. [Christian Gonzalez](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/christian-gonzalez.php) has followed [DK Metcalf](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dk-metcalf.php), [Garrett Wilson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/garrett-wilson.php), and [Jaxon Smith-Njigba](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jaxon-smith-njigba.php) this season on 65.4-86.4% of their routes. Wilson and Metcalf were held scoreless and below 50 receiving yards when lined up opposite Gonzalez. Sit Odunze this week, if possible.

He could easily be adding to the 20-plus fantasy point total this week. Rodgers has flip-flopped between low aDOT point guard and Jameis Rodgers this season. Despite his weekly volatility, Rodgers could have a nice Week 7 against a struggling Saints pass defense. New Orleans has allowed the seventh-most yards per attempt, the fourth-highest passing success rate, and the sixth-highest CPOE.
**[DK Metcalf (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dk-metcalf.php)**
[DK Metcalf](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dk-metcalf.php) is heating up as the WR32 in fantasy points per game with top 20 finishes in two of his last three games in weekly scoring (WR16, WR8). Metcalf has a 22.1% target share with 1.35 YPRR, three red zone targets, 12 deep targets (third most among wide receivers), and a 31.5% first-read share. Metcalf should shred the Saints secondary this week. New Orleans has the third-highest rate of single high (64.2%) in the NFL and has struggled to defend deep passing. Against single high, Metcalf’s TPRR has increased from 20% to 23% while his YPRR has risen from 1.17 to 1.44. Historically Metcalf has been MUCH better against single high. Metcalf should also convert his robust downfield role into fantasy production this week against a secondary that has allowed the eighth-most deep passing yards and the highest passing success rate to downfield passing. The Saints have allowed the ninth-most fantasy points per opportunity to perimeter wide receivers while ranking 15th in receiving fantasy points per game to the position.
**[Germie Bernard (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/germie-bernard.php)**

The Saints have allowed the 12th-most yards per target to slot receivers while ranking 15th in fantasy points per opportunity against the position.
**[Juwan Johnson (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/juwan-johnson.php)**
