##  **_Matchups Analysis - Sunday 9_** _:30 EST Game (Paris)_
## **Pittsburgh Steelers at New Orleans Saints**
**Matchups We Love:**
**Steelers RBs**
**Update: Dowdle is listed as questionable**
The Steelers running back room has a prime opportunity in Week 7. The Saints have allowed the most fantasy points, most rushing yards, and most rushing touchdowns to opposing RBs this season. [Jaylen Warren](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23479/Jaylen+Warren) remains the top RB in this offense after playing 75% of the team's snaps and seeing 16 touches in last week's win. Warren will be a high-end RB2, while [Rico Dowdle](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21952/Rico+Dowdle) has some flex appeal in deeper formats.
**TE[Pat Freiermuth](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22507/Pat+Freiermuth) **
Pat Freiermuth was held in check last week, catching just two passes for 11 yards. But fantasy managers could expect a bounce-back performance from Freiermuth in Week 7. The Saints have given up the fifth-most fantasy points, fourth-most receptions, and fifth-most receiving yards to opposing TEs this season, which included allowing [Isaiah Likely](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23256/Isaiah+Likely) to total 23.9 PPR fantasy points last week. He is a low-end TE1.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**QB[Tyler Shough](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23187/Tyler+Shough) **
Tyler Shough has a tough matchup ahead. The Steelers have been one of the best defenses against fantasy QBs in the early going, allowing the fourth-fewest fantasy points and sixth-fewest passing touchdowns. Only one QB has scored above 14 fantasy points and thrown for multiple passing touchdowns against this defense this year. That makes Shough more of a QB2 this week.
**Other Matchups:**
**QB[Aaron Rodgers](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/2593/Aaron+Rodgers)**
Aaron Rodgers has been a reliable QB1 in recent weeks. He has finished as a top-12 fantasy QB in three of the last four weeks and has thrown multiple passing touchdowns in four straight contests. Rodgers will now face a Saints secondary that has allowed over 18 fantasy points to a QB room in three of the past four games. The veteran signal-caller will be a low-end QB1.
**RB[Alvin Kamara](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/18878/Alvin+Kamara) **
Alvin Kamara hasn't run the ball well since [Travis Etienne Jr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21696/Travis+Etienne+Jr.) went down. Kamara has yet to run for over 40 yards in a game and is averaging just 3.4 yards per carry on the season. Still, the Saints running back has two top-12 fantasy RB finishes over the last three weeks, thanks to his usage in the passing game. That makes him a potential flex play against a Steelers defense that ranks 22nd in both receptions and receiving yards to RBs.
**Steelers WRs**
Both [DK Metcalf](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/20875/DK+Metcalf) and [Germie Bernard](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/26750/Germie+Bernard) were heavily involved in last week's win. Metcalf caught 11 of his 15 targets for 93 yards, while Bernard caught 11 of his 12 targets for 87 yards and a touchdown. This week, both Steelers WRs will WR2 plays. The Saints have allowed the 11th-most fantasy points to outside WRs and the 12th-most fantasy points to slot WRs this season. That will help Metcalf (outside) and Bernard (slot) this week.
**Saints WRs**
Fantasy managers can likely only trust [Chris Olave](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22565/Chris+Olave) in this passing attack right now. Olave will be a must-start WR1 against a Steelers secondary that has allowed the seventh-most fantasy points to outside WRs. As for [Devaughn Vele](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/25198/Devaughn+Vele), he will come in as more of a WR4. He has scored under 10 PPR fantasy points in three of the last four weeks.
**TE[Juwan Johnson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21738/Juwan+Johnson)**
Juwan Johnson had his worst fantasy performance of the season in Week 6. He didn't catch either of his two targets and was held to zero fantasy points. However, Johnson still has some TE1 appeal in 12+ team leagues this week. The Steelers have allowed a top-12 fantasy TE finish in three of the last four weeks. With a 15.1% target share on the season, he could be a nice start.
**Injuries:**
None
##  **_Matchups Analysis - Sunday_** _1:00 EST Games_
## **Cincinnati Bengals at Baltimore Ravens**
**Matchups We Love:**
**WR[Zay Flowers](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23120/Zay+Flowers)**
Zay Flowers is a must-start WR1 play this week against the Bengals. Flowers ranks sixth in fantasy points per game, eighth in receptions per game, and fifth in receiving yards per game among all WRs this season. He now gets a matchup against a Bengals secondary that has given up the most fantasy points to slot WRs over the last three weeks. You're starting Flowers in all formats.
**TE[Mark Andrews](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/19803/Mark+Andrews)**
Mark Andrews' fantasy value is obviously declining. He has scored under six PPR fantasy points in three of his last four games and is coming off a one-catch, four-yard performance against the Browns last week. However, Andrews has some TE1 appeal in deeper leagues this week. The Bengals have given up the fourth-most receptions and second-most receiving yards to opposing TEs.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**None**
**Other Matchups:**
**QB[Joe Burrow](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21693/Joe+Burrow)**
Joe Burrow will enter this Week 7 matchup against the Ravens trending up. He has scored above 22 fantasy points in three straight games and has thrown eight passing touchdowns during this stretch. While Baltimore has given up the third-fewest passing touchdowns this season, Burrow is still a solid QB1 start this week.
**QB[Lamar Jackson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/19781/Lamar+Jackson)**
**Update: Jackson is listed as out.**
There is a chance that Jackson returns in Week 7. He practiced in a limited capacity on Thursday, and that could put him on track to play on Sunday. If he suits up, Jackson will be a strong QB1 play against a Bengals defense that has given up the fifth-most rushing yards per game to opposing QBs this season. If he can't go, [Tyler Huntley](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21810/Tyler+Huntley) will be a solid QB2 play in Superflex formats.
**RB[Derrick Henry](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/17959/Derrick+Henry)**
