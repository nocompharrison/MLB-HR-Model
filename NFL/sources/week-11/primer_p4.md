#### **Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Stefon Diggs (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/stefon-diggs.php)**
[Stefon Diggs](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/stefon-diggs.php) is the WR24 in fantasy points per game. Since Week 7, he has led the team with a 21.6% target share with 2.12 YPRR, two red zone targets, three deep targets, and a 23.6% first-read share. Diggs has run 61.3% of his routes from the slot in his last three games. He won’t be shadowed by [DJ Turner](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dj-turner.php), who has only seen 3.3% of his snaps in the slot this season. Diggs should eat this week. Over their last five games, Cincy has allowed the 11th-most yards per target and the seventh-most fantasy points per opportunity to slot receivers.
#### **Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Jacory Croskey-Merritt (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jacory-croskeymerritt.php)**
I’m expecting Bill to be back this week. In the four games that he has played with Rachaad White this season, he has averaged 15,3 touches and 57.2 total yards. In that sample, he has had a 44.4% snap rate, 46% of the rushing work, and a 21.1% route share (2.4% target share). Bill will be the early component of Washington’s backfield committee this week. Bill hasn’t had a lot of luck on early downs this season behind a putrid Washington offensive line. He has only a 6.2% explosive run rate, 2.09 yards after contact per attempt, and a 6% missed tackle rate. Bill could still be a solid volume-driven flex this week if he sees 12-15 touches and gets into the end zone. The Bengals remain a nice rushing matchup for Bill. Over their last five games, Cincy has allowed the second-highest explosive run rate, the second-highest rushing success rate, the fourth-highest rushing yards over expectation per attempt, and the fourth-most yards after contact per attempt.
**[Antonio Williams (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/antonio-williams.php)**
Last week, [Antonio Williams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/antonio-williams.php) made the most of his two targets with 42 receiving yards and a score. Since Week 7, Williams has had only a 14.4% target share with 1.30 YPRR, zero red zone targets, two deep targets, and an 18.1% first-read share. That’s not amazing usage. He’s just a middling flex again this week. Over their last five games, Cincy has allowed the ninth-fewest yards per target to perimeter wide receivers while ranking 15th in fantasy points per opportunity allowed to the position.
**[Mike Gesicki (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/mike-gesicki.php)**
After two strong games of snap usage and route share, [Mike Gesicki](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/mike-gesicki.php) saw his role crater last week with a 40% snap rate and a 45.7% route share. Gesicki had only a 4.5% target share. It’s just a horrible reminder that his role is quite volatile. Overall, he has a 54.5% route share, an 8.2% target share, 1.30 YPRR, six scores, six red zone targets, two deep targets, and a 10% first-read share. Gesicki is on the streaming radar again this week, though. The matchup with Washington is nice. Over their last five games, they have allowed the 11th-most yards per target and the second-most receiving fantasy points per game to tight ends.
**[Chig Okonkwo (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/chigoziem-okonkwo.php)**
Since Week 7, [Chig Okonkwo](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/chigoziem-okonkwo.php) has had a 71.9% route share, a 19.6% target share, 1.38 YPRR, one red zone target, one deep target, and a 22.2% first-read share. Okonkwo is on the streaming radar this week. Over their last five games, Cincy has ranked 15th in receiving fantasy points per game allowed to tight ends and 19th in fantasy points per opportunity.
#### **Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**
_Players in consideration for lineups, but temper your expectations this week._
**[Rachaad White (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rachaad-white.php)**
With Bill back in the mix this week, I expect Rachaad White to operate as the passing-down portion of a Washington committee backfield. In their four games played together earlier this season, White averaged 9.8 touches and 47.8 total yards. In those games, he had a 48.6% snap rate with only 23% of the rushing work and a 40.8% route share (8% target share). White is only a middling PPR flex option this week. Over their last five games, Cincy has held backs to the sixth-fewest yards per target and the 11th-fewest receiving yards.
