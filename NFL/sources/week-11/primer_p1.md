#### **Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Jonathan Taylor (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jonathan-taylor.php)**
[Jonathan Taylor](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jonathan-taylor.php) is the RB11 in fantasy points per game, despite the Colts’ broken team and offense. Last week, he played 66.7% of the snaps with 47.1% of the rushing work and a 76% route share (18.2% target share). Taylor is still splitting early down work with [Seth McGowan](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/seth-mcgowan.php) despite the Colts refusing to trade him. He has a strong 13.9% explosive run rate and 18% missed tackle rate. Despite [Daniel Jones](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/daniel-jones.php), Taylor should still produce RB1/2 numbers this week. Houston’s run defense has been more pliable recently. Over their last five games, Houston has allowed the fifth-highest rushing success rate, the 12th-most rushing yards over expectation per attempt, and the tenth-highest missed tackle rate.
**[David Montgomery (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/david-montgomery.php)**
[David Montgomery](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/david-montgomery.php) will be back this week. With [Woody Marks](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/woody-marks.php) out, I expect Montgomery to lead the backfield in touches this week, but we’ll likely see [Emari Demercado](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/emari-demercado.php) mixed in. Montgomery hasn’t been effective when on the field this season. He has only a 2.1% explosive run rate, 1.65 yards after contact per attempt, and a 13% missed tackle rate. The context that needs to be applied here, though, is that the offensive line has given him zero room to operate with only 1.00 yards before contact per attempt on his carries, and he has been hit at the line or behind it with 53.8% of his carries. Montgomery should have some room to get loose this week, though. The Colts’ run defense has fallen apart. Over their last two games, they have allowed the ninth-most rushing yards, the ninth-highest explosive run rate, and the fifth-most yards before contact per attempt. Montgomery should see 12-15 touches in this game and return decent RB2 production.
**[Tank Dell (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/nathaniel-dell.php)**
Unfortunately, [Tank Dell](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/nathaniel-dell.php)‘s role didn’t grow last week. In Week 9, Dell had a 45.5% snap rate and 52.5% route share. Last week, those numbers looked similar, with a 47.6% snap rate and 54.8% route share. Last week, Dell’s usage dropped with the limited snap role, and he only saw a 10.5% target share and 12% first-read share, but I think his numbers could look better in Week 11. In his last two games, he has had a 17.3% target share with 2.86 YPRR, a 30% TPRR, one red zone target, five deep targets, and a 25% first-read share. Dell played more on the perimeter in Week 9 (66.7%), but he operated out of the slot last week (52.2%), so we’ll see where he lines up this week. The coverage matchup is in his favor, though. Since Week 7, Indy has had the 12th-highest rate of two high (53.2%), with three of those four games operating with 54.1-58.6% two high. Over the last two games, against two high, Dell has led the team with a 31% TPRR and 5.69 YPRR. Dell is a strong flex play this week against an Indy defense that, across their last five games, has allowed the 12th-most yards per target and the 14th-most fantasy points per opportunity to receivers.
**[Xavier Hutchinson (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/xavier-hutchinson.php)**
[Xavier Hutchinson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/xavier-hutchinson.php) has been a WR2 in two of his last three games in weekly scoring (WR21, WR33, WR16). Across his last two games, Hutchinson has operated 50-65.6% out of the slot with an 18.7% target share, 1.36 YPRR, two red zone targets, three deep targets, and a 20.8% first-read share. Since Week 7, Indy has had the 12th-highest rate of two high (53.2%), with three of those four games operating with 54.1-58.6% two high. Over the last two games, against two-high, Hutchinson has had a 28% TPRR with 3.28 YPRR. Zoom out further to his last three games, and against two-high, he has a 35% TPRR with 3.22 YPRR. Hutchinson is a strong flex this week. Across their last five games, Indy has allowed the 12th-most yards per target and the 14th-most fantasy points per opportunity to receivers.
#### **Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Seth McGowan (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/seth-mcgowan.php)**
[Seth McGowan](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/seth-mcgowan.php) is a flex option this week. Over the last two games, he has averaged 9.5 touches and 59.5 total yards with a score in each game. McGowan has been amazing on a per-touch basis this season. He has 3.22 yards after contact per attempt and an insane 27% missed tackle rate. He could get into the endzone this week and pay off for fantasy again. Houston’s run defense has been more pliable recently. Over their last five games, Houston has allowed the fifth-highest rushing success rate, the 12th-most rushing yards over expectation per attempt, and the tenth-highest missed tackle rate.
**[Kayshon Boutte (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kayshon-boutte.php)**
[Kayshon Boutte](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kayshon-boutte.php) is in play as a desperation flex in DEEP leagues this week. Boutte’s role in this passing offense is well known as the field stretcher. Boutte has a 15% TPRR, 14.8 aDOT, and six deep targets (20% of his target volume this season). Across their last five games, Indy has allowed the 12th-most yards per target and the 14th-most fantasy points per opportunity to receivers. During that span, Indy has also allowed the ninth-most deep passing yards and the 13th-highest passer rating to deep passing.
**[Dalton Schultz (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dalton-schultz.php) **
[Dalton Schultz](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dalton-schultz.php)‘s role in the Houston passing attack has withered. Over his last two games, he has had only a 55% snap rate, a 58.5% route share, a 6.7% target share, 1.10 YPRR (53 receiving yards), and a 4.2% first-read share. Schultz is the ultimate desperation, deep league streaming option this week, and it’s only because of the matchup. Across their last five games, Indy has allowed the second-most yards per target and the most fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends.
#### **Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**
_Players in consideration for lineups, but temper your expectations this week._
**[Daniel Jones (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/daniel-jones.php)**
[Daniel Jones](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/daniel-jones.php) is the QB31 in fantasy points per game. He hasn’t posted more than 12.5 fantasy points in a game since Week 6, which was the only time that has happened all season. Last week, in a wonderful matchup against the Dolphins, Jones struggled with a 50% completion rate, 108 passing yards, 4.9 yards per attempt, and two picks (two passing touchdowns as well). I have zero faith in Jones at this point to produce, even when the matchup is in his favor. Sit him.
**[Tyler Warren (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tyler-warren.php)**
I don’t trust [Daniel Jones](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/daniel-jones.php) at all. The only Colts’ pass catcher I’ll remotely consider for a fantasy lineup until further notice is [Tyler Warren](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tyler-warren.php). Last week, with the Colts’ passing-catching room decimated, Warren only had a 13.6% target share, two receptions, one receiving yard, and a 7.1% first-read share. Someone on the Indy coaching staff needs to explain that to me like I’m five years old because I don’t get it. Overall, Warren is the TE7 in fantasy points per game with a 21.4% target share, 1.18 YPRR, five red zone targets, and a 20.4% first-read share. I don’t expect Warren to have a wonderful game in Week 11. Over their last five games, Houston has allowed the 12th-fewest yards per target and the seventh-fewest fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends.
