##  **_Matchups Analysis - Sunday 1:00_** _EST Games_
## **Arizona Cardinals at Kansas City Chiefs**
**Matchups We Love:**
**QB[Patrick Mahomes](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/18890/Patrick+Mahomes)[ II](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/18890/Patrick+Mahomes)**
Patrick Mahomes had his best fantasy game of the season last week, scoring a season-high 35.4 fantasy points behind a five-touchdown game. Mahomes could deliver another big fantasy performance against the Cardinals in Week 11. Arizona has given up the second-most fantasy points to QBs this season and a top-10 fantasy QB in three of the last four weeks. Mahomes is an easy QB1 this weekend.
**TE[Travis Kelce](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/15048/Travis+Kelce)**
Travis Kelce missed most of last week's game with an ankle issue, so his two-catch, 30-yard performance isn't much of a surprise. Fantasy managers should expect him to get back on track against the Cardinals in Week 11. Arizona has allowed the fourth-most fantasy points and fourth-most receiving touchdowns to opposing TEs this season. Kelce should be in most lineups as a TE1.
**TE[Trey McBride](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23251/Trey+McBride)**
Trey McBride's fantasy value takes a massive hit with [Josh Johnson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/6489/Josh+Johnson). He scored a season-low 8.7 PPR fantasy points behind a five-catch, 37-yard receiving day. While he is still a TE1 start in most cases, both his ceiling and floor are both lower with the uncertain play under center. The good news is that Kansas City ranks 28th in fantasy points and 29th in receptions allowed to the position this year.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**QB[Carson Beck](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24994/Carson+Beck) **
**Update: Beck has been ruled out.[Josh Johnson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22652/Josh+Johnson) will get the start and is viewed as a low-end QB2.**
Beck didn't throw the ball well in his first career start. Beck completed just 11 of 18 passes for 108 yards and two interceptions. Unfortunately, things don't get easier for him this week against the Chiefs. Kansas City has given up the third-fewest fantasy points, second-fewest passing touchdowns, and only two top-16 fantasy QB finishes this year. Beck is hard to trust in Superflex formats.
**Other Matchups:**
**Cardinals RBs**
[Bam Knight](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23847/Bam+Knight) (54%) and [Tyler Allgeier](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23250/Tyler+Allgeier) (46%) split this backfield almost evenly last week. Neither really did much with their opportunities, as Knight finished with 1.9 PPR fantasy points and Allgeier had 1.4 PPR fantasy points. With Arizona as big underdogs in this game, both will be lower-end flex options. Allgeier ran only five routes in last week's loss.
**RB Emmett Johnson**
**Update:[Kenneth Walker](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23214/Kenneth+Walker) is out. Emmett Johnson will lead the backfield once again.**
All signs point to Johnson starting out of the Chiefs backfield for the second straight week. Kansas City will remain super careful with [Kenneth Walker](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23214/Kenneth+Walker)[ III](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23214/Kenneth+Walker) (groin), and that will put Johnson on RB1 radars again in Week 11. The rookie handled 22 carries for 101 yards on the ground while also catching six passes for 70 yards and two touchdowns last Sunday. You're starting him as a high-end RB1.
**Chiefs WRs**
Both [Rashee Rice](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24120/Rashee+Rice) and [Josh Downs](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24145/Josh+Downs) had solid fantasy performances last week. Rice caught five passes for 82 yards, and Downs caught four passes for 53 yards and one touchdown across just 11 snaps. Both should be started in most leagues this week. Rice will be a low-end WR2 against this Cardinals secondary, while Downs will be a potential flex option in 12+ team leagues. Downs should play more snaps as he continues to learn the playbook.
**Injuries:**
  * [Kenneth Walker](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23214/Kenneth+Walker)[ III](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23214/Kenneth+Walker) (groin) - out
  * Carson Beck (back) - out


## **Baltimore Ravens at Carolina Panthers**
**Matchups We Love:**
**RB[Derrick Henry](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/17959/Derrick+Henry)**
Derrick Henry has struggled to run the ball since [Lamar Jackson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/19781/Lamar+Jackson) went down. Henry has rushed for under 60 yards in five straight games and has scored under 15 PPR fantasy points in four of those five contests. But this will be the veteran's best matchup yet. He will face a Panthers defense that ranks 30th in fantasy points allowed, 22nd in rush yards allowed, and 31st in rush touchdowns allowed to RBs. Henry is a solid RB2 play.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**QB[Bryce Young](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23132/Bryce+Young)**
Bryce Young scored under 12 fantasy points for the third time in four weeks last week. In each of those three contests, he has failed to throw for more than 210 yards and throw multiple passing touchdowns. That does not bode well for him entering the week against a Ravens defense that has given up the 12th-fewest passing yards and sixth-fewest passing touchdowns this season. He's more of a QB2.
**Other Matchups:**
**QB[Jacoby Brissett](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/18018/Jacoby+Brissett) **
Jacoby Brissett threw for a season-high 340 yards in his Ravens debut last week. This week, he will come in as a mid-tier QB2 in Superflex formats. The Panthers have given the 12th-fewest fantasy points to opposing QBs, and only one QB has scored above 17 fantasy points against this defense since Week 5.
**RB[Chuba Hubbard](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21691/Chuba+Hubbard) **
