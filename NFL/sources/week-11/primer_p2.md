#### **Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Jared Goff (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jared-goff.php)**
[Jared Goff](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jared-goff.php) is the QB13 in fantasy points per game. He’s having a very strong season, but he’ll be tested this week. The Bucs’ pass defense has been lights out. Goff could still succeed this week, though. Among 31 qualifying passers, Goff is sixth in yards per attempt, third in passing success rate, 14th in highly accurate throw rate, and third in hero throw rate. Over their last five games, the Bucs have allowed the 11th-lowest yards per attempt, the eighth-lowest passing success rate, the third-lowest CPOE, and the fifth-fewest fantasy points per dropback. Goff has the passing chops, skill players around him, and the excellence against the blitz to still have a strong day in Week 11. Across their last five games, Tampa Bay is second in blitz rate (48.5%) behind only Minnesota. Against the blitz, Goff is fourth in yards per attempt, and he leads all quarterbacks in passing success rate. Goff can still post QB1 numbers this week.

**[Kenneth Gainwell (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kenneth-gainwell.php)**
Since Week 7, [Kenneth Gainwell](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kenneth-gainwell.php) has been the RB28 in fantasy points per game, averaging 15.7 touches and 101.3 total yards. In those three games, he has had a 39.7% snap rate, 33.1% of the rushing work, and a 30.8% route share (8.7% target share). Gainwell has been amazing on a per-touch basis with a 14.5% explosive run rate, 2.94 yards after contact per attempt, and a 27% missed tackle rate. The Detroit run defense will suffer without [Aidan Hutchinson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aidan-hutchinson.php), but I expect them to remain strong against the run. This means Gainwell is only a middling flex option this week. Over their last five games, Detroit has allowed the second-lowest rushing success rate, the fewest yards after contact per attempt, and the fifth-fewest rushing yards over expectation per attempt.
**[Jameson Williams (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jameson-williams.php)**

Another feather in his cap this week during that stretch is that his TPRR has risen from 17% to 24% when [Jared Goff](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jared-goff.php) has been blitzed while posting 1.86 YPRR. Williams can have a solid game this week, but I’m not expecting a monster outing. Over their last five games, Tampa Bay has held perimeter wide receivers to the 12th-fewest yards per target and the eighth-fewest fantasy points per opportunity.
