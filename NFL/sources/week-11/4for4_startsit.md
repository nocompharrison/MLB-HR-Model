Welcome to another edition of Hoopes There It Is! We’re previewing every game in Week 11, highlighting advanced stats like EPA, success rate, pass rate over expected (PROE), and completion percentage over expected (CPOE) to help make the best decisions for your fantasy teams. I’ll provide a quick start/sit section at the top that stems from the longer write-ups. And if you’re interested in more, which I hope you are, you can [jump to any of the 13 games this week](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it#links). Let’s get started!
## Start ‘Em, Sit ‘Em
Ja’Kobi Lane (**Start**): With six teams on bye and seemingly endless injuries, we’ll focus on starts today. The thesis for Ja’Kobi Lane going into Week 10 was twofold: he’d get both more and better passing volume with [Jacoby Brissett](https://www.4for4.com/player/jacoby-brissett) and he would fill the role that [Michael Wilson](https://www.4for4.com/player/michael-wilson) had in Arizona. Lane only managed a 3/61/0 line last week, but the peripherals indicate we could have hit both parts of the thesis. The Ravens passed at a rate 6.8% above expectation last week, their highest mark of the season. That led to 45 team targets, or 2.5 times the number that the Bills had, as an example. And Lane earned 10 of those 45 targets, the most of any Raven. Like Wilson, Lane’s targets are very valuable as he’s getting air yards and looks near the goal line; his typical target is 21% more valuable than the average receiver’s target because of that. This Panthers’ pass defense is great at limiting explosives but is below average in efficiency and success rate allowed and dead-last in sack rate. They also allow the 5th most plays against them in the league, as their opponents pass on them at the 7th highest rate versus expectation. Valuable volume against a below average defense is the Lane bet this week.
[Omarion Hampton](https://www.4for4.com/player/omarion-hampton) (**Start**): I’m nearly certain this is the first time this season I’ve suggested to start a Charger. Their offense has been atrocious, ranking in the bottom eight of nearly every stat I track. But the Chargers got their first win of the season last week. And they did so via a strong run game, as they hit 64th percentile efficiency and a good 50% success rate. That success rate is about 15% above their season average. [Omarion Hampton](https://www.4for4.com/player/omarion-hampton) was the engine of that success, taking 22 of the team’s 27 RB carries and finally separating himself from [Kimani Vidal](https://www.4for4.com/player/kimani-vidal) and [Keaton Mitchell](https://www.4for4.com/player/keaton-mitchell) in snap rate. With the Chargers 3.5-point home favorites, against a very bad Jets’ run defense, it’s at least possible to see Hampton as an RB2 again. We’ll just need the coaching staff to remember what worked in Week 10.
[Adonai Mitchell](https://www.4for4.com/player/adonai-mitchell) (**Start**): For a deeper dive on [Adonai Mitchell](https://www.4for4.com/player/adonai-mitchell), check out the breakout model article this week. But the summary is that, like Lane, Mitchell is getting very valuable targets deep down the field. He earns those targets more frequently with Omar Cooper sidelined and against zone coverage, both of which could happen for him this week. And [Geno Smith](https://www.4for4.com/player/geno-smith) has quietly been very accurate on passes down the field this season. There is extreme volatility here, but at least there is upside unlike with so many of our other WR options in Week 11.

##  Colts at Texans (O/U 44.5, HOU -12.5)
### Colts (16.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Yikes on 16 implied points.
  * The Colts passed at a rate 10% below expectation last week, and justifiably so, given they hit 1st percentile passing efficiency.
  * Their 30.8% pass success rate was tied with the Raiders for the lowest among teams playing a game in Week 11.
  * They only had 22 team targets, and those were really spread around, with [Anthony Gould](https://www.4for4.com/player/anthony-gould) and [Jonathan Taylor](https://www.4for4.com/player/jonathan-taylor) pacing the team with just four each.
  * [Tyler Warren](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-warren) is still TE5 in expected fantasy points per game this season, but I’d look hard at my other options; you’re not starting any other Colts receivers.
  * At RB, we’re still getting more [Seth McGowan](https://www.4for4.com/player/seth-mcgowan) in our lives than I expected post-trade deadline.
  * Taylor took 40 snaps to McGowan’s 17, with Taylor dominating the routes 20 to two but only taking four more carries.
  * McGowan also scored a TD, but there’s flukiness here.
  * Taylor has three goal-line rushes to McGowan’s two over the last four games, but Taylor hasn’t converted on any of them while McGowan converted both of his, according to JJ Zachariason.
  * I don’t think Taylor has suddenly forgotten how to convert goal-line carries.
  * You’re starting Taylor, but with only 16 implied points, we’ll have to temper our expectations now and going forward.


### Texans (28.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * A really fantastic implied point mark; we’ll just have to hope we get the Texans from the 1st half last week instead of the 2nd half.
  * The Texans ended last week with an average pass efficiency mark, but that hides their amazing +0.61 EPA per dropback in the 1st half followed by a miserable -0.61 EPA per dropback in the 2nd half.
  * The Texans’ 35 yards and 10% success rate in the 2nd half last week were the lowest for any team in a half this season, according to Sumer Sports.
  * CJ Stroud was pressured at the same rate in both halves, according to Sam Hoppen, but Stroud’s accurate throw rate in the 2nd half was a brutal 36.9%.
  * Overall, though, it’s been a really strong year for Stroud, and it’s a great matchup for him on paper.
  * The Texans have the 7th-highest explosive pass rate partially due to their league-leading air yards per attempt; well, the Colts’ pass defense gives up explosives at the 2nd-highest rate, and their opponents attack them with the 4th-highest aDoT.
  * And the Texans remembered they had [Nico Collins](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/collin1) last week, peppering him with 16 of the team’s 35 targets.
  * [Xavier Hutchinson](https://www.4for4.com/player/xavier-hutchinson) unfortunately remains a thing in this offense, and he earned nine targets last week.
  * But there are some very small, slow signs that [Tank Dell](https://www.4for4.com/player/tank-dell) could be eating into Hutchinson’s slot role.
  * Even with [Marvin Harrison Jr.](https://www.4for4.com/player/marvin-harrison-jr) inactive, Dell only ran 23 of the team’s 42 routes, or a very small increase from Week 9. 
  * But Dell did see more of his work from the slot in Week 10, so the dream is still alive that Dell overtakes Hutchinson for the slot role at some point this season.
  * At RB, [Woody Marks](https://www.4for4.com/player/woody-marks) suffered a concussion in the 3rd quarter last week, and given it’s a short week, my assumption is that Marks sits.
  * [David Montgomery](https://www.4for4.com/player/david-montgomery) was a full participant at Monday’s walkthrough and practiced Tuesday, so my assumption is he plays and gets the great majority of the work, but that’s still uncertain at this point.
  * It’s a testament to how bad Montgomery and the Texans’ rushing attack have been this season that you’re still not excited to play him with a full workload against the Colts’ run defense.


##  Saints at Bears (O/U 48.5, CHI -11.5)
### Saints (18.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Saints are incapable of running the ball.
  * They hit 0th (not a typo) percentile rushing efficiency last week against a Panthers’ run defense that was 25th in the league in rushing efficiency allowed going into the week.
  * Their 11.1% rush success rate was not just last; it was half the number of the next-lowest team.
  * In response to their inability to run, the Saints spammed a lot of short passes, with [Tyler Shough](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-shough-ore)’s 4.3 air yards per attempt more than a yard below the next lowest QB.
  * And the beneficiary of that was [Jordyn Tyson](https://www.4for4.com/player/jordyn-tyson), who earned a whopping 13 targets on 31 routes via 6.2 air yards per target, the 2nd lowest among all WRs with 5+ targets.
  * Hey, we’ll take that in fantasy, though.
  * I’m still pretty annoyed the Saints’ staff said they weren’t sure when Tyson would be ready to play a full game, only to give him 72% of the routes the following week; please don’t put in the newspaper that I got mad.
  * But this team is pretty easy for our purposes, in that you’re starting [Chris Olave](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-olave) and Tyson and no one else.
  * It’s a mess at RB, with [Audric Estime](https://www.4for4.com/player/audric-estime) leading the team in snaps and routes, [Alvin Kamara](https://www.4for4.com/player/alvin-kamara) leading in targets, and [Travis Etienne](https://www.4for4.com/player/travis-etienne) leading in carries.
  * According to PFF, Etienne was the lead back, Estime the 3rd-down back and primary backup, and Kamara limited to mostly two-minute drill snaps.
  * Yes, Kamara scored two TDs last week, but we unfortunately don’t get to take those fantasy points with us into Week 11.


### Bears (30.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * We’re cooking with 30 implied points here.
  * The Bears are coming off their bye and a close win over the Buccaneers in Week 9.
  * The three Bears we care about, [Luther Burden](https://www.4for4.com/player/luther-burden), [Rome Odunze](https://www.4for4.com/player/rome-odunze), and [Colston Loveland](https://www.4for4.com/player/colston-loveland), each earned 6+ targets in Week 9.
  * We did get more [Kalif Raymond](https://www.4for4.com/player/kalif-raymond) than usual with seven targets, though (does that make Raymond Goldilocks in this scenario?).
  * Burden and Loveland are in must-start territory, and Odunze is WR31 in expected fantasy points per game on a team with 30 implied points, so he’s a fine start as well.
  * At RB, D’Andre Swift dominated the work in Week 9 and remains a must-start.
  * But we should get [Kyle Monangai](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyle-monangai) back this week as well; Monangai is actually RB19 in expected fantasy points per game thanks to that one 30-carry performance.
  * But again, with 30 implied points, I’d fire up Monangai as a flex play against the 2nd worst run defense in the league.


##  Titans at Cowboys (O/U 54.5, DAL -8.5)
### Titans (23.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * A healthy 23 implied points here for the Titans thanks to the Cowboys’ 2nd worst pass defense and league-worst run defense.
  * The passing attack wasn’t good last week, hitting 36th percentile efficiency, a bad 34.9% success rate, and a low 5.6 air yards per attempt.
  * But they’ve at least reversed course sharply in recent weeks with their highest pass rate over expectation (PROE) marks of the season.
  * If [Carnell Tate](https://www.4for4.com/player/carnell-tate) plays, he’s the only Titans receiver I’m playing in fantasy football; the latest report I saw was that the coaching staff is ‘hopeful’ he plays this week.
  * If Tate sits, [Elic Ayomanor](https://www.4for4.com/player/elic-ayomanor) is viable after a nine-target performance in Week 10.
  * And you can also start [Tony Pollard](https://www.4for4.com/player/tony-pollard), who tied Ayomanor with nine targets last week in addition to taking four more carries than [Tyjae Spears](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyjae-spears).
  * Pollard’s pass work has increased a lot; he played on five 3rd downs last week after taking just 12 of those snaps over the first eight games, according to PFF.
  * Pollard also played five snaps in two-minute drill situations after seeing only eight of those in the first eight games.


### Cowboys (31.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * 31.5 implied points; you love to see it.
  * The Cowboys’ offense was awesome last week, hitting 76th percentile passing and 96th percentile rushing efficiency.
  * We do need to remember they had a massive 83 offensive plays, or about 17 more than their season average.
  * That context is needed to explain [George Pickens](https://www.4for4.com/player/george-pickens)’ 18 targets, [CeeDee Lamb](https://www.4for4.com/player/ceedee-lamb)’s 13, and [Ryan Flournoy](https://www.4for4.com/player/ryan-flournoy)’s seven.
  * There’s nothing actionable here on Pickens and Lamb; they’re both must-start players every week.
  * But I think you could do worse than Flournoy; I know he’s the WR3 on the Cowboys, but I’d prefer him over the de facto WR1 on a lot of NFL teams.
  * And [Javonte Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/javonte-williams) remains a must-start but, as has been the case all season, he’s TD-dependent; luckily he’s on the team with the highest implied points of the week.


##  Buccaneers at Lions (O/U 45.5, DET -6.5)
### Buccaneers (19.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Buccaneers are coming off their bye and a close loss to the Bears in Week 9.
  * This is such a weird team in terms of PROE, as they were totally normal through six games and then went into prehistoric mode from Week 7 to 9. 
  * Every team passes on the Lions’ defense as they know they have to score points to keep up, and the Lions’ pass defense is bottom 10 in every stat I track except sack rate.
  * But even that sack rate might start falling with Aidan Hutchinson done for the year.
  * So, do the Buccaneers act like a functional NFL team again, or do they continue to pass at a rate 20% below expectation?
  * I’d prefer not to risk my own fantasy team with the answer to that question, so my preference is to only start [Bucky Irving](https://www.4for4.com/player/bucky-irving) from the Bucs.
  * Irving led Kenny Gainwell Week 9 in snaps 47 to 26, routes 18 to 11, and carries 20 to five. 
  * It’s not a good matchup for Irving, as the Lions’ run defense is much stronger than their pass defense, but the volume makes him a must-start.


### Lions (26.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Lions are perhaps the easiest team to parse for fantasy football.
  * [Jahmyr Gibbs](https://www.4for4.com/player/jahmyr-gibbs) leads all flex players in expected fantasy points per game, with only [Kenneth Walker](https://www.4for4.com/player/kenneth-walker) in the same range.
  * [Amon-Ra St. Brown](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/stbra1) is WR6 in expected fantasy points but has outperformed his expectation by about 2.5 points per game; elite players can dunk on expected point models.
  * That really only leaves [Jameson Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/jameson-williams) and [Sam LaPorta](https://www.4for4.com/player/sam-laporta) as question marks in our lineups.
  * Williams is WR28 in expected points, and [Sam LaPorta](https://www.4for4.com/player/sam-laporta) is TE7, so I’d be surprised if you have stronger options than either.
  * But LaPorta has been on a nasty cold streak here, failing to hit 34 receiving yards or a TD in four straight games.
  * It’s also not a good matchup for the passing attack, with the Buccaneers ranking in the top eight of every stat I track except sack rate.
  * LaPorta is a low-end TE1 in 4for4’s rankings this week, but again, options are thin at the position, so you’re likely still starting him.


##  Dolphins at Bills (O/U 45.5, BUF -16.5)
### Dolphins (14.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Wooooooof on 14.5 implied points.
  * The Dolphins were bad last week but not outlier-level bad, hitting 26th percentile passing and 42nd percentile rushing efficiency.
  * But that was against an awful Colts’ defense that traded away its best players.
  * Now they face an average Bills defense that is particularly good at limiting completions, something the Dolphins have already struggled to do offensively.
  * [Caleb Douglas](https://www.4for4.com/player/caleb-douglas) paced the team with seven targets last week, and he’s the only Dolphins WR in the top 36 of 4for4’s ranks this week. 
  * [Ollie Gordon](https://www.4for4.com/player/ollie-gordon) similarly is in startable range at RB, and he dominated the work last week, taking 49 snaps to [Jaylen Wright](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylen-wright)’s 12, including 20 routes and 20 carries.
  * I’m here for Douglas the talent (he’s up to WR11 in ESPN Analytics’ open score), but you have to really temper your expectations if you’re starting him given the Dolphins’ implied points.


### Bills (31.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Bills crushed the Jets last week and ended the day with only 18 targets, or the same number that [George Pickens](https://www.4for4.com/player/george-pickens) earned on his own in Week 10.
  * That’s a big problem for the pass catchers, especially since this week sets up in a very similar way.
  * DJ Moore paced the team with six targets, so he’s my preferred start, but he was the only Bill with more than three targets last week.
  * [Dalton Kincaid](https://www.4for4.com/player/dalton-kincaid) has been great when running a route this season, but he only ran 15 of the team’s 25 routes last week; he’s a TE1 in 4for4’s ranks given his upside exists, but there’s basically no floor here either.
  * [James Cook](https://www.4for4.com/player/james-cook) is a must-start given the implied points and his talent.
  * Cook took 35 of the Bills’ 57 snaps last week, seeing 18 of the 21 RB carries but only 10 of the team’s 25 routes.
  * The Dolphins’ defense is awful across the board, including bottom 10 in every rushing stat I track.


##  Steelers at Eagles (O/U 38.5, PIT -4.5)
### Steelers (21.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Just sim this game to the end.
  * Week 10 was a pretty typical Steelers game in that they were bad passing with 38th percentile efficiency and a 35.9% success rate, but fantastic running the ball with 77th percentile efficiency and a 52.4% success rate.
  * [DK Metcalf](https://www.4for4.com/player/dk-metcalf) and [Jaylen Warren](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylen-warren) led the team with six targets each, with Metcalf saving his fantasy day with a TD.
  * I think Metcalf is a fine if uninspiring start against a below-average at best Eagles’ pass defense.
  * I’d sit article favorite [Germie Bernard](https://www.4for4.com/player/germie-bernard) as, despite running almost every route in recent weeks, he’s stopped earning targets at a viable rate.
  * Warren is a must-start given the six targets last week, and he should have a lot more than seven carries in this one.
  * The Steelers pulled their starters at the end of the game last week because they were getting blown out, explaining why [Lew Nichols](https://www.4for4.com/player/lew-nichols) got six carries; that shouldn’t be an issue against the Eagles.
  * I think even [Rico Dowdle](https://www.4for4.com/player/rico-dowdle) is an uninspiring flex play given the projected game script.


### Eagles (17.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * It’s tough to suggest starts on the Eagles for two reasons: they’re awful and incredibly injured.
  * [DeVonta Smith](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/smithd4) is a flex play, I guess, if he plays; I’d prefer not to play any other Eagles’ receivers given the implied points and projected volume.
  * [Saquon Barkley](https://www.4for4.com/player/saquon-barkley) is probably a low-end RB2 at best.
  * Barkley did play over 50% of the snaps in Week 9 for the first time since Week 3, according to PFF.
  * And you could see a world where he plays closer to a normal snap count with the additional rest he got during the Eagles’ bye week.
  * But he’s been inefficient on his carries, and you could easily make the opposite argument that the Eagles have no reason to force a large workload on him.
  * The Steelers’ run defense is also excellent, ranking in the top seven of every stat I track, with their opponents passing on them at the highest rate in the league versus expectation.
  * I just don’t know if the Eagles are willing to do that with [Joe Milton](https://www.4for4.com/player/joe-milton) under center.


##  Cardinals at Chiefs (O/U 41.5, KC -16.5)
### Cardinals (12.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * My goooooooodness, 12.5 implied points; this week, man.
  * I mentioned in the Dolphins write-up that they were just bad in a normal way, not an outlier way.
  * Well, the Cardinals were bad in an outlier way last week.
  * They hit 0th percentile passing efficiency.
  * Worse, for our purposes, is that they only had 35 team plays; compare that to the 83 the Cowboys had last week.
  * That was the 2nd fewest plays for a team in any game this season, edging out the Jets in Week 4.
  * [George Pickens](https://www.4for4.com/player/george-pickens) had more targets on his own than the entire Cardinals team last week, as they only managed about 18 minutes of time of possession and couldn’t string together a functional offense.
  * There’s no guarantee the Cardinals’ offense will be *this* bad every week, but it’s such a bad sign that this is possible.
  * [Trey McBride](https://www.4for4.com/player/trey-mcbride) is the only player you’re considering for fantasy, and he earned about 30% of the targets last week, but again, that only added up to five targets.
  * And they’re playing a Chiefs pass defense that is top 10 in every stat I track except sack rate.


### Chiefs (29.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Chiefs beat down the Falcons last week, passing at a rate 10% above expectation and hitting 83rd percentile efficiency in the process.
  * That PROE is good news for us in fantasy because that increases potential volume for us in the likely event that the Chiefs are beating down the Cardinals in this one.
  * [Emmett Johnson](https://www.4for4.com/player/emmett-johnson) actually led the team with seven targets and put up a fantastic fantasy day with [Kenneth Walker](https://www.4for4.com/player/kenneth-walker) sidelined.
  * You’re starting Walker every week against any opponent, but I think Johnson is viable even if Walker starts given the implied points and projected game script.
  * I don’t need to tell you to start Johnson if Walker sits.
  * [Rashee Rice](https://www.4for4.com/player/rashee-rice) was 2nd on the team with six targets last week and is a good start once again.
  * Personal favorite [Josh Downs](https://www.4for4.com/player/josh-downs) was 3rd on the team with five targets, but that was insane because he only ran eight routes.
  * Now, he won’t earn a target on an otherworldly 63% of his routes moving forward, but it’s a strong debut that threatens the work for essentially every other pass catcher on the offense.
  * 100% of Downs’ work came out of the slot in Week 10, which pushed Rice to the outside, with Rice’s outside rate about 20% higher than his season average.
  * I’d prefer to keep Downs on the bench until we see anywhere near a normal route rate, but he could easily pull a [Jordyn Tyson](https://www.4for4.com/player/jordyn-tyson) and hit a 75% route rate as soon as this week.
  * And it's great news for the entire offense that [Patrick Mahomes](https://www.4for4.com/player/patrick-mahomes) had more deep attempts, completions, and touchdowns in Week 10 than he did in his previous four games combined, according to Sumer Sports.
  * [Travis Kelce](https://www.4for4.com/player/travis-kelce) only ran 14 routes last week due to an ankle injury; he’s startable against the Cardinals’ league-worst pass defense if the coach speak indicates he’ll have his normal role.


##  Ravens at Panthers (O/U 43.5, CAR -2.5)
### Ravens (20.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * We got our first taste of [Jacoby Brissett](https://www.4for4.com/player/jacoby-brissett) leading this offense, and as expected, we got a lot more pass volume.
  * The Ravens hit +6.8% PROE last week, their highest rate of the season, as every coach lets Brissett drop back a million times a game.
  * That led to 45 team targets last week, or 2.5 times the number that the Bills had, for example.
  * Breakout article cover boy Ja’Kobi Lane had 10 targets last week; now he only caught three of them, but I think we can hang the banner with that many targets.
  * [Mark Andrews](https://www.4for4.com/player/mark-andrews) had eight targets and is a low-end TE1 given the volume.
  * And you’re starting both [Zay Flowers](https://www.4for4.com/player/zay-flowers) and [Derrick Henry](https://www.4for4.com/player/derrick-henry) every week, with Henry’s 25 routes and six targets a welcome surprise; that was easily a season-high for him.
  * This Panthers’ run defense was fantastic last week, but that was against the Saints, who haven’t been able to run the ball all season.


### Panthers (23.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Panthers were bad last week, hitting terrible 38.5% pass and 29% rush success rates.
  * They also passed at their lowest rate relative to expectation for the entire season, hitting -6.3% PROE.
  * [Tetairoa McMillan](https://www.4for4.com/player/tetairoa-mcmillan) led the team with six targets, and he’s the only must-start receiver on the offense.
  * Article favorite [Jalen Coker](https://www.4for4.com/player/jalen-coker) only earned four targets last week, and he’s suffered with the entire offense since their offensive line injuries happened.
  * [Bryce Young](https://www.4for4.com/player/bryce-young)’s percentage of throws with less than two air yards has almost doubled from Weeks 7 to 10 relative to Weeks 1 to 6, according to JJ Zachariason.
  * And that’s because the Panthers have allowed the 4th-highest pressure rate over the last four weeks.
  * [Chuba Hubbard](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/hubbac1) remains a must-start given his insane volume; he took 59 of the team’s 70 snaps in Week 10, including 27 of the 30 RB carries.
  * The Ravens’ defense is good at limiting explosive runs but is otherwise below average in efficiency and success rate allowed.


##  Jets at Chargers (O/U 41.5, LAC -3.5)
### Jets (19.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * [Geno Smith](https://www.4for4.com/player/geno-smith) was in and out of the lineup last week due to a concussion scare and an ankle injury.
  * My assumption is that Smith plays this week after some positive news with his ankle imaging, but that’s still uncertain at the time I’m writing.
  * Speaking of injuries, Omar Cooper only played one snap after aggravating an ankle injury he was nursing going into the game; it’s the same ankle that prompted an IR stint earlier this season, so I’m assuming Cooper sits in this one.
  * With Cooper out, [Garrett Wilson](https://www.4for4.com/player/garrett-wilson) and [Adonai Mitchell](https://www.4for4.com/player/adonai-mitchell) combined for 21 of the team’s 37 targets.
  * Wilson is a must-start every week against any opponent, so I’ll wax poetic about personal favorite Mitchell for a bit.
  * Mitchell has a poor 48.7% catch rate this season, but that’s because he has the 6th-highest aDoT among all WRs with 100+ routes.
  * Those air yards will make his performance volatile on a weekly basis, but it at least provides us with a real ceiling.
  * He’s had four games with 7+ targets this season; he’s WR24 in ESPN Analytics’ open score, and he has a 26.5% targets-per-route run against zone coverage, according to SIS.
  * The Chargers’ pass defense plays zone at a rate slightly higher than league average, so I’d be willing to fire up Mitchell as a WR3 for any lineup in need of some volatility (Smith in and Cooper out assumptions apply here).
  * And then I’d view [Breece Hall](https://www.4for4.com/player/breece-hall) as an RB2 and [Braelon Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/braelon-allen) as more of a flex play given their near-even snap split last week; Hall has been better at turning his routes into targets.


### Chargers (22.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Chargers got their first win of the season last week.
  * And they did so via a strong run game, which is new for this team, as they hit 64th percentile efficiency and a good 50% success rate.
  * [Omarion Hampton](https://www.4for4.com/player/omarion-hampton) was the engine of that success, taking 22 of the team’s 27 RB carries and separating himself from [Kimani Vidal](https://www.4for4.com/player/kimani-vidal) and [Keaton Mitchell](https://www.4for4.com/player/keaton-mitchell) in snap rate.
  * With the Chargers 3.5-point home favorites, it’s at least possible to see Hampton as an RB2 again.
  * I don’t know [Ladd McConkey](https://www.4for4.com/player/ladd-mcconkey)’s status at the time I’m writing, but I guess I’d be willing to fall for it again and play him if he’s active.
  * If McConkey sits, Tre’ Harris is a WR3 in my mind after earning 33% of the team’s targets in Week 10; now, that was only five targets, but he looked good in the process.


##  Jaguars at Giants (O/U 46.5, JAX -6.0)
### Jaguars (26.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Jaguars crushed the Titans through the air last week, hitting 97th percentile efficiency and a 71.8% success rate, which is the highest number in recent memory.
  * The Titans only pressured [Trevor Lawrence](https://www.4for4.com/player/trevor-lawrence) on 18% of his dropbacks, which was the 6th lowest of any defense in a game this season, according to Sumer Sports.
  * [Parker Washington](https://www.4for4.com/player/parker-washington) was the engine of their success, turning his nine targets into a 7/152/1 line as he continues to eat with vertical routes from the slot.
  * The Jaguars haven’t trailed in a game since Week 6, so the only thing holding Washington back is the fact that they don’t pass in the 2nd halves of games.
  * If the Giants provide any pushback here, we could see another Washington spike game.
  * Washington remains a must-start along with [Bhayshul Tuten](https://www.4for4.com/player/bhayshul-tuten), who took 52 of the team’s 74 snaps last week, including 26 of the 30 RB carries.


### Giants (20.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Giants were above average passing the ball last week, hitting 61st percentile efficiency and a 46.3% success rate.
  * Neither are special numbers, but that success rate is really strong for a JJ McCarthy-led offense.
  * [Isaiah Likely](https://www.4for4.com/player/isaiah-likely) (11) and [Malik Nabers](https://www.4for4.com/player/malik-nabers) (seven) paced the team with targets, run essentially every route, and remain must-starts going forward.
  * And then [Cam Skattebo](https://www.4for4.com/player/cam-skattebo) has entered must-start territory after his workload in Week 10.
  * Skattebo took 58 of the team’s 65 snaps last week, including 18 of the team’s 19 RB carries.
  * That 89% snap rate was about 12% higher than in any other game of his career.
  * We just have to hope the Giants’ coaching staff doesn’t try to teach him any more lessons going forward.


##  Raiders at Broncos (O/U 40.5, DEN -10.0)
### Raiders (15.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * About 15 implied points would usually be the lowest on the week, but not with the current state of the Cardinals.
  * [Brock Bowers](https://www.4for4.com/player/brock-bowers) only played 11 snaps last week due to an ankle injury, and my assumption is that he’ll sit this week, though that is still uncertain.
  * If Bowers sits, the only Raider you’re really considering is [Ashton Jeanty](https://www.4for4.com/player/ashton-jeanty).
  * Jeanty split the carries with Mike Washington last week but ran 26 routes to Washington’s eight, so Jeanty would have to get there through receiving as has been the case all season.
  * [Jack Bech](https://www.4for4.com/player/jack-bech) actually paced the team with seven targets last week, and he functionally filled the Bowers role despite the two technically playing different positions.
  * But Bech had eight total targets going into the game, and my prior on Bech is that he is barely an NFL-caliber player.
  * I’d prefer not to test that theory against a top-10 Broncos’ pass defense.


### Broncos (25.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Broncos are coming off their bye and a close win over the Panthers in Week 9.
  * [Jaylen Waddle](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylen-waddle) only played 14 snaps in that one due to a concussion, but since he had the bye week to rest, my assumption is he’ll play this week.
  * If that’s the case, Waddle is my preferred start among the pass catchers as a WR3.
  * [Jonah Coleman](https://www.4for4.com/player/jonah-coleman) is the only must-start on the team; life comes at you fast.
  * Coleman out-snapped JK Dobbins 58 to 11 in Week 9, running 20 more routes and taking 13 more carries.
  * [RJ Harvey](https://www.4for4.com/player/rj-harvey) isn’t coming back into this RB room until at least Week 13.
  * And there should be plenty of volume for Coleman with the Broncos 10-point favorites against a below-average Raiders’ run defense that is 2nd worst at allowing explosives.


##  Vikings at 49ers (O/U 45.5, SF -3.0)
### Vikings (21.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Vikings welcomed back [Kirk Cousins](https://www.4for4.com/player/kirk-cousins) and immediately passed at their highest rate relative to expectation of the season at +6.9%.
  * And they were just above average from an efficiency perspective, with [Justin Jefferson](https://www.4for4.com/player/justin-jefferson) earning 13 targets or an insane 44.8% of the team total; that’s his highest target share since Week 5 of 2024, according to Sumer Sports.
  * I’d expect Cousins to keep hitting the Jefferson button with Addison needing thumb surgery and missing at least the next couple of weeks.
  * I don’t know if TJ Hockenson will play this week, but I’d view him as a low-end TE1 if he does, given he’s TE10 in expected fantasy points per game this season.
  * Things cleared up a bit at RB as [DeeJay Dallas](https://www.4for4.com/player/deejay-dallas) only saw two snaps last week; at least we’re down to only [Jordan Mason](https://www.4for4.com/player/jordan-mason) and [Aaron Jones](https://www.4for4.com/player/aaron-jones).
  * The two essentially split the routes and carries with both high-end RB3s in 4for4’s rankings this week.
  * My preference is Jones, though, as he played four of the five goal-line snaps and seven of the eight snaps within the opponent’s 10 yard-line, according to JJ Zachariason.


### 49ers (24.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The 49ers’ passing attack is a behemoth; they hit 99th percentile passing efficiency again last week, and it seemingly doesn’t matter what pass catchers they have active.
  * But we’re going to test that theory this week against a Vikings’ pass defense that is 1st in efficiency, success rate, and CPOE allowed.
  * [George Kittle](https://www.4for4.com/player/george-kittle) paced the team with eight targets last week, and he’s 4for4’s TE1 overall for this week.
  * [Keenan Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/keenan-allen) was impressively second on the team with seven targets despite only a 60% route rate because he just joined the team; no matter where Allen goes, he just earns targets.
  * But I think Allen’s more of a WR4 this week given the matchup and that [Mike Evans](https://www.4for4.com/player/mike-evans) is expected to be back for this one.
  * [Deebo Samuel](https://www.4for4.com/player/deebo-samuel) only had three targets and three carries and is a clear sell high after his two-TD performance.
  * And then you’re starting [Christian McCaffrey](https://www.4for4.com/player/christian-mccaffrey) every week despite the tough matchup.
  * The Vikings’ only weakness is allowing explosive runs at the 6th-highest rate in the league.


##  Bengals at Commanders (O/U 52.5, CIN -6.5)
### Bengals (29.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Bengals and Chiefs are essentially the only teams willing to consistently pass the ball at a rate above expectation.
  * The Bengals hit a fantastic +15.1% PROE last week and 97th percentile passing efficiency in the process.
  * After a lot of offseason discussion about more under center work for [Joe Burrow](https://www.4for4.com/player/joe-burrow), he had zero dropbacks from under center in Week 10, according to Sumer Sports.
  * They’re just dropping back in shotgun and slinging the ball to their all-pro WRs.
  * Ja’Marr Chase led the team with 14 targets last week, and while [Tee Higgins](https://www.4for4.com/player/tee-higgins) only earned six, both are must-starts every week against any opponent.
  * [Chase Brown](https://www.4for4.com/player/chase-brown) finally delivered for our consolation-bracket-bound squads, scoring two receiving TDs, his first score since Week 1.
  * His usage has been improving recently; Brown was 2nd on the team in targets, has been playing the majority of 3rd down snaps recently, and also handled the two-minute drill snaps last week, according to PFF.
  * In addition to Brown’s usage improving, so has his efficiency, as he has a 53% rushing success rate over his last two games, compared to 33% before that, according to JJ Zachariason. 
  * Brown is a low-end RB1 in 4for4’s ranks this week, and theoretically the Bengals will score a rushing TD with an RB again this season.


### Commanders (23.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w11/fantasy-football-week-11-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Week 10 was encouraging for the Commanders; they were only slightly above average in both passing and rushing efficiency but their 63.4% pass success rate was 4th best of the week.
  * [Stefon Diggs](https://www.4for4.com/player/stefon-diggs) and Chig Okonkwo dominated the targets, combining for 18 of the team’s 33.
  * Both need context, though, as Diggs only ran 30 of the team’s 39 routes, which is pretty consistent with his usage all season.
  * Okonkwo only ran 25 of the routes, but that was because he injured his back in the 2nd quarter; he’s had one of the higher route shares at the TE position this season.
  * I’d view Diggs as a WR2 and Okonkwo as a low-end TE1 this week, with the Bengals’ defense historically susceptible to TE production.
  * [](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/willia3)[Antonio Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/antonio-williams) is out there for basically every route, but he only earned two targets; at least one of them was for a TD.
  * Williams is probably best viewed as a low-end WR3.
  * And it’s about to be a mess again at RB.
  * [Rachaad White](https://www.4for4.com/player/rachaad-white) and [Austin Ekeler](https://www.4for4.com/player/austin-ekeler) split the snaps, routes, and carries in Week 10, but my assumption is that [Jacory Croskey-Merritt](https://www.4for4.com/player/jacory-croskey-merritt) returns from IR this week.
  * If JCM returns, I just don’t know how you feel confident about any of these RBs, even against a bad Bengals’ run defense.
