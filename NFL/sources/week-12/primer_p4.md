Corum has been awesome this season with his workload. Among 55 qualifying rushers, he is first in rushing success rate, fourth in explosive run rate, and 12th in missed tackle rate. He should post another nice stat line this week. Over their last five games, Green Bay has allowed the tenth-highest rushing success rate, the sixth-highest yards after contact per attempt, the third-highest missed tackle rate, and the third-highest rushing success rate to gap runs (57.3% gap).
#### **Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Tyler Higbee (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tyler-higbee.php)**
In Week 10, [Tyler Higbee](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tyler-higbee.php)‘s route share dropped to 32.1%, but the Rams went extremely run-heavy. Higbee still had a 67.6% snap rate and tied for the team lead with a 15.4% target share. Since Week 3, Higbee has had a 73.6% snap rate, a 65.3% route share, a 12.7% target share, 1.34 YPRR, a 15.4% first-read share, and five red zone targets. Higbee could return low-end TE1 value this week. Over their last five games, Green Bay has allowed the ninth-most yards per target to tight ends while ranking 18th in fantasy points per opportunity.
#### **Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**
_Players in consideration for lineups, but temper your expectations this week._
**[Matthew Stafford (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/matthew-stafford.php)**
In their last game before the bye, with a depleted receiving room without [Davante Adams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/davante-adams.php) and [Puka Nacua](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/puka-nacua.php), [Matthew Stafford](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/matthew-stafford.php) was the QB11 for the week. We do have to remember that it was against the Cardinals, though. Stafford won’t have an easy road like that to travel this week. Stafford also has quietly been playing some bad football as he guts it out with an elbow ailment. Since Week 7, among 21 qualifying passers, he has the sixth-lowest yards per attempt, the third-lowest aDOT, the seventh-highest off-target rate, and the sixth-lowest highly accurate throw rate. Stafford, with his downgraded weapons, is only a midrange QB2 with a bad matchup this week. Over their last five games, Green Bay has allowed the eighth-lowest yards per attempt, the 14th-lowest passing touchdown rate, the 13th-lowest CPOE, and the tenth-lowest EPA per dropback. Also, during those five games, Green Bay has had the fourth-best pressure rate and the second-best quick pressure rate.
**[Kaleb Johnson (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kaleb-johnson-rb.php)**
Kaleb Johnson should handle the bulk of the early-down work for Green Bay this week. In his last two games, he has averaged 12 rushing attempts and 55 rushing yards. In Week 10, with Chris Brooks back and MarShawn Lloyd active, Johnson had a 25.9% snap rate, 58.8% of the rushing work, and a 5.4% route share. He’s an early-down-only option in the Green Bay offense and a touchdown-dependent flex. With Johnson’s value exclusively tied to his rushing production, he’s not a player that I want to depend on as a flex this week. Over their last five games, the Rams have been a STRONG run defense, allowing the third-lowest explosive run rate, the lowest rushing success rate, and the fifth-lowest missed tackle rate.
**[Konata Mumpfield (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/konata-mumpfield.php)**
In Week 10, [Konata Mumpfield](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/konata-mumpfield.php) was the WR35 in weekly scoring (PPR). He led the team with a 75% route share with a 15.4% target share, 3.86 YPRR (81 receiving yards), one deep target, and a 9.1% first-read share. That first-read share is scary, but the rest of his metrics look promising. Mumpfield is a low-end PPR flex this week who is likely better served on the bench for Week 12. Over their last five games, Green Bay has allowed the sixth-fewest yards per target and the 12th-fewest fantasy points per opportunity to perimeter wide receivers.
