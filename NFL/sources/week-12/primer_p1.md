I don’t know if anyone is still holding onto any hope for [Courtland Sutton](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/courtland-sutton.php) this season, but I figured I’d include his outlook for Week 12, anyway. Since Week 5, Sutton has been the WR65 in fantasy points per game with one double-digit PPR outing. During this span, he has had a 13.4% target share with 1.18 YPRR, four red zone targets, four deep targets, and a 16% first-read share. Sit Sutton again this week.
