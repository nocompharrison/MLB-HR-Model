Welcome to another edition of Hoopes There It Is! We’re previewing every game in Week 12, highlighting advanced stats like EPA, success rate, pass rate over expected (PROE), and completion percentage over expected (CPOE) to help make the best decisions for your fantasy teams. I’ll provide a quick start/sit section at the top that stems from the longer write-ups. And if you’re interested in more, which I hope you are, you can [jump to any of the 16 games this week](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it#links). Let’s get started!
## Start ‘Em, Sit ‘Em
[Chris Rodriguez](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-rodriguez) (**Start**): [Bhayshul Tuten](https://www.4for4.com/player/bhayshul-tuten) is clearly a better RB than [Chris Rodriguez](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-rodriguez), as the Jaguars’ playing time choices have shown all season. But Tuten has been dealing with both ankle and foot injuries, and he limped off the field last week with five minutes left in the game. That’s been far too common a sight in recent weeks. Now, I don’t know Tuten’s status at the time I’m writing this article. If Tuten sits, Rodriguez is a clear start as the Jaguars are two-touchdown home favorites against the Titans. But I’d consider Rodriguez even if Tuten plays. Obviously, we’ll need to stay tuned to the coach speak on Tuten’s health and role leading up to the game. But if I’m the Jaguars, I’m limiting Tuten’s work this week in a game they’ll easily win without him. And that’d be excellent for Rodriguez in fantasy.
[Jack Bech](https://www.4for4.com/player/jack-bech) (**Sit**): [Jack Bech](https://www.4for4.com/player/jack-bech) is coming off easily his best game of the season. He turned nine targets into an 8/88/1 line last week. For context, his previous game high in receiving yards was 25 back in Week 2. But that’s now back-to-back games for Bech with at least seven targets, so you might be thinking this is sustainable for him. I think this role disappears as soon as [Brock Bowers](https://www.4for4.com/player/brock-bowers) steps back on the field. Yes, Bowers is a TE and Bech is a WR. But they functionally play the same role for the Raiders’ offense, with Bech obviously a less talented version. The Browns have a top-10 pass defense, but the matchup almost doesn’t matter. If Bowers is back, Bech can safely hit waivers.
[](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/willia3)[Antonio Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/antonio-williams) (**Start)** : Here’s a flash performance that I am willing to chase. [](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/willia3)[Antonio Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/antonio-williams) earned 11 targets last week, which was three more than his previous career high. And he turned those targets into a 9/122/0 line. That was nearly double his previous game high in receiving yards. But I think there’s some potential for sustainability here in Week 12. Williams is running nearly all the team’s routes; he was 1st on the team in routes last week with four more than [Stefon Diggs](https://www.4for4.com/player/stefon-diggs). And now Williams plays an atrocious Cardinals’ pass defense that is dead last in the league in efficiency allowed. And looking ahead, he gets the Titans in Week 13.

##  Packers at Rams (O/U 44.5, LAR -2.5)
### Packers (21.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Packers are coming off a well-timed bye that followed a 20-point loss to the Vikings.
  * They only managed 55 offensive plays, about six below their season average, with [Matthew Golden](https://www.4for4.com/player/matthew-golden)’s nine targets dominating the 26 team total, followed by four each for [Christian Watson](https://www.4for4.com/player/christian-watson) and [Tucker Kraft](https://www.4for4.com/player/tucker-kraft).
  * The target consolidation we typically see for Golden and Watson, plus the frequency that those targets are deep down the field, keeps Golden and Watson in must-start territory.
  * But things aren’t much easier this week against a Rams pass defense that is 4th in efficiency allowed and prevents completions versus expectation at the best rate in the league.
  * It’s also an interesting matchup in terms of air yards, as the Packers have the 4th highest air yards per attempt but opponents of the Rams attack them with the 6th lowest aDoT in the league.
  * It’s a mess at RB with [Josh Jacobs](https://www.4for4.com/player/josh-jacobs) unlikely to play again this week.
  * [](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-brooks)[Chris Brooks](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-brooks-0) led the RBs in Week 10 in snaps and routes, [MarShawn Lloyd](https://www.4for4.com/player/marshawn-lloyd) led in targets, and [Kaleb Johnson](https://www.4for4.com/player/kaleb-johnson) led in carries. 
  * You can’t start any Packers’ RB given the rotation, the team’s utter inability to run efficiently, and the Rams’ run defense that is top-four in every stat I track.


### Rams (23.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Rams are also coming off their bye week, which followed a 34-6 beatdown over the lowly Cardinals.
  * They had a whopping 20 more rush attempts than team targets in Week 10.
  * And the targets that existed were evenly split between [Kyren Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyren-williams), [Blake Corum](https://www.4for4.com/player/blake-corum), [Konata Mumpfield](https://www.4for4.com/player/konata-mumpfield), and [Tyler Higbee](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-higbee).
  * None of the TEs ran more than nine routes, and with [Terrance Ferguson](https://www.4for4.com/player/terrance-ferguson) now officially done for the season, no one is emerging to consolidate targets in this room.
  * If you had to play a Rams’ WR, it would be Mumpfield, given he led the team with 25 routes in Week 10 and was tied for 2nd on the team in targets.
  * My preference would be to sit all Rams except the two RBs, Williams and Corum.
  * Williams and Corum both saw 39 snaps in Week 10 and were frequently on the field together as the Rams tried to find ways to get their best remaining offensive players on the field together.
  * Williams ran eight more routes and earned one more target, while Corum saw five more carries.
  * Like usual, we see a lot more Corum than usual in games where the Rams lead, while Williams dominates snaps when the Rams are in catch-up mode.
  * If you look at the box score, [Ronnie Rivers](https://www.4for4.com/player/ronnie-rivers) had 15 carries in Week 10, but that was on 17 snaps during garbage time; you don’t have to worry about Rivers going forward.
  * The Packers’ run defense is mostly average this season but does allow successful runs at the 5th-highest rate in the league, while successful runs remain the Rams’ superpower. 


##  Bears at Lions (O/U 55.5, CHI -2.5)
### Bears (29.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Bears had a similar implied point total last week but disappointed fantasy managers.
  * They passed at a rate 8.8% below expectation, partially explaining the low 20 team targets, and were about average in passing efficiency.
  * The 20 targets were also spread around, with [Rome Odunze](https://www.4for4.com/player/rome-odunze) and [Kalif Raymond](https://www.4for4.com/player/kalif-raymond) tying for the team lead with four, followed by [Luther Burden](https://www.4for4.com/player/luther-burden), [Zavion Thomas](https://www.4for4.com/player/zavion-thomas), and [Colston Loveland](https://www.4for4.com/player/colston-loveland) each earning three.
  * I’d still start the big three Bears receivers in Burden, Loveland, and Odunze given the Bears’ implied points and the Lions’ pass defense that is 26th in efficiency and 25th in success rate allowed.
  * It’s suddenly a bit tricky at RB.
  * D’Andre Swift saw five more snaps, three more routes, and five more carries than [Kyle Monangai](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyle-monangai) last week.
  * But Swift lost a fumble early in the 4th quarter and didn’t play a snap after that, leading to a lot of [Salvon Ahmed](https://www.4for4.com/player/salvon-ahmed). 
  * There’s at least a chance that [Ben Johnson](https://www.4for4.com/player/ben-johnson) punishes Swift for the fumble in this one, especially given the short week, so stay tuned for the coach speak on this situation.
  * The Lions’ run defense is much stronger than their pass defense this season, but Monangai would see a crazy amount of volume if Swift’s role is dinged by the fumble.


### Lions (26.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * There’s not much to say about the Lions (complimentary).
  * They did pass at their highest rate versus expectation of the season last week, hitting +8.3% PROE.
  * Their expected pass rate was low, though, so they only had two more carries than targets anyway.
  * And they were efficient in both phases, hitting 87th percentile passing and 95th percentile rushing efficiency. 
  * [Amon-Ra St. Brown](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/stbra1) and [Sam LaPorta](https://www.4for4.com/player/sam-laporta) led the team with six targets each, followed by four each for [Jameson Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/jameson-williams) and [Jahmyr Gibbs](https://www.4for4.com/player/jahmyr-gibbs).
  * Gibbs’ route rate was a bit low, but there’s nothing actionable here as he still has two more expected fantasy points per game than any other flex player in the league.
  * The Bears’ pass defense is no joke, ranking in the top eight in efficiency and success rate allowed, with Jeffery Simmons helping to boost their sack rate to 2nd in the league; I’m still starting the big four Lions skill players.


##  Eagles at Cowboys (O/U 47.5, DAL -8.5)
### Eagles (19.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Eagles were unsurprisingly bad and run-heavy last week, hitting 16th percentile passing efficiency, 37th percentile rushing efficiency, and a -10.6% PROE. 
  * [DeVonta Smith](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/smithd4) absolutely dominated the targets, earning 10 of the team’s only 19 targets; he’s the only receiver you can start at this point.
  * And [Saquon Barkley](https://www.4for4.com/player/saquon-barkley) similarly dominated the RB work for most of the game, taking 34 of the first 40 snaps, according to PFF.
  * But Barkley left with a stinger in the 4th quarter, and [Will Shipley](https://www.4for4.com/player/will-shipley) took the final 13 snaps of the game.
  * Assuming Barkley plays, I’d start him against the worst run defense in the league.
  * But we have to temper our expectations for the entire offense, despite the matchup, as only [Daniel Jones](https://www.4for4.com/player/daniel-jones) and [Kyle McCord](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyle-mccord) have been less efficient than [Joe Milton](https://www.4for4.com/player/joe-milton) since Milton became the starter, according to JJ Zachariason.


### Cowboys (28.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Cowboys’ offense was unbelievable last week.
  * They hit 99th percentile passing and 87th percentile rushing efficiency against a truly terrible Titans’ defense.
  * Their 68.3% offensive success rate was the highest for any team in a game since at least 2018, according to Sumer Sports.
  * They were the first team this season to score on all their non-kneel-down possessions.
  * [CeeDee Lamb](https://www.4for4.com/player/ceedee-lamb) and [George Pickens](https://www.4for4.com/player/george-pickens) combined for 22 of the team’s 33 targets and are must-starts every week.
  * As is [Javonte Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/javonte-williams), given his 24 goal-line carries this season are the most by an RB through 11 weeks since at least 2016, according to JJ Zachariason. 
  * It’s not quite as fantastic a matchup this week as it was versus the Titans, but the Eagles are still 24th in passing efficiency and 29th in rushing efficiency allowed.


##  Chiefs at Bills (O/U 47.5, BUF -3.0)
### Chiefs (22.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Chiefs’ passing attack was awesome again last week, hitting 94th percentile efficiency and, importantly, passing at a rate 2.6% above expectation; they join the Bengals as the only team with a positive PROE in every game this season.
  * [Rashee Rice](https://www.4for4.com/player/rashee-rice) and [Travis Kelce](https://www.4for4.com/player/travis-kelce) tied for the team lead with seven targets; I view Rice as a low-end WR2 and Kelce as a low-end TE1, so both are starts.
  * Rice’s route share continues to be lower than last year, and he hasn’t hit a 70% route share since his Week 4 injury, according to JJ Zachariason; the [Josh Downs](https://www.4for4.com/player/josh-downs) trade isn’t helping matters.
  * Speaking of personal favorite Downs, he’s a risky flex play given he only ran 13 of the team’s 41 routes last week as he is being very slowly ramped up on his new team; he still earned four targets on that tiny route share and has looked awesome in his limited opportunities.
  * [Emmett Johnson](https://www.4for4.com/player/emmett-johnson) dominated the RB work last week, taking 59 of the 72 snaps, running 33 of the team’s 41 routes, and taking 23 of the 29 RB carries; that is elite RB1 usage.
  * [Kenneth Walker](https://www.4for4.com/player/kenneth-walker) practiced on Tuesday, but his status for this week is still uncertain at the time I’m writing.
  * You’re starting Walker if he goes and Johnson if Walker doesn’t, as the Bills’ run defense is much worse than their pass defense this season.


### Bills (25.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Bills passed at a rate 10.3% above expectation last week, their highest mark of the season and the first time in the last four weeks they had a positive PROE.
  * That led to 43 team targets, something we very rarely see from this Bills team.
  * DJ Moore paced the team with 10 targets, followed by nine for [Khalil Shakir](https://www.4for4.com/player/khalil-shakir) and eight for [Ty Johnson](https://www.4for4.com/player/ty-johnson).
  * Moore left in the 2nd quarter with a knee injury but ultimately returned to the game and ran 39 of the team’s 49 routes.
  * Moore is a WR2 in my view this week against a tough Chiefs’ pass defense that is top-nine in every stat I track except sack rate.
  * I’m a lot more interested in Shakir if [Dalton Kincaid](https://www.4for4.com/player/dalton-kincaid) sits again this week, as Shakir played a season-high 81% of snaps in Kincaid’s absence, according to PFF. 
  * My assumption is that part of the reason Kincaid missed last week was to set him up to play in this game, but his status is still uncertain at the time I’m writing.
  * At RB, [James Cook](https://www.4for4.com/player/james-cook) remains a must-start despite the matchup, given the offense and that he took 22 of the 23 RB carries last week; it would be nice if he ran more routes than Johnson, though.


##  Broncos at Steelers (O/U 38.5, DEN -3.0)
### Broncos (20.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Broncos played surprisingly well on offense last week, hitting 61st percentile passing and 72nd percentile rushing efficiency; their 56.2% rush success rate was particularly good.
  * [Evan Engram](https://www.4for4.com/player/evan-engram) came out of nowhere to lead the team with eight targets; he only ran 26 of the team’s 37 routes, but that was just two off [Jaylen Waddle](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylen-waddle)’s lead.
  * I’m personally not chasing the Engram targets from last week, with Waddle the only Broncos’ pass catcher I’d be willing to start.
  * But [Jonah Coleman](https://www.4for4.com/player/jonah-coleman) is an absolute must-start until further notice.
  * Obviously, the three TDs were fantastic last week, but his workload is insane; Coleman took 62 of the team’s 73 snaps, ran 23 of the team’s 37 routes, and took 24 of the 26 RB carries.
  * JK Dobbins only played one snap in Week 11 and was just suspended for Week 12 for “conduct detrimental to the team”.
  * Only Ja’Marr Chase and [Jahmyr Gibbs](https://www.4for4.com/player/jahmyr-gibbs) are averaging more PPR points per game than Coleman since Week 8, according to JJ Zachariason.
  * This Steelers’ run defense is no joke, ranking top six in efficiency, success rate, and explosive run rate allowed; you’re starting Coleman anyway.


### Steelers (17.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * If you’re looking for positives from last week, at least the Steelers passed at a rate 6.3% above expectation, their first positive PROE in their last four games.
  * They had sub-31st percentile efficiency both passing and running and had sub-37% success rates in both phases as well.
  * [DK Metcalf](https://www.4for4.com/player/dk-metcalf) only earned four targets last week, but that was partially because he was ejected with a third of the game left.
  * In Metcalf’s absence, [Germie Bernard](https://www.4for4.com/player/germie-bernard) earned 10 targets but managed only 40 yards on them; someone named [Max Hurleman](https://www.4for4.com/player/max-hurleman) earned seven targets, and then [Roman Wilson](https://www.4for4.com/player/roman-wilson) earned six.
  * It doesn’t sound like Metcalf will be suspended, and Hurleman only had a 3% route rate before Metcalf was ejected, so you can safely forget about him.
  * Only Metcalf ranks in the top-48 WRs in 4for4’s initial projections, and I’d agree with that; you don’t really want to start anyone on this passing offense given Rodgers is unwilling or unable (both?) to throw the ball down the field and the Broncos’ pass defense is a top-11 unit across the board.
  * At RB, [Jaylen Warren](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylen-warren) took 48 snaps to [Rico Dowdle](https://www.4for4.com/player/rico-dowdle)’s 23; that included 18 more routes and four more carries, though Dowdle somehow earned five targets to Warren’s three.
  * Dowdle won’t earn a target on a third of his routes going forward.
  * Warren is clearly the more talented back and is getting more opportunities than Dowdle, though the split should really be more dramatic if we’re being honest.
  * It’s a fine matchup for Warren, though, as the Broncos’ run defense is their clear weakness, ranking 23rd in efficiency and 20th in explosive play rate allowed.


##  Titans at Jaguars (O/U 47.5, JAX -14.5)
### Titans (16.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Titans are implied to score about a touchdown less this week compared to last; you unfortunately don’t get to play the Cowboys every game.
  * Their -14.3% PROE indicates they didn’t really try to win, though they hit 60th percentile passing efficiency and a good 53.8% pass success rate when they did pass.
  * Their running game was awesome with 78th percentile efficiency and a fantastic 64.3% rushing success rate. 
  * [Chimere Dike](https://www.4for4.com/player/chimere-dike) actually led the team with seven targets, followed by six for [Calvin Ridley](https://www.4for4.com/player/calvin-ridley), and five for [Carnell Tate](https://www.4for4.com/player/carnell-tate).
  * My preference remains to start only Tate among the receivers, especially given the low implied point total, but Dike is at least worth adding to your watchlist.
  * Dike was 2nd on the team in routes, behind Tate, because he was the slot receiver in 11 personnel but also joined Tate on some snaps in 2-WR sets, according to PFF; that really hurt [Elic Ayomanor](https://www.4for4.com/player/elic-ayomanor)’s playing time.
  * At RB, [Tony Pollard](https://www.4for4.com/player/tony-pollard) dominated the work with [Tyjae Spears](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyjae-spears) on IR, taking 54 of the team’s 68 snaps and 21 of the 25 RB carries. 
  * Pollard ran 67% of the routes but didn’t earn a target in the process; he’s a volume-based mid-range RB2 who *typically* has limited receiving upside, though he did have nine targets in Week 10 against this same Jaguars team.


### Jaguars (31.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * A healthy 31 implied points given the Titans gave up 51 points to the Cowboys and 40 points to this same Jaguars team just two weeks ago.
  * The Jaguars’ passing attack struggled to 18th percentile efficiency last week as Abdul Carter had 10 pressures, the 4th most for any player in a game this season, according to Sumer Sports.
  * But we at least had volume as the Jaguars passed at a rate 6% above expectation, their highest rate of the season.
  * [Parker Washington](https://www.4for4.com/player/parker-washington) earned a whopping 17 targets; this is really bullish given this was the first time in over a month that the Jaguars were in a competitive game.
  * Washington is a clear WR1, and 4for4’s ranks have [Jakobi Meyers](https://www.4for4.com/player/jakobi-meyers), who earned 10 targets last week, on the WR2/3 borderline; the Titans’ pass defense is dead-last in success rate, explosive play rate, and CPOE allowed.
  * You’re not starting [Brian Thomas Jr.](https://www.4for4.com/player/brian-thomas-jr), but he ran 32 of the team’s 52 routes last week, earning three targets and a TD in the process; just something promising for our 12th-place best ball teams.
  * At RB, [Bhayshul Tuten](https://www.4for4.com/player/bhayshul-tuten) remains the clear RB you want in the backfield when he’s healthy; the problem is, he hasn’t been healthy in a while.
  * Tuten has been dealing with both ankle and foot injuries, and he left the game with five minutes left in the 4th quarter last week.
  * [Chris Rodriguez](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-rodriguez) is a clear start if Tuten sits this week and might even be playable if Tuten goes, given the Jaguars are two-TD favorites; if I were the Jaguars, I’d limit Tuten’s work in this one.

  * Bech finished the day with an 8/88/1 line, though I wouldn’t chase it if Bowers is back this week; Bowers is the only pass catcher I’d want to start on the Raiders.
  * [Ashton Jeanty](https://www.4for4.com/player/ashton-jeanty) dominated the RB work last week, taking 58 snaps to Mike Washington’s 15, including 30 more routes and two more carries.
  * And Jeanty had two big explosives, including a 46-yard run and a 68-yard receiving TD; 4for4 has Jeanty on the RB1/2 border this week, and he’s a clear start.
  * The Browns’ run defense is much worse than their pass defense, and they allow the highest rate of explosive runs against them in the league.


### Browns (23.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Browns are coming off their bye week and a surprise 27-24 win over the Texans in Week 10.
  * [Harold Fannin](https://www.4for4.com/player/harold-fannin) led the team with nine targets, followed by eight for [KC Concepcion](https://www.4for4.com/player/kc-concepcion), and six for [Dylan Sampson](https://www.4for4.com/player/dylan-sampson).
  * Fannin is a clear start given his recent target earnings and the fact that the Raiders’ pass defense is bottom-five in almost every stat I track.
  * I’d also fire up Concepcion; he’s on the WR2/3 borderline in 4for4’s ranks, and again, it’s a great matchup here.
  * And my bet is that this is a [Quinshon Judkins](https://www.4for4.com/player/quinshon-judkins) week more than a [Dylan Sampson](https://www.4for4.com/player/dylan-sampson) week, though Sampson saw three more snaps than Judkins in Week 10.
  * But with the Browns 4.5-point home favorites, that should prevent Judkins from being game-scripted out; he’s on the RB2/3 borderline.


##  Panthers at Buccaneers (O/U 43.5, TB -3.0)
### Panthers (20.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Panthers’ offense was pretty good against the Ravens last week, hitting 60th percentile passing and 72nd percentile rushing efficiency. 
  * That included a strong 57.1% passing success rate, with [Bryce Young](https://www.4for4.com/player/bryce-young)’s 7.5 yard aDoT only the 11th lowest on the week and about a half yard higher than in his previous game.
  * [Tetairoa McMillan](https://www.4for4.com/player/tetairoa-mcmillan) and [Jalen Coker](https://www.4for4.com/player/jalen-coker) went back to dominating the targets, taking a combined 15 of the team’s 29.
  * Coker did get hurt near the end of the game and entered concussion protocol but he’s already cleared it.
  * It’s a really tough matchup for the WRs this week, given the Buccaneers’ pass defense is 2nd in efficiency, 6th in explosive play rate, and 6th in CPOE allowed.
  * McMillan is a WR2, while personal favorite Coker is more of a WR4 given he hasn’t gotten home in fantasy for us since Week 6, around the time the Panthers started having offensive line injuries.
  * [Mitchell Evans](https://www.4for4.com/player/mitchell-evans) was third on the team in targets with five, but I wouldn’t chase that as he only ran 11 of the team’s 33 routes.
  * And then [Chuba Hubbard](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/hubbac1) is a must start with one of the best workloads in the league, taking 58 of the team’s 71 snaps last week, including 21 of the 33 routes and 27 of the team’s 33 RB carries; it’s a tough matchup for him, though.


### Buccaneers (23.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w12/fantasy-football-week-12-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * While the Buccaneers continued to pass at a rate below expectation last week, their -5.5% PROE was in a normal range compared to the three games before that.
  * [Ted Hurst](https://www.4for4.com/player/ted-hurst) popped up for easily the best game of his career, turning his team-leading seven targets into a 5/56/2 line.
  * I wouldn’t start Hurst this week until we see his target earning solidify a bit, but he did run 32 of the team’s 44 routes last week, so it’s not like last week’s target earning isn’t at least possible going forward.
  * The only other Buccaneers receiver you’re considering is [Emeka Egbuka](https://www.4for4.com/player/emeka-egbuka), who finished 2nd on the team last week with five targets; Egbuka is on the WR3/4 borderline in 4for4’s ranks this week and justifiably so given Egbuka only has three TDs on the season.
  * At RB, you’re starting [Bucky Irving](https://www.4for4.com/player/bucky-irving), who led Kenny Gainwell in snaps last week, 47 to 28, routes 21 to 18, and carries 18 to four.
  * The Panthers’ run defense has been above average in efficiency and success rate allowed but susceptible to explosive runs, something the Buccaneers have been great at this season.
