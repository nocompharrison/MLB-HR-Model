**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Travis Etienne Jr. (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/travis-etienne.php)**
Last week, Travis Etienne was the RB27 in weekly scoring. He had a 45.1% snap rate, 63.6% of the rushing workload, and a 25% route share (zero targets). Etienne had 14 rushing attempts and 76 rushing yards. Etienne still didn’t look fully healthy, though. He broke a long run last week that normally we’d see him house for a touchdown, but he seemed to run out of gas in the open field as he was ultimately tackled before the goal line. In his last two games, he has had only a 5% explosive run rate and a 9% missed tackle rate, so those numbers are telling a similar story. Much like the Bears’ struggling run defense last week, maybe Cincy’s horrible run defense can help prop him up this week. Etienne is a flex with RB2 upside. Over their last five games, Cincy has allowed the second-highest rushing success rate, the third-most yards after contact per attempt, and the 12th-highest missed tackle rate. 
**[Jordyn Tyson (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordyn-tyson.php)**
[Jordyn Tyson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordyn-tyson.php) had a banner Week 10. Tyson was the WR12 while seeing a 72.1% snap share and route share. He gobbled up a 37.1% target share with 80 receiving yards (2.58 YPRR), one red zone target, two deep targets, and a 41.7% first-read share. Tyson also had a 23.1% designed target rate that week. Well, Week 11 didn’t build upon that big game. Last week, Tyson had only a 49.3% snap rate, a 42.3% route share, a 2.3% target share, one reception, six receiving yards, and a 7.1% first-read share. Kellen Moore said that Tyson’s playing time decrease was merely the result of them utilizing no huddle and not switching out personnel, which does track with the fact that they were second in no huddle plays last week with 15 behind only the Titans. I lean that Tyson will have a bounce-back game this week. He could be heavily utilized again if [DJ Turner](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dj-turner.php) is shadowing [Chris Olave](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/chris-olave.php). Over their last five games, Cincy has allowed the 12th-most receiving fantasy points per game to slot receivers while also giving up the 14th-most fantasy points per opportunity to the position.
