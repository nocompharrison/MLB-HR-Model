**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Rachaad White (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rachaad-white.php)**
In his first start before the bye week, [Rachaad White](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rachaad-white.php) finished with 11 touches and 57 total yards as the RB10 for the week. White played 70.5% of the snaps with 41.2% of the rushing work and a 51.1% route share (12.5% target share). I expect White to see a similar workload this week. White is a volume play only. His per-touch efficiency has been decent with 2.06 yards after contact per attempt and a 17% missed tackle rate. White should have a decent outing. Over their last five games, Philly has allowed the tenth-highest rushing success rate, the ninth-most rushing yards over expectation per attempt, the fifth-highest yards after contact per attempt, and the third-highest missed tackle rate.
**[Stefon Diggs (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/stefon-diggs.php)**
With [Terry McLaurin](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/terry-mclaurin.php) out for the season, [Stefon Diggs](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/stefon-diggs.php) will be leading the way for Washington’s aerial attack moving forward. Diggs is the WR24 in fantasy points per game with a 20.9% target share, 1.60 YPRR, five red zone targets, four deep targets, and a 25.4% first-read share. Diggs should be a strong WR2 this week. Over their last five games, Philly has allowed the 13th-most yards per target, the most receiving fantasy points per game, and the seventh-most fantasy points per opportunity to slot receivers.
**[Dallas Goedert (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dallas-goedert.php)**
Over the last two games, with [Tanner McKee](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tanner-mckee.php) under center, [Dallas Goedert](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dallas-goedert.php) has been the TE14 and TE16 in weekly scoring. Goedert has had an 18.8% target share with 1.85 YPRR, one red zone target, and a 25% first-read share. Goedert is a solid TE1 this week. Over their last five games, Washington has allowed the eighth-most yards per target, the seventh-most receiving yards, and the third-most fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends.
**Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Tanner McKee (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tanner-mckee.php)**
Over the last two games, [Tanner McKee](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tanner-mckee.php) has been the QB21 and QB22 in fantasy points per game. Among 40 qualifying passers, he is bottom of the barrel, ranking 39th in yards per attempt, passing success rate, and catchable target rate. Despite a promising matchup this week, McKee couldn’t take advantage of a struggling Dallas secondary last week, so I have zero faith that he’ll be able to do much against Washington this week. Over their last five games, Washington has allowed the sixth-highest passing touchdown rate, the tenth-highest passing success rate, and the sixth-highest passer rating.
**[Antonio Williams (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/antonio-williams.php)**
[Antonio Williams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/antonio-williams.php) will see an elevated role in the Washington passing attack with [Terry McLaurin](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/terry-mclaurin.php) out for the rest of the season. Since Week 4, Williams has had a 13.7% target share, 1.12 YPRR, two deep targets, and a 16.7% first-read share. Williams is a decent flex play this week. Over their last five games, Philly has allowed the 12th-most yards per target, the most receiving fantasy points per game, and the ninth-most fantasy points per opportunity to wide receivers.
**[Makai Lemon (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/makai-lemon.php)**
Well, it happened. [Makai Lemon](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/makai-lemon.php) had his best game of the season against a horrible Dallas secondary. Lemon was the WR13 for the week. Over the last two weeks, Lemon has had a 16.3% target share with 1.13 YPRR, one red zone target, and a 21.4% first-read share. Lemon offers some flex upside this week. Over their last five games, Washington has allowed the 13th-most fantasy points per opportunity to slot receivers while ranking 16th in yards per target allowed to the position.
**[Chig Okonkwo (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/chigoziem-okonkwo.php)**
In his last two games, before the bye week, [Chig Okonkwo](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/chigoziem-okonkwo.php) was the TE15 and TE11 in weekly scoring. In those games, he had a 72.6% route share, a 16.9% target share, 2.11 YPRR, one deep target, and a 23.4% first-read share. Okonkwo could post another TE1 stat line this week. He’s a strong streaming option. Over their last five games, Philly has allowed the 11th-most yards per target to tight ends while ranking 15th in fantasy points per opportunity allowed to the position.

**[Sam Darnold (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/sam-darnold.php)**
[Sam Darnold](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/sam-darnold.php) has been a ghost for most of the season as the QB27 in fantasy points per game. In his last four games, he has had more than 15 fantasy points in any game. That could easily change this week. Among 34 qualifying passers, Darnold is 15th in yards per attempt, 21st in passing success rate, 13th in highly accurate throw rate, and fourth in catchable target rate. Darnold is a strong streamer this week. He could easily post a QB1 stat line this week. Over their last five games, Chicago has allowed the most yards per attempt, the seventh-highest passing touchdown rate, and the fifth-highest passer rating.
**Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Caleb Williams (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/caleb-williams.php)**
[Caleb Williams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/caleb-williams.php) is the QB6 in fantasy points per game. Since returning from his hamstring injury, Williams has averaged only 10.5 rushing yards, so I don’t see that being a huge part of his game this week. If his hamstring is back to 100%, maybe he surprises me, though. Among 34 qualifying passers, Williams is fifth in yards per attempt, fifth in passing success rate, second in highly accurate throw rate, and first in catchable target rate. He is having a wonderful season as he has improved his accuracy. This will be a huge test for how much he has grown this week. Seattle’s pass defense is a buzz saw. Over their last five games, Seattle has allowed the third-fewest yards per attempt, the sixth-lowest passing success rate, the tenth-lowest CPOE, and the third-lowest EPA per dropback. In most leagues, despite the horrible matchup, you’re still starting Williams.
**Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**
_Players in consideration for lineups, but temper your expectations this week._
**[Emanuel Wilson (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/emanuel-wilson.php)**
Last week, [Emanuel Wilson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/emanuel-wilson.php) bounced back as the RB8 for the week. Wilson could easily lead the backfield again in touches this week, but we have to wait on [Zach Charbonnet](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/zach-charbonnet.php) news. I’ll update his outlook on Saturday.
**[D’Andre Swift (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dandre-swift.php)**
[D’Andre Swift](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dandre-swift.php) is the RB12 in fantasy points per game, averaging 19.7 touches and 89.7 total yards. Swift has had a 60.4% snap rate, 47.2% of the rushing work, a 38.8% route share, and a 10.4% target share. Among 42 qualifying rushers, Swift is 19th in yards after contact per attempt and seventh in missed tackle rate. He’ll have to lean on his volume and touchdown equity this week against an elite Seattle run defense. Over their last five games, Seattle has allowed the fourth-lowest explosive run rate, the second-lowest rushing success rate, and the fourth-fewest yards after contact per attempt.
**[Luther Burden (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/luther-burden-iii.php)**
[Luther Burden](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/luther-burden-iii.php) is the WR24 in fantasy points per game and looks electric any time he touches the ball. [Luther Burden](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/luther-burden-iii.php)‘s route share dipped to 41.5% in Week 6, but he was back up at 66.7% last week. Overall, Burden has a 19.5% target share, 2.70 YPRR, nine red zone targets, three deep targets, and a 25% first-read share. This week, he’ll face a Seattle pass defense that has the seventh-highest two-high rate in the NFL (60.2%). Against two-high, Burden has a 25% TPRR and 3.07 YPRR. Those numbers are incredibly strong. Burden has a nasty matchup this week, but he also has the talent to walk away with a solid stat line this week despite it. Across their last five games, Seattle has allowed the fewest yards per target and the fewest fantasy points per opportunity to slot receivers.
**[Rome Odunze (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rome-odunze.php)**
While I think [Luther Burden](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/luther-burden-iii.php) can overcome a horrible matchup, I don’t share the same optimism for [Rome Odunze](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rome-odunze.php) this week. Odunze is the WR55 in fantasy points per game with a 15.4% target share with 1.70 YPRR, five red zone targets, eight deep targets, and a 17.6% first-read share. Across their last five games, Seattle has allowed the tenth-fewest receiving fantasy points per game and the 14th-fewest yards per target to perimeter wide receivers. Sit Odunze this week.
**[Colston Loveland (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/colston-loveland.php)**
Over the last two weeks, with [Caleb Williams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/caleb-williams.php) back, [Colston Loveland](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/colston-loveland.php) has been the TE4 and TE3 in weekly scoring. In those two games, Loveland has had a 26.1% target share with 2.39 YPRR, four red zone targets, one deep target, and a 31.7% first-read share. He has been ELITE! This was exactly who we thought we were drafting this summer. Like [Luther Burden](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/luther-burden-iii.php), Loveland has a tough mountain to climb this week, but he could still succeed despite the horrible matchup. Over their last five games, Seattle has allowed the second-fewest yards per target and fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends. Just note that [Trey McBride](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/trey-mcbride.php) did post 18.1 fantasy points against this defense in Week 2.
_*[Jaxon Smith-Njigba](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jaxon-smith-njigba.php) is the only fantasy relevant receiving option for Seattle. Their entire passing attack runs through him. I’m not starting another receiver from Seattle for fantasy this week.*_
