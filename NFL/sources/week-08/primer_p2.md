**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Jordan Addison (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-addison.php)**
[Jordan Addison](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-addison.php) has been almost non-existent this season, as the WR65 in fantasy points per game. Addison has a 17.9% target share with 1.25 YPRR, one red zone target, five deep targets, and a 24.1% first-read share. Addison has failed to surpass 60 receiving yards in all but one game this season. Addison could double that output after this week. Detroit has the eighth-highest rate of single-high (56.9%). Against single-high, Addison’s usage has gone up, with this TPRR rising from 12% to 21% and his YPRR jumping from 0.78 (against two-high) to 1.77. Now, Detroit has tightened up its defense against perimeter receivers over the last five games, allowing the 11th-fewest yards per target and the eighth-fewest fantasy points per opportunity, which leaves Addison as a middle-of-the-road flex.
**[T.J. Hockenson (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tj-hockenson.php)**
[T.J. Hockenson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/tj-hockenson.php) is having a resurgent season as the TE9 in fantasy points per game. Among 42 qualifying tight ends, Hockenson is sixth in target share (18.9%), sixth in YPRR (2.14), ninth in red zone targets (six), and ninth in first-read share (19.8%). Hockenson is headed toward another TE1 week against Detroit. Over their last five games, Hockenson’s former team has allowed the fifth-most yards per target to tight ends, the second-most fantasy points per opportunity, and the most receiving touchdowns to tight ends. 
**Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Kyler Murray (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kyler-murray.php)**
[Kyler Murray](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kyler-murray.php) has been a disaster at quarterback this season as the QB34 in fantasy points per game. He has run more in his last two games, averaging 7.5 attempts and 45 yards on the ground, but in the passing department he has been wretched. Among 34 qualifying passers, he is 23rd in yards per attempt, 31st in passing success rate, 28th in aDOT, and dead last in highly accurate throw rate. Murray could have a nice game this week, but I don’t have any faith in him to take advantage of a nice matchup. Across their last five games, Detroit has allowed the fifth-highest passing touchdown rate, the third-highest passing success rate, the fourth-highest passer rating, and the ninth-highest CPOE. Detroit in that span also has the fourth-best pressure rate and has ranked 12th in quick pressure rate. If Detroit’s pass rush can rattle Murray this week, it could be his undoing.
**[Jared Goff (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jared-goff.php)**
[Jared Goff](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jared-goff.php) has been crushing it this season as the QB3 in fantasy points per game. Among 34 qualifying passers, Goff is eighth in yards per attempt, fourth in passing touchdown rate and passing success rate, third in hero throw rate, and sixth in catchable target rate. Goff will have to deal with a Minnesota pass defense that has been easily the best pass defense in the NFL. Ok, fine, Seattle is also in that convo, but Minny has been amazing. Over the last five games, Minny has allowed the fewest yards per attempt, the lowest passing success rate, and the lowest passing touchdown rate. Overall, Minnesota has created havoc weekly, leading the NFL in blitz rate and pressure rate. Goff has navigated the blitz well this season, but he will have to be elite in this regard this week. Among 29 qualifying passers, Goff has been quite good against the blitz, ranking sixth in yards per attempt and third in passing success rate. Goff could get it done this week, but there’s a sizable range of outcomes for him this week.
**[Jameson Williams (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jameson-williams.php)**
Ok, Jamo…I’m sorry. I was down on Jamo last week, and he made me pay for it. [Jameson Williams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jameson-williams.php) is the WR25 in fantasy points per game after the big game. Williams has a 16.6% target share with 1.75 YPRR, seven red zone targets, ten deep targets (eighth among wideouts), and a 23.4% first-read share. After last week, Williams’ numbers against two high look much better, which will help him this week. Minnesota has the fourth-highest two-high rate (63.3%). Against two-high, Williams has a 19% TPRR (second on the team) and 1.81 YPRR. [Jared Goff](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jared-goff.php) will be blitzed into oblivion this week, and Williams should be a trusted weapon on those plays. Williams has a 19% TPRR and 2.04 when Goff has been blitzed. It’s a tough matchup for Williams, so consider him a volatile player this week as usual. Over their last five games, Minny has allowed the third-fewest yards per target and the second-fewest fantasy points per opportunity to perimeter wide receivers.
