**Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Brenton Strange (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/brenton-strange.php)**
[Brenton Strange](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/brenton-strange.php) is quietly the TE12 in fantasy points per game. He’s having a strong season with a 17.9% target share, 2.02 YPRR, three red zone targets, one deep target, and a 19.5% first-read share. I’d love it if the Jags started feeding him more high-cholesterol usage downfield near the goal line. Despite those factors lacking, Strange could easily be a TE1 again this week. Indy has the 12th-highest single-high rate (52.1%). Against single-high, Strange is second on the team with a 30% TPRR while posting 2.82 YPRR. Over the last five games, Indy has faced the second-fewest tight end targets, but they have given up the sixth-most receiving touchdowns (tied) and the eighth-most fantasy points per opportunity to the position during that span.
**Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Daniel Jones (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/daniel-jones.php)**
[Daniel Jones](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/daniel-jones.php) has been a disaster this season. He doesn’t have a single game with at least 275 passing yards or multiple passing touchdowns this season. Jones has one game with more than 15 rushing yards. I’m not surprised by the lack of ground game from Jones coming off the Achilles, but the fact that he is a statue who also can’t throw the ball is a problem. Among 34 qualifying passers, Jones has the fourth-fewest yards per attempt, the 12th-lowest passing success rate, the fourth-lowest catchable target rate, and the fifth-highest off-target rate. Jones has finished higher than QB22 in weekly scoring only once this season (QB16 last week). Jones could have a decent yardage outing, but I don’t see him stacking multiple touchdowns this week. Across their last five games, Jacksonville has allowed the seventh-most yards per attempt, the 14th-highest CPOE, and ranked 17th in passing success rate, but they have held passers to the eighth-lowest passing touchdown rate. Jones is a low-end QB2 again this week.
**[Keenan Allen (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/keenan-allen.php)**
[Keenan Allen](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/keenan-allen.php) has been producing. Across his last four games, Allen has finished as the WR20, WR35, WR17, and WR44 in weekly scoring. Since Week 3, he has led the team with a 22.2% target share and 25.7% first-read share while posting 1.94 YPRR with three red zone targets and two deep targets. Allen could be a strong flex again this week. Across their last five games, the Jags have given up the fourth-most yards per target and the 12th-most fantasy points per opportunity to perimeter wide receivers.
**[Jakobi Meyers (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jakobi-meyers.php)**
[Jakobi Meyers](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jakobi-meyers.php) is the WR37 in fantasy points per game with three top-36 weekly finishes (WR28, WR17, WR27). Meyers has a 15.2% target share with 1.85 YPRR, six red zone targets, three deep targets, and a 17.3% first-read share. He has quietly taken a step back and become the third option in the passing game this season behind [Parker Washington](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/parker-washington.php) and [Brenton Strange](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/brenton-strange.php). This week, he could easily post a strong flex-worthy stat line. Indy has the 12th-highest single-high rate (52.1%). Against single-high, Meyers has a 17% TPRR and 2.02 YPRR. Meyers could succeed this week, but it’s not an easy matchup. Across their last five games, Indy has allowed the seventh-fewest yards per target and the ninth-fewest fantasy points per opportunity to perimeter wide receivers.
**[Josh Downs (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/josh-downs.php)**
[Josh Downs](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/josh-downs.php) has been slumping. I’m not laying this at the feet of Downs. It’s a [Daniel Jones](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/daniel-jones.php) problem. Downs hasn’t had more than 50 receiving yards in a game since Week 3. Over the last four games, Downs hasn’t finished higher than WR41 in any game. Since Week 4, Downs has had a 13.4% target share with 0.86 YPRR, one red zone target, four deep targets, and a 17% first-read share. Indy would be wise to feature him this week to drive his trade stock up, but we’ll see if that happens. Downs is flex-viable again this week. In their last five games, Jacksonville has allowed the ninth-most yards per target to slot receivers while ranking 17th in fantasy points per opportunity.
