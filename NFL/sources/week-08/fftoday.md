GB FPts Allowed vs. TEs: No.31 **No Brainers:** RB [Chuba Hubbard](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/17603/Chuba_Hubbard), WR [Tetairoa McMillan](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/19441/Tetairoa_McMillan) **Favorites:** N/A **On the Fence:** WR [Jalen Coker](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/19207/Jalen_Coker) A year ago, when the Panthers came to Green Bay, they sprang a massive upset by riding the legs of Rico Dowdle (25-130-2). It wouldnï¿½t surprise me at all to see them try to replicate that approach with Hubbard, who ran for just 17 yards in the 2025 matchup. In that game, then-WR2 Xavier Legette had one catch for 22 yards while McMillan led the way with a 4-46-0 line. While I donï¿½t expect the Panthers to skew that heavily to the run, tightening things up seems entirely plausible in the aftermath of Sundayï¿½s brutal loss to Tampa Bay. Of course, if they fall behind, that approach goes out the window. View Coker as a risky WR3 or flex. **Fades:** QB [Bryce Young](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/18704/Bryce_Young) / TE [Darren Waller](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/15340/Darren_Waller) In that 2025 meeting with the Packers, Young completed just 11 passes. He looked like a far different quarterback through Carolinaï¿½s first five games, averaging 310 yards per game, but you wonder what kind of ripple effect is coming from the blowout loss to the Bucs in which he was sacked five times and threw for just 163 yards and 2 INTs. Green Bay leads the NFL in interceptions (9) despite having multiple picks erased on shaky penalty calls, and Carolina may want to try to keep Jordan Love on the sidelines here. As for Waller, the veteran had a 50-yard TD versus Tampa Bay, but he had just 36 yards in the teamï¿½s two previous games combined. Itï¿½s a bad matchup as well with only the Vikings allowing fewer fantasy points to the position than the Packers. Both players are better left on your bench this week, though Young could at least push for low-end QB1 value if Carolina is forced to play catch-up. ![](https://www.fftoday.com/common/logo_gb_50.gif)

CAR FPts Allowed vs. TEs: No.15 **No Brainers:** WR [Christian Watson](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/18237/Christian_Watson), WR [Matthew Golden](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/19370/Matthew_Golden)(shoulder/foot), TE [Tucker Kraft](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/18570/Tucker_Kraft) **Favorites:** RB [Josh Jacobs](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/16611/Josh_Jacobs) Making his 2026 season debut in Week 7, Jacobs was uneven. He averaged just 3.3 yards per carry but finished second on the team in receiving with 55 yards on five receptions. He also scored his first touchdown of the season but dropped a second score that wouldï¿½ve put the team up late in the fourth quarter. The Packers have been dismal when it comes to running the football, and theyï¿½ll be without their center and right guard, though the latter might be addition by subtraction if Kevin Zeitler makes his debut for the team. In terms of matchup, it doesnï¿½t get much better. Carolina has given up 156 total yards and 1.5 TDs per game to RBs, and the Bucs gashed them for 258 yards and three touchdowns on 49 carries. Plus, Jacobs should be among the freshest players on the field with just one gameï¿½s worth of attrition on his body. He can fill an RB2 slot, and he boasts RB1 potential.  
  
**On the Fence:** QB [Jordan Love](https://www.fftoday.com/stats/players/17146/Jordan_Love)  
  
Once again, Love’s final numbers -- 274 yards, 2 TDs, 1 INT -- aren’t particularly reflective of how he played with his receivers again struggling to catch the ball and Jacobs dropping what should have been Love’s third TD pass of the afternoon. His outlook for Thursday night rests heavily on how much Carolina can keep the ball away from Green Bay’s offense, and whether the Packers are finally able to generate a decent running game against a team that has struggled to defend it. If the offensive line changes and Jacobs’ presence jump-start that part of the offense, Matt LaFleur may lean into it. If the Packers struggle again, however, it’ll fall right back on Love to move the ball. He’s a fringe QB1 option with risk/reward potential.
