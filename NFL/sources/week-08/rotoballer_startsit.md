##  **_Matchups Analysis - Sunday_** _1:00 EST Games_
## **Arizona Cardinals at Dallas Cowboys**
**Matchups We Love:**
**QB[Dak Prescott](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/18055/Dak+Prescott) **
Dak Prescott put together an all-around solid performance last week against the Eagles, throwing for 212 yards and three touchdowns. He'll now look to carry that momentum into a plus matchup against the Cardinals in Week 8. Arizona has given up a top-10 fantasy QB finish in five of the last six weeks, which included letting Nix score 26.2 fantasy points last week. Prescott will be a high-end QB1.
**QB[Jacoby Brissett](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/18018/Jacoby+Brissett) **
Jacoby Brissett has been hit-or-miss for fantasy managers this season. He has finished as a top-16 fantasy QB in three of the last six weeks but has also scored fewer than 14 points in the other three contests. For this week, Brissett will be a viable QB1 streaming option. The Cowboys have given up the seventh-most fantasy points to opposing QBs and over 20 fantasy points to a QB in three of the last five weeks.
**RB[Jeremiyah Love](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/26768/Jeremiyah+Love)**
**Update: Love will play.**
There's a strong possibility that Jeremiyah Love returns to the field in Week 8. If he does, he'll get a favorable matchup in his first game back. Dallas has given up the fifth-most rush yards and fourth-most rushing touchdowns to opposing RBs this season and just let [Saquon Barkley](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/19766/Saquon+Barkley) and [Dameon Pierce](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23223/Dameon+Pierce) combine for 109 yards and two touchdowns on the ground last week. Love will be a low-end RB2.
**WR[Michael Wilson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24173/Michael+Wilson)**
Michael Wilson has been a major disappointment since [Marvin Harrison Jr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24974/Marvin+Harrison+Jr.) was traded to the Houston Texans. Wilson has scored 7.3 PPR fantasy points (Week 5), 7.2 PPR fantasy points (Week 6), and 10.6 PPR fantasy points (Week 7) in three consecutive games. However, Wilson will be a strong WR2 play this week against the Cowboys. Joey Porter Jr. primarily stays on his side of the field and doesn't shadow opposing WR1s.
**Cowboys WRs**
Both [CeeDee Lamb](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21679/CeeDee+Lamb) and [George Pickens](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23153/George+Pickens) are must-start WRs in this matchup against the Cardinals. The Cardinals have allowed the second-most receiving touchdowns to opposing WRs this season and just let both [Amon-Ra St. Brown](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22587/Amon-Ra+St.+Brown) and [Jameson Williams](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23234/Jameson+Williams) have solid fantasy days a few weeks ago. Lamb will be a high-end WR1, while Pickens is a high-end WR2.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**None**
**Other Matchups:**
**RB[Javonte Williams](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22558/Javonte+Williams)**
Javonte Williams had a career performance in Week 7 against the Eagles, rushing for 155 yards and one touchdown. Williams has now scored a touchdown in six of seven games and currently ranks as the RB9 in fantasy points per game this year. The Cowboys running back will be a solid RB1 play this week against a Cardinals defense that allowed [Jahmyr Gibbs](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23200/Jahmyr+Gibbs) to run all over them (151 yards and two touchdowns) back in Week 5.
**TE[Trey McBride](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23251/Trey+McBride)**
Trey McBride remains the only Cardinals playmaker fantasy managers can truly trust. He has scored over 20 PPR fantasy points in two of the last three weeks and is coming off a 10-catch, 111-yard, one-touchdown performance against the Broncos last week. McBride will no doubt be a must-start TE1 in all formats this weekend.
**Injuries:**
None
## **Atlanta Falcons at Tampa Bay Buccaneers**
**Matchups We Love:**
**RB[Bucky Irving](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24967/Bucky+Irving)**
Bucky Irving continues to be a workhorse for this Buccaneers offense. He has seen at least 20 touches in three straight games and just ran for 106 yards and two touchdowns in last week's blowout win over the Panthers. Irving will come in as a solid RB1 against the Falcons this week. Atlanta has allowed over 20 PPR fantasy points to an RB in four straight contests.
**TE[Kyle Pitts Sr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22508/Kyle+Pitts+Sr.) **
[Kyle Pitts](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22508/Kyle+Pitts) is starting to stack some solid performances in a row. He scored 15.2 PPR fantasy points in Week 5, 23 PPR fantasy points in Week 6, and 13.2 PPR fantasy points in Week 7. This recent stretch, plus this favorable matchup against the Buccaneers, makes Pitts a low-end TE1 this week. Tampa Bay has given up the seventh-most fantasy points to opposing TEs this season.
**Matchups We Hate:**
