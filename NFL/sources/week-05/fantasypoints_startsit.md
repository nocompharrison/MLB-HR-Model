# 2026 Week 5 Fantasy Football Start 'Em, Sit 'Em Advice
Joe DolanOCT. 10, 2026 12:10PM EDT
Welcome to Week 5 of Fantasy Football Start ‘Em, Sit ‘Em!
I’m the new author of this column this year, so I’ll introduce a new format! I will break down every game by position and player. After listing the player (and perhaps some interesting stats or news I’ve found in my research), I’ll pop that player into one of several buckets, which are as follows:
  * **Crown Him** — This player is an every-week starter, someone you almost never take out of your lineup _(90-100% likelihood of starting for an average fantasy team)_
  * **Start Him** — More than likely, this player should be in your lineup because of his role, recent production, or matchup, but you might have to think about it just a little _(70-90% likelihood of starting for an average fantasy team)_
  * **Stream Him** — This player isn’t a weekly starter (at least not yet), but circumstances could lead to him being in your optimal lineup this week _(50-70% likelihood of starting for an average fantasy team)_
  * **Stash Him** — This player is worthy of being on your roster, but ideally I want to see more before putting him in a lineup _(25-50% likelihood of starting for an average fantasy team)_
  * **Sit Him** — This player should not be in your lineup because of lack of recent production, a difficult matchup, or injury _(10-25% likelihood of starting for an average fantasy team)_
  * **Drop Him** — This player shouldn’t be on your roster _(0-10% likelihood of starting for an average fantasy team)_


For simplicity’s sake, this column will focus on a 12-team PPR league with a base lineup of **1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE, and 1 W/R/T FLEX.** Obviously, different league sizes and formats will change recommendations… and that’s what our [Fantasy Points Front Office](https://www.fantasypoints.com/fphq/import), with its multiple start/sit tools, is for.

### **Tampa Bay at Dallas**
> Thursday, October 8, 2026, 8:15 PM Eastern
##### Quarterbacks
**Dak Prescott** (Dal) — The Cowboys are leading the NFL in points per drive and are 2nd to the 49ers in EPA per dropback. Dak is currently the QB9 in scoring but is actually significantly _underperforming_ — he’s the QB2 by XFPG. The Bucs have allowed the 9th-fewest FPG to opposing QBs, but they run single-high coverages at the 3rd-highest rate in the NFL, which Prescott shreds to the tune of .41 EPA/dropback. _**[Start Him]**_
**Jalon Daniels** (TB) — If you’re absolutely desperate, the matchup is pristine — Dallas has given up the 3rd-most FPG to opposing QBs, and Daniels will at least take off and run, posting 8/55 rushing against the Packers last week. The projected total of 47.5 is the 3rd-highest of the entire week. You can almost certainly do better in 1-QB formats, but superflex players might have an interesting decision to make. _**[Sit Him]**_
##### Running Backs
**Javonte Williams** (Dal) — Williams’ efficiency metrics might be the canary in the coal mine (see below), but the fact of the matter is his role is so robust in one of the NFL’s best offenses that it likely won’t matter. Williams is 4th in XFPG and 7th in FPG, while posting top-6 snap, designed-carry, and inside-the-5-carry shares, and a top-15 route share at the RB position. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Bucky Irving** (TB) — The Week 4 role for Irving was precisely the role I anticipated when I faded him in fantasy this off-season. I’m not going to go too far out of the way to pat myself on the back, because the Bucs were starting an undrafted rookie QB, but Irving hasn’t had an inside-the-10 _snap_ since Week 1 (**Sean Tucker** got the goal-line work in Week 4), and his 36.1% route share in Week 4 was his second-lowest of the year. The Bucs are talking about getting Tucker and **Kenny Gainwell** more involved this week. It’s possible Bucky has all the empty-calorie touches on a bad football team as heavy underdogs. He’s more of a low-end RB2/high-end FLEX. _**[Stream Him]**_
**Kenny Gainwell** (TB) — Has just 25 opportunities (carries + targets) in four games, but his 38.9% route share in Week 4 was his highest of the year, and the Bucs are talking about getting him more involved. I wouldn’t be shocked if his route share goes up more with the Bucs heavy underdogs in Week 5, but I’d have to be pretty desperate to put him in a lineup. _**[Stash Him]**_
**Sean Tucker** (TB) — Played just 3 snaps in Week 4, but he got his first 3 carries of the year… including the Bucs’ only goal-line rush attempt. That was after coaches said he needed to get more involved, so I’m inclined to believe them when they say they need him even _more_ involved now (see below). Unfortunately, he’s a TD-only fantasy asset on a heavy underdog. _**[Sit Him]**_
##### Wide Receivers
**CeeDee Lamb** (Dal) — Has reestablished himself as Dallas’ alpha. The WR1 in XFPG and the WR2 in XFPG. _**[Crown Him]**_
**George Pickens** (Dal) — Look, when Lamb is catching 17 passes, it’s going to be hard for Pickens to get his. I still think he’s an excellent buy-low candidate — while he’s just the WR30 in XFPG, he’s 14th-worst in the difference between XFPG and FPG. And this offense is plenty good enough for both Lamb and Pickens to get theirs. Dallas can probably play with its food and still beat the Bucs this week, so this would be a good game to start forcing him the ball. _**[Start Him]**_
**Emeka Egbuka** (TB) — His 83.3% route share in Week 4 was his highest of the season, but he posted just 2/13 receiving on 4 targets from rookie **Jalon Daniels**. In theory, it’s a good matchup against Dallas, but I wonder if the Cowboys will have **Joey Porter Jr**. man up on him — Porter didn’t allow a single reception in his primary coverage on 17 coverage snaps in his Cowboys debut. Egbuka is a WR3 on the hopes the Bucs throw it a bunch as they play from behind. _**[Stream Him]**_
**Ryan Flournoy** (Dal) — Only worth rostering as a “handcuff” to Lamb or Pickens. He runs close to a full-time route share, but has just 13/104 receiving on 21 targets in four games. _**[Sit Him]**_
**Chris Godwin** (TB) — Has a paltry 3.67 aDOT on the year, which was all the way down to 2.29 in Week 4 as the Bucs tried to scheme up some easy throws for **Jalon Daniels**. Godwin still looks washed to me, but he’s actually 15th among WRs in YAC/R. The Cowboys have given up the 5th-most FPG to opposing slot WRs, and allow a league-worst 2.73 YPRR to the position. If you’re ever going to play Godwin, this is the week. _**[Stash Him]**_
##### Tight Ends
**Cade Otton** (TB) — Still leads all TEs in snap share this year and is 6th in route share, but has done nothing with that — his best fantasy game was 8.8 FP. Nonetheless, his 22.0% 1st-read target share is second on the team to **Emeka Egbuka** (23.1%). That role is probably worth rostering as a last-bench-spot kind of guy, but I doubt **Jalon Daniels** will get the best out of him. _**[Stash Him]**_
**Jake Ferguson** (Dal) — Ferguson needs a TD to come through — he’s totaled just 6.7 FP in his two games without scoring this year. That’s because he’s absolutely buried in the Cowboys’ progressions — his 9.6% 1st-read target share is 4th on the team and 34th of 49 qualified TEs. The good news is **Dak Prescott** trusts him in the red area (4 inside-20 targets). The Bucs have given up the 7th-most FPG to TEs this year, so a viable streamer. _**[Stream Him]**_

Nonetheless, he ran fewer routes than **Darius Cooper** last week, and I wouldn’t be surprised if Hurts trusts Cooper more given his great play in the red zone this year. _**[Stash Him]**_
**Brian Thomas Jr.** (Jax) — Scored his first TD of the season in Week 3, but his route share was just 63%, and he saw only 2 targets, so he hasn't reached even 10 FP in a single game this year. The Eagles have allowed the 7th-fewest FPG to outside WRs. _**[Sit Him]**_

**Dallas Goedert** (Phi) — Looking like he’ll make his return from an MCL sprain. He’s a low-end TE1. _**[Stream Him]**_

With the Chargers’ WR position an absolute mess, I wouldn’t be shocked if they continue to spam Mitchell out of the backfield, and they’re underdogs in this game. A mediocre FLEX play. _**[Stash Him]**_
##### Wide Receivers
**Jaylen Waddle** (Den) — From this week’s [Everything Report](https://www.fantasypoints.com/nfl/articles/2026/the-everything-report-2026-fantasy-football-week-5): “Waddle ranks a middling 35th among WRs in XFP/G, just marginally ahead of **Denzel Boston** and **Dontayvion Wicks**. His 19% target share is actually second on the team to **RJ Harvey** (20%). **Bo Nix** isn’t making matters any easier, either. Nix has struggled in the opening month and is currently bottom-12 in both catchable throw rate and CPOE.” The reason for hope for Waddle managers is a simple one: **Sean Payton** is taking back over playcalling this week. That’s enough to put Waddle on the WR2 radar for me. _**[Start Him]**_
**Tre Harris** (LAC) — Ran a season-high 75.6% route share in Week 4, posting 3/60 on 5 targets against the Seahawks. He also ran a season-high 71% of his routes from out wide, which is notable because the Broncos will not have top CB **Patrick Surtain** (ankle) this week and also might not have **Riley Moss** (ribs). I prefer him to **Quentin Johnston** if **Ladd McConkey** (foot) doesn’t play. _**[Stream Him]**_
**Quentin Johnston** (LAC) — His only catch last week came on perhaps **Justin Herbert** ’s best play of the year — a 44-yard fourth-quarter strike after escaping pressure. But Johnston now has just 7 catches on the season on 17 targets, 3 of which were dropped. He has some theoretical upside if the Broncos are down both of their top CBs, but I prefer Harris. _**[Stream Him]**_
**Courtland Sutton** (Den) — With **Pat Bryant** out for multiple games, I wouldn’t be shocked if the Broncos try to get Sutton more involved this week. But in reality, Sutton’s struggles are simple — he is 10th in the NFL with 150 uncatchable air yards and tied for 3rd with 9 uncatchable targets. Will **Sean Payton** taking back over playcalling help? I’m sure a bit, but he needs **Bo Nix** to play better. _**[Sit Him]**_
**Ladd McConkey** (LAC) — Seems questionable at best with his foot injury. Was held catchless last week on a 31.7% route share, so I’m not exactly champing at the bit to play him. _**[Sit Him]**_
> **UPDATE** : Johnston is OUT with a chest injury. McConkey remains questionable. Even if McConkey plays, the goose egg he gave us last week gives me serious pause to playing him. I have Harris as a low-end WR2 this week.
##### Tight Ends
**Oronde Gadsden** (LAC) —5/36 receiving last week, but on just a 46.3% route share despite **Ladd McConkey** barely playing. I think he’s a Ponzi scheme. _**[Sit Him]**_
**Evan Engram** (Den) — Hasn’t caught more than 1 ball since Week 1. _**[Drop Him]**_
### Detroit at Arizona
> Sunday, October 11, 2026, 4:25 PM Eastern
##### Quarterbacks
**Jared Goff** (Det) — The total in this game is a monstrous 54.5, with the Lions’ 30.0 implied team total the highest we have seen for any team so far this year. The Cardinals give up the 5th-most FPG to opposing QBs, they play two-high shells at the league’s 2nd-highest rate (Goff shreds two-high), and they play indoors. Wheels up! _**[Start Him]**_
**Jacoby Brissett** (Ari) — The Lions have allowed the most FPG to QBs this year. That includes the top overall QB performance (**Josh Allen** , Week 2), three of the top 16, and four of the top 36. Brissett is 9th in the NFL in dropbacks per game and obviously will need to put up a lot of points in this environment. _**[Start Him]**_
##### Running Backs
**Jahmyr Gibbs** (Det) — We believe our 28.5-FP PPR projection for Gibbs is the highest we’ve ever projected for a non-QB in Fantasy Points history. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Jeremiyah Love** (Ari) — I guess things are back to being weird with Love, who apparently is dealing with ankle soreness unrelated to his August high-ankle sprain. From this week’s [Everything Report](https://www.fantasypoints.com/nfl/articles/2026/the-everything-report-2026-fantasy-football-week-5): “Love’s snap share fell to 46.0% (from 63.0%), his route participation rate fell to 23.1% (from 38.9%), and his XFP fell to 8.1 (from 22.8). Love saw six more rush attempts than **Tyler Allgeier** , but Allgeier played all seven snaps and got the only touch inside the 10-yard line (Allgeier now has a 68% snap share and 3 of 8 backfield opportunities there on the season). In other words, Love had the role of a 50/50 committee back at best, not far off from what happened in Weeks 1-2.” More concerning for me was Love’s post-game interview, in which he insinuated he’s completely fine with losing touches to Allgeier. Still, a must-start in this scoring environment. _**[Start Him]**_
**Tyler Allgeier** (Ari) — I know I post Allgeier’s deep-red heat map here every week as a bit, but I have to admit that a guy who got all 7 of the Cardinals’ inside-the-10 snaps last week might be an appealing bye-week fill-in this week, given the projected game total of 54.5 is the highest we’ve seen all year so far. _**[Stash Him]**_
##### Wide Receivers
**Amon-Ra St. Brown** (Det) — Small dip in production the last two weeks, but still third among all WRs in routes per game and first in red-zone targets. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Michael Wilson** (Ari) — Wilson leads all WRs in route share (94%); he ranks WR5 in total targets, and WR7 by XFP/G. He has more first-read targets than **Trey McBride**. The game total here is 54.5. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Jameson Williams** (Det) — Jamo has yet to have a single game this year in which he met or exceeded his XFP, but he at least came close in Week 4, posting 6/102 on 8 targets. He’s now the _2nd_ -worst WR in XFPG-to-FPG difference (-5.46) and remains a buy-low candidate for me on a team that can’t stop anyone from scoring. _**[Start Him]**_
**Marvin Harrison Jr.** (Ari) — Scored last week on his only catch. Has 1 or fewer catches in three of four games this year and is already being bandied about in trade rumors. _**[Drop Him]**_
> **UPDATE** : Harrison is actually going to be traded… but we don’t know where yet. He will be inactive on Sunday. If you are desperate for a low-end option, **Kendrick Bourne** could see increased usage against this despicable Lions secondary.
##### Tight Ends
**Trey McBride** (Ari) — Has not seen fewer than 8 targets since Week 5 of _last_ season. Has seen double-digit targets in 17 of his last 29 games. The Lions have allowed the most FPG to opposing TEs. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Sam LaPorta** (Det) — Our guys Ryan Heath and Graham Barfield called for LaPorta’s explosion in the [Everything Report](https://www.fantasypoints.com/nfl/articles/2026/the-everything-report-2026-fantasy-football-week-5) for weeks. That explosion happened in Week 4. “LaPorta’s 31.0% first-read target share was his highest in any game since Week 8 of 2024, and he exceeded a 75.0% route participation rate for the 4th consecutive game. LaPorta now leads all TEs in routes run (143 or 35.8 per game, more than any TE aside from Trey McBride averaged last year), ranks 4th in XFP/G (14.7), and averages 15.6 FPG across 22 career games above a 75.0% route share.” The Cardinals allow the 4th-most FPG to opposing TEs. _**[Crown Him]**_
### San Francisco at Seattle
> Sunday, October 11, 2026, 4:25 PM Eastern
##### Quarterbacks
**Brock Purdy** (SF) — Purdy is fantasy’s QB2 (25.4 FPG) and ranks third in raw passing FP. He’s the league’s most efficient passer by EPA/DB and ANY/A. However, the Seahawks give up the 2nd-fewest FPG to opposing QBs, and in three games against Seattle last year (including playoffs), Purdy threw 2 TD to 4 INT. If he’s truly going to make an MVP push, a strong performance against a defense he struggled with last year would be a hell of a place to start. Given his production, he’s hard to sit for fantasy. _**[Start Him]**_
**Sam Darnold** (Sea) — Has 6 TD passes in his two full games this season, and while San Francisco has allowed the 7th-fewest FPG to opposing QBs this year, the Niners could be down top pass rusher **Nick Bosa** and nickel CB **Upton Stout**. The Seahawks have an almost neutral 2.2% PROE in Darnold’s two full games. He’s a mid-tier QB2 for fantasy. _**[Stash Him]**_
##### Running Backs
**Christian McCaffrey** (SF) — CMC has run a higher route share than any RB, but his 18.6% target share is down from 21.3% last year… could that be because **Deebo Samuel** is back? _**[Crown Him]**_
**Emanuel Wilson** (Sea) — In his first full game without **Jadarian Price** (chest), Wilson clearly led the backfield with a 75% carry share, including both of the team’s attempts inside the 10. He also surprisingly scored a receiving TD. The Seahawks are favored by 3 points this week, so the game script should be at least neutral, which favors Wilson over **George Holani** , who handles the passing-down work. He’s a solid RB2, but probably a good trade-away candidate before **Zach Charbonnet** weasels his way back in here. _**[Start Him]**_
**George Holani** (Sea) — Theoretically the Seahawks’ passing-down back… but he’s seen exactly 1 target in three of four games this year. You can talk yourself into Holani if you think the Seahawks will trail in this game, but that’s a leap of faith. _**[Sit Him]**_
**Kaelon Black** (SF) — Strictly a handcuff. _**[Sit Him]**_
##### Wide Receivers
**Jaxon Smith-Njigba** (Sea) — Had just 5 catches for 76 yards last week. Bum. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Mike Evans** (SF) — Despite playing through a rib injury, Evans posted a season-high 8 targets and 76 receiving yards in a win over the Broncos last week. He’s still not 100%, and this matchup is nasty on paper — the Seahawks give up the 2nd-fewest FPG to all WRs. But if you’re looking for a reason to be optimistic, they at least allow “only” the _10th_ -fewest FPG to perimeter WRs. **Deebo Samuel** has the tougher matchup in the slot. _**[Start Him]**_
**Deebo Samuel** (SF) — Looks unbelievably rejuvenated, with a TD in three of four games this year. But it’s a credit to **Kyle Shanahan** that Deebo is getting all these opportunities, because he’s separating at a bottom-10 rate in the NFL. Deebo also runs nearly half his routes from the slot, where he’ll see a lot of **Devon Witherspoon**. Nonetheless, if you need him as a WR3 this week, the gimmicky stuff and designed targets should be enough to get you a few points. He’s a low-end WR3/FLEX. _**[Stash Him]**_
**Rashid Shaheed** (Sea) — Is it possible that Shaheed is the Seahawks’ passing-down back? He had just 1 catch for 20 yards last week — meaning he’s had no more than 3 catches in any game this year — but he also had 2 carries for 33 yards and a score. I’d like to see the Seahawks find more creative ways to use him. But until that happens consistently, he’s not a fantasy option. _**[Sit Him]**_
##### Tight Ends
**George Kittle** (SF) — The TE3 by FPG on the year is now fully ramped up, running a route on 89% and 85% of the 49ers' pass plays in his last two games. _**[Crown Him]**_
**AJ Barner** (Sea) — If you’re playing Barner, you’re basically begging for a Tush Push touchdown — he’s effectively their QB sneak option. As a receiver, he has just a 13.1% first-read target share because JSN hogs them all. _**[Drop Him]**_
### Baltimore at Atlanta
> Sunday, October 11, 2026, 8:20 PM Eastern
##### Quarterbacks
**Michael Penix** (Atl) — While Penix has played well in his two starts, he’s been useless for fantasy because… the Falcons are suddenly an absolute _wagon?_ He has just 46 dropbacks in two games. On those dropbacks, he leads all QBs in CPOE and YPA, while also getting the ball out quicker than any QB (2.27 seconds). His Falcons are 3.5-point favorites against **Snoop Huntley** ’s Ravens, so I’m not expecting 40 dropbacks here, either. _**[Sit Him]**_
**Tyler Huntley** (Bal) — Almost certainly going to start this week for **Lamar Jackson** (ankle). In Huntley’s 12 career games as a starter, he’s averaged 14.6 FPG, the equivalent of QB26 this season. Only rosterable in superflex formats against a Falcon defense pressuring QBs at the NFL’s 2nd-highest rate. _**[Drop Him]**_
##### Running Backs
**Bijan Robinson** (Atl) — If we’re not grading on a positional curve, I think Bijan is the best football player on the planet right now. **Derrick Henry** agrees. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Derrick Henry** (Bal) — It’s a small sample, but Henry has actually posted 32.4 FPG in two games with **Tyler Huntley** as the Ravens’ starting QB. The Falcons have allowed the 8th-fewest FPG to opposing RBs, but keep in mind their last two opponents (Packers and Saints) can’t run the ball at all. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Brian Robinson** (Atl) — I think this week’s [Everything Report](https://www.fantasypoints.com/nfl/articles/2026/the-everything-report-2026-fantasy-football-week-5) said it best: “I know that **Brian Robinson** is averaging 18.4 FPG in two games with **Michael Penix** starting (to **Bijan Robinson’s** 31.5 FPG), but I’m not really moved by this. The former Robinson has seen just a 27.9% snap share and 0 of 4 carries inside the 10-yard line on plays the Falcons haven’t led by multiple scores in the past two weeks. I’d be pretty surprised if he continued to provide real standalone value, though he’s obviously a good handcuff/contingent stash.” If you think the Falcons will lead by multiple scores again this week (against a backup QB), maybe you can roll the dice and chase that 7. _**[Stash Him]**_
**Justice Hill** (Bal) — Hill is 11th among all RBs in route share and has done nothing with it — 5/44 on 6 targets. Maybe you can talk yourself into Hill if you think the Ravens will trail the entire game. _**[Drop Him]**_
##### Wide Receivers
**Drake London** (Atl) — Has a preposterous 42.1% first-read target share in **Michael Penix** ’s two starts (would be #2 behind only **Jaxon Smith-Njigba**). The only downside is Penix has thrown just 45 passes in those two games. Still a locked-in WR1 with zero target competition. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Zay Flowers** (Bal) — Is now dealing with a foot injury in addition to his hamstring tweak. It’s hard to sit him given how good he's been this year, but two nagging injuries and a backup QB will hurt his chances of coming through. _**[Start Him]**_
**Rashod Bateman** (Bal) — Has just 4 catches in the two weeks since his Week 2, **Zay Flowers** -less blowup. Now he's also practicing in a non-contact jersey while dealing with a shoulder injury. Oh, and he’s playing with a backup QB this week. Thanks for your service in Week 2, brah. _**[Drop Him]**_
##### Tight Ends
**Mark Andrews** (Bal) — Believe it or not, Andrews actually averages more FPG in games that **Tyler Huntley** starts (13.1, 11 games) than **Lamar Jackson** (11.94, 70 games) in his career. Of course, that’s dating back to 2021, when Andrews was a much bigger Mandrews than he is now — he averaged just 6.6 in two Huntley starts last year. Nonetheless, both **Zay Flowers** and **Rashod Bateman** are dinged up, and the Falcons have allowed the 12th-most FPG to TEs this year. _**[Stream Him]**_
**Kyle Pitts** (Atl) — Ran just _8 routes_ in Week 4, but turned them into 3/47 receiving. If that gets you excited, well, what you do in your bedroom is none of my business. _**[Sit Him]**_
### Buffalo at Los Angeles Rams
> Monday, October 12, 2026, 8:15 PM Eastern
##### Quarterbacks
**Josh Allen** (Buf) — Threw a bad game-ending INT last week. But he’s scored 7 rushing TD in four games. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Matthew Stafford** (LAR) — Played poorly for three quarters last week, but the Eagles gave him too many opportunities, and he eventually capitalized with the healthy-again **Puka Nacua**. And this Buffalo defense is a much easier matchup — the Bills give up the 4th-most FPG to opposing QBs and have had some bickering in the back end, with **Chauncey Gardner-Johnson** flapping his gums, ostensibly, about teammate **Maxwell Hairston** (what else is new?). With an implied Rams team total of 29, start Stafford. _**[Start Him]**_
##### Running Backs
**Kyren Williams** (LAR) — Ryan Heath wrote a lot about Kyren in this week’s [Everything Report](https://www.fantasypoints.com/nfl/articles/2026/the-everything-report-2026-fantasy-football-week-5), so go read that. But here’s the biggest takeaway I had: “But _by far_ the most impactful change for Williams has been his increased receiving role. In 2025, he commanded just a 7.9% target share (~RB30). His career-high in any previous season was a 10.7% target share (~RB18) in 2023, when he averaged 21.4 FPG and finished as the overall RB2. So you should get pretty excited when I tell you that so far in 2026, Williams is up to a 15.2% target share (~RB6)! Even before Week 4 — in which Williams had a career-high 25.5% target share — he was at 10.5%, about on par with his 2023 number.” A third-round league winner I wish I didn’t galaxy-brain myself into not drafting. _**[Crown Him]**_
**James Cook** (Buf) — Has now scored in 3 straight games, and the offensive line is opening a ton of holes for him. It’s a small sample, but Cook actually averages more FPG with the Bills as underdogs over the last four seasons. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Blake Corum** (LAR) — Has just 36 yards on 14 touches over the last two weeks and is getting completely role-mogged by **Kyren Williams**. I thought Corum would be more than a high-value handcuff. I was wrong. _**[Sit Him]**_
##### Wide Receivers
**Puka Nacua** (LAR) — I still worry that the groin/hip injury will linger, but he played a full route share in Week 4, posting 127 yards from scrimmage and a TD. A glorious matchup against the 3rd-worst WR defense in the NFL. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Davante Adams** (LAR) — I know Puka’s back, but that just means the targets get condensed a little bit more. Adams now gets a terrible Bills secondary after having to deal with **Patrick Surtain** and **Quinyon Mitchell** in back-to-back weeks. _**[Crown Him]**_
**Khalil Shakir** (Buf) — If **DJ Moore** is out, we’re projecting Shakir to get the better matchup than **Keon Coleman** , who might face pseudo-shadow coverage from **Trent McDuffie**. Posted a season-high 7/82 receiving against the Patriots last week and runs nearly 60% of his routes from the slot. The Rams are merely mediocre against slot WRs (17th in FPG allowed) as opposed to great against outside WRs (6th-fewest FPG allowed). _**[Stream Him]**_
**Keon Coleman** (Buf) — Coleman is playing the best football of his career, now with 6 catches in two of his last three games, and he’s earning **Josh Allen** ’s trust by making some very difficult grabs. If **DJ Moore** (shoulder) doesn’t play in this game, Coleman will be the Bills’ top perimeter WR, which unfortunately means he’ll see a lot of **Trent McDuffie** , who erased **Dontayvion Wicks** last week. That makes Coleman more of a low-end WR3/FLEX than you might anticipate, but at least Allen will allow him the opportunity to make plays. _**[Stash Him]**_
**DJ Moore** (Buf) — Not seen practicing Thursday (shoulder), so could be the dreaded Monday night game-time decision. I’d prepare to park him if I didn’t have someone else from this game (like Coleman). _**[Sit Him]**_
##### Tight Ends
**Dalton Kincaid** (Buf) — Saw a season-low 42.9% route participation rate in Week 4, in line with his 2025 average. Yes, we know he’s a bad blocker, but the Bills have to try to do something to get him going with **DJ Moore** iffy to play this week. The Rams have allowed the 2nd-fewest FPG to TEs this year, but the only real threat they faced was **Isaiah Likely** in a game in which **Jaxson Dart** got injured (**George Kittle** was less than 100% in Week 1). The usage last week sucked, but we’re still counting on Kincaid to be a heavy part of the gameplan here with Buffalo 3.5-point ‘dogs. _**[Stream Him]**_
**Tyler Higbee** (LAR) — 13 catches over the past two games with **Terrance Ferguson** (on IR) and **Colby Parkinson** out. Would be right back in the streaming conversation if Parkinson misses again. _**[Stash Him]**_
