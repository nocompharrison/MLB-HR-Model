Welcome to another edition of Hoopes There It Is! We’re previewing every game in Week 5, highlighting advanced stats like EPA, success rate, pass rate over expected (PROE), and completion percentage over expected (CPOE) to help make the best decisions for your fantasy teams. I’ll provide a quick start/sit section at the top that stems from the longer write-ups. And if you’re interested in more, which I hope you are, you can [jump to any of the 15 games this week](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it#links). Let’s get started!
## Start ‘Em, Sit ‘Em
[Mike Gesicki](https://www.4for4.com/player/mike-gesicki) (**Start**): The Bengals are projected for about 25 points despite likely missing both Ja’Marr Chase and [Tee Higgins](https://www.4for4.com/player/tee-higgins). Bah gawd, that’s [Mike Gesicki](https://www.4for4.com/player/mike-gesicki)’s music! The Bengals didn’t even bother trying to run against the Jaguars last week, and they’re committed to the pass in a way that is extremely rare in 2026. The Dolphins’ defense just let TJ Hockenson earn 13 receptions against them in Week 4. Why can’t we get that for Gesicki here with even worse target competition?
[Mark Andrews](https://www.4for4.com/player/mark-andrews) (**Sit**): The Ravens are likely to be without [Lamar Jackson](https://www.4for4.com/player/lamar-jackson) due to an ankle injury that sidelined him for the entire second half of Week 4. Without Jackson, the team is only projected for 20 points. Meanwhile, Andrews ran only 24 of the Ravens’ 34 routes last week. Matt Hibner and [Josh Cuevas](https://www.4for4.com/player/josh-cuevas) combined for three of seven snaps on 3rd downs, according to PFF, while Hibner’s playing time especially increased in 12 personnel. Andrews did score a TD last week, but unfortunately, we don’t get to take those points with us into this week.
[Braelon Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/braelon-allen) (**Start**): There’s not much else to do besides throw out the Jets’ entire Week 4. CeeLee Lamb easily out-targeted the entire team, and [Kyle Monangai](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyle-monangai) nearly had as many carries as the Jets had snaps. They only had the ball for about one quarter of the game, with only one drive lasting for more than five plays. Besides, well, all of that negative stuff, [Braelon Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/braelon-allen)’s role looked great as he took all 14 of the RB carries and ran 13 of the team’s 18 routes. Meanwhile, the Browns’ run defense is awful, ranking 28th in success rate and dead-last in explosive run rate allowed. Just wipe Week 4 from your memory.
[Quinshon Judkins](https://www.4for4.com/player/quinshon-judkins) (**Sit**): This is obviously a tougher call as Judkins both scored a TD and saw a massive receiving spike in Week 4. But he still only ran 15 routes, and I’ll root for the Cowboys if Judkins maintains a better targets-per-route run rate than [Jaxon Smith-Njigba](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaxon-smith-njigba). The Browns are only projected for about 19 points, meaning the TD opportunities should be few and far between. I think his typical week will look more like 16 scoreless carries and two targets.
[Carnell Tate](https://www.4for4.com/player/carnell-tate) (**Start**): I’m writing this, so I get to be logically inconsistent if I want to be! The Titans are projected for an abysmal 16 implied points. But Tate is starting to dominate volume on this bad offense. According to JJ Zachariason, the only other rookie WRs since 2016 with a higher target share than Tate through four games were [Malik Nabers](https://www.4for4.com/player/malik-nabers), [Drake London](https://www.4for4.com/player/drake-london), and [Puka Nacua](https://www.4for4.com/player/puka-nacua). Tate is one of two players in the league with over 50% of their team’s air yards; he’s WR19 in expected fantasy points per game, WR10 in ESPN’s open score, and is facing a Texans’ defense that is bottom-four in explosive pass rate allowed. Maybe Tate can get half of the Titans’ points this week like he’s getting half of their air yards?
Saints Not Named [Chris Olave](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-olave) (**Sit**): This is just based on the matchup. The Vikings’ defense is first overall in six of the categories I track. You’re starting Olave every week against any opponent. But I wonder how much will be left over, as the Vikings have done a good job limiting play volume for their opponents? And play volume has been the key thing driving the value for Saints’ skill players all season.

##  Buccaneers at Cowboys (O/U 47.5, DAL -8.5)
### Buccaneers (19.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The 19.5 points are more a testament to this Cowboys’ defense than anything positive for the Buccaneers’ offense.
  * Some credit to [Jalon Daniels](https://www.4for4.com/player/jt-daniels) as the Bucs’ 33rd percentile passing efficiency and 45.9% pass success rate were much better than I expected.
  * But the ceiling here for the pass catchers is so low because the Bucs passed at a rate 5.9% below expectation and Daniels’ 4.2-yard aDoT was two yards shorter than any other qualified passer this season.
  * With the air yards in mind, [Cade Otton](https://www.4for4.com/player/cade-otton) and [Chris Godwin](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-godwin) paced the team with six targets each, followed by four for [Emeka Egbuka](https://www.4for4.com/player/emeka-egbuka). 
  * Egbuka was the leader of the three with an aDoT of…5.9 yards.
  * It’s a fantastic matchup for the passing attack, but it’s more than fair to question whether the Bucs can take advantage of it?
  * [Bucky Irving](https://www.4for4.com/player/bucky-irving) dominated the carries with 16 but split the routes with Kenny Gainwell and was ultimately out-targeted by Gainwell two to zero.
  * And then [Sean Tucker](https://www.4for4.com/player/sean-tucker) saw three snaps, which you’d typically expect to be a nothing burger on most teams, but on the Bucs that meant Tucker was on the field for the Bucs’ first true goal-line snap the season, scoring a TD on that goal-line carry.
  * Week 4 was the nightmare scenario for Irving managers, with him losing receiving work to Gainwell, losing goal-line work to Tucker, and then the Bucs offense generally being terrible.


### Cowboys (28.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Cowboys are up to 3rd in pass rate over expectation (PROE) on the season and passed 1.5% more than expected in Week 4.
  * And they were efficient as usual against the Texans, hitting 77th percentile passing and 73rd percentile rushing efficiency.
  * The team had 41 targets, with 20 (!) of them in the direction of [CeeDee Lamb](https://www.4for4.com/player/ceedee-lamb), who went scorched earth against the Texans.
  * [Javonte Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/javonte-williams) was next in line with five targets, meaning Lamb saw four times as many targets as any of his teammates. 
  * Speaking of Williams, he again dominated the RB carries, taking 19 of the 22, and ultimately scored three TDs.
  * What’s interesting, though, is Williams saw a season-low 65% of early-down snaps, according to PFF, as [Tyler Goodson](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyler-goodson) got more involved.
  * You’re starting both Lamb and Williams, but I’m sure you’re frustrated if you’re a [George Pickens](https://www.4for4.com/player/george-pickens) manager.
  * He only saw three targets last week and is underperforming his expected fantasy points per game at the 5th-highest rate among qualifying WRs.
  * The big problem to me seems to be the Cowboys’ structural decision to lower [Dak Prescott](https://www.4for4.com/player/dak-prescott)’s aDoT by a full yard relative to last year, and then not hitting on their deep passes when they do attempt them, with the Cowboys 31st in explosive pass rate through four weeks.
  * The Buccaneers’ defense is a good matchup for what the Cowboys have been doing, as they both limit explosives and opposing QB aDoTs but are 7th-worst in CPOE.
  * That hits me again as a Lamb game, not a Pickens game.


##  Eagles at Jaguars (O/U 42.5, JAX -6.5)
### Eagles (18.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * That’s right; the Eagles are projected for 1.5 fewer points than the [Jalon Daniels](https://www.4for4.com/player/jt-daniels) Buccaneers.
  * It’s all red for the Eagles’ offense, as they’re not better than 18th in any of the offensive stats I track.
  * They hit only the 22nd percentile in passing efficiency last week, and even worse, the 7th percentile in rushing efficiency.
  * The Eagles’ saving grace was 3rd down efficiency through three weeks, and then they went 0/12 on 3rd down in Week 4.
  * On the ground, [Saquon Barkley](https://www.4for4.com/player/saquon-barkley) played only four snaps due to a hamstring injury that should cost him at least this week.
  * And then backup [Tank Bigsby](https://www.4for4.com/player/tank-bigsby) just landed on IR as he needs core muscle surgery.
  * That leaves [Will Shipley](https://www.4for4.com/player/will-shipley) and an assortment of practice squad RBs (is it finally happening for [Jaydon Blue](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaydon-blue)?!?) to take on a fantastic Jaguars’ run defense.
  * The Bengals opted to not even try to run the ball on them last week, hitting +8.3% PROE for 53 (!) targets compared to only 13 carries.
  * But [Chase Brown](https://www.4for4.com/player/chase-brown) set a new career high in receptions, meaning if Shipley gets there for fantasy, it’ll be through the air.
  * Speaking of the air, [DeVonta Smith](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/smithd4) is not expected to play again this week.
  * In his absence last week, [Darius Cooper](https://www.4for4.com/player/darius-cooper) led the way with seven targets and two TDs.
  * I still lean toward [Dontayvion Wicks](https://www.4for4.com/player/dontayvion-wicks) as my preferred play here, and I think the Jaguars’ 6th-ranked EPA per play pass defense is a bit fraudulent, as they’re much worse in both success rate and explosive play rate.
  * But [Jalen Hurts](https://www.4for4.com/player/jalen-hurts) has been bad this season, with consecutive games of -0.35 EPA per play, going 5/15 for 46 yards against the blitz last week.
  * And now Lane Johnson just retired; this is as an Eagles fan and not a fantasy analyst, but it’s so over.


### Jaguars (24.5 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Jaguars only had 22 team targets last week, barely beating [CeeDee Lamb](https://www.4for4.com/player/ceedee-lamb).
  * That’s the glaring issue for this team in fantasy, as they’re 20th in PROE and 30th in plays per game.
  * [Brenton Strange](https://www.4for4.com/player/brenton-strange) led the way with eight targets last week, but I’d guess that is more a Bengals’ defense stat than anything we can expect moving forward.
  * If the [Parker Washington](https://www.4for4.com/player/parker-washington) manager in your league is panicking, this might be a good time to buy.
  * On the ground, [Bhayshul Tuten](https://www.4for4.com/player/bhayshul-tuten) had two overturned TDs, so he was *this* close to a nuclear Week 4.
  * He led [Chris Rodriguez](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-rodriguez) in routes 11 to six, targets two to zero, and carries 17 to six, with [LeQuint Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/lequint-allen) involved for seven routes as well.
  * This could be the week for Tuten against a struggling Eagles’ run defense.


##  Bengals at Dolphins (O/U 42.5, CIN -7.0)
### Bengals (24.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Bengals opted to only pass the ball against a great Jaguars’ run defense, finishing the day with 53 targets compared to 13 carries.
  * Ja’Marr Chase only ran 10 routes before entering concussion protocol; my assumption is he misses Week 5 but is back for Week 6.
  * [Tee Higgins](https://www.4for4.com/player/tee-higgins) paced the team with a fantastic 16 targets, but then he also left the game in the 4th quarter with a leg injury; my assumption is he misses Week 5 as well.
  * That led to a new career high in receptions for [Chase Brown](https://www.4for4.com/player/chase-brown), nine targets for [Dohnte Meyers](https://www.4for4.com/player/dohnte-meyers), seven for [Mitchell Tinsley](https://www.4for4.com/player/mitchell-tinsley), and five for [Mike Gesicki](https://www.4for4.com/player/mike-gesicki).
  * Brown played more than he typically does on 3rd down last week because the Bengals were putting him in the slot at times with [Samaje Perine](https://www.4for4.com/player/samaje-perine) in the backfield.
  * Brown remains a buy-low at least until he scores his 2nd TD of the season, underperforming his expected fantasy points by 2.6 per game.
  * But he’s been bad on the ground as he’s only better than [MarShawn Lloyd](https://www.4for4.com/player/marshawn-lloyd) in yards after contact per attempt.
  * If both Chase and Higgins are out, I genuinely think you can start all of Meyers, Tinsley, and Gesicki against a terrible Dolphins’ pass defense on a Bengals team that is 1st in PROE.
  * Meyers is my preferred play at WR as he ran 54 of the team’s 58 routes last week, highest on the team.
  * And this Dolphins defense just let TJ Hockenson earn 13 receptions against them last week, meaning Gesicki could see some volume.


### Dolphins (17.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * Our expectations were low for the Dolphins’ passing attack against the Vikings last week, but holy fuck!
  * The Dolphins passed at a rate 13.2% below expectation and hit only 2nd percentile rushing efficiency compared to all games from 2021 to 2025.
  * They had three fewer targets as a team than [CeeDee Lamb](https://www.4for4.com/player/ceedee-lamb) last week.
  * They only had 39 snaps, which was less than half the snaps of a few other teams in Week 4.
  * It’s a better matchup this week, I suppose, though I’m worried about [Malik Willis](https://www.4for4.com/player/malik-willis)’ sack rate against a Bengals defense that is 3rd in the league in that stat.
  * The Bengals pass defense always gives up fantasy points to opposing TEs, but can [Malik Willis](https://www.4for4.com/player/malik-willis) get the ball to [Greg Dulcich](https://www.4for4.com/player/greg-dulcich)? It’s a stoppable force meeting a movable object moment.
  * On the ground, [Ollie Gordon](https://www.4for4.com/player/ollie-gordon) was ultimately the RB you wanted, but the game didn’t start out that way.
  * [Jaylen Wright](https://www.4for4.com/player/jaylen-wright) was the starter but then lost work to Gordon as he struggled, with Gordon ultimately playing 14 snaps in the 2nd half compared to Wright’s two, according to PFF.
  * Gordon is easily the guy I want here given his performance last week, but we’ll need explosives like his 53-yard run last week again given the Dolphins’ rank dead-last in plays per game.


##  Bears at Packers (O/U 45.5, CHI -3.0)
### Bears (24.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * It didn’t translate for fantasy, but [Tyson Bagent](https://www.4for4.com/player/tyson-bagent) and the Bears hit 80th percentile passing efficiency last week, including an excellent 65% passing success rate.
  * Bagent ultimately just didn’t throw for a TD pass as the team had 30 targets compared to an unbelievable 53 carries.
  * One positive for fantasy was that [Colston Loveland](https://www.4for4.com/player/colston-loveland) saw nine targets, while [Rome Odunze](https://www.4for4.com/player/rome-odunze) and [Luther Burden](https://www.4for4.com/player/luther-burden) each saw six, compared to only two for [Kalif Raymond](https://www.4for4.com/player/kalif-raymond).
  * [Kyle Monangai](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyle-monangai) had a massive two-TD day, leading D’Andre Swift in snaps 50 to 33 and carries 30 to 15 while splitting the routes.
  * Swift had his usual lead role in the offense until he fumbled and then sat on the bench for the final three drives of the game.
  * This feels like an excellent sell-high moment for Monangai and/or buy-low moment for Swift, as these silly fumbling punishments don’t tend to extend to the next game.
  * Also, the Bears won’t run 89 (!) plays every week, as that was a top-15 mark for any team since at least 2016, according to JJ Zachariason.


### Packers (21.25 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * It looked better in the box score, but from an efficiency standpoint, the Packers were still awful running the ball in Week 4, hitting 15th percentile efficiency.
  * If you had to pick one of the backs, [MarShawn Lloyd](https://www.4for4.com/player/marshawn-lloyd) is the guy you want as he doubled [Kaleb Johnson](https://www.4for4.com/player/kaleb-johnson) in snaps and routes, earning five targets to Johnson’s zero.
  * Meanwhile, [](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-brooks)[Chris Brooks](https://www.4for4.com/player/chris-brooks-0) left in the 1st quarter last week due to a leg injury.
  * It’s a good matchup against a bad Bears’ run defense, but I’d still prefer not to start any Packers RB.
  * In the pass game, [Matthew Golden](https://www.4for4.com/player/matthew-golden) and [Tucker Kraft](https://www.4for4.com/player/tucker-kraft) paced the team with seven targets each.
  * [Christian Watson](https://www.4for4.com/player/christian-watson) had a rough day with only three targets, and he ran six fewer routes than Golden on the day as the Packers used 13 personnel more frequently with Golden as the lone WR on the field.
  * It’s a tough matchup on paper for the Packers passing game, but this is still the Bears defense that let the Panthers throw for 361 yards in Week 1.
  * And we’re surprisingly getting pass volume from the Packers this year, given their inability to run the ball, as they’re 7th in PROE compared to 18th last year.


##  Texans at Titans (O/U 38.5, HOU -7.5)
### Texans (23.0 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)

![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Falcons’ offense was unbelievable in Week 4, hitting 97th percentile passing and 99th percentile rushing efficiency. 
  * The team only needed 18 targets to easily beat the Saints, with London earning six of them.
  * Meanwhile, [Kyle Pitts](https://www.4for4.com/players/profile/pittsk1) only ran nine routes, with three other TEs combining for 16 routes.
  * I don’t know if it was a punishment or some odd strategy, but Pitts barely played on the first two drives before bouncing back up to his typical snap rate for the rest of the game.
  * That was probably a weird blip, but he hasn’t earned more than three targets in a game this season; you can safely look elsewhere at TE.
  * And you’re starting [Bijan Robinson](https://www.4for4.com/player/bijan-robinson-0) because he’s insane despite losing a larger chunk of playing time than we’d like to [Brian Robinson](https://www.4for4.com/player/brian-robinson).


##  Bills at Rams (O/U 54.5, LAR -3.0)
### Bills (25.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * We have to wait until Monday night for the second 50+ game total of the week.
  * Week 4 was probably the worst game we’ve seen from this passing attack, hitting a bad 35.9% success rate relative to our expectations for [Josh Allen](https://www.4for4.com/player/josh-allen).
  * That was also the 2nd consecutive game where the Bills passed at a rate below expectation, reversing course from Weeks 1 and 2.
  * DJ Moore only ran 14 routes as he aggravated his shoulder injury; this could be a real nuisance this year as he could have more days like this where he plays but then exits early after landing on his shoulder.
  * In Moore’s absence, [Khalil Shakir](https://www.4for4.com/player/khalil-shakir) and [Keon Coleman](https://www.4for4.com/player/keon-coleman) paced the team with eight and seven targets, respectively, with both solid starts if Moore sits.
  * [Dalton Kincaid](https://www.4for4.com/player/dalton-kincaid)’s route rate dropped to 55% in Week 4, a notable decline from Weeks 1-3, as the Bills relied less on their TEs through more 11 personnel.
  * That’s now two straight dud games from Kincaid after getting off to a scorching start to the season.
  * It’s a very difficult matchup for the passing attack this week, so I think it’s completely reasonable to look at your alternative TE options.
  * At RB, [James Cook](https://www.4for4.com/player/james-cook) took 12 of the 14 RB carries but split the routes with [Ty Johnson](https://www.4for4.com/player/ty-johnson), which will continue to limit Cook’s ceiling.


### Rams (28.75 Implied Points)
![](https://www.4for4.com/2026/w5/fantasy-football-week-5-start-em-sit-em-every-game-previewed-hoopes-there-it)
  * The Rams had a massive 84 snaps in Week 4 and passed at a rate 4.2% above expectation as they went into comeback mode against the Eagles.
  * They ended the day with an insane 50 targets, with [Kyren Williams](https://www.4for4.com/player/kyren-williams) earning his typical number of targets in a season (13) in just one game.
  * The Rams are now up to 9th in RB target share after finishing among the bottom-two teams every season since 2020, according to JJ Zachariason.
  * Williams dominated the RB work in Week 4, taking 70 snaps to [Blake Corum](https://www.4for4.com/player/blake-corum)’s 13, as Williams always plays over Corum when the Rams are down and chasing.
  * And then [Puka Nacua](https://www.4for4.com/player/puka-nacua) earned 12 targets, while [Davante Adams](https://www.4for4.com/player/davante-adams) earned nine. 
  * At nearly 29 implied points, against a bad Bills’ defense, you’re starting Nacua, Adams, and Williams.
