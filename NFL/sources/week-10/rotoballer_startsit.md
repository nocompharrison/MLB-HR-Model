##  **_Matchups Analysis - Sunday 9_** _:30 AM EST Game (Munich)_
## **New England Patriots at Detroit Lions**
**Matchups We Love:**
**QB[Drake Maye](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/24958/Drake+Maye)**
Drake Maye continues to produce as a top fantasy QB. He has scored above 21 fantasy points in six straight games and has thrown at least three touchdown passes in four of those six contests. Now Maye gets a favorable matchup against this Lions secondary. Detroit has allowed the most fantasy points, most passing yards, and most passing touchdowns to opposing QBs this season. He's a must-start QB1.
**Patriots WRs**
**Update: Brown is listed as questionable.**
It remains to be seen whether [A.J. Brown](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21042/A.J.+Brown) will be healthy enough to play in this game. If he is able to suit up, he will be a solid WR2. The Lions have given up the seventh-most fantasy points, third-most receptions, and the sixth-most receiving yards. Douglas will also have some flex appeal in this matchup even if Brown suits up. Douglas has scored above 15 PPR fantasy points in four of the last five weeks.
**Lions WRs**
**Update: St. Brown is listed as questionable.**
[Amon-Ra St. Brown](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22587/Amon-Ra+St.+Brown) and [Jameson Williams](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23234/Jameson+Williams) could both be in for solid games in Week 10. Although Williams will likely be matched up against Christian Gonzalez for most of the game, Gonzalez just got torched by [Christian Watson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23395/Christian+Watson) last week (171 yards and one touchdown). Since New England ranks 31st in fantasy points allowed to the WR position, St. Brown will be a must-start WR1, while Williams is a middle-of-the-pack WR2.
**Patriots TEs**
Both [Hunter Henry](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/17975/Hunter+Henry) and [Eli Raridon](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/26863/Eli+Raridon) have some TE1 potential this week against the Lions. Detroit ranks dead last in fantasy points allowed, receptions allowed, receiving yards allowed, and receiving touchdowns allowed to the TE position. This defense has also allowed multiple top-10 fantasy TE finishes in the same week on three different occasions. That puts Henry and Raridon both as low-end TE1s.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**RB[Corey Kiner](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/25968/Corey+Kiner)**
**Update: Stevenson is listed as questionable. If Stevenson were to play, he would carry solid RB2 upside.**
Corey Kiner could operate as the top back in this New England offense in Week 10. Both [Rhamondre Stevenson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/22546/Rhamondre+Stevenson) (knee) and [TreVeyon Henderson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23245/TreVeyon+Henderson) (illness) are dealing with different things. While fantasy managers could be looking to start Kiner this week, expectations should be tempered for the young running back. The Lions have allowed the second-fewest fantasy points and sixth-fewest rushing yards to RBs this season. He will be a risky RB2.
**RB[Jahmyr Gibbs](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23200/Jahmyr+Gibbs)**
Jahmyr Gibbs should continue to be in all fantasy lineups in Week 10. He ranks first in fantasy points per game, second in rushing yards per game, second in rushing touchdowns per game, and fourth in receptions per game among all RBs.
**Other Matchups:**
**QB[Jared Goff](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/17922/Jared+Goff)**
Jared Goff has posted subpar fantasy numbers in back-to-back weeks. Goff scored 10 fantasy points against the Vikings in Week 8 and 6.3 fantasy points against the Dolphins last week. For this week, Goff will come in as a low-end QB1 in a game that could feature a ton of points. The Patriots have allowed a 300-yard passer in consecutive games, which included [Jordan Love](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/21841/Jordan+Love) throwing for 372 yards and three touchdowns last weekend.
**TE[Sam LaPorta](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23141/Sam+LaPorta)**
Sam LaPorta has been a bit quiet in the passing game over the last few weeks. He has finished outside the top 20 at the TE position in three straight games and hasn't scored above eight PPR fantasy points since Week 5. However, LaPorta will still come in as a low-end TE1 play this week. [Colston Loveland](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/25869/Colston+Loveland) just totaled seven catches for 69 yards and one touchdown against this Patriots defense a few weeks back.
**Injuries:**
  * Rhamondre Stevenson (knee) - questionable
  * A.J. Brown (knee/ankle) - questionable
  * TreVeyon Henderson (illness) - out


##  **_Matchups Analysis - Sunday_** _1:00 EST Games_
## **Buffalo Bills at New York Jets**
**Matchups We Love:**
**Jets WRs**
[Garrett Wilson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/23122/Garrett+Wilson) missed last week's contest due to a concussion, but he should return in Week 10. He will return to being a solid WR2 option in a plus matchup. Buffalo has given up the 12th-most fantasy points per game to outside WRs. [Omar Cooper Jr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/26760/Omar+Cooper+Jr.) and Mitchell will also have some fantasy value. Both will come in as potential flex plays, with Mitchell ranked slightly higher since he had nine targets the last time Wilson suited up.
**Matchups We Hate:**
**None**
**Other Matchups:**
**QB[Josh Allen](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/19801/Josh+Allen)**
Josh Allen is a must-start QB1 in all formats. Although he scored a season-low 13.9 fantasy points in a tough matchup against the Vikings last week, expect a bounce-back performance from the Bills signal-caller. New York has allowed a top-12 fantasy QB finish in four straight weeks.
**QB[Geno Smith](https://www.rotoballer.com/nfl/player/14895/Geno+Smith)**
Geno Smith should only be viewed as a QB2 play in Superflex formats this week.
