The top fantasy football starts for Week 10 include Josh Allen at QB; Jahmyr Gibbs, Christian McCaffrey, and James Cook III at RB; Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Ja’Marr Chase at WR; George Kittle at TE; and the Houston Texans at D/ST, all earning A+ grades in our Quick Grades. On the other end, Daniel Jones receives a D- at QB, while Kimani Vidal at RB, AJ Barner at TE, and the Las Vegas Raiders at D/ST also land D- grades. Our start/sit grades for every QB, RB, WR, TE, D/ST and kicker are built from FantasyPros’ consensus projections and rankings, along with [Derek Brown’s weekly Primer](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/09/the-primer-week-10-edition-2026-fantasy-football/).
