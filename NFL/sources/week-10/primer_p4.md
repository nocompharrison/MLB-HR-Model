#### **Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Rico Dowdle (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rico-dowdle.php)**
Since Week 7, [Rico Dowdle](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rico-dowdle.php) has been the RB23 in fantasy points per game, averaging 15 touches and 71 total yards. In those two games, Dowdle has had a 45.9% snap rate, a 38.1% rushing share, and a 45% route share (10.9% target share). Among 51 qualifying backs, Dowdle is 11th in yards after contact per attempt and 29th in missed tackle rate. Dowdle is a strong flex at his floor, but he could easily be an RB2 again this week. Over their last five games, Cincy has allowed the third-highest explosive run rate, the fourth-highest rushing success rate, and the seventh-highest yards after contact per attempt.
**[Germie Bernard (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/germie-bernard.php)**
Since Week 5, [Germie Bernard](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/germie-bernard.php) has been the WR23 in fantasy points per game. He should be busy this week, with [DJ Turner](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dj-turner.php) following [DK Metcalf](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dk-metcalf.php). Since Week 5, Bernard has had a 22.2% target share with 1.85 YPRR, five red zone targets, and a 22% first-read share. Over their last five games, Cincy has been destroyed by slot receivers, allowing the seventh-most yards per target and the fourth-most fantasy points per opportunity to the position.
#### **Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Aaron Rodgers (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aaron-rodgers.php)**
[Aaron Rodgers](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aaron-rodgers.php) is the QB20 in fantasy points per game. He has three games this season with at least 20 fantasy points per game, but he hasn’t had one of those outings since Week 5. Among 31 qualifying passers, he is 23rd in yards per attempt and passing success rate, 15th in highly accurate throw rate, 23rd in catchable target rate, and 26th in hero throw rate. Rodgers should check in with solid QB2 numbers this week, but I also wouldn’t be shocked if he flirts with low-end QB1 production if Pittsburgh is forced to play catch-up or keep pace with Cincy’s high-octane offense. Over their last five games, Cincy has allowed the 14th-most yards per attempt, the eighth-highest passing success rate, the 12th-most EPA per dropback, and the 14th-most fantasy points per dropback.
**[Mike Gesicki (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/mike-gesicki.php)**
[Mike Gesicki](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/mike-gesicki.php) is the TE12 in fantasy points per game, but that is really just fueled by six touchdowns in eight games played. Over the last two games, his role has grown with a 69.4% route share, 10.4% target share, 1.47 YPRR, and a 13.5% first-read share. Gesicki is on the streaming radar again this week. Over their last five games, Pittsburgh has allowed the third-most yards per target and the 11th-most fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends.
**[Darnell Washington (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/darnell-washington.php)**
Ok, we’re going back to [Darnell Washington](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/darnell-washington.php) this week as a streaming option. Yep, you guessed it. It’s because of single-high coverage. The last time they played Pittsburgh, they utilized single high with 59.5% of their defensive snaps. In three of their last four games, they have utilized single high 52.5-67.4% of their defensive snaps. The last time Washington faced this defense, he secured three of his four targets with 67 receiving yards (zero touchdowns). Against single high, Washington has a 27% TPRR and 2.72 YPRR. With [DK Metcalf](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dk-metcalf.php) dealing with [DJ Turner](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dj-turner.php) all game, Washington could see a decent workload. Washington is tied for the team lead with seven red zone targets and has three scores this season. He could be a TE1 this week. Over their last five games, Cincy has allowed the tenth-most yards per target to tight ends while ranking 15th in receiving fantasy points per game.
#### **Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**
_Players in consideration for lineups, but temper your expectations this week._
**[Chase Brown (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/chase-brown.php)**
[Chase Brown](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/chase-brown.php) is the RB20 in fantasy points per game as a weekly volume play. He has had horrible touchdown luck, with zero touchdowns since Week 1 and only one score this season. Since Week 5, Brown has averaged 15.8 touches and 73.5 total yards while splitting the early-down work with [Samaje Perine](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/samaje-perine.php). Since Week 5, Brown has had a 66.9% snap rate, a 47.9% rushing share, a 60% route share, and a 15.1% target share. Across his last three games, Brown’s efficiency has begun to improve as Cincy’s offensive line has gotten their act together. In those games, Brown has had 2.38 yards before contact per attempt, which has allowed him to rip a 13.3% explosive run rate and 2.38 yards after contact per attempt. Just for context, with that small 32-carry sample size. If Brown puts that sample up against all backs with at least 32 carries this season (62 rushers), he’d rank 15th in explosive run rate and 19th in yards after contact per attempt. Brown faces a tough matchup this week, but he should see enough volume to be at least a decent RB2. Over their last five games, the Steelers have allowed the seventh-lowest explosive run rate, the third-lowest rushing success rate, and the fewest yards before contact per attempt.
**[DK Metcalf (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dk-metcalf.php)**
[DK Metcalf](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dk-metcalf.php) is headed for a long day in Week 10. He’ll likely see shadow coverage from [DJ Turner](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dj-turner.php) again. Metcalf is the WR43 in fantasy points per game, with three games this season with at least 12 PPR points. The last time he tangled with [DJ Turner](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dj-turner.php), he was shadowed on 77.1% of his routes, securing three of his five targets in his coverage with a score. That touchdown only occurred because Turner ran into another Cincy player, which left Metcalf wide open. Last year, in their two meetings, Turner followed Metcalf on 78.1-90.3% of his routes, holding him to 50 or fewer receiving yards in both games (zero scores). Overall, Metcalf has a 22.1% target share with 1.31 YPRR, five red zone targets, 15 deep targets, and a 31.4% first-read share. Sit Metcalf this week.
**[Pat Freiermuth (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/pat-freiermuth.php)**
The Bengals have been a wonderful matchup for tight ends this season, but I’m projecting another down game for [Pat Freiermuth](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/pat-freiermuth.php) this week. The last time he faced Cincy, he secured two of his four targets with 30 scoreless receiving yards. This all goes back to the coverage structure for Cincy. The last time they played Pittsburgh, they utilized single high with 59.5% of their defensive snaps. In three of their last four games, they have utilized single high 52.5-67.4% of their defensive snaps. Freiermuth disappears against single high with a 13% TPRR and 0.98 YPRR. Sit Freiermuth this week.
