#### **Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[J.J. McCarthy (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jj-mccarthy.php)**
[J.J. McCarthy](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jj-mccarthy.php) is good to go this week after picking up a hip pointer last week. His success on the field hasn’t equated to fantasy success over the last two games, but that could change this week. Over the last two games, he has scored zero touchdowns, which is holding his fantasy stats down. Washington’s pass defense has improved. Over their last five games, Washington has allowed the sixth-lowest passing touchdown rate, the 12th-lowest passing success rate, the ninth-fewest fantasy points per dropback, and the 12th-lowest EPA per dropback. McCarthy is a solid QB2 this week who’ll need to continue to be on his A game to be a QB1.
**[Jayden Daniels (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jayden-daniels.php)**
The last time that [Jayden Daniels](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jayden-daniels.php) faced this defense, he was the QB2 in weekly scoring, but that was related more to his 103 rushing yards and two rushing scores than to what he did as a passer. Since his return in Week 5, Daniels has been the QB7 in fantasy points per game, averaging 4.8 rushing attempts and 38 rushing yards. Among 31 qualifying passers, Daniels is 18th in yards per attempt, 22nd in passing success rate, ninth in highly accurate throw rate, and 15th in catchable target rate. Daniels will need all the rushing production he can get this week. The Giants’ pass defense has been much improved. Over their last five games, they have allowed the fewest yards per attempt, the lowest passing success rate, the seventh-lowest passer rating, and the fourth-lowest EPA per dropback.
**[Antonio Williams (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/antonio-williams.php)**
With [Terry McLaurin](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/terry-mclaurin.php) out of the lineup, [Antonio Williams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/antonio-williams.php) has had an 18.8% target share, 1.32 YPRR, two deep targets, zero red zone targets, and a 22.9% first-read share. During this stretch, Williams has led the team in target share and first-read share. He’s a middling flex this week. Over their last five games, the Giants have held perimeter wide receivers to the third-fewest yards per target while ranking 16th in fantasy points per opportunity.
#### **Concerning Starts & Players to Fade**

**[Cam Skattebo (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/cam-skattebo.php)**
[Cam Skattebo](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/cam-skattebo.php) will be back this week (hamstring). He practiced in full on Tuesday and Wednesday. He doesn’t carry an injury designation into Week 10. The last time we saw Skattebo, he was racking up 18 touches and 121 total yards against the Texans as the RB7 for the week. In that game, he had a 55.4% snap rate, 50% of the rushing work, and a 32.3% route share. I expect him to see a similar workload (if not elevated) this week. Among 45 qualifying backs, Skattebo is 17th in yards after contact per attempt and 11th in missed tackle rate. Skattebo is a volume-based RB2 this week with a tough matchup. Over their last five games, Washington has allowed the eighth-lowest explosive run rate, the fifth-fewest yards after contact per attempt, and the sixth-lowest missed tackle rate.
**[Rachaad White (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rachaad-white.php)**
Since Week 6, [Rachaad White](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/rachaad-white.php) has been the RB25 in fantasy points per game, averaging 11 touches and 62 total yards. In those games, he has had a 57.4% snap rate, 40.3% of the rushing work, and a 40.2% route share (10.6% target share). Among 45 qualifying rushers, White is 32nd in yards after contact per attempt and 13th in missed tackle rate. White is a volume-based RB2/3 this week against an improved Giants’ run defense. Over their last five games, New York has allowed the ninth-lowest rushing success rate, the 13th-lowest yards after contact per attempt, and the second-lowest missed tackle rate.
**[Stefon Diggs (WR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/stefon-diggs.php)**
[Stefon Diggs](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/stefon-diggs.php) is the WR25 in fantasy points per game with a 19.9% target share, 1.63 YPRR, six red zone targets, seven deep targets, and a 24.1% first-read share. Without [Terry McLaurin](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/terry-mclaurin.php) in the lineup, Diggs has had a 17.2% target share with 1.71 YPRR and a 20.8% first-read share. He’s a volume-based flex this week who will need a score to pay off. The Giants have been shutting down slot receivers. Over their last five games, New York has allowed the fourth-fewest yards per target and the third-fewest fantasy points per opportunity to the position.
**[Chig Okonkwo (TE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/chigoziem-okonkwo.php)**
Since Week 7, [Chig Okonkwo](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/chigoziem-okonkwo.php) has been the TE25 in fantasy points per game, with one outing with double-digit PPR points. He has had a 17.2% target share with 1.51 YPRR, one red zone target, and an 18.8% first-read share. Sit Okonkwo this week. Over their last five games, New York has allowed the third-fewest yards per target and the sixth-fewest fantasy points per opportunity to tight ends.
