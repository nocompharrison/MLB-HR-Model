#### **Strong Starts**
_Here are players that should be in starting consideration for most fantasy teams._
**[Jordan Mason (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-mason.php)**
Over the last three games, [Jordan Mason](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-mason.php) has taken over as Minny’s lead back with a 46.7% snap rate, a 49.2% rushing share, and a 29.2% route share (5.9% target share). He has averaged 10.4 touches and 43.3 total yards. Mason has a strong 14.3% explosive run rate and a decent 2.02 yards after contact per attempt. He could return low-end RB2/solid flex numbers this week. Over their last five games, Green Bay has allowed the 13th-highest rushing success rate, the sixth-most yards after contact per attempt, and the highest missed tackle rate.
#### **Fantasy Football Flex & Deep Plays to Consider**
_Let’s fill out those flex spots._
**[Jordan Love (QB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-love.php)**
After last week’s shellacking of the Pats, [Jordan Love](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-love.php) is the QB4 in fantasy points per game. Among 30 qualifying passers, Love is seventh in passing touchdown rate, first in deep ball rate, 21st in catchable target rate, and fourth in hero throw rate. He’ll have his work cut out for him this week. Over their last five games, Minnesota has allowed the third-fewest yards per attempt, the second-lowest passing touchdown rate, the 13th-lowest passing success rate, and the fewest fantasy points per dropback. In that span, Minny has also had the highest blitz and pressure rates in the NFL. Love has crushed the blitz, ranking ninth in passer rating, tenth in EPA per dropback, and eighth in passing touchdown rate against it. Love has a wide range of outcomes this week against a formidable foe. He could cook again this week or get cooked if the Vikings’ pass rush consistently gets home against the Packers’ offensive line.
**[Kaleb Johnson (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kaleb-johnson-rb.php)**
Last week, [Kaleb Johnson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kaleb-johnson-rb.php) finished with 14 carries, 68 yards on the ground, and a score. I expect him to be the early-down option for Green Bay’s committee backfield. Johnson has been awesome on a per-touch basis this season with 2.83 yards after contact per attempt and a 26% missed tackle rate. The Green Bay offensive line has only managed to generate 1.26 yards before contact per attempt for him, though. I don’t expect that number to be higher this week, but if he can find a crease, he could do some damage against this Minny run defense. Over their last five games, Minnesota has allowed the tenth-highest explosive run rate, the most yards after contact per attempt, and the fourth-highest missed tackle rate. Johnson could be a decent flex option this week.
**[Aaron Jones (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aaron-jones.php)**
Since [Jordan Mason](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/jordan-mason.php)‘s return, [Aaron Jones](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/aaron-jones.php) has taken a step back with a 32.9% snap rate, a 30.5% rushing share, and a 22.9% route share. He has averaged only 7.3 touches and 29 total yards. Unless his role changes this week, this will be the last week that Jones is a must-write-up for the Primer, as he is just a change of pace/back up option for Minnesota right now. Jones is a low-end flex option this week, though. Over their last five games, Green Bay has allowed the 13th-highest rushing success rate, the sixth-most yards after contact per attempt, and the highest missed tackle rate.
**[MarShawn Lloyd (RB)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/marshawn-lloyd.php)**
[MarShawn Lloyd](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/marshawn-lloyd.php) missed last week’s game with a knee issue. He opened this week not on the injury report. I expect Lloyd and [Kaleb Johnson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/kaleb-johnson-rb.php) to split the backfield work this week, with Lloyd handling the passing downs more and chipping in on early downs. Lloyd has only 1.37 yards after contact per attempt and a 16% missed tackle rate this season. As a passing-down option, he has a 24.8% route share and 5.4% target share this season. None of this is incredibly appealing. Over their last five games, Minnesota has allowed the tenth-highest explosive run rate, the most yards after contact per attempt, and the fourth-highest missed tackle rate, but the Packers don’t have the offensive line talent to consistently open running lanes for their backs. Lloyd is a low-end flex option this week.
