## Pistons vs. Magic pick: Pistons -2.5
  * Time: 7 p.m. ET
  * TV: DAZN-Detroit, DAZN-Orlando
  * Analyst: [Quinn Allen](https://www.covers.com/writers/quinn-allen)


**Top pick:[Pistons -2.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Quinn Allen is backing the [Detroit Pistons](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/detroit-pistons/odds) to snap a two-game losing streak against the [Orlando Magic](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/orlando-magic/odds) in his [Pistons vs. Magic prediction](https://www.covers.com/nba/pistons-vs-magic-prediction-picks-nov-24-2026).
The Pistons boast a +8.9 net rating advantage over the Magic, and their defense has been elite, allowing just 108.6 points per game, the fifth-fewest in the league.
Meanwhile, Orlando ranks 27th in scoring at 108.8 PPG and has struggled mightily at home, going 1-5. Paolo Banchero's absence also leaves the Magic without their primary offensive weapon. The Pistons are 7-3 on the road and have covered in two of their last three away games.
## Celtics vs. Wizards pick: Paul George Over 29.5 points + rebounds + assists
  * Time: 8 p.m. ET
  * TV: NBC
  * Analyst: [Todd Cordell](https://www.covers.com/writers/todd-cordell)


**Top pick:[Paul George Over 29.5 points + rebounds + assists](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Todd Cordell expects the [Boston Celtics](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/boston-celtics/odds) to end a four-game skid and cover as double-digit favorites against the [Washington Wizards](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/washington-wizards/odds) in his [Celtics vs. Wizards prediction](https://www.covers.com/nba/celtics-vs-wizards-prediction-picks-nov-24-2026) — largely because of Paul George.
George has cleared 30 PRA in both games without Mitchell Robinson, averaging 35 minutes with a small spike in usage.
Extra responsibility will serve him well against the Wizards, who rank 13th in pace and 25th in defensive rating over their past 10 games.
They have struggled to defend transition and the pick-and-roll this season, two of George’s primary scoring methods.
He has gone Over 29.5 PRA in five of six vs. bottom-10 transition defenses, and six of seven against bottom-15 teams defending P&R.
## Mavericks vs. Nuggets pick: Nikola Jokic Over 9.5 assists
  * Time: 10 p.m. ET
  * TV: NBC
  * Analyst: [Todd Cordell](https://www.covers.com/writers/todd-cordell)


**Top pick:[Nikola Jokic Over 9.5 assists](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Todd Cordell's [Mavericks vs. Nuggets prediction](https://www.covers.com/nba/mavericks-vs-nuggets-prediction-picks-nov-24-2026) is high on Nikola Jokic as a facilitator.
Jokic has recorded double-digit assists in six of eight games when excluding top-10 teams in defensive rating and assists allowed. The Mavericks rank 12th and 19th, respectively.
Jokic also excels against teams struggling to defend post-ups. He has dropped 10+ dimes in four of five games vs. bottom-10 defenses against post-ups this season, and 22 of 29 since the beginning of the 2025-26 campaign.
The NBA Cup gives Jokic an extreme minutes floor, and he is a one-man offensive hub who makes the most of that opportunity.
