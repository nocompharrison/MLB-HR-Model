## Expert NBA ATS picks: Nov. 24
### Pistons (-1.5) vs. Magic: ![Pistons](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/det.svg?width=25&height=25) Pistons -1.5
[Trade 53% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
I expected a larger spread because there's quite a gap between these two teams. The [Detroit Pistons](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/detroit-pistons/odds) have a +8.9 net rating advantage over the [Orlando Magic](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/orlando-magic/odds), and their defense is elite (as I broke down in my [Pistons vs. Magic predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/pistons-vs-magic-prediction-picks-nov-24-2026)). I expect the Pistons to cover against a Magic team that's just 1-5 at home. 
### Celtics (-10.5) vs. Wizards: ![Celtics](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/bos.svg?width=25&height=25) Celtics -10.5
[Trade 50% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Boston Celtics](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/boston-celtics/odds) are allowing a league-low 102.1 ppg, and their net rating is 14.1 points better than the [Washington Wizards](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/washington-wizards/odds). While the Wizards are playing better basketball at the moment, I have to side with Boston's elite defense against Washington's subpar 26th-ranked offense. 
### Mavericks vs. Nuggets (-8.5): ![Mavericks](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/dal.svg?width=25&height=25) Mavericks +8.5
[Trade 53% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
I'm liking the [Dallas Mavericks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/dallas-mavericks/odds) as an ATS underdog tonight. They've won three straight, and [Cooper Flagg](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/368264/cooper-flagg) has averaged 27.7 points during that span. The [Denver Nuggets](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/denver-nuggets/odds) are in good form as well, but they needed OT on Sunday to beat the Bulls, and they've only covered this number twice in their last five contests. I expect a close game, and Dallas could threaten to win outright.
