### **Boston Celtics vs. Washington Wizards**
The Celtics are in the middle of a very ugly four-game losing streak in which they've scored 105 points or fewer in every game. In back-to-back contests, the Celtics have scored fewer than 100 points, including on Saturday night against the Kings, a team that ranks 25th in scoring defense, allowing 119.4 points per game.
The Celtics are favored by 10.5, and while I expect them to win this game, I'm still worried about the offense. While the Wizards are allowing 115.4 points per game, they've held three consecutive opponents below 100 points.
And even if the Celtics break out of their slump against an average-at-best defense, there's no reason to trust the Wizards' offense in this game. For as bad as the Celtics' offense has been, the defense has been outstanding, holding three consecutive opponents to 105 points or less. The Celtics are allowing just 102.1 points per game, the fewest in the league, which is why I don't expect the Wizards to get anywhere near their season average of 110.3.

### **Dallas Mavericks vs. Denver Nuggets**
I really don't understand what the Nuggets have done this season to be favored by 8.5 points over the Mavericks. While they've won three consecutive games, all of those wins were by nine points or fewer. The Nuggets’ most recent victory came by six points in overtime against the 5-12 Bulls. The Nuggets are 10-5, but only three of their wins are by double digits.
While the Mavericks are dealing with a slew of injuries, Cooper Flagg is still healthy, and PJ Washington has a chance to play for the first time since Nov. 18. Without Washington, the Mavericks are 2-0, as Flagg and Kyrie Irving have totally taken over.
Though the Mavericks are shooting 34.6% from 3-point range this season, they've shot at least 38% in three consecutive games. Each of those contests resulted in a Dallas victory. I expect the Mavericks to continue to shoot well from beyond the arc tonight, as the Nuggets are allowing opponents to make 36.4% of their 3-point attempts. That ranks 23rd in the league.
**Pick: Mavericks +8.5 ([-115](https://dksb.sng.link/As9kz/zdg8?_dl=https%3A%2F%2Fsportsbook.draftkings.com%2Fgateway%3Fs%3D801206430%26wpcid%3D255224%26wpsrc%3D1756%26wpcn%3DNFLPlayoffs2023%26wpscn%3DBet5Get200%26wpcrn%3DFP1%26wpscid%3Dxx%26wpcrid%3Dxx&pcid=255224))**

### **Detroit Pistons vs. Orlando Magic**
While Cade Cunningham is on the injury report, he is probable for tonight's game. Though he scored just five points in Sunday's loss to the Hawks, he's been the Pistons' most consistent scorer all season, averaging 24.2 points per game.
While Cunningham being on the court is obviously important to the Pistons ultimately winning this game, turnovers are ultimately why I think they will leave Orlando with the win. This season, the Magic are averaging a turnover on 15.6% of their possessions. The Pistons are averaging a turnover on just 13.9% of their possessions, which ranks 10th-best in the league.
I expect the Magic to continue to turn the ball over at a high rate, as the Pistons are forcing 17.2 turnovers per game, which is the fourth-best mark in the league. While the Magic are forcing 14.9 turnovers per game and a turnover on 14.7% of opponent possessions, the Pistons are forcing a turnover on 16.9% of opponent possessions.
**Pick: Pistons -2.5 ([-106](https://dksb.sng.link/As9kz/zdg8?_dl=https%3A%2F%2Fsportsbook.draftkings.com%2Fgateway%3Fs%3D801206430%26wpcid%3D255224%26wpsrc%3D1756%26wpcn%3DNFLPlayoffs2023%26wpscn%3DBet5Get200%26wpcrn%3DFP1%26wpscid%3Dxx%26wpcrid%3Dxx&pcid=255224))**
