**Top pick:[Grizzlies -3.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Quinn Allen envisions a clear edge for the [Memphis Grizzlies](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/memphis-grizzlies/odds) against the [Charlotte Hornets](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/charlotte-hornets/odds), despite Memphis dealing with key absences.
