## Expert NBA ATS picks: Nov. 21
### Bucks vs. 76ers: ![76ers](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/phi.svg?width=25&height=25) 76ers -12.5
[Trade 48% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
These two teams are on completely opposite sides of the spectrum. The [Philadelphia 76ers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/philadelphia-76ers/odds) have a 15.5-point net rating advantage over the [Milwaukee Bucks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/milwaukee-bucks/odds), who are a horrendous 1-15, losing 12 straight. I like Philly to cover, but only if [Tyrese Maxey](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/187862/tyrese-maxey) plays, since [Jaylen Brown](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/428/jaylen-brown) is already sidelined. Maxey is questionable. 
### Trail Blazers vs. Knicks: ![Trail Blazers](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/por.svg?width=25&height=25) Trail Blazers +4.5
[Trade 48% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
This pick definitely hinges on the availability of [Deni Avdija](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/224214/deni-avdija). He's missed the last two games, and the 25-year-old is the [Portland Trail Blazers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/portland-trail-blazers/odds)' best player. Portland and the [New York Knicks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/new-york-knicks/odds) have basically identical net ratings, and the Blazers are averaging eight points more per game. If Avdija plays, Portland is worth a look. If not, I expect NY to cover. 
### Clippers vs. Mavericks: ![Mavericks](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/dal.svg?width=25&height=25) Mavericks -5.5
[Trade 51% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Dallas Mavericks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/dallas-mavericks/odds) have been dominant at home, going 6-2. The [Los Angeles Clippers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/la-clippers/odds) are winless on the road and have lost back-to-back games by a combined 37 points. Dallas is a much better defensive team, and they also have the rest advantage. I'll back the Mavs. 
### Grizzlies vs. Timberwolves: ![Grizzlies](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/mem.svg?width=25&height=25) Grizzlies +5.5
[Trade 48% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Memphis Grizzlies](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/memphis-grizzlies/odds) are averaging a league-best 122.1 ppg, and they will likely get top scorer Ty Jerome back tonight after he missed the last week. While the [Minnesota Timberwolves](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/minnesota-timberwolves/odds) are stout defensively, this is a Grizzlies team unbeaten on the road (8-0). Memphis has also won five straight. 
### Pelicans vs. Spurs: ![Spurs](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/sa.svg?width=25&height=25) Spurs -17.5
[Trade 49% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
I don't usually like these large spreads, but there's an 18.1-point gap in net rating between the [New Orleans Pelicans](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/new-orleans-pelicans/odds) and [San Antonio Spurs](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/san-antonio-spurs/odds). The Pels are allowing the most points of any team in the Association, and the Spurs have a rest advantage. New Orleans has played four times in the last six nights. I expect a big victory for San Antonio. 
### Celtics vs. Kings: ![Celtics](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/bos.svg?width=25&height=25) Celtics -13.5
[Trade 49% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Sacramento Kings](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/sacramento-kings/odds) are a disaster, and they've lost five straight by an average of 13 points. The [Boston Celtics](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/boston-celtics/odds) are one of the most well-rounded teams in the NBA, allowing the fewest points and ranking fifth in offensive rating. The C's will overwhelm Sacramento.
