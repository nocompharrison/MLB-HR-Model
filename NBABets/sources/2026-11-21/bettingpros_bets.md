### **Milwaukee Bucks at Philadelphia 76ers**
The Bucks battle the 76ers in the only afternoon game, and given how different these teams’ records are, I expect the public to be all over the 76ers as 11.5-point favorites. However, it almost seems too obvious that the 13-3 76ers will blow out the 1-15 Bucks at home. Looking deeper, it's clear the 76ers will win, but it may not be as lopsided as expected.
Despite being 10 games above .500, the 76ers are averaging just 2.9 more points per game than they're allowing. They've won only two games by 12+ points. When the 76ers faced the Bucks earlier this season, they defeated them by only 11. Jaylen Brown played in that game and scored 27 points, but he won't be on the court today. Additionally, Tyrese Maxey, who scored 26 points in that game, is questionable.
The Bucks have lost 12 consecutive games. However, they’ve lost each of their last five games by 11 points or fewer. The Bucks’ real struggles have come against top defensive teams like the Celtics and Knicks, who they scored fewer than 100 points against the last time they faced each. While the 76ers rank 10th in scoring defense, allowing 111.6 points per game, they're allowing 121 points per game over their last three.
**Pick: Bucks +11.5 ([-105](https://dksb.sng.link/As9kz/zdg8?_dl=https%3A%2F%2Fsportsbook.draftkings.com%2Fgateway%3Fs%3D801206430%26wpcid%3D255224%26wpsrc%3D1756%26wpcn%3DNFLPlayoffs2023%26wpscn%3DBet5Get200%26wpcrn%3DFP1%26wpscid%3Dxx%26wpcrid%3Dxx&pcid=255224))**

### **Portland Trail Blazers at New York Knicks**
The Knicks' defense continues to be a problem for nearly every opponent. They're allowing just 103.5 points per game, and they've held 13 of their 15 opponents to 111 points or fewer. The Knicks have held four consecutive opponents to fewer than 110 points, despite one of those games going to overtime, and they’re allowing opponents to make just 43.8% of their field goals this season.
The problem for the Knicks is that their offense hasn't been very good. The Knicks are 9-6 almost entirely because of their defense, as they’re scoring just 109.5 points per game. New York has scored 112 points or fewer in five consecutive contests, despite two of those five opponents ranking 18th or worse in points allowed per game.
The Trail Blazers are allowing just 111.2 points per game and have held five of their last six opponents below 110 points. While the offense ranks eighth at 117.5 points per game, it hasn't mattered how much a team scores against the Knicks' defense. This will be a defensive struggle, and I wouldn't be surprised if this game ends with fewer than 210 combined points.
**Pick: Under 218.5 Points ([-110](https://dksb.sng.link/As9kz/zdg8?_dl=https%3A%2F%2Fsportsbook.draftkings.com%2Fgateway%3Fs%3D801206430%26wpcid%3D255224%26wpsrc%3D1756%26wpcn%3DNFLPlayoffs2023%26wpscn%3DBet5Get200%26wpcrn%3DFP1%26wpscid%3Dxx%26wpcrid%3Dxx&pcid=255224))**

### **Memphis Grizzlies at Minnesota Timberwolves**
The Grizzlies lead the NBA in scoring, averaging 122.1 points per game. They've scored at least 124 points in five straight games, reaching at least 136 points twice. The Timberwolves are allowing just 105.8 points per game, but I think that oddsmakers are giving Minnesota's defense too much credit and not respecting the Grizzlies' offense enough.
While Cameron Boozer will miss this game, Memphis’ leading scorer Ty Jerome is probable. He is averaging 25.4 points per game, but has missed the last four games with a hip injury. Still, the Grizzlies lit up the scoreboard.
While I'd feel more confident in this play if both Jerome and Boozer were on the floor, I still don't think it's justified to put this total more than 11 points below the Grizzlies' season average. After all, while losing Boozer is bad, Jerome is averaging 8.8 more points per game than Boozer. With that in mind, the offense should actually look better in this game than it has over the last week, even against a really good defense.
**Pick: Grizzlies Team Total Over 111.5 Points ([-110](https://dksb.sng.link/As9kz/zdg8?_dl=https%3A%2F%2Fsportsbook.draftkings.com%2Fgateway%3Fs%3D801206430%26wpcid%3D255224%26wpsrc%3D1756%26wpcn%3DNFLPlayoffs2023%26wpscn%3DBet5Get200%26wpcrn%3DFP1%26wpscid%3Dxx%26wpcrid%3Dxx&pcid=255224))**
