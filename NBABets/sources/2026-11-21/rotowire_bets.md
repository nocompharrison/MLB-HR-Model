Saturday's six-game NBA slate offers three intriguing player-prop opportunities, with [Victor Wembanyama](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/victor-wembanyama-5809) looking to capitalize on New Orleans' defensive struggles, [Darius Acuff](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/darius-acuff-6931) aiming to maintain his substantial scoring workload against Boston, and [LaMelo Ball](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/lamelo-ball-5151) seeking to rediscover his perimeter shooting touch against Memphis.
## **NBA Best Bets and Predictions Today: Player Props & Picks for Saturday, Nov. 21**
##  **Spurs vs Pelicans:**[**Victor Wembanyama**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/victor-wembanyama-5809)**over 29.5 points, -105 @ DraftKings**
Wembanyama enters Saturday's matchup averaging 28.6 points per game, putting him within striking distance of the proposed 29.5-point threshold. The San Antonio superstar has been particularly dangerous when given an extended offensive workload, scoring at least 30 points in five of his last nine appearances while averaging 30.9 points per game and shooting 57.6 percent from the field in that stretch.
His latest outing was another encouraging sign, as Wembanyama scored 32 points in Thursday's 124-112 victory over the Clippers, converting 13 of 26 field-goal attempts while adding 11 rebounds. He has attempted at least 21 shots in his last three appearances, so the volume to keep producing is clearly there. With the Pelicans allowing a league-worst 123.0 points per game this season, including at least 120 points in four of their last six contests, this is a favorable matchup for the Spurs' star center.
##  **Celtics vs Kings:**[**Darius Acuff**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/darius-acuff-6931)**over 23.5 points, -110 @ FanDuel**
Acuff has quickly become Sacramento's primary offensive option, averaging 25.7 points per game in his rookie campaign. The Kings guard has exceeded Saturday's 23.5-point threshold in four of his last six appearances, including three games of at least 29 points during that stretch. His recent scoring production has been somewhat inconsistent, but his offensive workload remains encouraging. Acuff erupted for 36 points against Phoenix on Wednesday, and he's averaging 21.8 field-goal attempts per game since the beginning of November.
Boston presents a difficult assignment, particularly given its effectiveness defending opposing guards, as well as the fact that the Celtics own the league's best defense with just 103.4 points allowed per game while also posting the second-best Defensive Rating (106.0) entering Saturday's slate. Nevertheless, Acuff has averaged 26.5 points across his last 10 appearances while playing more than 36 minutes per game. His substantial shot volume provides a credible path to reaching 24 points.
##  **Grizzlies vs Timberwolves:**[**LaMelo Ball**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/lamelo-ball-5151)**over 3.5 three-pointers, -120 @ BetMGM**
Ball has been one of Minnesota's most aggressive perimeter shooters this season, connecting on at least four three-pointers in eight of his 16 appearances. The Timberwolves guard has demonstrated considerable upside from beyond the arc, making six triples against Dallas on November 12 and four against Chicago two days later. However, he has failed to hit this line in his last three appearances. His shooting volume remains the strongest argument for Saturday's selection, as Ball has attempted at least eight three-pointers in each of his last five games, including a remarkable 19 attempts against Chicago.
The lack of efficiency could be an issue, as Ball has made just eight of his 27 three-point attempts across his last three appearances. Memphis enters Saturday following a convincing 126-98 victory over Golden State on Thursday, but Ball's offensive role should continue to provide substantial perimeter opportunities. He has made at least four triples in six of his last 11 appearances, and his recent shooting volume suggests another successful over remains attainable even without exceptional efficiency.
