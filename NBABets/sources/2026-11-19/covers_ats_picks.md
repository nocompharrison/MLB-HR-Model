## Expert NBA ATS picks: Nov. 19
### Blazers vs Bucks: ![Blazers](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/por.svg?width=25&height=25) Blazers -9.5
[Trade 53% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Portland Trail Blazers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/portland-trail-blazers/odds) have gone 6-3 on the road this season, and they're up against a [Milwaukee Bucks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/milwaukee-bucks/odds) team that has lost 11 straight. The Bucks are also just 1-7 at home and have allowed 120.9 points per game. That's dangerous against the Blazers, who are averaging 117 points (eighth in the NBA). I'll comfortably take Portland to cover. 
### Warriors vs Grizzlies: ![Grizzlies](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/mem.svg?width=25&height=25) Grizzlies -7.5
[Trade 51% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Memphis Grizzlies](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/memphis-grizzlies/odds) are scoring the second-most PPG in the Association, while ranking first in 3-point FG% and FT%. The [Golden State Warriors](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/golden-state-warriors/odds), meanwhile, are missing several key players, and they've lost by at least 20 points in their last three games. I have to back the surging Grizzlies. 
### Clippers vs Spurs: ![Clippers](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/lac.svg?width=25&height=25) Clippers +15.5
[Trade 50% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [San Antonio Spurs](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/san-antonio-spurs/odds) have been outstanding, owning the NBA's best net rating (+11.1) and allowing just 105.9 PPG. However, laying 15.5 points against the [Los Angeles Clippers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/la-clippers/odds) is a massive ask, even with L.A. struggling. San Antonio is coming off a 12-point loss to Detroit, and the Clippers stay within 16 points. 
### Knicks vs Timberwolves: ![Timberwolves](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/min.svg?width=25&height=25) Timberwolves +1.5
[Trade 49% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
This is one of my favorite picks of the night. The [Minnesota Timberwolves](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/minnesota-timberwolves/odds) are 7-2 at home, and they've continued to play quality basketball without [Anthony Edwards](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/187698/anthony-edwards). Minny just secured back-to-back wins over a solid Blazers team, and the [New York Knicks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/new-york-knicks/odds) have gone just 4-3 on the road this season. With a superior net rating, I expect the Wolves to cover.
