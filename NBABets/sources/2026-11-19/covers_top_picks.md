## Clippers vs. Spurs pick: Clippers +15.5
  * Time: 8 p.m. ET
  * TV: KTLA, DAZN-San Antonio
  * Analyst: [Quinn Allen](https://www.covers.com/writers/quinn-allen)


**Top pick:[Clippers +15.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Quinn Allen is backing Los Angeles to keep this game within striking distance against a San Antonio team laying 15.5 points at home. The Spurs have been tremendous with a 12-3 record, but they've covered just six of 15 games and have won by at least 16 points only six times.
San Antonio is also coming off a 105-93 loss to Detroit and will be without Dylan Harper, who averages 18.4 points per game. The Clippers have been inconsistent but still rank eighth in effective field-goal percentage (54.9%) and have covered in seven of 13 games. Los Angeles also ranks second in the NBA in 3-point percentage at 39%, giving it another way to stay competitive against a Spurs team allowing opponents to shoot 37.4% from deep. 
_Read Allen's full[Clippers vs. Spurs predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/clippers-vs-spurs-prediction-picks-nov-19-2026)._
## Knicks vs. Timberwolves pick: Timberwolves moneyline
  * Time: 8 p.m. ET
  * TV: NBA TV
  * Analyst: [Shawn Krest](https://www.covers.com/writers/shawn-krest)


**Top pick:[Timberwolves moneyline](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Shawn Krest sees value in backing Minnesota to pull off the home upset against the reigning champion Knicks. The Timberwolves have continued to win without Anthony Edwards, losing just twice in their last 10 games since the star suffered an injury. Minnesota has also beaten playoff-caliber opponents, including Portland twice and Houston.
New York's reputation as the defending champion may be influencing its status as a road favorite, but the Knicks have struggled against winning teams. Their last seven victories have come against opponents with losing records, and they're just 1-4 against teams above .500. Minnesota has put together a better record despite facing a tougher schedule, going 8-2 straight up and against the spread since Edwards went down. Krest would back the Timberwolves on the moneyline at odds of up to 53%.
_Read Krest's full[Knicks vs. Timberwolves predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/knicks-vs-timberwolves-prediction-picks-nov-19-2026)._
## Trail Blazers vs. Bucks pick: Trail Blazers -9.5
  * Time: 8 p.m. ET
  * TV: NBA League Pass
  * Analyst: [Todd Cordell](https://www.covers.com/writers/todd-cordell)


**Top pick:[Trail Blazers -9.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Todd Cordell is laying the points with Portland, which has been one of the NBA's best teams at both ends of the floor. The Trail Blazers rank inside the Top 8 in both offensive and defensive rating, while Milwaukee sits 29th in each category.
Both teams play at a fast pace, ranking inside the league's Top 5 in possessions per game. That should create more opportunities for Portland's talent advantage to shine through. The Trail Blazers have also handled inferior competition, winning four of five games against teams outside the play-in picture by double digits. Milwaukee is coming off a game Wednesday night, giving Portland another advantage with fresher legs. 
_Read Cordell's full[Trail Blazers vs. Bucks predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/trail-blazers-vs-bucks-prediction-picks-nov-19-2026)._
## Warriors vs. Grizzlies pick: Grizzlies -6.5
  * Time: 8 p.m. ET
  * TV: DAZN-MEM
  * Analyst: [Chris Walder](https://www.covers.com/writers/chris-walder)


**Top pick:[Grizzlies -6.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Chris Walder is backing Memphis to cover as a 6.5-point favorite against a Golden State team dealing with injuries and fatigue. The Grizzlies have won five straight games, including four by at least 16 points, while the Warriors have dropped four consecutive contests by an average of 22 points.
Golden State has struggled offensively without Stephen Curry, ranking last in offensive efficiency and averaging just 92.9 points per 100 possessions since the star went down. Memphis, meanwhile, has averaged 132.3 points per 100 possessions in three games without its leading scorer, with five players averaging at least 16 points. The Grizzlies are also generating 35.3 assists per game during that stretch, leading the NBA.
_Read Walder's full[Warriors vs. Grizzlies predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/warriors-vs-grizzlies-prediction-picks-nov-19-2026)._
