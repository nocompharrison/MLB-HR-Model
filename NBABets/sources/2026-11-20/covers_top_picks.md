## Hornets vs Hawks pick: Hawks -6.5
  * Time: 7 p.m. ET
  * TV: DAZN-Charlotte, Peachtree Sports Network
  * Analyst: [Quinn Allen](https://www.covers.com/writers/quinn-allen)


**Top pick:[Hawks -6.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
The Hawks have won seven of their last 10 games, including an impressive victory over the Thunder on Wednesday. Charlotte is 4-10 with the worst offense in the NBA and just 1-6 on the road, and Atlanta already dismantled it 128-81 on Oct. 24, so Quinn Allen is confidently backing the Hawks up to 58% in his [Hornets vs. Hawks predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/hornets-vs-hawks-prediction-picks-nov-20-2026).
## Pacers vs Cavaliers pick: Cavaliers -5.5
  * Time: 7:30 p.m. ET
  * TV: DAZN-Indiana, DAZN-Cleveland
  * Analyst: [Ed Scimia](https://www.covers.com/writers/ed-scimia)


**Top pick:[Cavaliers -5.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Cleveland has scored over 120 points in each of its last four games and ranks fifth in the NBA in offensive rating (117.9). Indiana is allowing 114.8 points per game, has given up an average of 125 over its last two losses and ranks 22nd in offensive efficiency, so Ed Scimia is taking the Cavs up to -7.5 in his [Pacers vs. Cavaliers predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/pacers-vs-cavaliers-prediction-picks-nov-20-2026).
## Raptors vs Pistons pick: Pistons -7.5
  * Time: 7:30 p.m. ET
  * TV: Prime Video
  * Analyst: [Chris Walder](https://www.covers.com/writers/chris-walder)


**Top pick:[Pistons -7.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Seven of Detroit's 10 wins have come by double digits, and Toronto is without Kawhi Leonard while RJ Barrett and Collin Murray-Boyles will now be missing time as well. The Pistons are scoring 117.8 points per 100 possessions (10th) over their last five outings and get Cade Cunningham back, so Chris Walder loves them up to -9.5 in his [Raptors vs. Pistons predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/raptors-vs-pistons-prediction-picks-nov-20-2026).
## Wizards vs Bulls pick: Matas Buzelis Over 24.5 points + rebounds + assists
  * Time: 8 p.m. ET
  * TV: MNMT, CHSN
  * Analyst: [Todd Cordell](https://www.covers.com/writers/todd-cordell)


**Top pick:[Matas Buzelis Over 24.5 points + rebounds + assists](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Matas Buzelis has played at least 29 minutes with a 20.4% usage rate across four games without Wilson, averaging 27 PRA and clearing 24.5 in three of them. Dating back to last season, he's averaging 28.7 PRA and has registered 25+ in 76.1% of games when playing 28+ minutes with a usage rate of 18% or higher. The Wizards rank 25th in defensive rating and 26th in rebound percentage, and Todd Cordell says to play it to 57% in his [Wizards vs. Bulls predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/wizards-vs-bulls-prediction-picks-nov-20-2026).
## Pelicans vs Thunder pick: Under 229.5
  * Time: 8 p.m. ET
  * TV: GCSEN, KSBI
  * Analyst: [Geoff Clark](https://www.covers.com/writers/geoff-clark)


**Top pick:[Under 229.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
The Pelicans and Thunder have the lowest and second-lowest 3-point attempt rates in the NBA, along with below-average offensive ratings. Oklahoma City has the best defensive field goal percentage at the rim and runs the third-most half-court offense, which should keep possessions down, and Geoff Clark likes the Under down to 226.5 in his [Pelicans vs. Thunder predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/pelicans-vs-thunder-prediction-picks-nov-20-2026).
## Kings vs Lakers pick: Kings +14.5
  * Time: 10 p.m. ET
  * TV: NBCS-California, Spectrum SportsNet
  * Analyst: [Geoff Clark](https://www.covers.com/writers/geoff-clark)


**Top pick:[Kings +14.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
The Lakers are 1-3 against the spread as 7-point favorites or greater, and Geoff Clark thinks this number is too big for a team that leans heavily on Luka Doncic and plays mediocre defense. Sacramento lost 116-103 to Los Angeles earlier this month and missed covering by half a point, and with Austin Reaves out for a fourth straight game, Clark would take the Kings down to +11.5 in his [Kings vs. Lakers predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/kings-vs-lakers-prediction-picks-nov-20-2026).
## Rockets vs Nuggets pick: Nuggets -1.5
  * Time: 10 p.m. ET
  * TV: Prime Video
  * Analyst: [Shawn Krest](https://www.covers.com/writers/shawn-krest)


**Top pick:[Nuggets -1.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Denver is at home and the 1.5-point spread seems slim, with Shawn Krest sticking with the Nuggets up to -2.5. Houston could be dangerously shorthanded up front, with Alperen Sengun (eye injury) and Steven Adams (quad) both questionable, leaving Oscar Tshiebwe as the only healthy big to match up with Nikola Jokic. Krest breaks it down in his [Rockets vs. Nuggets predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/rockets-vs-nuggets-prediction-picks-nov-20-2026).
