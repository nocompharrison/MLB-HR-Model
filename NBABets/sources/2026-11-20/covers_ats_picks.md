The Raps are struggling with six straight defeats, and [Kawhi Leonard](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/5517/kawhi-leonard) and [RJ Barrett](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/126726/rj-barrett) are both out. Take the hosts.

While the [Orlando Magic](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/orlando-magic/odds) haven't been much better, they've won four of five, and own an offensive edge lately.

Cleveland is scoring the fourth-most points per game in the league, and the [Indiana Pacers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/indiana-pacers/odds) remain depleted.
[Tyrese Haliburton](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/127634/tyrese-haliburton) is still out, and [Andrew Nembhard](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/126991/andrew-nembhard) is questionable. They've also lost two straight and scored only 104 points on Wednesday vs. Houston. 
### Wizards vs Bulls: ![Wizards](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/was.svg?width=25&height=25) Wizards +2.5
[Trade 49% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
Neither the [Washington Wizards](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/washington-wizards/odds) nor the [Chicago Bulls](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/chicago-bulls/odds) have impressed this season, but I don't see much separating these teams.
Washington is coming off a much-needed victory over Toronto, while Chicago owns a dreadful 1-4 home record. Both squads have struggled defensively, but the Wizards have a slightly better net rating. I like them as an ATS underdog. 
### Pelicans vs Thunder: ![Thunder](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/okc.svg?width=25&height=25) Thunder -11.5
[Trade 50% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The clear mismatch here is defense. The [New Orleans Pelicans](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/new-orleans-pelicans/odds) are 26th in defensive rating, while the [Oklahoma City Thunder](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/oklahoma-city-thunder/odds) rank fourth.
Even with numerous injuries, OKC can run away with this game. They're also 5-1 at home, while the Pels own a 1-4 road record. 
### Suns vs Jazz: ![Suns](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/pho.svg?width=25&height=25) Suns -4.5 
[Trade 50% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
Both teams have won two straight, with the [Utah Jazz](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/utah-jazz/odds) showing some life lately. However, they're still missing key frontcourt pieces, most notably [Lauri Markkanen](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/425/lauri-markkanen).
The [Phoenix Suns](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/phoenix-suns/odds) have won six of their last 10, and the Jazz are just 1-7 at home. I'll back the Suns. 
### Kings vs Lakers: ![Lakers](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/lal.svg?width=25&height=25) Lakers -14.5
[Trade 49% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
This is a massive spread, but the [Sacramento Kings](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/sacramento-kings/odds) have been horrendous. They've lost the last four games by 22, 13, 21, and 21 points. The [Los Angeles Lakers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/los-angeles-lakers/odds) are without [Austin Reaves](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/4139/austin-reaves), but they still have one of the best offenses in the Association, and the Kings have a league-worst -13.9 net rating.
L.A. will secure a convincing victory. 
### Rockets vs Nuggets: ![Rockets](https://img.covers.com/covers/data/svg_logos/nba/hou.svg?width=25&height=25)Rockets +1.5
[Trade 50% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Houston Rockets](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/houston-rockets/odds) have been one of the NBA's most balanced teams, posting a 12-4 record and an impressive +9.0 net rating. The [Denver Nuggets](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/denver-nuggets/odds), meanwhile, are just 3-3 at home and own a +0.7 net rating. 
As long as [Alperen Sengun](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/233468/alperen-sengun) plays, I like Houston's chances of keeping this close and potentially stealing a victory. If he's out, Denver is my pick.
