The [Philadelphia 76ers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/philadelphia-76ers/odds) are looking to bounce back after Monday's disappointing loss to Portland. Philly averaged 128.4 points during its previous five-game winning streak and has gone 7-3 at home. With such a small spread and Giannis Antetokounmpo questionable, [I'll back the Sixers](https://www.covers.com/nba/heat-vs-76ers-prediction-picks-nov-25-2026) to handle the [Miami Heat](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/miami-heat/odds).

[Trade 53% at Polymarket](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Charlotte Hornets](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/charlotte-hornets/odds) have struggled, but the [Chicago Bulls](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/chicago-bulls/odds) are missing two key scorers, Norman Powell and Caleb Wilson, who combine to average nearly 40 points per game. With Chicago's offense severely depleted, I'll take Charlotte to cover this manageable spread at home.
### Timberwolves vs. Thunder: Timberwolves +1.5
[Trade 50% at Polymarket](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)

They've won two straight and just put up 142 points vs. Brooklyn. The [Oklahoma City Thunder](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/oklahoma-city-thunder/odds) are definitely a step up in competition from the Nets, but OKC is severely depleted and just lost to the Pacers. I'll back Minny as an ATS underdog. 
### Cavaliers vs. Knicks: Cavaliers +2.5
[Trade 49% at Polymarket](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
I like the [Cleveland Cavaliers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/cleveland-cavaliers/odds) as an ATS underdog tonight. The [New York Knicks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/new-york-knicks/odds) are without OG Anunoby, and Karl-Anthony Towns is questionable. That's two big absences, and the Cavs already beat New York by eight points earlier this month. 
### Nets vs. Bucks: Bucks -1.5
[Trade 49% at Polymarket](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
Both teams are a disaster, with just five combined wins. But the [Milwaukee Bucks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/milwaukee-bucks/odds) at least enter with some momentum after beating the Raptors, and their bench has given them tremendous production. I expect Milwaukee to cover this small spread against a [Brooklyn Nets](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/brooklyn-nets/odds) team that has lost five straight. 
### Clippers vs. Grizzlies: Grizzlies -6.5
[Trade 50% at Polymarket](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Memphis Grizzlies](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/memphis-grizzlies/odds) have [the league's second-best offense](https://www.covers.com/nba/clippers-vs-grizzlies-prediction-picks-nov-25-2026), and they face the 25th-ranked defense tonight. While Memphis is without Cameron Boozer and Jerami Grant, it has enough firepower to take down the [Los Angeles Clippers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/la-clippers/odds), who have dropped five in a row. 
### Suns vs. Rockets: Suns +9.5
[Trade 52% at Polymarket](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [Phoenix Suns](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/phoenix-suns/odds) are playing quality basketball, winning three straight games and four consecutive road contests. While the [Houston Rockets](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/houston-rockets/odds) have been dominant at home, this spread is generous against a Phoenix team finding its offensive rhythm. I'll back the ATS underdogs. 
### Spurs vs. Trail Blazers: Spurs -2.5
[Trade 52% at Polymarket](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
This is an injury-dependent pick. The [San Antonio Spurs](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/san-antonio-spurs/odds) have won eight straight on the road, but Victor Wembanyama is questionable, while Dylan Harper is out. If Wemby suits up, San Antonio should win and cover, but [if he's sidelined](https://www.covers.com/nba/moneyline-predictions-picks-nov-25-2026), I like the [Portland Trail Blazers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/portland-trail-blazers/odds). 
### Kings vs. Warriors: Warriors -6.5
[Trade 48% at Polymarket](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
Even though the [Golden State Warriors](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/golden-state-warriors/odds) are very shorthanded, they just took down the Celtics and have an elite 7-1 home record this season. The [Sacramento Kings](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/sacramento-kings/odds) are 2-15 and remain one of the worst teams in the Association. I'll back the Dubs.
