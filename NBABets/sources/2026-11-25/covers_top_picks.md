## Heat vs 76ers pick: Bam Adebayo Over 19.5 points
  * Time: 5 p.m. ET
  * TV: ESPN
  * Analyst: [Quinn Allen](https://www.covers.com/writers/quinn-allen)


**Top pick:[Bam Adebayo Over 19.5 points](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
**Play up to 54%.**
[Bam Adebayo](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/644/bam-adebayo) has been balling out, and **Joel Embiid's absence softens this matchup for the big man** , who should impose his will inside.
The center has cleared his points line in back-to-back appearances, **scoring 24 against Oklahoma City and 26 against Milwaukee.** He's also attempted 22 free throws during that span, and Philadelphia ranks 16th in free throws allowed. Bam could have another healthy share of opportunities at the charity stripe.
The Sixers are also allowing 19.8 points per game to opposing centers. With Adebayo playing some of his best basketball and Philadelphia missing its top big man, I expect another productive offensive evening.

## Cavaliers vs Knicks pick: Cavaliers +3.5
  * Time: 7:30 p.m. ET
  * TV: MSG, DAZN
  * Analyst: [Chris Walder](https://www.covers.com/writers/chris-walder)


**Top pick:[Cavaliers +3.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
**I'm backing the[Cleveland Cavaliers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/cleveland-cavaliers) to cover, down to +2.5.** They can realistically advance to the knockout round, meaning wins and point differential still matter. The [New York Knicks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/new-york-knicks), meanwhile, need a lot of help to get through.
Cleveland already owns a road victory over the Knicks, winning by eight on Nov. 11. OG Anunoby will miss his third straight game, and he's an elite perimeter defender who held players he personally guarded to 4-for-11 shooting that day, albeit in defeat.
**The Knicks rank last in pace at 94.98** , so the Cavaliers (100.25, eighth) will look to get them out of their comfort zone by getting up and down the floor, especially with smaller lineups. More possessions equate to more scoring chances and a better opportunity to cover.
New York is winning, but barely, and its latest wins came with opposing stars Anthony Edwards, Kawhi Leonard, and Tyrese Haliburton sitting, which makes those victories lose some of their luster.
**The Cavaliers rank seventh in offense over their last six games without Evan Mobley** , and I believe they'll generate enough points (121.8 over their last five games, fifth) to stay within arm's reach.

## Timberwolves vs Thunder pick: Timberwolves moneyline
  * Time: 7:30 p.m. ET
  * TV: ESPN
  * Analyst: [Chris Walder](https://www.covers.com/writers/chris-walder)


**Top pick:[Timberwolves moneyline (47%)](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
**I really love the[Minnesota Timberwolves](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/minnesota-timberwolves) tonight at 47%, or any number below 50%**, as they're 3-3 straight up this season when entering a matchup as an underdog, with five covers in six tries. Of those three defeats, two came by three points or fewer.
Without Anthony Edwards, Minnesota ranks third in both net rating (8.7) and defensive rating (105.5). At 10-4 without Edwards, the Timberwolves look undervalued against an [Oklahoma City Thunder](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/oklahoma-city-thunder) team that's 6-1 at home but has beaten just two teams with winning records.
Everything the Thunder does runs through [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/5018/shai-gilgeous-alexander) (35.8 points over his last eight games), while **Minnesota has had six different players lead the team in scoring** during a 14-game stretch.
**Oklahoma City is a .500 team (4-4) when the reigning two-time MVP scores 32 points or fewer** , and no player has scored that much against the Timberwolves this month.
