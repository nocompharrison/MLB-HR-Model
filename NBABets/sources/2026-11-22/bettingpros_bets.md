While the Celtics are playing the second half of a back-to-back, Tatum is expected to return to the lineup. Boston also ranks second in the NBA in defensive rating, so they should be able to shut down the Warriors.

The Warriors (8-8) have lost four straight games, including three by double digits. Golden State has struggled without Steph Curry, who’s sidelined with an oblique injury. Curry’s absence has taken away the focal point of the Warriors’ offense, and things won’t get any easier against one of the league’s best defensive teams. Golden State is back home after a difficult road trip, but it’s hard to trust this team without its best player.

I know the Celtics are on the second night of a back-to-back, but I actually like this spot for Boston.
