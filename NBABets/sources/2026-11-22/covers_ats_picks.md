### Pistons vs Hawks: Hawks +1.5

That's enough to take the Hawks to win (and cover) at home against the [Detroit Pistons](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/detroit-pistons).

The [Miami Heat](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/miami-heat) have won six straight and should roll at home tonight.
### Magic vs Wizards: Magic -4.5
[Trade 53% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)

Orlando has won five of six and should cover in [Washington](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/washington-wizards) tonight.
### Mavericks vs Jazz: Mavericks -1.5

Utah is 28th in offensive rating (107.7) and won't keep up with Dallas tonight.
### Nuggets vs Bulls: Bulls +9.5
[Trade 49% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
With [Nikola Jokic](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/1850/nikola-jokic) looking downright human this season, I can't justify the [Nuggets](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/denver-nuggets) laying this many points on the road. The [Chicago Bulls](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/chicago-bulls) have won three of five and have allowed just 110.8 ppg over that span, which will keep them close enough to cover.
### Pacers vs Knicks: Pacers +8.5
[Trade 51% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
The [New York Knicks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/new-york-knicks) have won four straight, but they're in the second game of a back-to-back and are playing a low-scoring, defensive style that doesn't lend itself to covering big spreads. I like the [Indiana Pacers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/indiana-pacers) — who beat the Knicks outright a week ago — to cover in New York.
### Celtics vs Warriors: Celtics -8.5
[Trade 50% at Polymarket→](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)
Both teams are struggling, but without [Stephen Curry](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/2001/stephen-curry) on the court, the [Golden State Warriors](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/golden-state-warriors) have lost five straight and don't have what it takes to compete with the NBA's elite. The [Boston Celtics](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/boston-celtics) will break their own three-game losing streak and cover tonight.
