## Thunder vs Heat pick: Heat -3.5
  * Time: 6 p.m. ET
  * TV: KSBI, Thunder App
  * Analyst: [Tom Oldfield](https://www.covers.com/writers/tom-oldfield)


**Top pick:[Heat -3.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Tom Oldfield backs the [Miami Heat](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/miami-heat) to cover against the [Oklahoma City Thunder](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/oklahoma-city-thunder), with Miami averaging 133.8 points during its current homestand.
The Heat have won six straight and covered three of their last four games, while Oklahoma City has gone 1-5 against the spread over its past six. 
[Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/5018/shai-gilgeous-alexander)'s questionable status adds uncertainty for the Thunder's shorthanded offense, helping explain Oldfield's Heat -3.5 selection in his [Thunder vs. Heat predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/thunder-vs-heat-prediction-picks-nov-22-2026).
## Magic vs Wizards pick: Under 210.5
  * Time: 7 p.m. ET
  * TV: WRQD-IND, MNMT
  * Analyst: [Ed Scimia](https://www.covers.com/writers/ed-scimia)


**Top pick:[Magic -4.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Ed Scimia backs the [Orlando Magic](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/orlando-magic) to cover after winning five of their last six games despite [Paolo Banchero](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/238533/paolo-banchero)'s absence. [Franz Wagner](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/188039/franz-wagner) has led that improvement, averaging 31.3 points per game over his last seven outings.
The [Washington Wizards](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/washington-wizards) have won two straight following an eight-game losing streak, but they rank 27th in true shooting percentage (56.6%) and last in defensive efficiency (119.6).
Scimia sees Orlando's recent success as more sustainable and would back the Magic at up to -5.5, as he explains in his [Magic vs. Wizards predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/magic-vs-wizards-prediction-picks-nov-22-2026).
## Pacers vs Knicks pick: Knicks -9.5
  * Time: 8 p.m. ET
  * TV: DAZN-Indiana, MSG
  * Analyst: [Chris Walder](https://www.covers.com/writers/chris-walder)


**Top pick:[Knicks -9.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
Chris Walder backs the [New York Knicks](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/new-york-knicks) against an [Indiana Pacers](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/indiana-pacers) team missing [Tyrese Haliburton](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/127634/tyrese-haliburton) and [T.J. McConnell](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/1726/t-j-mcconnell), with [Pascal Siakam](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/1764/pascal-siakam) also questionable.
Since their Nov. 13 meeting, New York has won four straight while Indiana has lost three in a row by an average of 10.3 points.
The Knicks have also beaten losing teams by an average of 25 points in their past three home games against such opponents, supporting Walder's spread selection in his [Pacers vs. Knicks predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/pacers-vs-knicks-prediction-picks-nov-22-2026).
## Celtics vs Warriors pick: Under 210.5
  * Time: 8:30 p.m. ET
  * TV: NBA League Pass
  * Analyst: [Zak Hanshew](https://www.covers.com/writers/zak-hanshew)


**Top pick:[Under 210.5](https://www.covers.com/betting/sportsbookredirect?sportsbookId=403&slug=polymarket&location=editorial)**
The [Golden State Warriors](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/golden-state-warriors) have averaged an NBA-low 95.8 points over their last five games, while the [Boston Celtics](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/teams/main/boston-celtics) have scored 106.2 per game during the same stretch.
Both teams rank among the seven slowest over that span, and Golden State's offensive rating drops from 119.5 with [Stephen Curry](https://www.covers.com/sport/basketball/nba/players/2001/stephen-curry) to 105.4 without him.
With Curry out and Boston playing on consecutive nights, Zak Hanshew backs Under 210.5 in his [Celtics vs. Warriors predictions](https://www.covers.com/nba/celtics-vs-warriors-prediction-picks-nov-22-2026).
