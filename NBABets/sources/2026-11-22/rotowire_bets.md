## NBA Best Bets and Predictions Today: Player Props & Picks for Sunday, Nov. 22
##  **Detroit Pistons vs. Atlanta Hawks:**[**Cade Cunningham**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/cade-cunningham-5336)**over 25.5 points, -121 @ DraftKings**
Cunningham enters Sunday's matchup averaging 25.6 points per game, putting his season-long production almost exactly in line with this proposed 25.5-point threshold. The Detroit superstar has exceeded that mark in seven of his 14 appearances, including three of his last four outings, while averaging an impressive 33.8 points during that four-game stretch. The volume bodes well for Cunningham's chances of hitting this line as well, as he's attempted at least 20 shots in his last four games and in six of the last eight.
Cunningham has a probable tag for Sunday's game, but he's widely expected to suit up and be the focal point of an offense missing [Duncan Robinson](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/duncan-robinson-4470) (quadriceps) and [Jalen Duren](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/jalen-duren-5703) (hamstring). His recent scoring surge has featured several outstanding performances. Cunningham erupted for 52 points against Washington on November 12, followed by 33 against Miami and 27 against San Antonio. Atlanta enters Sunday allowing approximately 114.6 points per game, ranking in the bottom half of the league in that category. Cunningham has also averaged 28.3 points on the road this season, providing another encouraging trend ahead of this matchup.

##  **Orlando Magic vs. Washington Wizards:**[**Wendell Carter**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/wendell-carter-4407)**under 8.5 rebounds, -114 @ FanDuel**
Carter has been Orlando's leading rebounder this season, averaging 8.7 boards per game. Although that figure sits just above Sunday's 8.5-rebound threshold, recent results suggest it wouldn't be shocking if he stays below that mark again. Carter has failed to grab more than eight rebounds in each of his last five appearances, and he hasn't surpassed this line since he posted back-to-back 11-rebound outings against the Warriors and Kings on Nov. 7 and Nov. 10, respectively.
Washington enters the matchup with a 6-11 record, and while Carter's responsibilities around the basket should create opportunities to capitalize on missed shots, this is a Wizards team trending in the right direction after back-to-back wins and boasting a massive frontcourt pairing in [Alex Sarr](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/alex-sarr-6240) and [Anthony Davis](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/anthony-davis-3297). The difficult matchup and the recent trends are enough to believe Carter will fail to reach this threshold for a sixth consecutive contest.

##  **Denver Nuggets vs. Chicago Bulls:**[**Nikola Jokic**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/nikola-jokic-3612)**over 47.5 points + rebounds + assists, -119 @ Betr**
Jokic continues to deliver outstanding all-around production for Denver, averaging 24.2 points, 10.4 rebounds and 9.1 assists per game. Those numbers translate to 43.7 combined points, rebounds and assists, and while that's below the proposed PRA mark for this game, the favorable matchup should work in his favor. The Nuggets will take on a Bulls team that ranks fifth-worst in Defensive Rating (118.2) and dead last in points allowed per game (122.8) entering Sunday's action.
Furthermore, the three-time MVP has shown he can exceed that 47.5 P+R+A mark through different statistical combinations. Jokic finished with 50 PRA against Phoenix on Nov. 16, but that's the only time he's hit this mark in his last five appearances. While it would be easy to discard Jokic at first glance, his impact is evident, and he should have enough touches to make a difference. Jokic has played at least 37 minutes in each of his last five appearances, averaging 26.2 points, 9.8 rebounds and 9.0 assists during that stretch. His combined average of 45.0 PRA remains below the proposed line, but it also demonstrates that a modest improvement in any category could be enough to clear it.
