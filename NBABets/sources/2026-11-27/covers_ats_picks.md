Despite being without Cade Cunningham and Duncan Robinson, this Pistons team is deep enough to secure a convincing win.

The Raps have been very inconsistent and have failed to cover this number in eight straight. The Nets also just beat the Bucks.

There's only a 6.9-point gap in net ratings, and Atlanta has won four in a row. This is not a team I expect to lose by double digits tonight.

He's questionable due to an ankle injury.
His availability is key for this pick, but even if Ball does suit up, I like a well-rounded Grizzlies team in this underdog spot.

The hosts just lost to the Rockets by 12, and Dallas is in better form.

Those are two big absences, and LA's high-octane offense can give San Antonio's solid defense a run for their money.
