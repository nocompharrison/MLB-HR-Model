## **NBA DFS Core Plays**
**[Immanuel Quickley](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002550/Immanuel+Quickley), TOR - PG**
  * **DraftKings: $6.2K**
  * **FanDuel: $6.4K**


In the early wave of games, Quickley stands out as a great anchor for lineups at only a little over $6K as the Raptors visit the Wizards in Washington. The Wizards have been one of the best point guard matchups to attack over the last several years, and they gave up a big game to [Tyler Herro](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002284/Tyler+Herro) even in their season-opening win.
Quickley has proven he can put up big numbers when he's healthy, and he looks ready for a huge season at the helm of the improved Raptors' offense. He had 44.3 FanDuel points and 44.5 DraftKings points in his first game of the season, contributing 23 points and seven assists to his team's win over the Bulls.
With such a favorable matchup, he's an affordable way to start a balanced attack from the early slate, or he can slide in alongside the pay-up stars you decide to build around. From the early wave, my top elite plays to pair with IQ are **[Giannis Antetokounmpo](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000497/Giannis+Antetokounmpo) ($10.2K DK, $11.3K FD) **or **[Paolo Banchero](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002863/Paolo+Banchero) ($9K DK, $8.8K FD)**.
**[Ty Jerome](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002307/Ty+Jerome), MEM - PG/SG on DK, SG on FD**
  * **DraftKings: $7.7K**
  * **FanDuel: $7.5K**


I usually lean into power forwards or centers as the centerpiece of my lineup, but with the current salaries, it's another point guard who has my attention as the core play in the late wave. Jerome had one of the best performances of the early season in his first game of the year.
He dropped 45 points against the Jazz and added excellent non-scoring numbers to finish with 66.75 DraftKings points and 64.4 FanDuel points.
The 29-year-old was impressive last season in Memphis, when he was available but was limited to 15 games. Health has long been a major obstacle for Jerome throughout his career, but he has stepped up when he has been given the opportunity.
The Grizzlies look ready to let him run the show this year, and while he is unlikely to be quite as impressive as he was in Game 1, he does still bring an elite ceiling and plenty of multi-category upside as he faces one of his former teams in Golden State on Friday night in the later wave.

## **NBA DFS Value Plays**
**[Naji Marshall](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002600/Naji+Marshall) - SF**
  * **DraftKings: $4.5K**
  * **FanDuel: $4.9K**


With so many options across both slates, it's hard to narrow it down to just one value play from each wave. In the early games, though, Marshall continues to be a recurring character in my lineups since he is very reliable but also offers a great ceiling.
Marshall had 16 points, six assists, two rebounds, and one steal in his 29 minutes in the opener against the Rockets, producing 29.5 DraftKings points and 28.4 FanDuel points. He played 29 minutes off the bench for Dallas in the opener and should continue to get enough run to be an excellent fantasy value option in that supporting role.
On Friday night, the Mavs are on the road in what should be an up-tempo contest in Indianapolis, and Marshall brings both a high ceiling and a high floor in that contest.
**[Isaiah Jackson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002673/Isaiah+Jackson) - PF/C (C only on DK)**
  * **DraftKings: $4.9K**
  * **FanDuel: $4.4K**

He made the most of his limited time, though, producing 13 points, five rebounds, and one steal for 21 FanDuel points and 20.75 DraftKings points.

[Brook Lopez](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000588/Brook+Lopez) had a big game off the bench against the Kings, but I still think Jackson is the better value play on Friday and the better option long-term for the Clippers and fantasy teams.
