### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Anthony Davis (PF, C – WSH)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anthony-davis.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $8,300**
  * **FanDuel: $10,400**


In his opening performance for the Wizards, Anthony Davis tallied 34 points, 12 rebounds, four assists, two steals and a block against Milwaukee. This is the Anthony Davis we all know can play at a high level.
Now, we don’t have to bet on him playing more than half the games this season; we just need him to perform today. I think he could put up even better numbers at home against the Toronto Raptors.
### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Anthony Edwards (SG – MIN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anthony-edwards.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $9,500**
  * **FanDuel: $9,200**


Anthony Edwards and [LaMelo Ball](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/lamelo-ball.php) will be electric together this season. Edwards recorded 45 points, 10 rebounds, three assists, two steals and a block in their first game together.
I know this isn’t exactly a cheap daily fantasy option. But I fully expect Edwards to be more than worth it. The season just started, and Minnesota’s energy is clearly high. I think Edwards can put up another double-double against Orlando.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[Steph Curry (PG – GSW)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/stephen-curry.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $7,500**
  * **FanDuel: $7,600**


Steph Curry put up 31 fantasy points in the Warriors’ season-opening loss to the Lakers. He made 2-of-7 three-pointers, grabbed two rebounds and recorded two steals against the Lakers. But the season is still young, and I expect Curry to play at a higher level, at least early in the season.
