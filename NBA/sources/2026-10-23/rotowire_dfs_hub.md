The Wizards present a bit more of a challenge on the defensive end, though Barnes has room for improvement after failing to record a steal or block Wednesday despite averaging 1.4 steals and 1.5 blocks per game last season. 
[**Naz Reid**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4806)**, Hornets![CHA](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100CHA.png?v=10) ($6,600) vs. Hawks**

Reid attempted 19 field goals and should continue to take on a heavy workload after finishing with the second-highest usage rate on the team behind [Brandon Miller](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5896) on Wednesday. 
[**Dennis Schroder**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3444)**, Hornets![CHA](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100CHA.png?v=10) ($4,900) vs. Hawks **
