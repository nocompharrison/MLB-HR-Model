## Bulls vs. Bucks
[![Chicago Bulls Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/chi.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/chicago-bulls)
Friday, October 23
4 p.m. ET
NBA League Pass
[![Milwaukee Bucks Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/mil.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks)
Under 239.5, Game to Go to Overtime
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
### Under 239.5
This is a [Bulls](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/chicago-bulls)-centric cap. The [Bucks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks) scored pretty well against the [Wizards](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/washington-wizards) and defended poorly. Their pace was moderate.
Chicago is the interesting side here. There are three things to figure out about their opening-night evisceration by the [Raptors](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/toronto-raptors).
Was that more about how good the Raptors are than how bad the Bulls are?
If the Bulls’ defense isn’t actually as bad as it looked, which was the worst in the league among all teams in their first games, then it’s possible-to-likely they defend well enough to force an under.
If the Bulls’ offense was as bad as it looked, then this probably goes under because they can’t even score on Milwaukee enough.
The outcome where the over hits is that the Bulls’ offense is better than it looked vs. Toronto (a top-end defense, so that’s likely) and their defense is just as bad.
Chicago shot 11% from 3-point range, of course they’ll score better. But they also missed tons of shots at the rim and played at the fastest pace in the league.
I make this line 231, still a high-scoring affair, but enough to bring it to the under.
This could be an up-and-down track meet, but the Bulls have athleticism and size defensively, while the Bucks have smarts and control.
Chicago had the No. 1 turnover rate in the league on Wednesday, which then caused them to try and run back to score because teams get stuck in that loop.
With a normal turnover rate or even just a lower one, their pace slows somewhat. I can’t buy either of these offenses at this number.
**Pick:[Under 239.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3103539634&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**
### Game to Go to Overtime (+1300)
I bet overtimes if the line is within a possession and I project it as such on power rating.
I bet [Hawks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/atlanta-hawks)–[Magic](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/orlando-magic) and we missed despite it being a one-possession game with a minute to go. That’s how it goes.
But I make this one Bulls -0.6. So, I’ll put a small amount on overtime.
**Pick: Under 239.5**

That’s right, I’m picking the [Knicks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/new-york-knicks) to go 0-2.
The Knicks just don’t seem right. This isn’t just about blowing banner night. It’s preseason. It’s the vibes. It’s the disconnect. It’s the sluggishness.

This version of Boston, with more athletes in the frontcourt, will be harder for the Knicks to beat with physicality.

## Timberwolves vs. Magic
[![Minnesota Timberwolves Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2023/08/nba-updated-logo-minnesota-timberwolves.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/minnesota-timberwolves)

Jaden McDaniels Over 15.5 Points
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
### Jaden McDaniels Over 15.5 Points
When I went back and watched the [Timberwolves](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/minnesota-timberwolves)' offense today, the biggest thing that stood out to me is that there is nothing super complicated about it, which is actually a really good thing when you have a talented roster getting used to playing with each other.
If you have their high-level athleticism, skill, and floor spacing, you can just naturally manipulate the court with basic but effective ball movement.
But the primary action they are doing a lot right now is having Jaden McDaniels set a screen at half-court for LaMelo Ball, and then immediately slip it. It is very hard for opposing wings to be able to manage that specific switching dynamic.
If Franz Wagner guards him—which I think he will for stretches, even though Franz looked horrible and physically unwell in the opener—or even if it's Tristan da Silva, Orlando just doesn't have a lot of good defensive options to counter what Jaden brings to the table.
McDaniels is very confident right now catching that ball out of the short roll, manipulating into space, and hitting those short jumpers. He can also pump fake from the corner, drive inside, and hit the spot-up three.
I think this Minnesota offense is going to be pretty high-powered, at least for a while, and Jaden McDaniels is going to be a much bigger part of it than people realize.
There is going to be so much defensive attention focused on Anthony Edwards and LaMelo Ball that opposing teams will likely neglect what McDaniels is bringing to the table as a scorer.
McDaniels was dynamite in the preseason, he's going to get a massive share of high-quality opportunities in this specific action. I'll back him to clear 15.5 points against the Magic.

### Hornets +2.5
Atlanta’s win over the Magic was an exciting one, going down to the wire against a divisional opponent. Now they’re on the road again for another one.
The [Hornets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/charlotte-hornets) had a routine win over the [Nets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/brooklyn-nets) to open the season but I liked the way they played.
Atlanta’s defense gave up a lot of holes to Orlando but their offense saved the day.
The challenge with the Hornets is how they win the math game by taking threes and preventing yours, and then their ability to crash the glass with their myriad bigs. They have so many good frontcourt players, and Atlanta has a bit of a size problem down low.
I make the Hornets just one-point 'dogs in this matchup and I think Charlotte continues to surprise people who wrote them off when LaMelo Ball was traded.

Reid had seven assists in the Nets game and operated as the lead ball-handler (?!) a fair amount of the time.
Reid was the centerpiece of the LaMelo Ball trade for Charlotte. GM Jeff Peterson loves him and coach Charles Lee knows inventive ways to get him involved.
This line might be wildly off what his season average winds up being, especially with their injury issues.
