The spread is 8.5 here, so perhaps the Suns, who have been playing a bit better, can actually make a game of this. Because if we get 30 minutes or more from Castle, he'll crush this number.
**[Stephen Curry](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000485/Stephen+Curry) OVER 28.5 Points (51%)**
