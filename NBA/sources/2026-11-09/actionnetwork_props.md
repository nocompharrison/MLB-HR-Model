## Draymond Green Assists Escalator

Draymond Green 7+ Assists (-112) | 8+ Assists (+155) | 10+ Assists (+433)
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
Green hardly shoots these days, but does create for others at a high level.
The Golden State [Warriors](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/golden-state-warriors) forward is among the league's leading assist men. He's averaging 7.8 dimes per game. That's his highest mark since the 2020-2021 season, when he averaged 8.9. Yaxel Lendeborg's arrival has opened up the Dubs' offense.
The New Orleans [Pelicans](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/new-orleans-pelicans)' bottom-five scoring offense is giving up 120.8 [points](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/points) per game. Only five squads are allowing more [assists](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/assists) per game (27.8). That should give Green plenty of opportunities to feast.
Green amassed 24 total assists in his previous two outings, so his Monday outlook is strong. He's dished out 60 helpers on the year, and 32 have gone to Stephen Curry and Lendeborg. That duo's combined point total for Monday is 48, so bettors should expect plenty of collaboration between Green and his team's top-two scorers.

## Miles Bridges Rebounds Ladder

[![Suns Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/875040_Suns.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/phoenix-suns)
Miles Bridges 6+ Rebounds (-159) | 8+ Rebounds (+195) | 10+ Rebounds (+550)
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
Nobody expected Bridges to become the Phoenix [Suns](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/phoenix-suns)' most impactful rebounder in 2026-27, but here we are.
The former Charlotte [Hornets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/charlotte-hornets) wing leads Phoenix with 8.1 boards per contest ahead of Monday's matchup against the San Antonio [Spurs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/san-antonio-spurs). He's shown no signs of slowing down so far, even against elite rebounding teams.
Bridges snared 23 total [rebounds](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/rebounds) against the [Rockets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/houston-rockets) and Orlando [Magic](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/orlando-magic) in his previous two outings. Both are top-10 teams in fewest rebounds allowed per game, but neither could stop Bridges.
Victor Wembanyama can be an unstoppable force on the glass when he's on. However, no other member of the Spurs' three-guard starting lineup is taller than 6'6". Bridges can benefit from their small-ball lineups as a 6'7" power forward who is undersized for his position.
Wemby spends plenty of time on the perimeter on offense, and shoots 5.5 triples per contest. Bridges can help Phoenix's lack of length if he crashes the glass with intent.
