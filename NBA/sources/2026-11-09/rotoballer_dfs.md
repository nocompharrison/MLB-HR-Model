## **NBA DFS Core Plays**
**[Luka Doncic](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001984/Luka+Don%C4%8Di%C4%87), PG - LAL**
  * **DraftKings: $12,000**
  * **FanDuel: $12,400**


You have to verify that Luka Doncic is in the lineup tonight, as he sat out yesterday's game with back spasms. However, the Lakers/Hawks game this evening should be a fantasy point feast that Luka can absolutely take advantage of. The Hawks now rank first in pace factor in the league, and this game has a tight spread and a 238.5 O/U.
Luka is a cheat code this season. He's averaging 40.6 PPG on an insane 40.8% usage rate, and the Lakers are going to continue running their entire offense through him. There is no one remotely capable of slowing him down on the Atlanta, and Doncic has scored over 60 fantasy points in seven straight games.
There are a multitude of top-priced studs on this slate, which means opportunity cost is really high. You're going to have to pay for one of them, and Luka possesses the highest floor/ceiling combo. If Luka happens to sit again, [Austin Reaves](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002720/Austin+Reaves) becomes a must-play (he went ballistic last game without Doncic).
**[Kyshawn George](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003202/Kyshawn+George), SG - WAS**
  * **DraftKings: $6,400**
  * **FanDuel: $6,500**


[Anthony Davis](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000468/Anthony+Davis)' status matters for this play, but not so much that Kyshawn George is fadeable. George is proving that there's more than enough points and usage to go around in the Washington offense so he can thrive as well. George's last four games have posted scores of 40, 40, 57.5, and 38.5 DK points, soaring past his value threshold every time.
Over that span, George is averaging 23.5 PPG and 5.5 APG on a 24.4% usage rate. He's playing massive minutes and isn't slowed down by the presence of the stars and #1 overall picks playing alongside him. This DET/WAS game has the second-highest O/U on the slate, and George is still far too cheap.
