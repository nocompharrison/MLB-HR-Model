## Victor Wembanyama Over 27.5 Points (-120)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Joe Dellera](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/joe-dellera)
The [Spurs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/san-antonio-spurs) face off against the [Pistons](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/detroit-pistons) today, and I’m locking in on Victor Wembanyama in this spot.
Jalen Duren is out again with a hamstring strain, and he’s only played in two of Detroit’s 12 games. That leaves Paul Reed and John Collins to handle the middle, and neither one has an answer for Wemby.
Wembanyama is averaging 28.6 points per game on 53.5% shooting — and with De'Aaron Fox listed as day-to-day with a hip issue, there could be even more usage on the table for him.
Without Duren, bigs have been feasting on this Pistons frontcourt. Anthony Davis dropped 44 on Detroit last Monday and then put up 30 and 15 three nights later, while Alex Sarr also added 30 on 12-of-14 shooting. Giannis Antetokounmpo followed that up with 34 and 14 on Saturday.
The Pistons have allowed 124.2 points per game over their last five contests, and they rank 23rd in defensive rebounding, which doesn't bode well entering a matchup against the best rebounding team in the league.
Wemby dropped 38 points on the Pistons in March, and this version of Detroit is much thinner inside.

## Kevin Huerter Over 12.5 Points (-112)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Andrew O'Connor-Watts](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/andrew-oconnor-watts)
I don't necessarily trust the Pistons to get the job done as a team tonight, but I trust Kevin Huerter to get some stuff done offensively.
With Duncan Robinson ruled out, the bulk of Detroit's vacated wing minutes flow directly to Huerter. While it is a relatively small sample size, we have already seen this same scenario play out three times this season.
In those games without Robinson, Huerter’s production skyrockets; he averages 34.6 minutes, 18.7 points, and 8.3 combined [rebounds](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/rebounds) and assists.
I am specifically attacking the points market here because of how San Antonio structures their defense. The Spurs are perfectly fine with conceding the mid-range as long as they are protecting the paint and limiting three-point attempts — and the mid-range is exactly where Huerter shoots a huge portion of his overall volume.
Huerter operates very well in that intermediate space, and San Antonio’s scheme will practically invite him to take those shots.
Even if Robinson were playing tonight, Huerter’s offensive trajectory has been pointing straight up. Over his first eight games, he was averaging just 20 minutes and 6.5 points per night. Over his last four games, those numbers leaped to 32 minutes and 16.8 points per game.
Throughout the entire month of November, Huerter is averaging 14 points per game and has posted scoring totals of 20, 11, 12, and 24 in his last four outings. My projections have him closer to 13.6 points tonight.
#### **Pick:[Kevin Huerter Over 12.5 Points](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3211919672&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-112)**

## Luka Doncic Over 4.5 Three-Pointers-Made (-125)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Brandon Kravitz](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/brandon-kravitz)
This one seems like an absolute layup. Luka Doncic has knocked down at least five three-pointers in 10 of his 12 games this season, and he has already recorded seven different performances of 40 or more points.
In what universe do we honestly believe the [Hornets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/charlotte-hornets) are going to be the team that figures out how to slow him down? Charlotte currently ranks dead last in the NBA at defending the three-ball, allowing opponents to shoot a blistering 41 percent on all non-corner three-point attempts.
If you dig into the Cleaning the Glass metrics, the only specific area on the perimeter where the Hornets play halfway decent defense is the corner three. Fortunately for us, Luka hardly ever shoots from the corners, so that singular defensive strength is irrelevant to this prop.
To sweeten the setup even further, the Lakers are going to be playing without Austin Reaves tonight. That should result in an even higher usage rate and offensive burden for Doncic, as if he somehow needed an additional boost in that department.
The Hornets are going to surrender the exact above-the-break looks that Luka routinely buries. I expect Doncic to put on another high-volume shooting clinic.
#### **Pick:[Luka Doncic Over 4.5 Three-Pointers-Made](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3211992805&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-125)**
