###  **![BOS](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100BOS.png)**[**Payton Pritchard**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/payton-pritchard-5131)**Over 15.5 Points (-124) at Raptors**
_DraftKings Sportsbook, 4:30 PM ET_
**Kirien Sprecher:** Pritchard has scored in double figures in four straight games, but he's shot just 35.5 percent from the field in six contests since dropping 30 against the Bulls on Oct. 30. Over 11 total games, Pritchard is averaging only 10.5 attempts per game, which is 3.3 fewer than last season, when he averaged 17.0 points per night. The decline in attempts is likely due to [Paul George](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/paul-george-3114)'s resurgence, but the veteran won't be available Wednesday due to an illness after posting a game-high 36 against his former team. George's absence leaves plenty of opportunities for Pritchard, who usually capitalizes when his usage rises. When the sharpshooter has taken at least 12 shots this season (four times), he's averaged 22.0 points per game.
