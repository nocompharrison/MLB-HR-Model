## **Best NBA Bets Today (Monday, Nov. 23)**
###  [**Amen Thompson**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/amen-thompson-5931)**over 22.5 points + rebounds vs. LA Clippers (-105)**
_BetMGM, Noon ET_
**Nick Whalen:** Thompson is putting up 25 PTS+REB per game over his last seven and hit for 28 in just 26 minutes against Denver last time out. While there is some blowout risk with the Rockets sitting as double-digit favorites, both teams have been bottom-five defense over the last week, so I think the Clippers will be able to at least hang around. Los Angeles ranks dead-last in TRB% on the year and is giving up over 45 opponent boards per game over the last five. The Clips are also dead-last in _defensive_ rebound rate, which matters given that Thompson is among the best offensive rebounders in the NBA. His 2.8 OREB per game ranks behind only [Dyson Daniels](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/dyson-daniels-5385) among non-bigs.
