## Keyonte George Assists Ladder

Keyonte George 7+ Assists (-146) | 8+ Assists (+120) | 9+ Assists (+204)
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Keyonte George is well on his way to becoming one of the league's premier playmakers.
The [Jazz](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/utah-jazz) guard is on pace to record a career-high 7.1 assists per game in 2026-27, and his average has risen from 6.3 to 7.5 since October. Injuries have forced him to rely on his young supporting cast, and he's had no trouble setting the table to get his team into a rhythm. Limited self-creation from his teammates has forced him to create for others more often.
George has been especially effective working with Ace Bailey with Lauri Markkanen out. He's dished out 29 of his 120 total assists to the second-year wing, who's become a valuable scoring option. Darryn Peterson (17 assists) is George's second-most successful assist target among active Jazz players ahead of Monday's matchup against the [Lakers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/los-angeles-lakers).
Both can continue to feast on George's passing in this third meeting between Utah and Los Angeles this campaign. George amassed 10 assists in a win over the Lakers just last week.
George is averaging 0.6 more assists per game on the road this season. He's tallied nine or more assists in four of his eight away contests this year, compared with just twice in his nine home appearances.
Getting a second shot at a defense he's already effectively dissected bodes well for his production on Monday.

## Stephon Castle Assists Ladder
[![Spurs Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/sas.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/san-antonio-spurs)

[![Kings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/348219_kings.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/sacramento-kings)
Stephon Castle 7+ Assists (-166) | 8+ Assists (+106) | 9+ Assists (+179)
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Stephon Castle will get his hands on the ball a lot more often in the coming weeks. Dylan Harper is set to miss two to three weeks with a hip injury, and that will make room for other members of the [Spurs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/san-antonio-spurs) backcourt to make plays.
Castle is the top candidate to lead the way. He led the Spurs with 7.4 assists per game in 2025-26 and ranks first with 5.9 per contest this year, despite Harper's move to the starting lineup. Castle's numbers are already on the climb in just two games without Harper this year.
Castle posted 18 total assists across his previous two outings, peaking with a season-high 12 helpers against the [Clippers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/los-angeles-clippers).
San Antonio's emphasis on ball movement has helped it tally an impressive 28.5 team assists per game. That's the league's fourth-highest mark. The Spurs will utilize that strength against the [Kings](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/sacramento-kings)' bottom-five scoring defense, which is forking up 119.1 points per game.
De'Aaron Fox is amid a scoring and playmaking rut, so Castle will likely be the go-to guy in decisive moments. He's averaged 8.8 assists per contest through 12 games without Dylan Harper since last season.
