**Top NBA DFS Plays Today**
DraftKings' main slate for Monday features eight games, the first two of which tip off at 7:00 p.m. ET. [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5400) ($10,200) is the top salary on the board, though he's questionable. [Alperen Sengun](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5404), [Scottie Barnes](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5390) and [Donovan Mitchell](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4166) headline the healthy options, and [Joel Embiid](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3572) is also questionable. The Cavaliers-Bulls and Suns-Pelicans games carry the highest totals. Below, I'll highlight three players who could return strong value Monday.
[Donovan Clingan](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6254), Trail Blazers **(C, $7,300)**   
  
Clingan's rebounds and blocks keep his floor steady. He had 22 points, 13 rebounds, three 3-pointers and four assists for 50.8 DK points at New York on Saturday. Three games earlier, he had five blocks and 10 rebounds against Minnesota. His minutes have ranged from 22 to 32, so he can pay off without a full workload, but with tonight's matchup having one of the highest and tightest totals on the slate, the top end should be expected. [Joel Embiid](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3572) is questionable for Philadelphia, and if he's out Clingan faces a much thinner frontcourt.  
  
[Cedric Coward](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=XXXX), Grizzlies **(SG/SF, $7,200)**  
  
Memphis is short several rotation players, and Coward is a starting wing who keeps getting the touches. He scored 54.5 DK points at Brooklyn and 41.8 at Minnesota on Saturday. The Grizzlies didn't play Sunday, so there's no back-to-back. He's also priced well below the top of the board, which fits a lighter-chalk approach.
[Coby White](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4801), Hornets **(PG/SG, $5,700)**  
  
White has been on a minutes restriction that is slowly easing: 16 minutes early in the month, 20 for a few games, then 26 in each of his last two. With [Grayson Allen](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4120), [Kon Knueppel](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=7000) and [Dennis Schroder](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3319) out, he is Charlotte's lead guard. He scored 33.8 and 32.0 DK points in those two games, and he's still ramping up. If the restriction loosens again at home, $5,700 is a good price.
