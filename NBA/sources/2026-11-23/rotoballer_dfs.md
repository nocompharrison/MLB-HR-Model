**[Donovan Mitchell](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001844/Donovan+Mitchell), SG - CLE**
  * **DraftKings: $8,800**
  * **FanDuel: $8,600**

This game features the highest O/U by a mile at 241.5, so you're going to want multiple pieces. Mitchell experiences a 3.4% usage bump with Mobley out since the beginning of last year. His 24.5 PPG is fourth in the league, and the Bulls have allowed the eighth-most FPPG to opposing guards.
**[Josh Giddey](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002665/Josh+Giddey), PG - CHI**
  * **DraftKings: $7,400**
  * **FanDuel: $6,700**

On a slate like this, there are very few players that have the triple-double upside Giddey does.
This game is fantasy gold, as these two squads are tied for fourth in pace factor, and the O/U is off the charts. The Cavs are not the same defensively this year (22nd in overall team defense), and have allowed the 11th-most FPPG to opposing PGs. Giddey is a high-value playmaker that complements any Cavs (or Bulls) in both cash and GPP.
