## NBA Best Bets and Predictions Today: Player Props & Picks for Sunday, Nov. 15
##  **Houston Rockets vs. Washington Wizards:**[**Trae Young**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/trae-young-4369)**over 7.5 assists, -140 @FanDuel**
Young enters Sunday's matchup averaging 8.8 assists per game and remains one of the NBA's most productive playmakers despite fluctuating performances. The Washington guard has exceeded the 7.5-assist threshold in nine of his 13 appearances this season, an encouraging 69.2 percent success rate.
His latest outing reminded everyone of his ability to generate offense for teammates. Young delivered 14 assists against Detroit on Nov. 12, producing that total against a Pistons defense that ranks among the league's strongest by Defensive Rating (109.2, sixth-best). That performance suggests his playmaking can remain effective even against difficult defensive assignments. Young has also distributed at least seven assists in three of his last five appearances, demonstrating that his passing responsibilities remain substantial regardless of his scoring production.
Houston presents another challenging matchup, but Young's established role as Washington's primary offensive facilitator gives him opportunities. His season-long consistency is the strongest argument for this selection, with eight assists representing a manageable target for a player averaging nearly nine per game.
##  **Atlanta Hawks vs. Milwaukee Bucks:**[**Nickeil Alexander-Walker**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/nickeil-alexander-walker-4765)**over 3.5 three-pointers, -132 @ DraftKings**
Alexander-Walker has emerged as one of Atlanta's most important offensive contributors this season, averaging 21.5 points per game while shooting 35 percent from beyond the arc. His increased scoring responsibilities have translated into substantial perimeter volume, making his three-point production particularly appealing for player-prop bettors.
The Hawks guard has connected on at least four three-pointers in four of his last six appearances, clearing Sunday's 3.5 threshold at an encouraging rate. Although his shooting efficiency has fluctuated, the opportunities have remained remarkably consistent. Since the beginning of November, Alexander-Walker has averaged 12.5 three-point attempts per game while converting 34.7 percent of those shots. At that volume, even his recent shooting percentage would translate to approximately 4.3 made triples per contest.
Friday's loss to Chicago represented a frustrating exception, as Alexander-Walker connected on just one of his 11 attempts from deep. However, that performance also reinforced his willingness to keep shooting despite struggling with his efficiency. Milwaukee presents another opportunity for Alexander-Walker to capitalize on his expanded offensive role. With the Bucks allowing opposing teams to shoot 35.3 percent from deep, Alexander-Walker's volume could be enough to clear this line if he delivers a bounce-back performance.
##  **Los Angeles Lakers vs. Phoenix Suns:**[**Luka Doncic**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/luka-doncic-4396)**over 53.5 points + rebounds + assists, -110 @ BetMGM**
Doncic has been nearly unstoppable throughout the 2026-27 campaign, averaging an extraordinary 38.6 points, 7.5 rebounds and 7.6 assists per game. Those numbers translate to 53.7 combined points, rebounds and assists per contest, putting his season-long production slightly above Sunday's 53.5 PRA threshold.
The Lakers superstar has exceeded that mark in four of his last six appearances, demonstrating his ability to deliver massive statistical performances through a combination of scoring, rebounding and playmaking. However, his recent results have been somewhat less productive, with Doncic finishing below the proposed line in consecutive games.
His latest appearance against Milwaukee on Wednesday represented a relatively modest outing by his remarkable standards. Doncic finished with 32 points, five rebounds and five assists, totaling 42 PRA despite helping Los Angeles secure another victory. With the Suns allowing opposing teams to shoot 46.5 percent from the floor (19th) and currently having the 10th-worst rating in the NBA (minus 3.8), this is a favorable matchup for the star guard.
