[Karl-Anthony Towns posts double-double in loss to Pacers](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/news/620211/karl-anthony-towns-posts-double-double-loss-to-pacers.php)  

Sun, Nov 15th 8:47am EST  
By [Chris Schommer](https://www.fantasypros.com/news/correspondents/chris-schommer.php)
Karl-Anthony Towns posted a double-double for the Knicks Friday night, grabbing 15 rebounds and scoring 23 points (9-19 FG, 3-7 3PT, 2-3 FT) while also dishing out five assists and recording three steals in a 106-104 loss to the Pacers.
**_Fantasy Impact:_** Towns continues an impactful stretch on both ends of the floor, grabbing 11 or more rebounds and scoring 17 or more points in each of his last five games. The center has rewarded fantasy managers with a dynamic interior option as part of a versatile New York starting five, averaging 21.1 points and 13.7 rebounds per game while posting a usage rate of 25.1% so far this season.
