## Anthony Davis Over 24.5 Points (-106)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
Jalen Smith is one of my favorite players in the league. He’s also a terrible matchup for Anthony Davis.
Davis excels when he faces a smaller defender he can bully, and struggles when he has to use skill and range against bigger opponents.
Smith is undersized compared to Davis, and AD has averaged 42.6 [points](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/points) on 72.7 percent True Shooting against Smith over the last three seasons.
Nic Claxton does much better against him, but those minutes will be limited tonight.

## Donovan Mitchell Under 26.5 Points (-110)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
A weird stat: the [Pacers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/indiana-pacers), despite all their problems, are the No. 1 pick-and-roll defense against pick-and-roll ball-handlers.
Donovan Mitchell averages 37 percent of his possessions out of pick-and-roll, by far the most of his offense.
The Pacers play up at the level of the screen more than the average team, which makes the essential read for the ball-handler a pass to either the roll man (which they do a good job defending) or the wings.
The Pacers give up spot-up threes instead of the primary scorer. Mitchell’s the primary scorer. I make this line 23.3.

## Tristan da Silva Over 2.5 Three-Pointers Made (+116)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)

I was hoping to get another game of da Silva lined at 1.5, but the books adjusted. However, it’s a plus-number, which makes it worth it.
