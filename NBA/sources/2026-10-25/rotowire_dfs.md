Doncic's continued shot volume and favorable matchup support backing him to surpass 32.5 points for the first time this season.

## **Los Angeles Clippers![LAC](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100LAC.png) vs. Oklahoma City Thunder ![OKC](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100OKC.png)**
##  [**Shai Gilgeous-Alexander**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/shai-gilgeous-alexander-4387)**Over 38.5 Points + Rebounds + Assists, -110 @ FanDuel**
Gilgeous-Alexander has had mixed results through Oklahoma City's first two games. He opened the 2026-27 season with 32 points, six rebounds, and four assists against the Spurs on Tuesday, then posted "only" 17 points, two rebounds, and three assists against Denver, making it his first game with fewer than 20 points since October 2024. 
His combined average of 32.0 points, rebounds and assists is below Sunday's line, but he should be able to bounce back against a Clippers team that is currently playing at the 11th-fastest pace in the NBA. That should translate into plenty of opportunities for the two-time MVP winner to get involved. Gilgeous-Alexander averaged 31.1 points, 4.3 rebounds and 6.6 assists last season, totaling 42.0 P+R+A per game, and he also surpassed 38.5 combined statistics in two of his last three meetings with the Clippers. The stage is set for a bounce-back effort here.
## **Toronto Raptors![TOR](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100TOR.png) vs. Minnesota Timberwolves ![MIN](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100MIN.png)**
##  [**Anthony Edwards**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/anthony-edwards-5110)**Over 3.5 Three-Pointers Made, +110 @ BetMGM**
Edwards has demonstrated exceptional perimeter volume through Minnesota's first two games, attempting 26 three-pointers and converting 11, an average of 5.5 makes on 13.0 attempts per contest. He opened the campaign by going 9-for-17 against the Heat in a 45-point outburst, but he regressed against the Magic on Friday and went just 2-for-9 from beyond the arc. Still, one of Edwards' biggest developments over the last two seasons has been his three-point shooting, as he shot 39.5 and 39.9 percent from deep in 2024-25 and 2025-26, respectively. 
Edwards averaged 3.4 made three-pointers in the 2025-26 regular season, but his current volume provides a compelling foundation for reaching at least four -- something he achieved in 29 different games in 2025-26 between the regular season and the playoffs. Toronto allowed 12.5 three-pointers per game in 2025-26, while Edwards has already attempted at least nine in both appearances this season. With that shot volume and a favorable matchup, I'm backing Edwards at plus-money this Sunday.
