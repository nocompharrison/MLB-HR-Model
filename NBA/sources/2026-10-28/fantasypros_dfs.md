### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Cade Cunningham (PG – DET)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/cade-cunningham.php) vs. CHA**
  * **DraftKings: $9,900**
  * **FanDuel: $9,800**


We weren’t sure how Detroit would look after a quiet offseason, but Cade Cunningham looks like a legitimate MVP candidate. This former top pick is averaging over 44 DraftKings points per game through the opening week. What makes that even more impressive is that he’s doing that with a 29% usage rate and just 15 shots a night.
We expect that usage to creep above 30% and the shot total to rise to 20, so that could be Cunningham’s floor going forward. Squaring off with Charlotte is sensational too because Cunningham has collected at least 51 DraftKings points in five of his last seven matchups against the Hornets.
#### **[Jalen Johnson (PF – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-johnson-f-f.php) vs. MIA**
  * **DraftKings: $9,500**
  * **FanDuel: $9,400**


This will be a fun matchup between these up-tempo teams, with plenty of stars on display. You might call “superstar” a lofty word for Jalen Johnson, but he’s played like one since last season. Johnson broke out with nearly 50 fantasy points per game last season and has been even better this season.
Johnson has at least 41 DraftKings points in all four games, averaging 21.5 points, 9.8 rebounds, 8.5 assists, 1.8 steals and 0.8 blocks per game. That’s bad news for a struggling Miami team, with the Heat ranked 24th in total defense through the opening week. In their three matchups last season, Johnson averaged 23 points, 12 rebounds and 10.7 assists per game while scoring at least 53 fantasy points in each game.
### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Giannis Antetokounmpo (PF, C – MIA)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/giannis-antetokounmpo.php) vs. ATL**
  * **DraftKings: $10,200**
  * **FanDuel: $11,500**


Miami has struggled to find any rhythm with Giannis Antetokounmpo so far, but he continues to produce from a fantasy perspective. His 33% rate makes him valuable regardless, and he scored at least 60 DraftKings points in two of his last three outings.
A triple-double in his most recent game shows the role Miami wants Antetokounmpo to play, and that’s exactly what we want to see for DFS, since he’s been a top-five fantasy player throughout his career.
The matchup with Atlanta is amazing, with the Hawks ranked 18th in total defense last season. Not to mention, Antetokounmpo has scored at least 53 DraftKings points in six of his last eight matchups with Atlanta. Miami is in must-win territory, and you can bet Antetokounmpo will carry them to a win soon.
#### **[Franz Wagner (SG, SF – ORL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/franz-wagner.php) vs. TOR**
  * **DraftKings: $8,300**
  * **FanDuel: $8,000**


Orlando always seems to be missing one of its stars, and that’s when you want to use healthy Magic players for DFS. They’ve lost two starters this week: [Paolo Banchero](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/paolo-banchero.php) and [Jalen Suggs](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-suggs.php) are both out for multiple weeks. That will force Franz Wagner to take over the offense, who is posting a 41.2% usage rate with Banchero off the floor.
Not even Oklahoma City Thunder [Russell Westbrook](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/russell-westbrook.php) had that type of usage. Wagner is averaging 33.6 points, 14.8 rebounds and 9.4 assists per 100 possessions without Banchero. Granted, that’s only 63 minutes this season, but it’s a ridiculous stat line, and no surprise since we’ve seen similar numbers before. Toronto just became a terrific matchup too because Wagner won’t have to deal with [Kawhi Leonard](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/kawhi-leonard.php) either.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[Paul Reed (C – DET)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/paul-reed.php) vs. CHA**
  * **DraftKings: $5,900**
  * **FanDuel: $6,100**


[Paul Reed](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/paul-reed.php) has always been a per-minute stud throughout his career, and it’s exciting to see him starting at center with [Jalen Duren](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-duren.php) sidelined. He had 66 DraftKings points across 49 minutes over the last two games since that injury and needs to be bumped above $6,000 on both sites.
That doesn’t even include a masterpiece on Wednesday when Reed registered 43 DraftKings points. Over Reed’s 11 starts last season, he averaged 39 DraftKings points in 27 minutes a night. That’s on par with players like [Alperen Sengun](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/alperen-sengun-c.php) and [Anthony Davis](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anthony-davis.php), but he’s priced like [Derik Queen](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/derik-queen.php) and [Rudy Gobert](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/rudy-gobert.php).
That suggests something is off with the pricing, and Chicago is the best DFS matchup right now. The Bulls rank 28th in defensive efficiency and last in points allowed, while playing at the league’s fastest pace.
#### **[Derik Queen (C – NOP)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/derik-queen.php) vs. DEN**
  * **DraftKings: $5,800**
  * **FanDuel: $5,800**


It’s hard to believe that New Orleans is starting [DeAndre Jordan](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/deandre-jordan.php) ahead of Derik Queen with [Yves Missi](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/yves-missi.php) sidelined, but that might keep his roster percentage lower than it should be. Queen, who will play the majority of the center minutes, has dropped at least 29 DraftKings points in all three games this season.
What matters most is that Queen should flirt with 30 minutes with Missi sidelined. In the 35 games Queen played at least 27 minutes last season, he averaged nearly 40 fantasy points.
It would be hard to imagine Queen playing fewer than 27 minutes tonight, and it looks even better since [Nikola Jokic](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nikola-jokic.php) is one of the NBA’s worst defensive centers. That’s clear, since Queen has averaged 40 points in his last three matchups with the Nuggets.

### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[CJ McCollum (PG, SG – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/cj-mccollum.php) vs. MIA**
  * **DraftKings: $5,300**
  * **FanDuel: $5,300**


This $5,300 price tag doesn’t make sense. Sure, CJ McCollum isn’t as good as he used to be, but he’s still the starting point guard for a dynamic offense while averaging over 25 DraftKings points per game. That alone makes him a good value hovering around $5,000, especially since McCollum has been a $7,000 player throughout his career.
We also saw McCollum attempt 16 shots in 30 minutes in Atlanta’s most recent game, which is close to the role we expect for the rest of the season. The only real concern is a possible individual matchup against [Davion Mitchell](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/davion-mitchell.php), but we love that Miami ranks 24th in total defense.
####  [Pelle Larsson (SG, SF – MIA)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/pelle-larsson.php) vs. ATL
  * **DraftKings: $4,300**
  * **FanDuel: $4,300**


It’s shocking that Pelle Larsson remains so affordable. While he’s been coming off Miami’s bench, Larsson has been playing starter minutes. He’s played 34 and 36 minutes in his two most recent outings, scoring 25 and 31 DraftKings points.
Larsson needs only 20-25 fantasy points to be a value at $4,300, and these sites don’t seem to realize how important he is to this shorthanded rotation right now. We actually saw similar things from Larsson at the end of last season. Matching up with the Hawks is far from scary, too, with Larsson averaging over 31 fantasy points per game in their three meetings last season.
#### **[Jay Huff (C – IND)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jay-huff.php) vs. PHI**
  * **DraftKings: $3,800**
  * **FanDuel: $4,200**


Jay Huff and Paul Reed are in the same boat. Both are phenomenal per-minute producers, and both are stepping into starting center roles due to injuries. Huff is starting for [Ivica Zubac](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/ivica-zubac.php), scoring 42 DraftKings points across 44 minutes in the two games since that injury. That’s well below the per-minute numbers we’ve become accustomed to, with Huff averaging 23 DraftKings points per 21 minutes last season.
Huff was also special when given a full allotment of minutes, averaging over 29 fantasy points per game in the 42 outings he played at least 22 minutes. That’s pretty much the floor we expect to see with Zubac out. A player flirting with a 30-point average should be above $5,000 on both sites. Huff also had success against the 76ers last season, averaging nearly 30 DraftKings points per game in their four meetings.
