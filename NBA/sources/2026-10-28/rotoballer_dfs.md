## **NBA DFS Core Plays**
**[Nikola Jokic](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001441/Nikola+Joki%C4%87), C - DEN**
  * **DraftKings: $12,000**
  * **FanDuel: $12,000**


It's surprising to see the Nuggets struggle as much as they are to start the season. They should be able to get back on track here against the Pelicans. Jokic is in one of the best spots on the slate, and if the Pelicans can keep it close, then Jokic should see well over 60 fantasy points. He's already averaging nearly a triple-double and a near-29 % usage rate in the first three games of the season.
The matchup couldn't be any better. Only the Pacers are giving up more fantasy points to opposing centers this season than New Orleans. Centers are averaging just over 30 PPG against the Pelicans this season. A 30-point triple-double would start us off quite nicely, so look to start your lineups with Jokic and then work from there.
**[LaMelo Ball](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002528/LaMelo+Ball), PG - MIN**
  * **DraftKings: $7,700**
  * **FanDuel: $7,900**


The Minnesota Timberwolves were looking like one of the best teams in the league, and now they are down their best player for the next two months. [Anthony Edwards](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002523/Anthony+Edwards) going in for surgery came out of left field last night, but luckily for them, they traded for a great guard in LaMelo Ball earlier this year. Melo has had a solid role this season, but has taken the backseat a bit to Edwards; now it's his time to shine.
Ball should see a big jump in usage and get a solid matchup for his first game without Edwards. They take on the Warriors, who are giving up fantasy points like it's candy to PGs. They have given up the second most fantasy, while giving up the third most assists, most rebounds, and third most points. Take advantage of LaMelo while he is still reasonably priced, because it won't last for long as we see his usage soar.

## **NBA DFS Value Play of the Night**
**[Jared McCain](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003198/Jared+McCain),** **SG - OKC**
  * **FanDuel: $4,300**


The OKC Thunder are injury-riddled right now, and on top of that, Ajay Mitchell is potentially out of this one as well. That really leaves SGA, Jared McCain, and if you really want to get deep, you can take a shot on [Bennett Stirtz](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003726/Bennett+Stirtz).
