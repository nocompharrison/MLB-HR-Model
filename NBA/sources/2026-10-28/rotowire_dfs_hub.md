**Top NBA DFS Plays Today**
We have a four-game slate, and given its manageable size, we'll run down three options who may get overlooked but could be positive difference makers for you tonight. I'll also throw in some extra notes on things to consider when building lineups.
[**Egor Demin**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6559) **(PG, $5100)** - The smaller the slate, the more condensed everyone's player pool is, and most people tonight are likely going to pay up here. Between [Luka Doncic](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4396), [Jalen Brunson](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4405), [Cade Cunningham](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5336), and even the upper mid-range guys like [Damian Lillard](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3304), most people won't be going down to this level at point guard. Demin presents strong upside, though, if the Nets can keep this close. Boston having played last night increases that likelihood, even if it's still unlikely. Either way, if Demin gets 30-35 minutes, he's likely to give us a score we'll be happy to have.
[**Toumani Camara**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5987) **(SF, $5100)** - Camara is the modern NBA's quintessential 3&D player, an archetype that tends not to get much love in DFS. These guys can often thrive in spots where the spotlight on them gets a little bigger, and tonight against the Lakers, potentially matched up often with [Luka Doncic](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4396), this fits the bill. Even if [Jrue Holiday](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3029) ultimately gets more run on Doncic, Camara can still produce enough ancillary stats, and if he gets hot from three, he can put up a sizable score at a position without any obvious answers in the mid-range.
[**Walker Kessler**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5644) **(C, $7300)** - If you're not paying up for [Victor Wembanyama](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5809) here or going down to [Paul Reed](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5124), your options are fairly mixed. [Donovan Clingan](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6254) has similar upside and is cheaper, but he seems limited in minutes. [Domantas Sabonis](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3865) and the Kings are likely to get blown out by the Spurs, the Celtics' centers have limited upside, and most of the other options also have PF eligibility on DraftKings. Kessler fills the spot between the two likely most popular options, and he's capable of putting up a score close to Wembanyama's and well out of reach of where Reed is likely to finish.
A couple of added notes to consider:
  * If you play Demin and go with the concept of Brooklyn keeping it close, one of [Michael Porter](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4375) or [Julius Randle](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3574) will likely put up a great score alongside him. Also, Brooklyn keeping it close may just mean Boston is struggling, so it doesn't necessarily make any of the Celtics better plays, although [Jayson Tatum](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4108) may end up being the lowest-owned top-tier player tonight. If you really want to get different, consider [Moritz Wagner](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4389) at C. [Day'Ron Sharpe](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5418) isn't off to the greatest start, and it wouldn't be shocking to see Wagner come off the bench, hit a couple of triples, and earn an extended run.
  * Put [Mikal Bridges](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4386) in the honorable mention category. I considered writing him up over Camara but suspect Camara will be lower owned. They're basically 1A and 1B for me tonight among mid-range SFs. Bridges is far too talented to be $5K and continue to post these poor fantasy scores.
  * At the time of writing, whether [Ausar Thompson](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5932) and [Isaiah Joe](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5291) will play tonight is still up in the air. Thompson went through shootaround, so I suspect he'll suit up. However, if he doesn't and Joe remains out, [Daniss Jenkins](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6336), [Ronald Holland](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5939), and [Kevin Huerter](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4449) all become interesting cheap guard options.
  * The Spurs are likely to blow out the Kings, but with their massive 125.5 total, there's a good chance multiple Spurs put up strong scores before hitting the bench early if the game plays out as expected. Essentially, don't avoid them out of fear of not getting full run, but going overboard may be equally dicey.
