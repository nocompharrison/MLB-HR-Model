## **Best NBA Bets Today (Wednesday, Oct. 28)**
### **Atlanta Hawks -2.5 vs. Miami Heat (-110)**
_DraftKings, 1pm ET_
**Nick Whalen:** I'll keep on fading the Heat, who have been easily the biggest disappointment in the NBA through the first week of play. Atlanta is just 2-2, but the Hawks are sixth in NET rating, while Miami has both a bottom-five offense and bottom-five defense. The Giannis-Bam fit is a big problem for Miami, and while the Heat are shooting just 30% from three — a number that will rise — Atlanta is shooting only 33%, so I don't necessarily see this as a breakthrough spot for the Heat on the road.

###  [Caleb Wilson](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/caleb-wilson-6926) over 1.5 made threes (-103) vs. New York Knicks
_Kalshi, 4:40 PM ET_
**Alex Barutha** : The Knicks are on the second night of a back-to-back, traveling to Chicago to face the Bulls. That's a good situation for the energetic rookie Wilson to get some good looks. So far, he's been firing away from distance but missing (5-for-28; 17.9%). That will improve soon enough, and he's one of the only trustworthy players on the Bulls right now.
###  [**Jabari Smith**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/jabari-smith-5643)**O15.5 PTS vs. Milwaukee Bucks (+100)**
_DraftKings, 1pm ET_
**Nick Whalen:** The Rockets are down [Alperen Sengun](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/alperen-sengun-5404) and [Steven Adams](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/steven-adams-3445), so this should be a spot where Smith could be set for extended minutes against a 1-3 Bucks team that ranks second in the NBA in pace. Smith is off to a nice start, shooting-wise, and should see more opportunity given the aforementioned absences.
###  [**Tristan da Silva**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/tristan-da-silva-6267)**3+ threes made at Toronto Raptors (+100)**
_DraftKings, 1pm ET_
**Nick Whalen:** Orlando is down [Paolo Banchero](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/paolo-banchero-5700) and [Jalen Suggs](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/jalen-suggs-5379), while [Anthony Black](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/anthony-black-5910) could also sit out, so we're looking at increased opportunity — and likely a move into the starting lineup — for da Silva. Coming into the season, Sean Sweeney said he wanted da Silva taking "10+ threes per game", and while he hasn't quite hit that number, he's launched 26 attempts through three games and gone over this number in two of those contests. Toronto has been an excellent defensive team thus far, but Orlando will need da Silva to keep firing away if the Magic hope to keep pace.
