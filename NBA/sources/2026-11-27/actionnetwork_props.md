## Tyler Herro Points Escalator
[![Bucks Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/mil.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks)

[![Pistons Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/343538_pistons.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/detroit-pistons)
Tyler Herro 20+ Points (-162) | 25+ Points (+166) | 30+ Points (+422)
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Let's see if [Tyler Herro](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/tyler-herro-109948-pg) can keep up his recent hot streak in Friday's [Bucks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks)–[Pistons](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/detroit-pistons) matchup.
He has averaged 36.7 points per game over his previous three outings while shooting 59.3% from the field and 53.8% from long distance. The former [Heat](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/miami-heat) combo guard has also made 8.7 free throws per contest during that stretch.
Now he'll face one of the league's best defenses. Herro has less talent around him in Milwaukee than he did in Miami, and there's a decent chance the Bucks will get blown out on Friday. However, he performed at a high level against what was arguably a better Pistons defense last year. That's when he wasn't a clear-cut first option.
Herro averaged 24.5 points per game in two meetings with Detroit in 2025-26 on 16.5 field-goal attempts per contest. He's hoisted up 19.5 shots per outing during his current hot streak, and 8.7 of those have been three-pointers. That volume is promising against a Pistons team that won't be at full strength.
[Cade Cunningham](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/cade-cunningham-170957-pg) has been ruled out with a quad injury. [Ausar Thompson](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/ausar-thompson-244814-pf) will take on more responsibility than usual. That should free up space for Herro to get off some quality looks. He's up to a career-high 25.6 points per 36 minutes this campaign.

## Damian Lillard Three-pointers Escalator
[![Warriors Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/759063_Warriors.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/golden-state-warriors)

9:30 p.m. ET

[![Trail Blazers Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/312105_Blazers.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/portland-trail-blazers)
Damian Lillard 3+ Three-pointers (-186) | 4+ Three-pointers (+131) | 5+ Three-pointers (+288)
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
[Damian Lillard](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/damian-lillard-280-pg) is ramping up his three-point volume.
The [Trail Blazers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/portland-trail-blazers) point guard isn't as efficient as he used to be, but that's not stopping him from being ambitious. The 36-year-old is on pace to attempt at least 9.0 triples per game for the sixth time in his career. He's been especially bold from distance in the back half of November.
Lillard has attempted at least 11 three-pointers in four of his previous seven games. He made five or more on each of those occasions, which makes his impending Friday matchup with the [Warriors](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/golden-state-warriors) interesting.
The Dubs won't have [Stephen Curry](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/stephen-curry-378-pg) available for this meeting, but they've struggled to defend the long ball since he went down with an injury. Golden State has allowed 14.6 made three-pointers per game in the eight contests Curry has missed this season. For reference, only four teams have given up more than that in 2026-27.
Lillard will likely maintain his recent aggression on Friday with [Ja Morant](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/ja-morant-109954-pg) on the shelf, especially against one of his favorite Western Conference rivals.
