Normally, New York isn't a team I'd look to attack for [rebounds](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/rebounds). They allow the third-fewest boards in the league (40.6 per game), but the more I dug into this matchup, the more I liked this setup for Giannis tonight.
Mapping it all out defensively, with Bam taking on Karl-Anthony Towns, Davion taking on Jalen Brunson, and OG Anunoby out, I expect Giannis to spend a good chunk of this game on Josh Hart.
What's nice about that is Miami can afford to let Giannis roam off Hart, help on Brunson's drives and keep him closer to the basket.
We saw this last season in the November 28 Cup game when Giannis was still in Milwaukee. OG was out, Giannis defended Hart for 31 possessions and came away with 15 rebounds in just 28 minutes.
Think about how much New York likes to run Brunson-Towns ball screens, too. Miami has the luxury of switching those with Bam, and when he's pulled out to the perimeter, who's there to clean up the miss?
Something I found interesting was how New York played in seven games last season against some of the better two-big defenses in Miami, Cleveland, Milwaukee and OKC. Brunson went from averaging 19.9 shot attempts to 23.4, the Knicks' paint [points](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/points) dropped from 47.8 to 40.9, and they took more threes.
I think that plays right into Giannis' hands, given how much ground he can cover on the glass.
For as good as New York's overall rebounding numbers are, opposing bigs have rebounded right in line with their averages against them. It's really the guards and wings crashing that make New York so good on the glass.
We're also getting plenty of minutes with Giannis out there without Bam, since the two only share the floor for about 20 minutes a night.
Giannis has averaged 12.9 rebounds since the start of November and gone over this line in eight of his last nine.
I also love the implications of this Cup game. New York needs to win by seven or more to take the group, while Miami can still clinch with a close loss.
Assuming there are no groin restrictions, I can see Spo getting 35 minutes out of Giannis tonight.
