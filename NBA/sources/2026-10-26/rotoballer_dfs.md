## **NBA DFS Core Plays**
**[Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001999/Shai+Gilgeous-Alexander), PG - OKC**
  * **DraftKings: $9,800**
  * **FanDuel: $10,100**


Oklahoma City was decimated by injuries last game, headlined by a potentially season-threatening one to [Jalen Williams](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002896/Jalen+Williams). You have to feel for the guy at this point, as he just can't seem to avoid these major setbacks. We press on, however, as DFS players. Williams' injury opens up major usage for the rest of the Thunder, and [Cason Wallace](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003042/Cason+Wallace) and [Jaylin Williams](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002921/Jaylin+Williams) are also on injury watch as they both left last game as well.
Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is back to his old tricks, averaging 27.7 PPG on a 31.7% usage rate (tenth in the NBA), and he's under $10k to start the young season. OKC (last in pace) should get a significant boost against the Suns (12th), and Phoenix has allowed the fourth-most FPPG to opposing point guards. With all the values on this slate already, it's possible to fit SGA into your cash lineup with the guy below...
**[Giannis Antetokounmpo](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000497/Giannis+Antetokounmpo), PF - MIA**
  * **DraftKings: $9,900**
  * **FanDuel: $11,200**


Miami made the biggest waves in the offseason, trading for all-world big man Giannis Antetokounmpo. It hasn't yet translated to wins (as Miami is 0-3), but the offense is predictably running through Giannis. He's averaging 28.3 PPG and 8.1 RPG on a massive 36.6% usage rate (tied for second in the NBA with [Anthony Edwards](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002523/Anthony+Edwards)), and this is a team desperate for a win.
The matchup is pristine tonight, and it was known that we'd be attacking the Dallas Mavericks from all angles. Dallas is currently 26th in rebounding rate, and have allowed the fourth-highest at-rim frequency. They do not have anyone remotely capable of slowing Giannis down, and this may be the only time he's ever under $10k. Take advantage, and try to pair him with SGA if possible.

**[Jay Huff](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002735/Jay+Huff)[,](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002735/Jay+Huff)**[ ](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002735/Jay+Huff)**C - IND**
