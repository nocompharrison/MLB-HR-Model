## **Best NBA Bets Today (Monday, Oct. 26)**
###  [**Tyrese Haliburton**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/tyrese-haliburton-5114)**Under 29.5 Points + Assists (-121) at Milwaukee Bucks**
_DraftKings Sportsbook - 3 p.m. ET_
**Kirien Sprecher:** Haliburton finished Sunday's 136-109 win over the Nets with 24 points (8-13 FG, 4-7 3Pt, 4-5 FT), 10 assists and two steals, and he's not listed on Indiana's injury report for the second half of the back-to-back set. The All-Star guard was expected to face some restrictions this season after missing all of last year while rehabbing from a torn Achilles, but so far, he's handled his usual role, playing 34, 32 and 31 minutes in the Pacers' first three games, respectively. He's eclipsed this mark in two of those outings, but I expect his workload to be monitored very closely Monday night, especially if Indiana can get out to a big lead against an inferior opponent.
###  [Bam Adebayo](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/bam-adebayo-4153) under 1.5 made threes (-133) vs. Dallas Mavericks
_Novig, 4:56 PM ET_
**Alex Barutha** : Adebayo is 2-for-13 from distance in three games to start the campaign. The Heat also need a new gameplan after starting 0-3, which could involve Adebayo playing more around the basket. Plus, the Mavericks have some interesting switchable defenders who can race out to prevent Adebayo's three-ball, like [P.J. Washington](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/pj-washington-4781) and [Morez Johnson](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/morez-johnson-6971), while [Daniel Gafford](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/daniel-gafford-4824) deals with [Giannis Antetokounmpo](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/giannis-antetokounmpo-3456).

###  [Ajay Mitchell](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/ajay-mitchell-6289) O24.5 PTS+REB+AST (-113) vs. Phoenix Suns
_DraftKings, 3pm ET_
**Nick Whalen:** This may feel like low-hanging fruit after the [Jalen Williams](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/jalen-williams-5687) injury on Sunday night, but the Thunder will now lean much more heavily on Mitchell — especially in this spot with [Cason Wallace](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/cason-wallace-5898) (shoulder) also sitting out. Mitchell went for 18 points, six boards and four assists on Sunday night and now faces a Suns team that ranks dead-last in rebounding rate and defensive rating in the early going (extremely small sample size alert).
