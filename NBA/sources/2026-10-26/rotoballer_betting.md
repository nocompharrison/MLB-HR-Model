## NBA Prop Bet Picks

**[Moussa Diabate](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002903/Moussa+Diabat%C3%A9) OVER 18.5 Points + Rebounds (52%)**
The Charlotte center had an impressive opening night with 17 points and eight rebounds as the Hornets picked up a win over the Nets.
His energy on defense and ability to pound the boards is something that the Hornets need badly, and his 33 minutes in the opener is a great sign that he's really put some distance between himself and backup center [Ryan Kalkbrenner](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003468/Ryan+Kalkbrenner).
Last season when he got 32 minutes or more, he went for 19 or more points and rebounds 75% of the time (12 times in 16 games). Opposing centers have done this in 9 of Atlanta's last 10 games, too, including 22 points and 7 boards from another undersized center, [Wendell Carter Jr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001996/Wendell+Carter+Jr.), in the Hawks' opener.
**[Paolo Banchero](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002863/Paolo+Banchero) OVER 5.5 Assists (47%)**
Welcome to the point-Paolo era in Orlando. The sometimes mercurial, but ultra-talented point forward of the Magic dropped 10 dimes in Orlando's loss to Atlanta on opening night.
He looked like the best version of himself, something we haven't really seen from him in a while. It was no secret that he wanted Jahmal Mosley out as coach last season, and he got his wish as he was replaced by Sean Sweeney in the offseason.
Sweeney and Paolo have reportedly hit it off so far, and Sweeney has handed Paolo the keys to the offense. Banchero had 14 potential assists in the opener, and only one player who had that many potential assists (passes that led directly to shots for teammates) had fewer than six assists in their first game.
It also helps that Banchero played 41 minutes on Wednesday. With a lot of players having their minutes held in check for the first few weeks, it appears that Sweeney has no problem keeping his best player on the court for the vast majority of the game. This game has a 1.5-point spread and should be one of the most competitive of the evening, so I expect Banchero to pile up counting stats and play a ton of minutes again.
**[Tyrese Haliburton](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002537/Tyrese+Haliburton) OVER 9.5 Assists (50%)**
It was great to see Haliburton in top form in his first regular season game in over a year. The Pacers' floor general had 23 points and 12 assists in their opening night win against the Pelicans. Those 12 assists came on a league-leading 20 potential assists, so he could have easily had a few more, too.
Haliburton is one of the best passers in the game, and Indiana added an excellent pick-and-roll partner for him last year when they acquired [Ivica Zubac](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001694/Ivica+Zubac) from the Clippers. The Pacers have shooters and finishers surrounding Haliburton, and I won't be surprised if he averaged 10+ dimes per game this season.
The Mavs are a good target tonight, too. They allowed [Fred VanVleet](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001733/Fred+VanVleet) to stack up 11 dimes in their opening night loss to Houston.

**[Ausar Thompson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003036/Ausar+Thompson) OVER 6.5 Rebounds (52%)**
Another player who fantasy managers had to be thrilled to see get some solid run in their first game was Ausar Thompson. He played only around 25 minutes per game last season, but is a monster on the boards and one of the best defenders in the league. He played 33 minutes in Detroit's win over Boston on opening day and rolled up 11 boards in the process.
Last year, when he played 30+ minutes, he averaged right around 7 boards per game. The early signs here are that he's earned a bigger role this year, and his rebounds are the most projectable of his stats, even though he's also a good secondary shot-creator on offense, too.
**[Grayson Allen](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001986/Grayson+Allen) OVER 2.5 Three Pointers Made (54%)**
I expect Allen to have another big role tonight for Charlotte, even coming off the bench. He played 29 minutes in the opener and finished with 21 points, while drilling five of his eight three-point attempts.
Allen is one of the best shooters from distance in the league, and Charlotte really needs his offense to start the year as both [Kon Knueppel](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003451/Kon+Knueppel) and [Coby White](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002280/Coby+White) are out. Expect him to be firing away again tonight, and remember that he averaged 3.0 made threes per game last year across 28 minutes per contest.
**[Walker Kessler](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002889/Walker+Kessler) OVER 14.5 Points (56%)**
This bet feels like a slam dunk! Speaking of dunks, Kessler had ten (yes, TEN) of them in his Laker debut, finishing a perfect 12-for-12 from the field for 26 points in just 29 minutes.
Kessler was the beneficiary of good looks from both [Luka Doncic](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001984/Luka+Don%C4%8Di%C4%87) and [Austin Reaves](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002720/Austin+Reaves) all night against the Warriors, and I expect him to pad his stats with easy lob dunks all season. He's also a tenacious rebounder who can snag offensive boards and rack up points on putbacks.
The Clippers allowed [Domantas Sabonis](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001683/Domantas+Sabonis) to shoot 5-for-5 on opening night, and they'll struggle to match up with Kessler's size and physicality in this one. This number is already juiced a bit, but I am comfortable betting it up higher, and I think it's an excellent ladder candidate up to about 20 points!

## How King of the Court Works
King of the Court has quickly become very popular amongst DraftKings users. It involves picking who you believe will be the player with the highest PRA (Points/Rebounds/Assists) total for the night. You don't even have to win the prop bet to win the KOTC! You simply have to make a qualifying wager of $5 or more on the player who finishes as the leader in PRA.
There are a few ways to go about it; a lot of people will take the chalk and take the small bonus bets they get with it (since you have to split the pot with hundreds of thousands of other bettors). Or if you are like me, you like to get a little different, and that can lead to some big payouts!
I hit a few of these last season, including the game where [Bam Adebayo](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001836/Bam+Adebayo) went for 83 points! That led to me landing over 1k in bonus bets! Tonight, DraftKings has a 5 million-dollar pot to start the season off with a bang! That means everyone who picks the player with the highest PRA splits it evenly! Let's get to the picks, already!

## DraftKings King of the Court Picks: The Chalk
**[Luka Doncic](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001984/Luka+Don%C4%8Di%C4%87) over 40.5 Points and Assists (-117 DK)**
Luka very well could hit this number off points alone, but getting the extra bump in assists, where he will likely handle the ball full-time now that [LeBron James](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000571/LeBron+James) is gone. In 20 games without LeBron since joining the Lakers, Doncic has had at least five assists in 18 of them. He has double-digit assists in eight of them. Luka Doncic was easily one of the most played guys in KOTC last season, and I won't be surprised if that continues. His ability to do stuff all around the court is second to none.
That being said, the payout will likely be fairly low, even with a payout as big as DraftKings is doing for the first week of the NBA season. A lot of people bet at the beginning of the season, with all the bonus bets and boosts they are giving out, so I wouldn't be surprised if this is one of the KOTC's largest turnouts.
Luka grades out better than everyone in our model, and I wouldn't be surprised if he was the KOTC, but what's the fun in just going with Luka? Let's look at some other options as well.
**[Jalen Johnson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002657/Jalen+Johnson) over 30.5 Points and Rebounds (-120 DK)**
Jalen Johnson was someone I targeted a ton last year. He has consistently been one of those guys that flirt with a triple-double on a nightly basis. His biggest downside is the scoring, though; he isn't quite the scorer that some of these other guys are, which hurts a bit when it comes to getting those high totals. Orlando is also one of the tougher matchups on the slate, but as of right now, Jalen Johnson is going to be the main producer on this team.
With no [Trae Young](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001989/Trae+Young) on the team, Johnson will look to increase his scoring while keeping the rest of his game intact. The Hawks made some decent additions to their bench in [Aaron Wiggins](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002714/Aaron+Wiggins) and [Luguentz Dort](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002339/Luguentz+Dort), but nothing that will affect him in terms of production or usage.
Johnson had by far his best season, seeing a significant increase in scoring and assists while still averaging over 10 boards a game. If he can get by a tough Orlando defense, he should be there among the top guys on the slate.
## DraftKings King of the Court Picks: The Pivots
When you are going after players such as Luka or Giannis, you definitely have a higher probability of hitting, but everyone else is taking them, too. It makes it likely that the payout will be quite minimal. So having other options in great spots, and hoping they pop off while guys like Luka maybe have a bit of a low-key game, is ideal when trying to go for as big of bonus bets as possible!
**[Darius Garland](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002270/Darius+Garland) over 31.5 Points and Assists (-118 DK)**
This might be one of my favorite spots on the slate. We are getting Darius Garland as the only true star on this lowly Clippers team going against one of the worst teams in the NBA, the Sacramento Kings. The Kings also make for a prime matchup for Garland. The Kings were giving up the fourth-most fantasy points to opposing PGs last season, while giving up the second-most points, seventh-most rebounds, and seventh-most assists.
Garland's usage is about to explode; with no [Kawhi Leonard](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000646/Kawhi+Leonard) on the team, he will be the primary scorer and ball handler for the Clippers. As long as this game stays close, we should expect Garland to play 35+ minutes. I'm really excited to see how Garland looks in this offense, and it's just the perfect spot to start the season off. As long as Garland can get involved with the rebounds, then he could give some of the top guys a run for their money.
**[Deni Avdija](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002534/Deni+Avdija) over 29.5 Points and Rebounds (-105 DK)**
I think this could be a sneaky good option as well. I think a lot of people will see the addition of [Damian Lillard](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000619/Damian+Lillard) and [Ja Morant](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002266/Ja+Morant) to this Blazers squad and think there is too much going on with this team for Avdija to still get a large workload. But I think he is still going to get a ton of usage in this offense. I think he takes a small step back as a ball handler, so he won't get as many assists, but I think his scoring is still going to be there, and his aggressiveness when it comes to getting boards.
During preseason, he had two games of 23 points or better, both of which were played with 25 minutes or less. This goes to show how needed he will be in this offense. I think Micah Nori has made it fairly clear that he is going to get Deni the ball as much as possible. His aggressiveness in the paint and being able to get to the line can be good for a 30-point night at any time. If he can still muster up five or more assists along with eight-plus boards. He could be in for a 40+ PRA night.
**[Cameron Boozer](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003709/Cameron+Boozer) over 25.5 Points and Rebounds (-112 DK)**
Do you want an absolute longshot with very minimal chance of hitting? Why not, right? This will soon be Cameron Boozer's team, and it's only a matter of time, because this kid is going to be dynamite, and on a roster that needs offense, he really can be a bright spot for them. Outside of [Ty Jerome](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002307/Ty+Jerome) and maybe [Cedric Coward](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003477/Cedric+Coward), who will be producing on this team? That's why I think Boozer could be a sneaky good pivot.
Boozer had a solid preseason, while only playing less than 30 minutes. I imagine if he is playing well, then the Grizzlies will see what he can handle. In the final preseason game, he finished with 19 points, seven rebounds, and three assists. He is also getting a great matchup against a Jazz team that couldn't figure out how to play defense for the life of them. They are getting the former Grizzly in [Jaren Jackson Jr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001991/Jaren+Jackson+Jr.), which should help the defense a little bit.
Boozer was electric in his time with Duke, and it's only a matter of time before we start seeing double-double games consistently from him. Why not start the season off with a big game from the big man!
