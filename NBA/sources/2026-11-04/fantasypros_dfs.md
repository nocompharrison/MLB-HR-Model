That’s left us with another massive slate here, with 28 teams in action and only two teams sitting. Having that many options in the player pool can be difficult, but it also opens up a ton of value. Every smart fantasy manager was stacking Indiana on Monday when they only had six players available, and it’s funny to see those sorts of things this early in the season. We shouldn’t have any nonsense like that here with everyone off on Tuesday, so let’s get into it!

## **NBA DFS Picks: DraftKings & FanDuel DFS Primer**

### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Jamal Murray (PG – DEN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jamal-murray.php) vs. TOR**
  * **DraftKings: $8,700**
  * **FanDuel: $NA**


It’s hard to believe anybody can be better than [Nikola Jokic](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nikola-jokic.php), but Murray has been Denver’s best player this season. He looks like the “Bubble Murray” we saw a few years ago, averaging 27.3 points, 5.5 rebounds, 5.7 assists, and 1.2 steals per game. That even included a rare dud in the opener, with Murray maintaining a 52-point average since then. You can’t ask for any more from a player sitting below $9K, especially since he’s a Canadian boy facing his hometown team. He has at least 41 DraftKings points in five straight matchups against the Raptors while they own a 23rd OPRK against opposing guards.
#### **[Trae Young (PG – WAS)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/trae-young.php) vs. ATL**
  * **DraftKings: $7,900**
  * **FanDuel: $7,700**


It was unclear how Young would look for his new team, but this All-Star looks like the same exact player we saw in ATL. Young has one of the highest floors of any point guard due to his distributing, scoring at least 40 DraftKings points in five of seven games. He’s also never scored below 29 fantasy points and will surely be motivated against the team he spent his whole career with. They were also one of the worst defenses while he was there, and it should be a showcase game since he’s averaging 42 DK points per game. It’s rare to find a sub-$8K player with a career 40-point average, especially in a true revenge game.
### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (PG/SG – OKC)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/shai-gilgeous-alexander.php) vs. CLE**
  * **DraftKings: $10,300**
  * **FanDuel: $9,900**


SGA used to be in the $12K range with guys like Jokic and [Victor Wembanyama](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/victor-wembanyama.php), but he’s taken a step back in DFS due to all the depth on this OKC roster. That narrative has been thrown out the window, though, because the Thunder will be without [Jalen Williams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-williams.php), [Chet Holmgren](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/chet-holmgren.php), and [Cason Wallace](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/cason-wallace.php) for the majority of the season. That means Gilgeous-Alexander has to do everything for this team, which was on full display when he dropped a season-high 66 DraftKings points in his most recent outing. The fact that he attempted 35 shots and handled the ball on every possession across 39 minutes shows how imperative he is to this shorthanded team right now, and it’s the same exact matchup he went off against earlier this week.
#### **[Karl-Anthony Towns (PF/C – NYK)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/karl-anthony-towns.php) vs. CHI**
  * **DraftKings: $8,300**
  * **FanDuel: $8,400**


The Knicks have struggled through the opening weeks, but it’s not this guy’s fault. In fact, Towns is off to the best start of his career. The big man has a double-double in all seven games while scoring at least 39 DraftKings points in all of those. Only five-figure players have that type of floor, and it’s paired with a fantastic 45-point average. That number is likely to be his floor against a team like Chicago, which ranks 29th in defensive efficiency while playing at the fastest pace in the league. The Bulls are also surrendering the fifth-most fantasy points to opposing centers, and it’s simply the best matchup in DFS right now. KAT has scored at least 41 fantasy points in 15 straight meetings with the Bulls while averaging over 50 DK points per game!

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[Onyeka Okongwu (C – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/onyeka-okongwu.php) vs. WAS**
  * **DraftKings: $6,400**
  * **FanDuel: $6,000**


Big O got off to a slow start this season, but he’s starting to find some things over the last week. Okongwu is averaging 34 DraftKings points per game across his last three outings. That’s the same guy we saw throughout most of last season, and it’s surprising he’s still well below $7,000 since he’s the only true center in Atlanta. Our favorite part about this is the matchup with Washington. The Wizards surrendered the most fantasy points to opposing centers last season, with Okongwu averaging 41 DraftKings points per game across their last three matchups. We know this isn’t quite the same Wizards team, but the Hawks need Okongwu to play 35-40 minutes to oppose [Anthony Davis](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anthony-davis.php).
#### **[Day’Ron Sharpe (C – BKN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/dayron-sharpe.php) vs. CHA**
  * **DraftKings: $5,300**
  * **FanDuel: $5,000**


This is the ultimate GPP play. Sharpe has been an elite per-minute producer throughout his career, but the role is never consistent enough to trust him every time out. That’s simply how it works in the NBA at times, but the upside is impossible to overlook. Sharpe scored 28 DraftKings points across 28 minutes over the weekend. That was his highest minute total since the opener, when Sharpe had 30 fantasy points in 30 minutes. The production is a clear correlation with the playing time, but we’re willing to take that risk in this matchup. The Hornets surrendered the second-most fantasy points to opposing centers last season while sitting 18th this year. We’re talking about a guy who averages 1.3 DK points per minute throughout his career, so if he can somehow get to 25-30 here, Sharpe will be one of the steals of the day.

### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Paul Reed (PF/C – DET)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/paul-reed.php) vs. PHI**
  * **DraftKings: $6,500**
  * **FanDuel: $6,400**


Reed has been a DFS darling throughout his career because he’s always produced when given the opportunity. That’s exactly what he has right now, starting and playing 25-30 minutes for the injured [Jalen Duren](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-duren.php). In this newfound role, Reed is averaging 32 DraftKings points per game. A six-game sample size might look like a fluke, but Reed had a 39-point average across 11 starts last season. It’s clear this guy should be a $7K player whenever he’s starting, and we love that Reed scored 36 fantasy points in his only start against Philly last season. Duren is expected back soon, but Reed will be one of the most rostered players until then.
#### **[Aday Mara (C – OKC)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/aday-mara.php) vs. CLE**
  * **DraftKings: $4,300**
  * **FanDuel: $4,200**


We already talked about how shorthanded Oklahoma City is right now, and they need all of these guys to step up. The first-round pick from this season is one of the biggest beneficiaries, taking on a 25-minute role in the absence of Holmgren. In the last two games in this expanded role, Mara has maintained a 27-point average across 24 minutes a night. All you need is 25 DraftKings points for Mara to be an immense value, but we haven’t even seen a ceiling game yet. This is a great opportunity for Mara to receive 30 minutes for the first time in his career because [Jarrett Allen](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jarrett-allen.php) and [Evan Mobley](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/evan-mobley.php) form one of the biggest frontcourts in the NBA. This kid will produce at least 1.2 DK points per minute with his rebounding abilities, and that’s impossible to overlook at this price tag with so many players missing in this rotation.
