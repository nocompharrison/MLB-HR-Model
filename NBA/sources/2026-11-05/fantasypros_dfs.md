### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Franz Wagner (PF – ORL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/franz-wagner.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $8,700**
  * **FanDuel: $8,300**


Franz Wagner and the Magic roll into the Valley of the Sun to face the Phoenix Suns, who are on no rest and likely tired. That’s great news for Wagner, who should be able to operate in the paint with impunity.
Wagner went 10-of-18 for 24 points, six assists and three rebounds last time out against Washington on Monday, and that was considered a down game.
In his most recent road contest, Wagner posted 26 points, 11 assists, five rebounds and a steal. Expect a giant stat line, especially with just four teams to choose from.
#### **[Yaxel Lendeborg (SF, PF – GSW)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/yaxel-lendeborg.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $7,900**
  * **FanDuel: $7,400**


Yaxel Lendeborg is making a name for himself early on for the Warriors. He dropped 37 points against the Clippers on Sunday and has scored 26+ points in four of his past six outings.
In all eight of his NBA games so far, Lendeborg has hit at least one three-pointer, and he has a steal in three of his last four games, averaging 0.9 steals per game. He has also dished out five or more assists in two straight games, and in five of his last seven.
The main point is that Lendeborg can contribute in many ways, yet his salary remains remarkably low.
#### **[Steph Curry (PG – GSW)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/stephen-curry.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $8,200**
  * **FanDuel: $8,100**


There aren’t many high-priced options for a two-game slate, but we know what Steph Curry can do. Why not go with the tried-and-true? Especially against a Miami Heat team that has allowed teams to hit 40.5% from beyond the arc, ranking 25th in the NBA. That’s always been Curry’s calling card.
Curry just dropped five triples on the Suns, and he’s hit 19 three-pointers in his last three games, including at least three in each of his last five outings. He has also scored 23+ points in all six of his games. You know what you’re getting.

### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Desmond Bane (SG, SF – ORL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/desmond-bane.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $7,200**
  * **FanDuel: $6,600**


Desmond Bane has seen a little extra run and responsibility with [Paolo Banchero](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/paolo-banchero.php) out with a high-ankle sprain. He left the game on October 23rd with the injury, and he has been laid up ever since.
Bane has been a reliable contributor, especially in the past three games, averaging 22.3 points, 3.4 assists and 2.3 rebounds. His biggest contribution usually comes from the perimeter, and he hasn’t disappointed there, with 15 triples in the three-game span and at least four makes from distance in each outing.
#### **[Miles Bridges (PF – PHX)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/miles-bridges.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $7,100**
  * **FanDuel: $6,200**


There aren’t many value plays on the Suns, but Miles Bridges is one, especially on FanDuel. Bridges will face the Magic, who and their opponent rank of 29, which isn’t a tell-all every night, but it’s especially useful on a night with just two games. Always look for the best players with the best matchups. That’s rather obvious.
The Suns are off to a brutal start, but that’s not of Bridges’ doing. He is certainly pulling his weight, scoring 16+ points in each of the past five games while pulling down at least six boards in each outing.
Bridges is on a streak with at least two three-pointers in five straight games, and he’s posted two steals per game over his past four outings, with at least one in each.
#### **[Brandin Podziemski (PG, SG – GSW)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/brandin-podziemski.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $6,700**
  * **FanDuel: $6,400**


Remember that the Heat aren’t very good at defending three-pointers. Brandin Podziemski isn’t quite at Steph Curry’s level, but the former Santa Clara standout has hit nine three-pointers over the past three games, with at least two triples in five of his six outings this season.
More often than not, you’re going to get good Podziemski, too. He has 17+ points in five of his six games, with an eight-point game in Minnesota on October 28th as the outlier. The limited production was due to rare foul trouble.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[Davion Mitchell (PG – MIA)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/davion-mitchell.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $6,400**
  * **FanDuel: $5,800**


Davion Mitchell is a strong target on Thursday night because of his mid-tier salary and potential for a strong game against the Warriors. Golden State has an opponent rank of 26, so don’t be surprised if Mitchell exceeds his DFS projections.
The 28-year-old guard is coming off a double-double against the Clippers on Wednesday, posting 11 points, 10 assists and four steals. The concern is tired legs on the second end of a back-to-back.
Mitchell played a back-to-back on October 23-24, scoring 22 points and dishing out 10 dimes with three triples on no rest.
#### **[Anthony Black (PG, SG – ORL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anthony-black.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $6,000**
  * **FanDuel: $6,500**


Anthony Black is likely to have a solid game against the beleaguered Suns, and I wanted to make a Soundgarden joke about Black Hole Sun, but I couldn’t. At least it ran through my head. Most NBA fans probably don’t even listen to Soundgarden anyway. I am an oldhead. Moving on.
Black scorched the Celtics for 23 points and eight rebounds on Sunday, and he had 17 points, 10 assists and six rebounds in Toronto on Friday in his most recent road contest.
That’s two games in a row with tremendous production, well above DFS salary expectations.
#### **[Brandon Williams (PG – GSW)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/brandon-williams.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $4,900**
  * **FanDuel: $4,200**


Brandon Williams is a low-cost option on Thursday, one we might normally skip. However, we’ve highlighted Miami’s defense, or lack thereof, so the bench player has room to make noise at a very friendly price point.

### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Georges Niang (PF, SF – GSW)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/georges-niang.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $4,000**
  * **FanDuel: $3,900**


Georges Niang is interchangeable at either forward spot, depending on the platform. He should see some extended minutes, along with [Will Richard](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/will-richard.php), as [Gui Santos](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/gui-santos.php) (knee) remains on the shelf.
Williams exploded for 17 points on 7-of-11 shooting against the Clippers on Sunday, hitting three triples while adding three boards and three dimes. He might not hit those numbers, but a couple of treys and 10 points would be fine for the price.
#### **[Nikola Vucevic (C – ORL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nikola-vucevic.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $4,200**
  * **FanDuel: $3,800**


Nikola Vucevic isn’t seeing a huge role in the Orlando frontcourt, but he could be a factor against the Suns. At this low price, he’s at least a punt option with some upside, letting you spend heavily at other spots.
#### **[Oso Ighodaro (C – PHX)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/oso-ighodaro.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $4,500**
  * **FanDuel: $4,000**


If Nikola Vucevic isn’t to your liking out of the bargain bin, consider Oso Ighodaro. He is averaging 11.5 points, 4.5 rebounds and 4.5 assists in the past two games, while racking up a total of four blocks in two November outings. He has also logged 21+ minutes in each of his last three games. At this price, he is a steal.
