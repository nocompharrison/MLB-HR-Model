Below, I'll highlight three players who could deliver strong returns Wednesday.
[**Karl-Anthony Towns**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3741)**, Knicks![NYK](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100NYK.png?v=10) ($8,300) vs. Bulls**

The star big man has racked up seven double-doubles in as many regular-season games, averaging 20.3 points, 13.9 rebounds, 1.9 triples and 3.4 assists in 32.1 minutes per contest. 
[**Ja'Kobe Walter**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6261)**, Raptors![TOR](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100TOR.png?v=10) ($4,900) at Nuggets **
The 22-year-old swingman has started five straight games in the absence of [Kawhi Leonard](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3195) (knee), and Walter should continue to see a full workload Wednesday. The 2024 first-rounder has finished with at least 24.0 DK points in four of his last five games, during which he has averaged 13.2 points, 4.4 rebounds, 2.4 assists and 1.2 steals in 31.8 minutes per tilt. 
[**Mario Hezonja**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3578)**, Cavaliers![CLE](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100CLE.png?v=10) ($3,900) at Thunder**

The 31-year-old forward posted 12 points and eight rebounds in his last outing, and he's tallied 23.5 or more DK points in three straight games. Over that stretch, he's averaged 10.0 points, 8.3 rebounds, 3.0 assists and 1.0 steals across 21.0 minutes per showing.
