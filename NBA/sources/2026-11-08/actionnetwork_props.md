### Brooklyn Nets Betting Preview
The Brooklyn Nets enter Sunday night looking to snap out of an early-season funk after dropping a tight 124-121 contest on the road against the [Atlanta Hawks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/atlanta-hawks).
Brooklyn sits at 2-7 overall, occupying 12th place in the Eastern Conference. Head coach Jordi Fernández has seen his squad struggle to find an offensive rhythm, ranking 27th in Offensive Rating (107.6) and averaging just 108.2 points per contest (26th in the NBA).
While their defense has kept them competitive at 15th in Defensive Rating (113.5), their -5.9 Net Rating underscores a group that routinely finds itself playing catch-up in games played at a measured, middle-of-the-pack tempo (99.4 pace).

### Dallas Mavericks Betting Preview
The Dallas Mavericks, meanwhile, return to the American Airlines Center with momentum following a 112-100 home win over the [Utah Jazz](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/utah-jazz).
Dallas sits at 5-4, holding 10th in a crowded Western Conference under head coach Dusty May. The Mavericks have built a sturdy foundation on the defensive end, allowing only 110.9 points per game (10th in the NBA) alongside an 11th-ranked Defensive Rating of 111.9.
Offensively, Dallas operates efficiently in half-court sets (113.2 Offensive Rating, 17th) while controlling tempo at a deliberate 99.1 pace. With a positive Net Rating (+1.2) and a 9th-ranked Simple Rating System (+6.19), Dallas has shown steady form in front of their home crowd.

## Nets vs Mavericks Pick, Betting Analysis
[Action PRO](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/prop-projections) Projections have flagged a big edge on Dallas forward Caleb Martin’s rebounding total against Brooklyn, pinpointing exceptional value on the plus-money under.
After missing the first four games of the regular season, Martin has settled into a fluctuating role in the rotation for Dallas. Across five appearances, he is averaging 2.2 rebounds in 18.2 minutes per game. More importantly, his underlying involvement on the glass shows notable inconsistency.
While Martin started the previous game against Utah, he logged just 11:24 of playing time, failing to pull down a single board (0 total rebounds).
Martin has been held to two or fewer rebounds in three of his five games this season (0 vs. Houston, 2 vs. Oklahoma City, and 0 vs. Utah). His season total was heavily boosted by an outlier six-rebound performance against Sacramento on November 2, but he has failed to replicate that level of production since.
Operating primarily along the perimeter within Dallas' half-court scheme, Martin’s rebounding opportunities remain scarce, particularly when frontcourt companions control the defensive glass.
With PRO Projections setting Martin's expected rebounding output at just 1.73, well beneath the posted total of 2.5, bettors are capturing a sizable 32.6% edge at enticing +136 odds.
