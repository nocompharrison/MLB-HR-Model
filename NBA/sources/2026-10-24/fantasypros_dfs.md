## **NBA DFS Picks: DraftKings & FanDuel DFS Primer**

### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Jalen Johnson (PF – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-johnson-f-f.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $9,100**
  * **FanDuel: $9,500**


The basketball community doesn’t seem to realize just how special Jalen Johnson is. He’s the player Atlanta wants to build around, averaging 22.5 points, 10.3 rebounds and 7.9 assists in a breakout campaign last season. Foul trouble limited him to 28 minutes in his season debut, but Johnson still amassed 20 points, 14 rebounds and six assists.
Johnson posted 19 points, seven rebounds and eight assists in 29 minutes on Friday in a 47-point win. That shows he can be valuable in any role, but he should creep closer to 35 minutes here. We love the matchup against the Rockets because they surrendered the third-most fantasy points to opposing forwards in the opener.
This should be a competitive game between two solid teams, and Johnson feels like a triple-double threat in this type of showcase game.
#### **[Alperen Sengun (C – HOU)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/alperen-sengun-c.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $8,500**
  * **FanDuel: $8,400**


If we like this as a stackable matchup, we have to love Alperen Sengun on the other side. The Turkish center was actually one of Houston’s primary playmakers in the preseason, flirting with a triple-double average over the final two games. That’s what we saw in the season debut, with Sengun recording a double-double en route to 44 DraftKings points.
Sengun also had 29 points, seven rebounds and seven assists on Friday. Putting him in a [Nikola Jokic](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nikola-jokic.php)-type role will be massive for his fantasy value this season, and it’s scary to think of what he could do against Atlanta. The Hawks ranked 18th in total defense last season, and Sengun averaged over 40 fantasy points per game against them.
### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Nikola Jokic (C – DEN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nikola-jokic.php)**
  * **DraftKings: N/A**
  * **FanDuel: $11,900**


Nikola Jokic had one of his worst games in Denver’s Thursday debut, but the three-time MVP will bounce back. That dud might have lowered his rostership, but he’s a bet for 50 fantasy points on this slate.
Jokic leads the NBA with over 60 fantasy points per game over the last five years and he scored at least 49 DraftKings points in 66 out of 71 games last season. There’s no better floor around, and you know he’ll be hungry after Thursday’s nightmarish performance.
New Orleans is not the type of team that will slow Jokic down, scoring at least 59 DraftKings points in 19 straight games against them. That doesn’t even sound right, but it’s backed by a 66-point average against them in that seven-year span.
#### **[Anthony Davis (PF, C – WSH)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anthony-davis.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $8,700**
  * **FanDuel: $10,400**


One thing about Anthony Davis is that he’s always a monster when he’s healthy. That’s been hard to trust over the years, but Davis is always dangerous in DFS. He’s one of the few players with a true 80-point ceiling, scoring 62 DraftKings points in his Washington debut. Davis also averaged nearly 50 fantasy points per game last season and will be the focal point of this Wizards offense.
The Bulls were the best DFS matchup last season, and they could be again this year. They were not only 28th in total defense, but also played at the third-fastest pace in the NBA. That’s a godsend for a superstar like Davis, and he’s always more motivated against his hometown team. Chicago is also missing its starting center, but we’ll discuss that later.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[Jalen Smith (C – CHI)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-smith.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $5,200**
  * **FanDuel: $5,400**


We had Jalen Smith in this section on Wednesday, and he’ll stay here for the foreseeable future. These sites clearly haven’t reacted to the [Nic Claxton](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nicolas-claxton.php) injury, which guarantees Smith a 25-minute role as the starting center. That might sound silly since Smith played only 19 minutes in the opener, but the blowout was the main reason.
Providing 14 points and 13 rebounds across 24 minutes on Friday is more in line with what to expect here. What’s encouraging is that Smith scored 29 DraftKings points across those 19 minutes on Wednesday. That’s an elite per-minute rate, and the lack of playing time is keeping Smith’s price lower than it should be.
Look for Smith to creep closer to 25-30 minutes in what should be a more competitive game against one of the NBA’s worst defenses. Washington was 29th in points allowed and last in defensive efficiency last season while surrendering the most fantasy points to opposing centers.
#### **[Keon Ellis (SG – BKN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/keon-ellis.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $4,500**
  * **FanDuel: $4,300**


It was unclear what this Brooklyn rotation would look like, but Keon Ellis starting and playing big minutes makes him an enticing DFS target. He’s always been a great defensive stud in limited minutes, and he finally has an opportunity with the Nets. He played 34 minutes in his Brooklyn debut and scored 22 DraftKings points.
That might not sound like much, but you can’t find a player this cheap with a 34-minute role. He had at least 23 fantasy points in 11 of the 12 games he played over 27 minutes last season, and that looks like his floor after playing 34 minutes on Wednesday. This play has little upside, but there aren’t many better options to reach 5X value.
Not to mention, Ellis has a revenge game against a Cleveland team that acquired him at the deadline last season.

### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Jaime Jaquez Jr. (SF – MIL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jaime-jaquez-jr.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $5,700**
  * **FanDuel: $5,700**


Most people focus on [Tyler Herro](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/tyler-herro.php) and [Kel’el Ware](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/kelel-ware.php) as the key pieces in the [Giannis Antetokounmpo](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/giannis-antetokounmpo.php) trade, but everyone is forgetting Jaime Jaquez Jr. He quietly had a breakout campaign in Miami last season, averaging 15.4 points, five rebounds, 4.7 assists and 0.7 steals per game.
Those numbers could be his floor in a weaker Milwaukee rotation, with Jaquez putting up 33 fantasy points in his Wednesday debut and then 19 points, 11 rebounds and five assists on Friday. That mirrored what we saw when Jaquez faced Philadelphia last season, scoring at least 30 DraftKings points in all three matchups.
Jaquez was priced too low to start the season, and we expect him to be a $6,000 player on both sites in no time.
#### **[Yves Missi (C – DAL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/yves-missi.php)**
  * **DraftKings: N/A**
  * **FanDuel: $5,700**


[Derik Queen](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/derik-queen.php) should start at center in New Orleans, but they value Yves Missi over him. That’s a topic for another day. Missi is a great DFS value until this rotation changes.
Missi played 30 minutes in the opener, collecting 13 points, 14 rebounds, five assists, two steals and three blocks en route to 49 DraftKings points. That’s actually a career-high in fantasy points, and we can’t count out a full-on breakout from a 2024 first-round pick.
All of that has Missi priced way too low, especially since he matches up with Nikola Jokic. While Jokic is an MVP, he’s one of the NBA’s worst defensive centers.
**Update** _: With Missi possibly out after suffering an injury on Friday, Queen would be a great option as the fill-in starting center._
