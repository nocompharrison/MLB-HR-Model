## **Miami Heat![MIA](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100MIA.png) vs. Charlotte Hornets ![CHA](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100CHA.png)**
##  [**Giannis Antetokounmpo**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/giannis-antetokounmpo-3456)**Over 28.5 Points, -105 @ DraftKings**
Antetokounmpo has started the regular season with consecutive performances above this line, scoring 35 points against the Timberwolves and 36 against the Pistons. He converted 28 of 45 field-goal attempts across those contests, an impressive 62.2 percent shooting mark, while attempting 22 free throws. His 36-point performance against the Pistons was particularly dominant, as he also added 12 rebounds, five assists and two steals, and he seems to be adjusting well to his new surroundings in Miami. Saturday's matchup against Charlotte is another promising opportunity for the star forward.
The line is a bit lower for this matchup based on how good he's looked to open the year, and the individual matchup should put him against [Naz Reid](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/naz-reid-4806), who isn't an elite defender and doesn't have the size to slow down Antetokounmpo. At the end of the day, his scoring volume and recent efficiency support another outing in which he surpasses his proposed scoring line -- and even posting a season-low mark in scoring would do the trick here.

## **New Orleans Pelicans![NOP](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100NOP.png) vs. Denver Nuggets ![DEN](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100DEN.png)**
##  [**Nikola Jokic**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/nikola-jokic-3612)**Over 50.5 Points + Rebounds + Assists, -118 @ FanDuel**
Jokic endured an unusually difficult season opener against Oklahoma City, finishing with 13 points, seven rebounds and five assists across 35 minutes as the Nuggets scored just 84 points in a 96-84 defeat. However, every time Jokic has a down game, he usually bounces back with a dominant performance, and he should take advantage of a favorable matchup against the Pelicans at home this Saturday. 
Jokic is coming off a 2025-26 season in which he averaged 27.7 points, 12.9 rebounds and 10.7 assists, which combined for 51.3 P+R+A per game, and the Pelicans ranked 21st in defensive rating last season (117.4) and ranking 11th in pace (103.50), so Denver should have the ball enough for Jokic to control the game against a below-average defensive unit that didn't improve significantly over the offseason. In a favorable matchup at home and coming off a loss, the stage is set for Jokic to fill up the stat sheet Saturday. 
## **San Antonio Spurs![SAS](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100SAS.png) vs. Dallas Mavericks ![DAL](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100DAL.png)**
##  [**Stephon Castle**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/stephon-castle-6250)**Under 5.5 Assists, -115 @ Betr**
Castle has struggled to generate assists through San Antonio's first two games, recording just one against Oklahoma City and five against Houston. He averaged 7.4 assists per game in the 2025-26 regular season, but it's hard to see him taking over as the primary playmaker in a crowded backcourt that also features [De'Aaron Fox](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/deaaron-fox-4116) and [Dylan Harper](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/dylan-harper-6549), who should embrace a bigger role in his sophomore year. 
Fox is averaging 8.0 assists through the first two games, while Harper continues to handle playmaking responsibilities. Castle has also averaged 30.5 minutes, meaning his reduced assist production hasn't simply resulted from limited playing time, but rather from a role focused more on scoring -- he's averaging 19.5 points per contest early on. Although his established playmaking ability makes a rebound possible, San Antonio's distribution of ball-handling duties and his consecutive games below this threshold support backing the under here.
