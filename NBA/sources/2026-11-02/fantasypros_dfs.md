### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Nikola Jokic (C – DEN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nikola-jokic.php)**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $11,800**
  * **DraftKings Cost: N/A**


Nikola Jokic is not only one of the best players in the NBA, but he also plays at a position where production’s often hard to find. So I don’t mind overspending on him. And he’s off to a great start this season, averaging 51.6 fantasy points per game. He’s averaging 21.2 points, 9.8 rebounds and 9.2 assists per contest.
Jokic has a fantastic matchup today. He takes on the Portland Trail Blazers at home. Portland has been a fun team so far, but it also ranks 23rd in fantasy points allowed to centers. Expect Jokic to put up great numbers here.
### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Paul George (SF – BOS)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/paul-george.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $7,000**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $7,700**


When the Celtics and 76ers made a trade that sent [Jaylen Brown](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jaylen-brown.php) to Philadelphia, everyone wanted to talk about what it meant for Brown. But Paul George went to Boston as part of that deal, and he’s fit in with the Celtics — much more than I expected.
George is averaging 40.5 fantasy points, 20.7 points, six rebounds and five assists per game. And he’s been pretty steady, too, tallying over 40 fantasy points per game in each of his last three outings. Even when he scored just 17 points against Orlando on Sunday, George still put up 44.2 fantasy points because he contributed in several other ways. He added six boards, eight assists, two steals and two blocks.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[De’Aaron Fox (PF – SA)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/deaaron-fox.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $6,900**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $6,400**


Ever since his days in Sacramento, I’ve loved watching De’Aaron Fox play. And he’s found a great spot to thrive in San Antonio.
Fox is averaging 35.6 fantasy points per game and has steadily improved over the last three games, so he’s clearly finding a rhythm. After scoring 21.8 fantasy points in a revenge game against Sacramento, he totaled 32.6 against Utah and 41.1 against a Minnesota Timberwolves team that has played well defensively with or without [Anthony Edwards](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anthony-edwards.php).
I fully expect that upward trend to continue as the Spurs host the Jazz, who have allowed the sixth-most fantasy points to point guards in the NBA.
### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Fred VanVleet (PG – HOU)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/fred-vanvleet.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $6,400**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $5,900**


Fred VanVleet has missed a couple of games already this season, but when he plays, he’s been fantastic. He’s expected back in the lineup tonight against the Celtics, who give up the 10th-most fantasy points to point guards.
In his last two games, VanVleet put up 41.7 and 34 fantasy points. He recorded a combined 40 points, 11 rebounds and 11 assists in those matchups. In the first two games of the season, he put up 71 fantasy points.
