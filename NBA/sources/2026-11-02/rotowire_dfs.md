## **Best NBA Bets Today (Monday, November 2)**
###  [**Cooper Flagg**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/cooper-flagg-6548)**over 6.5 assists vs. Sacramento Kings (+113)**
_DraftKings, Noon ET_
**Nick Whalen:** Flagg enters the week having posted at least six assists in each of his last four games, including eight and 11 in wins over OKC and Miami, respectively. He now gets a matchup against the NBA's 29th-ranked defense in the Kings, who are giving up a league-high 30.0 assists per game. There's also a chance [Kyrie Irving](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/kyrie-irving-3186), who's listed as questionable, could be held out, which would push even more of the playmaking duties onto Flagg. If we really want to get wild, I don't mind looking at a Flagg triple-double at +1980.

###  **Parlay:**[**LeBron James**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/lebron-james-2344)**over 6.5 assists + under 17.5 points vs. Chicago Bulls (+258)**
_DraftKings, Noon ET_
**Nick Whalen:** James sat out the Sixers' win over the Hornets on Saturday but will be back in action against the 1-5 Bulls, who rank ahead of only the winless Kings in NET rating thus far. While the Sixers' offense hasn't been firing on all cylinders quite yet, Philly has been one of the best defensive units in the NBA and should have a good chance to become the latest team to blow out the Bulls. I see this as more of a distribution spot for James, who's more than happy to set up [Joel Embiid](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/joel-embiid-3572), [Jaylen Brown](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/jaylen-brown-3894) and [Tyrese Maxey](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/tyrese-maxey-5158) against the league's 28th-ranked defense.
###  [Lauri Markkanen](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/lauri-markkanen-4119) under 20.5 points (-148) at San Antonio Spurs
_Pinnacle, 4:41 PM ET_
**Alex Barutha:** Markkanen made his return on Halloween from a one-game absence due to a wrist injury. He struggled, posting 13 points on 3-of-14 shooting against the Trail Blazers. The matchup is much more difficult tonight, going up against the Spurs' 7th-ranked defense. Markkanen will find his shot eventually, but I'm not banking on it being tonight.
