## Yaxel Lendeborg Three-pointers Escalator
[![Celtics Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/bos.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/boston-celtics)
Sunday, Nov 22
8:30 p.m. ET
NBCS-BA
[![Warriors Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/759063_Warriors.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/golden-state-warriors)
Yaxel Lendeborg 2+ 3PM (-168) | 3+ 3PM (+170) | 4+ 3PM (+441) 
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
The Golden State [Warriors](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/golden-state-warriors)' temporary first option has a chance to break out of his scoring slump against the Boston [Celtics](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/boston-celtics) on Sunday.
Yaxel Lendeborg, the Dubs' standout rookie, has fallen short of 14 [points](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/points) in three of his previous four outings, but has the green light with Stephen Curry out. He's shooting a respectable 37.2% from deep this year, despite going 2-for-10 from beyond the arc in his previous five outings.
The Michigan product should be fired up to turn things around in a big-time matchup against Jayson Tatum. Paul George logged 37 minutes on Saturday in the first leg of the Celtics' back-to-back, so his workload could be smaller than usual. That will give Lendeborg fewer long wings to worry about on defense.
Boston boasts the league's best scoring defense, but plays at a rapid pace. Only the Charlotte [Hornets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/charlotte-hornets) shoot more triples (44.8) per game than the Celtics, and only three squads give up more long-range attempts per contest (40.6).
Bettors can count on Lendeborg's shot volume. He's attempted six more shots (16.3) per game than any other Warriors player since Curry went down, and he's eclipsed three made three-pointers three times this season.

## Andrew Nembhard Assists Escalator
[![Pacers Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/ind.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/indiana-pacers)
Sunday, Nov 22
8 p.m. ET
MSG
[![Knicks Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/740173_58419cc8a6515b1e0ad75a67.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/new-york-knicks)
Andrew Nembhard 7+ Assists (-151) | 8+ Assists (+119) | 9+ Assists (+202)
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Andrew Nembhard is filling in as the Indiana [Pacers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/indiana-pacers)' top playmaker with Tyrese Haliburton on the mend, and he hasn't slowed down with T.J. McConnell back in the mix.
The former Gonzaga Bulldog is averaging 7.7 [assists](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/assists) per game for a second consecutive season through 12 outings. While he fell short of his season average with six dimes in an upset over the New York [Knicks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/new-york-knicks) earlier this season, he's totaled 8.8 helpers per game over his previous five contests. McConnell's hamstring flare-up will likely require Nembhard to take on a larger role in round two of this pairing.
Nembhard is among the league leaders in assists per 36 minutes (8.4) and has a clear path to playing that much to close out the weekend. He's tallied nine or more assists five times in 12 games this campaign. He'll be a threat against the Knicks' strong defense, as long as he stays out of early foul trouble.
