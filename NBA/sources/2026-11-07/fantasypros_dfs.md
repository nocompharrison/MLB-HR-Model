### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Victor Wembanyama (C – SA)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/victor-wembanyama.php) vs. POR**
  * **DraftKings Cost: N/A**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $12,500**


Victor Wembanyama is literally breaking how basketball is played. He became the second player in NBA history with at least 50 points, 20 rebounds and five blocks in his most recent outing. That led to an absurd 107 DraftKings points, and we might not see a larger total all season.
What’s scary is that it wasn’t even that outlandish. Wembanyama scored at least 67 fantasy points 17 times last season. No player in the league has a higher ceiling, and it wouldn’t be surprising to see a few more of these 100-point games as the season progresses.
Not to mention, Portland surrenders the third-most fantasy points to opposing centers, with Wembanyama dropping 69 DraftKings points in their most recent matchup.
#### **[Franz Wagner (SF – ORL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/franz-wagner.php) vs. GSW**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $8,700**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $8,300**


It’s always fun to see Franz Wagner take over this offense when [Paolo Banchero](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/paolo-banchero.php) is sidelined. That’s Wagner’s role right now. He has a 33.6% usage rate when Banchero is off the floor. The averages are even more absurd, with Wagner averaging 27.4 points, nine rebounds and 12.2 assists per 100 possessions in those circumstances.
That might look outlandish, but it’s no surprise. Wagner has averaged 46 points over his last four fixtures. You can’t overlook him in this expanded role, especially against Golden State. Wagner has scored at least 36 fantasy points in three straight meetings with the Warriors, and they rank 22nd in defensive efficiency.
### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (PG – Oklahoma City)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/shai-gilgeous-alexander.php) vs. WSH**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $10,400**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $10,400**


We recommended Shai Gilgeous-Alexander in my most recent article, and we’ll keep him here until he creeps above $11,000 on both sites. His price dipped a bit last season along with his production, but that can’t happen now that three starters are missing in Oklahoma City. With [Jalen Williams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-williams.php), [Cason Wallace](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/cason-wallace.php) and [Chet Holmgren](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/chet-holmgren.php) all out, Gilgeous-Alexander is averaging over 30 shots a game while flirting with a 40% usage rate.
This former MVP averaged over 50 DraftKings points per game before his teammates went out, but we’ve seen Gilgeous-Alexander score 61 and 66 DraftKings points since those injuries. Don’t be surprised if Gilgeous-Alexander’s floor is 50 DraftKings points with so much usage coming his way.
#### **[Nickeil Alexander-Walker (PG, SG – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nickeil-alexander-walker.php) vs. BKN**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $7,200**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $6,700**


Let’s include the other Alexander, Nickeil Alexander-Walker, in this section. The MVP’s cousin has been one of the most improved players since last season, and he’s taken over this offense since [Jalen Johnson](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-johnson-f-f.php) got injured. It’s unclear whether Johnson will be ready to return, but we like Alexander-Walker either way.
Alexander-Walker broke out with 35 DraftKings points per game last season and is coming into this matchup with a 45-point average over his last two outings. Alexander-Walker has at least 32 DraftKings points in seven of nine outings and is clearly getting more work with [Trae Young](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/trae-young.php) out of the picture.
A matchup against Brooklyn couldn’t be better, with Alexander-Walker scoring at least 35 DraftKings points in three of their four meetings last year.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[Nolan Traore (PG – BKN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nolan-traore.php) vs. ATL**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $4,800**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $4,600**


The Nets have been searching for a starting point guard for years, and it feels like they’ve finally stumbled into one. Nolan Traore has been just that so far this season, starting in five straight fixtures. Most importantly, Traore has at least 30 DraftKings points in four of his last six outings.
You can’t ask for more from such an affordable player, especially since this 20-year-old sophomore is just scratching the surface of what he could become. Atlanta’s defense has improved, but it’s been one of the worst teams against point guards for nearly a decade. In his last start against them, Traore scored 31 DraftKings points.
#### **[Mikal Bridges (SF – NYK)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/mikal-bridges.php) vs. CLE**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $4,800**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $4,100**


This is a risky recommendation. Sometimes a player is so bad that their price falls too far, and that’s where Miles Bridges is. There’s no doubt he’s been one of the worst starters in the NBA, but seeing him at this price is wild. He’s been between $6,000-$7,000 throughout his career, and we have to assume he’ll get closer to that at some point.
Bridges has flirted with about 30 DraftKings points and 35 minutes a night throughout his career, and he’s not washed at 30. Some of these guys get off to slow starts, but this is a stock we’re willing to buy because it’s so low right now. In seven matchups against the Cavaliers last year, Bridges scored at least 26 fantasy points in all of them and averaged 34 DraftKings points per game. Either of those would make him the best value play of the day.

### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Aday Mara (C – Oklahoma City)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/aday-mara.php) vs. WSH**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $4,800**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $4,500**


Few organizations develop young players this well, and this is a fantastic opportunity for a player like Aday Mara. Oklahoma City wanted to develop him more before showcasing him, but the aforementioned injuries forced him into a prominent role. We love that from a DFS standpoint because Mara got his first career start in Oklahoma City’s most recent game.
This expanded role has helped Mara maintain a 26-point average over 23 minutes a night. We expect the big man to average about 1.3 DraftKings points per minute, and that per-minute production could make him a stud in a 25-minute role. The Thunder need him to play at least 25 minutes against [Anthony Davis](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anthony-davis.php) or his replacement, and we also don’t mind that Washington has been one of the NBA’s worst defenses in recent years.
#### **[Kingston Flemings (PG – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/kingston-flemings.php) vs. BKN**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $4,900**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $4,000**


It was shocking to see Kingston Flemings start over [CJ McCollum](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/cj-mccollum.php), but it’s a great opportunity for the top-10 pick. Despite a slow start, Kingston has averaged 27 DraftKings points per game over his last four outings. That includes a 40-point outburst, and Atlanta needs his playmaking, with Johnson unlikely to play.
It’s unclear whether this role will last long-term, but seeing this kind of upside from a top-10 pick in his first two weeks is encouraging. The matchup might be the best part because Brooklyn has the fourth-worst defense since the start of last year.
