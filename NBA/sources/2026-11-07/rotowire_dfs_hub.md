[**Franz Wagner**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5370)**, Magic![ORL](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100ORL.png?v=10) ($8,700) at Warriors**

Wagner has dished out at least 11 assists in three of his last four outings, posting between 37.8 and 54.0 DK points in all four of those games. In that four-game stretch, he's averaged 19.8 points, 11.0 assists, 5.5 rebounds and 1.5 steals in 38.8 minutes per tilt. 
[**Day'Ron Sharpe**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5418)**, Nets![BKN](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100BKN.png?v=10) ($5,100) at Hawks**
Some of the slate's top centers -- such as [Alperen Sengun](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5404) and [Karl-Anthony Towns](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3741) -- are eligible at the power forward spot, so spending down at the five is a viable strategy. Sharpe enters a relatively favorable matchup Saturday, as Atlanta ranks 25th in fantasy points per game allowed to opposing centers. The 25-year-old big man hasn't been very aggressive as a scorer but has still tallied 28.8 or more DK points in two of his last three outings, averaging 8.0 points, 8.0 rebounds, 5.0 assists and 1.3 combined steals-plus-blocks in 25.0 minutes per contest during that span. 
[**Alex Sarr**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6240)**, Wizards![WAS](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100WAS.png?v=10) ($4,800) at Thunder**
Sarr is coming off a 35.0 DK-point performance and has recorded between 25.0 and 36.5 in four of his last five appearances. Over that stretch, the third-year big man has averaged 11.4 points, 7.0 rebounds, 1.6 steals, 1.4 assists and 1.2 blocks in 26.8 minutes per contest. His upside would also increase further if [Anthony Davis](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3297) (ankle) is downgraded from questionable to out.
