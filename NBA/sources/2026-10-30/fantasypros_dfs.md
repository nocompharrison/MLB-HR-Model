### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Tyrese Maxey (PG – PHI)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/tyrese-maxey.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $8,400**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $9,000**


Tyrese Maxey is off to a blistering season. With the talent around him in Philadelphia, defenses can’t focus too much on him; otherwise, someone else will score.
Maxey is averaging 45.7 fantasy points per game in his first five outings. He’s averaged 28.4 points, 4.2 rebounds, 4.6 assists and 1.8 steals during that stretch. With this being a home game against a struggling Knicks team, I expect Maxey to deliver another great performance.
### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Bam Adebayo (C – MIA)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/bam-adebayo.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $7,500**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $7,800**


It isn’t always easy to find a talented center who has a favorable matchup. That’s the case with Bam Adebayo, though.
Adebayo has improved as the season has gone on, and as he’s getting used to playing with [Giannis Antetokounmpo](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/giannis-antetokounmpo.php). Over his last four games, Adebayo has scored 20.7, 37.6, 33.1 and 45.3 fantasy points. He scored 24 points, grabbed nine rebounds and tallied three assists and three steals in his most recent outing versus Atlanta.
The Heat face the Pacers, who allow the eighth-most fantasy points to centers in the NBA. I would gladly put Adebayo in my lineup.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[Ayo Dosunmu (SG, PG – MIN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/ayo-dosunmu.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $5,600**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $6,000**


It’s easy to look at Ayo Dosunmu’s season-long numbers and think he’s not a great value play. But context matters a great deal here.
Timberwolves franchise player [Anthony Edwards](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anthony-edwards.php) is out with a knee ailment; Dosunmu will start in his place. And in the Timberwolves’ first game without Edwards, a home game against the Warriors, he recorded 17 points, three rebounds, four assists and four steals for 38.6 fantasy points.
I expect Dosunmu will only get better as the season goes on and he becomes more comfortable in the Timberwolves’ starting lineup.
### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Derrick White (PG – BOS)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/derrick-white.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $6,700**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $5,900**


Derrick White has been fine for fantasy, averaging 27.7 points per game. He’s averaging 12.5 points, four rebounds and 6.5 assists per contest. He could be better as a shooter, though, shooting 31.8% from three.
And I expect White to perform better against a Bulls team that allows the sixth-most fantasy points to point guards in the NBA.
