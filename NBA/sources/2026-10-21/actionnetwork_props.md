### Over 219.5
This total is still taking steam down to 218, but I just have to go the other way from the [sharp](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/sharp-report) money here.
The [Nets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/brooklyn-nets) had the No. 1 offense in the preseason. Yes, it’s just the preseason, but they do have actual, proven offensive players like Michael Porter Jr. and Julius Randle. They have guards who can consistently create and wings who can finish.
On the other side, the [Hornets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/charlotte-hornets) are not magically going to be better defensively despite losing LaMelo Ball. They are also still going to bomb threes at a high volume, even with their best players on the shelf.
This feels exactly like a ragtag, high-variance game played in the 230s, not a defensive slugfest.

8 p.m. ET

Zach Edey Over 10.5 Rebounds, Ace Bailey Over 17.5 PRA, Overtime
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
### Zach Edey Over 10.5 Rebounds
Zach Edey is a monster on the glass, and the [Jazz](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/utah-jazz) are significantly undersized.
I like Jaxson Hayes in this lineup, but he is going to lose the rebounding battle down low.
Jaren Jackson Jr. is notoriously a lousy rebounder who also consistently gets into foul trouble.
Edey’s health is always a slight issue, but if he plays his minutes, he should smash the glass in this matchup.
**Pick:[Zach Edey Over 10.5 Rebounds](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3084163368&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**
### Ace Bailey Over 17.5 Points, Rebounds & Assists
Bailey went over this line 81% of the time last year.
I understand the argument that he theoretically loses some minutes with Darryn Peterson starting, but Bailey is now the full-time sixth man and will absolutely rack up stats against second units.
Bailey is huge, he's great at crashing the offensive glass, he has some nifty passing chops, and he possesses a full bag to score with.
**Pick:[Ace Bailey Over 17.5 Points, Rebounds & Assists](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3095236245&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**
### Game to Go to Overtime (+1700)
I grabbed this at [Grizzlies](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/memphis-grizzlies) +1.5 before the line flipped earlier this week, and now I don’t really have a strong lean on the [spread](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/point-spread).
My model makes it Grizzlies +0.6, so technically, there is now value on Utah. I still like Memphis in their home opener, though.
What I like even more is the chance that these two teams are evenly matched on the road and it goes to OT in a late-night League Pass fever dream.
#### Pick: Game to Go to Overtime (+1700)

### Rockets -8.5 / Over 229.5 (Leans)
I really want to bet this because I’m sky-high on the [Rockets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/houston-rockets) and super low on the [Mavericks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/dallas-mavericks) entering the year.

Based on last year's data, this is an under spot, but the Rockets’ offense might be absolute dynamite this year and completely roll this total over.
The impact of new shooting coach Chip Engelland is massive, but I don’t want to fully buy into their overall team shooting just yet; except for a couple of guys…

## Suns vs. Trail Blazers
[![Phoenix Suns Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/875040_Suns.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/phoenix-suns)
Wednesday, October 21
10 p.m. ET
NBA League Pass
[![Portland Trail Blazers Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/312105_Blazers.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/portland-trail-blazers)
Under 232.5, Devin Booker Under 5.5 Assists, Ja Morant Over 4.5 Assists
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Matt Moore](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/matt-moore)
### Under 232.5
What on earth is this line? When I first looked at the board, this was the first number that jumped off the page at me.
Based off last year's data and current pace metrics, my model makes this total 221.
I cannot find any sort of schematic or mathematical justification for an 11-point differential to push this up to 232.5.
#### **Pick:[Under 232.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3026951737&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**
### Devin Booker Under 5.5 Assists
Devin Booker came into the season explicitly talking about how he needed to get back to scoring the basketball. He’s a gifted passer and operated as the primary playmaker last year, but he frankly didn’t enjoy it.
Much of that playmaking burden is expected to transfer to Jalen Green this season. I want to test that theory tonight against a fundamentally good Portland defense, especially since I already like the under in this matchup.
#### **Pick:[Devin Booker Under 5.5 Assists](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3084174865&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**
### Ja Morant Over 4.5 Assists
Ja Morant’s most underrated ability has always been his passing.
I’m honestly terrified of his scoring line and almost played his under, but he shot surprisingly well on jump shots in the preseason, so I’m holding off.
That said, Morant is an elite passer, especially when he gets a head of steam past the first line of defense and fires those two-handed kick-outs to perimeter weapons like Damian Lillard.
**Pick:[Ja Morant Over 4.5 Assists](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3084258944&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**
### Game to Go to Overtime (+1700)
I make this game [Suns](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/phoenix-suns) -0.7, but I really don’t want to fade a home team in their opener.
I do think these two teams are incredibly evenly matched, though, and a tough, grind-it-out game that goes to overtime seems perfectly fitting.
It's close enough that I’m willing to sprinkle a shred of a unit on it at +1700.
#### Pick: Game to Go to Overtime (+1700)
