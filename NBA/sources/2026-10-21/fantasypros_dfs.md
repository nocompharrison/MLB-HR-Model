### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Domantas Sabonis (PF, C – SAC)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/domantas-sabonis.php) **
  * **DraftKings: $7,700**
  * **FanDuel: $8,100**


Domantas Sabonis dropped to this $8,000 level after struggling last season, but that was an outlier. This big man played just 19 games in an injury-riddled 2026 season, but he’s been above $9,000 for most of his career. Sabonis averaged 19.2 points, 12.7 rebounds and 6.4 assists across the previous six seasons.
That’s what we expect from Sabonis this season, and he could facilitate even more given this roster’s lack of talent. Facing the Clippers couldn’t be a better matchup either because it’s one of the few games where the Kings won’t be a massive favorite. Sabonis has at least 46 fantasy points in five straight outings against L.A. while averaging over 55 DraftKings points per game.
Few $8,000 players can reach 50 DraftKings points, but that’s close to Sabonis’ floor.

That’s probably why this price has dropped more than it should, but Markkanen is fully healthy now. ### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Luka Doncic (PG – LAL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/luka-doncic.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $11,000**
  * **FanDuel: $11,700**


We hate to recommend the highest-priced player on any slate, but Luka Doncic will post MVP numbers this season. He’s now running the show in L.A. without [LeBron James](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/lebron-james.php), posting a 42% usage rate when King James was off the floor last year. That would be the highest number in the league, and it’s no surprise that Doncic averaged 38.7 points, 9.1 rebounds and 9.0 assists per 75 possessions in those circumstances.
Those absurd numbers aren’t even far from his season averages, with Doncic leading this slate with over 60 fantasy points per game. It’s scary to think that could be his floor, but he could go off every single night. In addition, Doncic averaged 65 fantasy points per game in two matchups with the Warriors last year.
#### **[Tyrese Haliburton (PG – IND)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/tyrese-haliburton.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $8,400**
  * **FanDuel: $8,300**


The Tyrese Haliburton injury in the NBA Finals two years ago was one of the saddest moments I’ve seen as a sports fan, but this is the start of his redemption story. Haliburton looks as good as ever in his return to action, providing 41 points, eight rebounds and nine assists across 38 minutes over his last two preseason games.
Those are some ridiculous per-minute averages, but it’s no surprise since Haliburton was flirting with a five-figure salary before his injury. The only real concern is limited playing time, but that matters little since he’s barely cracking $8,000. He should pummel the Pelicans, too, with New Orleans ranked 23rd in defensive efficiency and 24th in points allowed last year.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[Day’Ron Sharpe (C – BKN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/dayron-sharpe.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $5,700**
  * **FanDuel: $5,400**


Day’Ron Sharpe has been a per-minute stud throughout his career, and it’s exciting that he’ll start for the first time. That could guarantee this big man 25-30 minutes of action, and he’d be a $7,000 player with that type of opportunity. Sharpe has averaged 6.9 points, 5.8 rebounds, 1.4 assists, 0.6 steals and 0.6 blocks in 15 minutes per game in his career.
If you double those averages for a 30-minute role, Sharpe would average nearly 40 fantasy points per game. It’s not quite that simple, but the $5,000 price tag doesn’t make sense for that kind of potential. Charlotte’s defense improved last year, but they surrendered the most fantasy points to opposing centers in 2025.
#### **[Jalen Smith (C – CHI)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-smith.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $5,000**
  * **FanDuel: $4,500**


Injury-based values matter on every single NBA DFS slate. Jalen Smith is one of the primary beneficiaries, starting for [Nic Claxton](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nicolas-claxton.php) in Chicago. That should guarantee him 20-30 minutes of playing time, and he’s been a beast in that role throughout his career.
In 23 games where Smith played at least 20 minutes last season, he averaged over 29 fantasy points per game. You only need 25 DraftKings points for Smith to be a great value, and he scored at least 23 fantasy points in 21 of those 23 games last year. The Bulls’ roster also lacks center depth, which could push Smith to 25-30 minutes.
Most players in this price range are bench or role players, but Smith is undervalued because of the Claxton injury.

### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Daniel Gafford (C – DAL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/daniel-gafford.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $5,400**
  * **FanDuel: $4,600**


Daniel Gafford and Day’Ron Sharpe are actually very similar players. Much like Sharpe, Gafford has always produced when given a full allotment of minutes. That’s expected early on because Dallas is missing [Dereck Lively](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/dereck-lively-ii.php). In Gafford’s 44 starts last season, he averaged just shy of 30 fantasy points per game.
That’s outstanding production for a $5,000 player, but Gafford can also reach 40-50 DraftKings points if he plays 30 minutes. That’s in play given this roster’s outlook, and Dallas needs him to play a ton against this huge Houston team. The Rockets have [Alperen Sengun](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/alperen-sengun-c.php), [Steven Adams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/steven-adams.php) and [Clint Capela](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/clint-capela.php), and Gafford is really the only true center who can guard them. He showed that when he averaged over 30 DraftKings points per game in their three matchups last season.
#### **[Dennis Schroder (PG – CHA)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/dennis-schroder.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $3,700**
  * **FanDuel: $4,300**


This could’ve been a cash-game option or value play, but we’ll put Dennis Schroder here because he’s way too cheap. This veteran has always been a great ball-handler for whatever team he plays on, and this shorthanded Charlotte team will need to lean on him.
Trading away [LaMelo Ball](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/lamelo-ball.php) is one thing, but they’re also expected to be without [Kon Knueppel](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/kon-knueppel.php) and [Coby White](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/coby-white.php) for the first few games. That leaves Schroder as one of the only ball-handlers, and he should be locked into a 30-minute starting point guard role.
That’s awesome for such an affordable player, especially since Schroder averaged 28 fantasy points per game as a starter last season. We love the matchup against Brooklyn, too, with the Nets ranked 25th in defensive efficiency last year.
