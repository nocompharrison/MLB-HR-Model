## **NBA DFS Core Play**
**[Luka Doncic](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001984/Luka+Don%C4%8Di%C4%87), LAL - PG**
  * **DraftKings: $11K**
  * **FanDuel: $11.7K**


This might seem like a no-brainer, but I think at least 30-40% of tournament lineups tonight will not have Luka in them, despite the fact that his projection crushes everyone else on the slate. His cash game rostership will push up into the 70-80% range, I would think. But I will not be making many lineups that don't include the Laker star.
With [LeBron James](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000571/LeBron+James) in Philly, we just might see Luka break DFS this year. He hits threes, which helps on DK with the extra half-point, and he's a threat to grab the double-double and triple-double bonuses on DK nightly. Turnovers are also punished less severely on DK (-0.5) compared to FD (-1.0), giving him an even higher floor and ceiling for DK players.
He could flirt with 40% usage this season, and we might see him at 12K on DK and 13K on FD before Thanksgiving. Don't overthink this one!

There are so many value plays to choose from tonight, so featuring just one guy was a tough choice, but I think Allen is going to be one my most-rostered players.
