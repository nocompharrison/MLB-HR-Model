## Wizards vs. Hawks
[![Washington Wizards Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/wsh.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/washington-wizards)
Friday, Nov. 6
7 p.m. ET
NBA League Pass
[![Atlanta Hawks Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/atl.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/atlanta-hawks)
Under 228.5 (-110)
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
The [Wizards](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/washington-wizards) are fraudulent. Schedule-adjusted, they have a bottom-10 offense and defense despite sitting at 4-4.
I am tempted to just play [Hawks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/atlanta-hawks) moneyline, but unfortunately, my model still shows value on the Wizards against the 6.5-spread.
However, the under has hit in seven straight Wizards games, and their defense is actually closer to functional than their offense.
The Wizards hardly take any threes, which helps contain their offensive ceiling, and they are actually holding opponents to the second-lowest eFG% in the league.
The Hawks scored 120 points on Washington in DC on Wednesday, and the under still cruised.
I make this total 222.9 with nearly seven points of value on the under.
Washington’s defense has only allowed its opponent to go over their team total in one game this season, which was Wednesday against the Hawks.
I don’t think that will repeat with this line a point higher than the last game.
The Wizards’ offense is inept, their defense a little less so, and that’s why I think this total stays below the number.
**Pick:[Under 228.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3174580296&context=action-articles&sport=basketball) (-110)**

## Celtics vs. Hornets
[![Boston Celtics Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2023/07/boston-celtics-logo-updated-2023.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/boston-celtics)
Friday, Nov. 6
7 p.m. ET
NBA League Pass
[![Charlotte Hornets Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2023/07/charlotte-hornets-logo-updated-2023.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/charlotte-hornets)
Brandon Miller Over 3.5 Three-Pointers Made (+110)
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Brandon Miller averages the most corner three-point attempts on the [Hornets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/charlotte-hornets), making one per game.
Boston gives up the second-most corner three attempts per game in the league and will likely be fine with Miller trying to beat them off ball.
Miller has to space the floor because Kon Kneuppel is out (again), and with Coby White back, he’ll have better opportunities against a defense that allows a high volume of three-pointers (oddly, and oddly successfully).
**Pick:[Brandon Miller Over 3.5 Three-Pointers Made](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3174850621&context=action-articles) (+110)**

## 76ers vs. Cavaliers
[![Philadelphia 76ers Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2023/07/philadelphia-76ers-logo-updated-2023.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/philadelphia-76ers)
Friday, Nov. 6
7:30 p.m. ET
Amazon Prime Video
[![Cleveland Cavaliers Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2023/08/nba-updated-logo-cleveland-cavaliers.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/cleveland-cavaliers)
76ers Moneyline (-142)
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
The spread implies this moneyline should be -166, and we’re getting -142. That’s it. That’s the handicap. Okay, that and the Sixers simply will not lose.
The [Cavaliers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/cleveland-cavaliers) are a good team but don’t seem to have any _oomph_ this season. They’ve already given up a catastrophic collapse to this team earlier this season.
The Sixers are learning more and more about who they are and what they want to do.
I actually put the spread inside the number at +1.5. But at under a possession, I don’t want to buy into the idea that the Cavs are going to get right after that collapse earlier this season.
Joel Embiid should continue to dominate, especially with the Cavs having both Peyton Watson and Jarrett Allen questionable for this game.
I’ll just take the Sixers to keep rolling along on a cheaper moneyline than the market spread conversion, and take the team that brings more firepower to the front.

## Raptors vs. Bucks
[![Toronto Raptors Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/tor.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/toronto-raptors)
Friday, Nov. 6
8 p.m. ET
NBA League Pass
[![Milwaukee Bucks Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/mil.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks)
Under 230.5 (-114)
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
I am not going to sugarcoat it: the [Bucks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks) are an awful defensive basketball team, particularly when it comes to defending the pick-and-roll and giving up three-pointers. However, the [Raptors](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/toronto-raptors) simply lack the personnel to exploit that weakness.
As we saw against Denver, Toronto just cannot shoot from the perimeter. RJ Barrett was taking a bunch of threes, which is not his natural game. Immanuel Quickley and Scottie Barnes need to get downhill to attack the rim, and outside of Ja'Kobe Walter, they don't have the shooting to punish Milwaukee.
Without Kawhi Leonard, the Raptors are basically the exact same team from last year, meaning they have a quality defense but a limited offensive ceiling.
Meanwhile, the Bucks are currently sitting at 29th in offensive efficiency despite adding Jaime Jaquez Jr. and Tyler Herro.
Using a composite of last year's figures—because a seven-game sample for this season is just too small to build a reliable total model—I have this game projected all the way down at 224.6. Even if I plug in this year's limited numbers, it lands at 225.
I'm showing over five points of value here, making the under a strong play.
**Pick:[Under 230.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/draftkings/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3173516321&context=%5B0%5D) (-114)**

Jaylen Wells Over 1.5 Three-Pointers Made (-135)
[![BetMGM Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/779359_BetMGM800x200@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/betmgm)
I love targeting three-point props against teams that surrender a high volume of outside shots, and the [Thunder](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/oklahoma-city-thunder) currently allow the highest number of corner threes per game in the entire league.
Tonight, the Thunder's perimeter rotations are going to be significantly degraded because they are missing Cason Wallace and Jalen Williams, who are two of their best help defenders. Without them, OKC will be forced to send extra help from the perimeter.
The Thunder will likely clamp down on Ty Jerome up top, which will inevitably leave guys like Cameron Boozer loose on the short roll. Isaiah Hartenstein will probably play off Boozer to protect the rim, and Boozer will hit the kick-out pass to the corner over and over again.
This sets up Jaylen Wells nicely. He is coming off a brutal game against the [Pacers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/indiana-pacers) where he couldn't hit the broad side of a barn, making this a great buy-low opportunity.
The [Grizzlies](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/memphis-grizzlies) want this offense to hum, and against a depleted OKC defense that bleeds corner threes, Wells should get plenty of clean catch-and-shoot opportunities to clear this 1.5 line.

[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)

The [Kings](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/sacramento-kings), meanwhile, are completely horrendous. They do not have the top-end talent or the structural ability to hang around in the pick-and-roll against a juggernaut like the Spurs. I'll lay the big number with San Antonio.
