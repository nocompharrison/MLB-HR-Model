### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Joel Embiid (C – PHI)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/joel-embiid.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $8,700**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $8,600**


After missing the 76ers’ Halloween night game, Joel Embiid has put up 26.1 fantasy points against Chicago and 46 against the Pistons. Scoring real-life points is of course a huge part of that, but so is rebounding.
Embiid tallied 15 boards in that trip to Detroit. And he already has four double-doubles in seven appearances this season. Now he faces a Cavaliers team that gives up the third-most fantasy points per game to centers.
### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Ayo Dosunmu (SG, PG – MIN)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/ayo-dosunmu.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $6,700**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $6,700**


I can’t believe Ayo Dosunmu doesn’t cost more. He’s stepped into the Timberwolves’ starting lineup and has been great so far.
In five starts, Dosunmu has put up at least 37 fantasy points in four of them and over 48 in back-to-back games against the Bucks and Spurs. He’s also tallied at least six assists or rebounds in all but one of those games, showing he can add fantasy points beyond just scoring.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[AJ Dybantsa (SF – WSH)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/aj-dybantsa.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $6,200**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $6,000**


After being drafted first overall this year, AJ Dybantsa averaged 19 points, 4.7 rebounds, 1.7 assists and 1.3 steals per appearance over the first three games he played. And the rookie is no longer on a minutes restriction due to a sprained left ankle.
While he slowed down a bit in his fourth game against Atlanta, tallying nine points, six rebounds, and three assists for 20.7 fantasy points, Dybantsa will bounce back. With another shot at Atlanta today, I expect him to be much better.
### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Myles Turner (C – MIL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/myles-turner.php)**
  * **DraftKings Cost: $4,900**
  * **FanDuel Cost: $5,100**


If you don’t like putting Joel Embiid in your lineup, Myles Turner is another center who could help your fantasy squad tremendously.
Even on a struggling Milwaukee Bucks team, Turner has tallied over 30 fantasy points in each of his last three contests. That includes 42.8 fantasy points at Boston a couple of days ago.
Now Turner takes on the Raptors at home, and I expect he’ll contribute in a lot of ways — rebounds, assists and blocks — just like he has through the last few games.
