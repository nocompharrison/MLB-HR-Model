## **Best NBA Bets Today (Friday, Nov. 13)**
###  ![ORL](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100ORL.png)[Desmond Bane](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/desmond-bane-5159) U27.5 PTS+REB+AST (-112) vs. Bucks
_DraftKings Sportsbook, 1pm ET_
**Nick Whalen:** Bane is coming off of one of his best outings of the season on Wednesday against utah, when he drilled seven threes en route to 25 points. But he added four rebounds and only two assists, as his passing production continues to drop off relative to the last few seasons. Bane is sitting at just 2.4 assists per game thus far after averaging 4.1 a year ago and 5.3 in 2024-25. While Milwaukee is up there with the most-exploitable defenses in the NBA, Bane will likely need another red-hot shooting night to approach this number -- especially with [Jalen Suggs](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/jalen-suggs-5379) set to (potentially) return tonight.
###  ![CHI](https://assets.rotowire.com/images/teamlogo/basketball/100CHI.png)[Matas Buzelis](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/matas-buzelis-5938) O25.5 PTS+REB+AST (-120) vs. Hawks
_BetMGM, 1pm ET_
**Nick Whalen:** It's been a disappointing start to the season for Buzelis, who's seen his numbers decline across the board amid a slew of lopsided blowouts for the struggling Bulls. That's led to a decline in minutes (26.3 MPG), as well, but Chicago showed some fight against the Spurs on Wednesday night and should have a chance to hang around against the Hawks at home. Beyond that, Chicago is without [Caleb Wilson](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/caleb-wilson-6926) (hand), who leads the team in usage rate, while [Norman Powell](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/norman-powell-3726) (second in usage) is questionable. Even Wilson's absence, alone, should clear the way for Buzelis to shoulder more scoring and rebounding.
