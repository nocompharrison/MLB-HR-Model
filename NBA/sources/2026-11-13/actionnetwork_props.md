[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
[Franz Wagner](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/franz-wagner-170952-sf) is doing it all for the shorthanded [Orlando Magic](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/orlando-magic).
The German wing will enter Friday having scored at least 30 [points](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/points) in three straight contests, while averaging 22.7 field-goal attempts and 8.3 free-throw attempts. He's fresh off dropping a season-high 38 points on Wednesday, and will surely remain aggressive against the [Milwaukee Bucks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks)' struggling defense.
Opposing teams are scoring 121.3 points per game against the Bucks. Only the [New Orleans Pelicans](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/new-orleans-pelicans) and [Chicago Bulls](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/chicago-bulls) have been worse in that statistical category so far in 2026-27.
Wagner has logged more than 37 minutes in eight straight contests and is operating in a more ball-dominant role than usual, with other playmakers out of the rotation. That's evident in his average of 7.7 [assists](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/assists) per 36 minutes.
He will create for others on Friday, but I expect him to defer less often against a team with glaring holes all over. Milwaukee has given up 32 or more points to the opposing team's leading scorer twice in its previous four games.
Wagner's outlook for Friday is strong, given his recent workload and his team's mounting injuries.

[![Heat Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/mia.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/miami-heat)
Giannis Antetokounmpo 12+ Rebounds (-143) | 14+ Rebounds (+156) | 16+ Rebounds (+342)
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
The Greek Freak is on pace to have his best rebounding year since the 2020-21 season.
[Giannis Antetokounmpo](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/giannis-antetokounmpo-223-sf) is among the league's rebounding leaders, hauling in 12.6 boards per contest for the [Miami Heat](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/miami-heat). He could have another strong performance on the glass against the [Cleveland Cavaliers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/cleveland-cavaliers) on Friday.
The Cavs' twin towers look great on paper, but they haven't been great at preventing other teams from securing offensive or defensive [rebounds](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/rebounds) in 2026-27. Cleveland is giving up the eighth most offensive (12.7) and defensive rebounds (45.6) so far this season.
Antetokounmpo has been a menace on both ends. Miami hasn't been a spectacular rebounding team, but the former Bucks star is clearing 30.0% of their team rebounds and ranks fifth in offensive rebounds per game (3.5).
Winning the battle down low will be paramount against a Cavs team with so much frontcourt length, so I expect Antetokounmpo to make crashing the glass a point of emphasis.
He has recorded 14 or more rebounds six times in 11 appearances and notched 18 in two of those contests. A few lucky bounces could help him complete this escalator.
