**Top NBA DFS Plays Today**
Monday evening features a seven-game slate on DraftKings, while FanDuel includes the two late-night contests for a total of nine. Two games have high totals: the Mavericks against the Heat and the Pacers against the Bucks. If each game plays out to its expectations, GPP-winning lineups will likely be scattered with players from those matchups. Frankly, I wouldn't argue against including anyone from those games in your player pool who is expected to play more than 25 minutes and has any kind of ceiling. The goal here is to point out some players who may get overlooked in the other games.   
  
[Ajay Mitchell](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6289) **(PG/SG, $4600)** - Due to the unfortunate injury to [Jalen Williams](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5687) in last night’s contest against the Clippers, there is going to be ample opportunity for other Thunder players to step up. Hopefully, more eyes will be moving toward the likes of [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4387), [Alex Caruso](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3952), and [Jared McCain](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6266). That may be wishful thinking, but either way, Mitchell has legitimate 50-point upside, which is rare to find in his price range.
[Grayson Allen](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4454) **(SG/SF, $5300)** - Allen probably isn’t flying under anyone’s radar, but the balance of floor and ceiling here is too good to ignore. As long as the Hornets continue to have key players absent, guys like Allen should thrive, with the biggest obstacle to fantasy success being the threat of getting blown out. Playing at home against Washington should wash out any fear there, with Charlotte a 2.5-point favorite at the time of writing.
[Alperen Sengun](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5404) **(PF/C, $8400)** - We have a lot of mid-priced players projecting well on a points-per-dollar basis at PF and C, and I don’t expect Sengun to get much traction as a play tonight. The big man arguably has as high a ceiling as anyone on the slate and has yet to put together the game he’s capable of this season, with his highest-scoring game also being his lowest-rebounding game.
