###  [Julius Randle](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/julius-randle-3574) under 19.5 points (-135) at Boston Celtics
_Prophet X, 4:23 PM ET_
**Alex Barutha:** Randle is averaging 22.0 points this season, but it's on inflated percentages (51.3 FG% and 45.5%). Now, he's going up against a Celtics defense that is limiting opponents to 107.7 points per game. It's the perfect storm for him to regress to the mean.
###  [**Sam Hauser**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/sam-hauser-5338)**3+ made threes vs. Brooklyn Nets (+152)**
_DraftKings, 4:30pm ET_
**Nick Whalen:** We'll take a stab at a juicy, plus-money prop that Hauser has already hit in two of three games this season. He and the Celtics now get a matchup against a Nets team that surrendering 43.0 opponent three-point attempts per game and allowing those to fall at a 40.3% clip. Of course, it's all small sample size at this juncture, but the Nets are also a bottom-10 defensive team and a top-five team in pace.
