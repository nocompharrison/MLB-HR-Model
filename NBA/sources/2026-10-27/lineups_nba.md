*   Derrick White Over 22.5 Points + Assists

### **Derrick White (Celtics vs. Bulls) Over Points + Assists**

The Bulls have lost two of their first three games to open the season, and their defense, or lack thereof, has played a big role in their struggles. Chicago has surrendered the third-most points per game (128.0), along with 47.5% shooting, including 35.5% from three-point range. However, when breaking matters down to the positional level, it bears noting the Bulls have especially struggled containing point guards.

Chicago has surrendered the sixth-highest offensive efficiency rating to ones (31.7), and the Bulls have also yielded the fourth-most assists (11.0) and fifth-highest three-point percentage (41.2) to the position. Meanwhile, White has averaged 21.5 points + assists over the first two games while shooting 50.0%, including 38.5% from behind the arc. To top it off, the Bulls are playing at the league’s fastest clip (112.4 possessions per game), which only serves to brighten White’s prospects for a game in which the Celtics carry a slate-high 124.25-point implied team total.

**Original Line Publish:** 22.5

### **Amen Thompson (Rockets vs. Hawks) Over Points + Rebounds + Assists**

Thompson is off to a fantastic start, filling the stat sheet in each of his first three games. The fourth-year guard is averaging 32.7 points + rebounds + assists over that span, shooting 48.7%, including a blistering 50.0% from three-point range. Thompson is averaging 18.0 field-goal + free-throw attempts per contest in that stretch as well, and as was often the case last season, head coach Ime Udoka is giving the 2023 fourth overall pick plenty of run (36.3 min per game).

The Hawks have been middle of the pack when it comes to defending shooting guards thus far, but Thompson doesn’t quite profile as a traditional two-guard. Thompson’s rebounding is particularly impressive, and that, combined with his shooting efficiency and the fact the Rockets have a solid 117.5-point implied team total put this prop very much within his range.

**Original Line Publish**: 24.5

### **Ty Jerome (Jazz vs. Grizzlies) Over Points + Rebounds + Assists**

Jerome has looked like a legitimate All-Star through the Grizzlies’ surprising 3-0 start, as the veteran point guard has been pivotal figure in that success. Jerome is averaging 34.7 points, 5.7 assists and 4.3 rebounds while shooting 56.3%, including 42.9% from behind the arc. The 29-year-old already provided a hint of what was to come late last season, when he averaged 28.2 points + rebounds + assists across 22.6 minutes per contest over 15 games at the tail end of the campaign.

Jerome now has a clear-cut starting role that’s affording him 31.5 minutes per game so far, and as his previously cited numbers indicate, he’s putting that extra playing time to very good use. Jerome boasts a massive 32.1% usage rate thus far as well, and he’s now facing a very fast-paced team in the Jazz that he went off for 58 points + rebounds + assists across 32 minutes in the season opener. Utah had already gone into Sunday’s 132-123 loss to the Lakers allowing 51.8% shooting to point guards and an NBA-high 40.5 offensive efficiency rating to the position, and that was before Luka Doncic erupted for 52 points, 10 assists and eight rebounds across 38 minutes.

**Original Line Publish**: 34.5

### **Tyrese Maxey (76ers vs. Cavaliers) Over Points + Rebounds + Assists**

Maxey immediately clicked with his new superstar teammates in Tuesday’s season opener against the defending champion Knicks, scoring a team-high 28 points despite going just 2-for-9 from three-point range and adding five assists, four rebounds, three steals and three blocks in a stellar all-around performance. Maxey encouragingly took 19 shots despite sharing the floor with several high-usage assets, and he got to the line on nine occasions as well.

Within the context of that season-opening performance, Maxey’s prop is very achievable. The star guard will face a Cavaliers squad that he went 2-1 to the Over on 29.5 PRA against last season, and he’ll be doing so without Cleveland having the luxury of focusing heavy defensive resources on him. Maxey averaged 37.0 points + rebounds + assists across 41 regular-season and postseason home games in 2025-26, furthering his case for the Over in Thursday’s marquee matchup.

**Original Line Publish:** 29.5

### **Donovan Mitchell (Cavaliers vs. 76ers) Over 3-Pointers Made**

Mitchell recommitted to the quest of bringing the Cavaliers another championship when he inked a $273 million extension this offseason, and he should be set for high usage right out of the gate in what should be a highly competitive game. Mitchell was as aggressive as ever last season, averaging 8.8 3-point attempts per contest and draining them at a solid 36.4% clip.

Mitchell came up empty on two three-point attempts against the Sixers in his fourth and final regular-season meeting with them last season, but prior to that, he racked up 15 made threes on 30 attempts across his first three games versus Philadelphia. The Sixers also surrendered the fourth-highest three-point shooting percentage of any team on its home floor last season and postseason (37.4%), as well as the third-most made threes per game in that split (14.3).

Mitchell drained at least a trio of treys on 52 occasions last regular-season and postseason, adding further fuel to this prop.

**Original Line Publish**: 2.5

### **DeMar DeRozan (Nuggets vs. Thunder) Over Points + Rebounds + Assists**

It will be interesting to see how DeRozan fits with the Nuggets, and the accomplished veteran could well prove to be the missing piece for a talented Denver squad that’s still spearheaded by Nikola Jokic. DeRozan will open the season on the second unit, so asking him to approximate the 25.4 points + rebounds + assists per game he generated as a starter in Sacramento is likely not realistic.

Nevertheless, this is a figure DeRozan is certainly capable of eclipsing in a higher-volume second-unit capacity. The ability to potentially avoid some of the stifling defense played by the Thunder’s first unit will also be a bonus, and even while playing as a starter in Sacramento last season, DeRozan averaged 24.4 points + rebounds + assists across 32.3 minutes per contest in three games against OKC, shooting a solid 46.2 percent in the process. With less pressure than he had on him as a starter and the chance to potentially face some lower-caliber defense, DeRozan’s PRA prop is in play.

**Original Line Publish**: 19.5
