## How King of the Court Works
King of the Court has quickly become very popular amongst DraftKings users. It involves picking who you believe will be the player with the highest PRA (Points/Rebounds/Assists) total for the night. You don't even have to win the prop bet to win the KOTC! You simply have to make a qualifying wager of $5 or more on the player who finishes as the leader in PRA.
There are a few ways to go about it; a lot of people will take the chalk and take the small bonus bets they get with it (since you have to split the pot with hundreds of thousands of other bettors). Or if you are like me, you like to get a little different, and that can lead to some big payouts!
I hit a few of these last season, including the game where [Bam Adebayo](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001836/Bam+Adebayo) went for 83 points! That led to me landing over 1k in bonus bets!

## DraftKings King of the Court Picks: The Chalk
All odds are current as of 7:00 AM and courtesy of DraftKings Sportsbook.
**[Giannis Antetokounmpo](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000497/Giannis+Antetokounmpo) over 42.5 Points and Assists (-122)**
This is our replacement for Luka this week. Giannis Antetokounmpo has been on a tear this year, even though the Heat haven't been the powerhouse most thought they would be so far.
Tonight, they get a decent matchup against a pretty poor Utah Jazz team. The Jazz are scrappy and keep games close, but haven't quite figured out a way to finish the job. The Heat are 10-point favorites in this one, and they have the highest implied team total on the slate.
Giannis is averaging just over 45 PRA a game this year, and has four games of 49 PRA or more. The Jazz have been solid against forwards to start the season, but the best power forward they have faced has been Zion, and he dropped 23 points on them in both meetings. They've been forced to play a lot smaller this year with [Jaren Jackson Jr.](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001991/Jaren+Jackson+Jr.) and [Jusuf Nurkic](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000827/Jusuf+Nurki%C4%87) out. [Lauri Markkanen](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001831/Lauri+Markkanen) is also questionable for this one, while Utah's top scorer, [Keyonte George](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003044/Keyonte+George), has already been ruled out.
Giannis likely runs through this team, and if the Jazz can keep it close, I'd be surprised if Giannis didn't finish with close to 50. But if the Heat win too easily, perhaps Giannis doesn't play his full allotment of minutes, and that's why we have pivots!
## DraftKings King of the Court Picks: The Pivots
When you are going after players such as Luka or Giannis, you definitely have a higher probability of hitting, but everyone else is taking them, too. It makes it likely that the payout will be quite minimal. So having other options in great spots, and hoping they pop off while guys like Luka maybe have a bit of a low-key game, is ideal when trying to go for as big of bonus bets as possible!
**[Karl-Anthony Towns](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001431/Karl-Anthony+Towns) over 12.5 Rebounds (-130)**
The big thing we have to worry about here is the blowout potential. This game has the highest spread on the slate, with the Knicks as massive 15.5-point favorites. However, the Bucks have only had two games this season where they lost by 25 points or more, so they have kept games relatively close.
The big thing I want to focus on here is the center position. The Bucks haven't been able to stop centers all season long, allowing the fifth-most points and rebounds.
KAT is averaging nearly 14 rebounds a game, and if he can continue to up the scoring, he's going to be a serious threat to finish as the slate leader in PRA. Towns is averaging 1.19 PRA/min this season, which trails only Giannis and [Jayson Tatum](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001829/Jayson+Tatum) on this slate.
KAT is in one of the best matchups on the slate, and the Bucks are going to have a hard time stopping him with a frontcourt that consists of [Myles Turner](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001424/Myles+Turner), who looks disinterested in playing basketball this season.
**Jayson Tatum over 33.5 Points and Assists (-116)**
There are certainly some fun storylines surrounding this game with [Jaylen Brown](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001672/Jaylen+Brown) and [Paul George](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000742/Paul+George) facing the teams that traded them for each other this offseason. But more than anything, it's a really great matchup between two Eastern Conference contenders and one of the few games on this slate that is guaranteed to be competitive until the final seconds.
The 218-point total is the lowest on the board, but the level of competition here is sure to bring out some ceiling performances from a few of the superstars involved.
While Jaylen Brown has to share usage with three other stars in Philly, Jayson Tatum is the clear alpha dog in Boston. His 35.1% usage rate trails only Luka Doncic and Giannis Antetokounmpo on this slate. He also has the second-best PRA/min rate (behind Giannis) at 1.21.
Tatum has played fewer than 30 minutes in Boston's last two games, which turned into blowouts. But I think we'll see him play minutes in the mid-to-upper 30s tonight in this spotlight game. He leads the team in assists, averaging over seven per game, and while his rebounds are down a bit this year, we know he has double-digit upside in boards. In fact, he has 10.4 boards per game against the Sixers over his last ten meetings with them.
**[Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001999/Shai+Gilgeous-Alexander) over 38.5 Points and Assists (-106)**
I don't know that I can really consider this a sneaky pivot, but I would be doing everyone a disservice not putting SGA up here. This OKC Thunder team has stayed competitive even while down a few guys, and this matchup is solid. This should be one of the closer games on the slate, and with SGA locked into a huge usage role right now, it's going to be hard to avoid. The Blazers have some solid guys on defense, but have struggled against the guard position.
Luka Doncic went for 53, [Devin Booker](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001460/Devin+Booker) for 33, and [Jamal Murray](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001666/Jamal+Murray) for 35, all against Portland within the last four games. The rebounds and assists have been a bit lackluster this year for SGA, which hurts his chances a bit and may have people going to other spots.
