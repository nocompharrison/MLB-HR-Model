**Top NBA DFS Plays Today**  
  
Tonight's main slate has seven games. The best-looking game for scoring is LAL@ATL, which has the highest total (236.5) and is nearly a pick'em, with Doncic leading a potentially thin Lakers group. Four games carry spreads of 8.5 or more: CLE is an 8.5-point favorite at CHA, DET is a 10.5-point favorite over WAS, HOU is an 8.5-point favorite over DEN, and SAS is an 8.5-point favorite at PHX. 
[**Luka Doncic**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4396)**, LAL, $12,000, PG**
Doncic is the highest-upside play on the slate, and the case gets stronger if [Austin Reaves](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5334) sits. Reaves is listed as questionable and Grimes is already out, so the Lakers are thin on creators. Doncic has averaged 66.9 DK points over his last 9 games on 35.9 minutes, with games of 84.5 and 86.8 and none under 38. LAL@ATL has the highest total on the board (236.5) and is nearly a pick'em (LAL +1.5), so his minutes should stay high. If Reaves is out, his usage should rise further. If Reaves plays, Doncic is still a good play at this number.
[**Julian Champagnie**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5728)**, SAS, $4,500, SG**
Champagnie is a cheap way to get exposure to San Antonio. [Devin Vassell](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5113) and [Tobias Harris](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3211) are both out, so he likely starts. He's averaged 21.1 DK points on 25.7 minutes over his last 10 games and has a high of 37.8. You could also easily go [Keldon Johnson](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4782) here, but my instinct says, Keldon will see more ownership, and really has no more upside here than Champagnie. 
[**Jakob Poeltl**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3870)**, TOR, $4,900, C**
Poeltl is a cheap center with real upside. He's only averaged 19.5 DK points on 21.5 minutes over his last 10 games, but also has a 44.2-point high. MEM@TOR has a 229 total and TOR is only a 3-point underdog, so we should expect the game to remain competitive. [Collin Murray-Boyles](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6585) potentially being absent for this one, would also give Poeltl a boost.
