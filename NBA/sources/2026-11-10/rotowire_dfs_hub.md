**Top NBA DFS Plays Today**  
  
Five games on the main slate (MIA@UTA, MIL@NYK, ORL@SAC, BOS@PHI, LAC@PHX), and none of the totals jump off the page. MIA@UTA is highest at 228.5, the rest sit between 217.5 and 224.5, and BOS@PHI is the tightest game on the board (Boston -1.5). MIL@NYK is the one to be wary of: the Knicks are 15.5-point favorites, which puts starters' fourth-quarter minutes at risk.
The chalk is easy to spot. Utah's injuries have turned [Isaiah Collier](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6260) into a $4,400 starter, with [Ace Bailey](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6551) and [Brice Sensabaugh](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5937) also absorbing minutes. [Jaxson Hayes](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4771), [Brook Lopez](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=2918) and [Landry Shamet](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4414) are the other popular value plays. All of them are good to outstanding options, but the field is going to be all over them. The leverage tonight is in players with real roles and ceilings who won't be in every lineup, and these three come from three different games at three different price points.
###  [Joel Embiid](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3572), C, PHI | $8,400 DK
Embiid arguably carries the best ceiling on the slate without being a lock to be everywhere. He's averaging 39 DK points through nine games, with 49 and 42 in two of his last four, and when he's on the floor, he is the focal point of Philadelphia's offense. Scoring, rebounds, blocks, and free throws all add up for him. 
BOS@PHI has the lowest total on the slate (217.5), but it's also the closest game (Boston -1.5), which means Embiid should get a full workload. 
**Bottom line:** A true ceiling center priced below the top tier, in a close game, who the field will tend to underplay.
###  [Anthony Black](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5910), PG, ORL | $6,900 DK
Black has become the engine of Orlando's offense. He's averaging 35.4 DK points per game in 30 minutes, with 30+ minutes in four of his last six, and he posted 51 in 37.7 minutes at Golden State on Saturday. The production comes from everywhere: scoring, rebounds from the guard spot (7 or more in three of his last four), assists and steals.
Orlando is a 6.25-point favorite at Sacramento in a game with a 223.5 total, a spot where he should stay on the floor all night. The ceiling is around 50. 
**Bottom line:** A mid-priced guard with a 30+ minute role and a 50-point ceiling, in a game that should stay competitive.
###  [Klay Thompson](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3197), SG/SF, MIA | $4,900 DK
Thompson looks too cheap. He's likely starting for Miami; his minutes have gone from 17 to 35 and 32 over his last six games, and he's coming off 46 and 39 DK points in two of his last three. He's averaging 22.4 on the season, which wouldn't crush you at $4,900, and the recent minutes suggest that's closer to a floor than a ceiling.
MIA@UTA has the highest total on the slate (228.5), with the Heat favored by 8.5, so the environment helps. His upside comes from the threes and a big scoring night when the minutes are there. The risk is a Miami win that gets out of hand and cuts his minutes late.
**Bottom line:** A cheap starter in the best scoring environment, with minutes trending up. He'll draw some ownership, but not at the level of the Utah value plays.
