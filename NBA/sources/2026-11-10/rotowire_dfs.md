_DraftKings Sportsbook - 3:00 CT_
**Kirien Sprecher:**[Lauri Markkanen](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/lauri-markkanen-4119) will miss a second straight game due to a wrist injury, and [Keyonte George](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/keyonte-george-5899) won't be available for the first time this season after spraining his right ankle during Sunday's loss to Minnesota. Bailey has recorded multiple assists only twice over the past six games, but he totaled 12 dimes in his first four appearances.
