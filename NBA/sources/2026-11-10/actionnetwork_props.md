## Kentavious Caldwell-Pope Over 0.5 Three-Pointers Made (-120)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
By [Andrew O'Connor-Watts](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/andrew-oconnor-watts)
I am paying a little homage to my colleague Bryan Fonseca and backing Kentavious Caldwell-Pope to knock down a three-pointer tonight.
You might have to pay a little bit of [juice](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/juice) to keep the line at a flat one make, but he literally just needs one single three-pointer to drop for us to cash this ticket, and then you can order DoorDash with the winnings or buy a custom KCP jersey (though they might charge you per letter for that long last name).
KCP has gone over this number in all but one game this season. While his overall volume isn't particularly heavy because he operates in a very specific complementary role, he doesn't need many attempts when he is currently shooting 48 percent from beyond the arc.
I expect him to hit his minutes ceiling in this matchup. The only games where his minutes have dipped this season have been blowouts. In competitive games against quality opponents—and this game is lined at just 1.5 [points](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/points)—he consistently sees 20-plus minutes of action.
The matchup against Boston is perfect for his specific shot profile. The Celtics' defensive scheme essentially dares role players to shoot, operating with a philosophy that says you can have threes and nothing else.
Three-pointers are practically the only shots KCP actively hunts in this offense. He will get the run, the defense will give him the looks, and he only needs one to connect.

## Andre Drummond Over 6.5 Rebounds (+115)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
By [Joe Dellera](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/joe-dellera)
The [Knicks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/new-york-knicks) host the [Bucks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks) tonight, and this is a fantastic matchup for Andre Drummond against a Milwaukee squad that has been an absolute joke on the boards this season.
The Bucks play at a very fast pace and allow the second-most [rebounds](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/rebounds) per game to their opponents.
On top of the great matchup, the situational spot is ideal. Josh Hart is questionable again today. Considering this is the front end of a back-to-back for New York, with a much tougher test against the [Cavaliers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/cleveland-cavaliers) looming tomorrow, I’d expect Hart to sit tonight and return for Cleveland.
I like this rebounding prop regardless of Hart's status, but if he's officially out, it opens up significantly more chances for Drummond to clean up the glass.
We also have to look at the expected game script. The Knicks are massive 15.5-point favorites tonight. In all four of New York's double-digit wins this season, Drummond has grabbed at least eight boards.
If this turns into a blowout, Drummond should get plenty of run to dominate the paint. You can take over 6.5 at +115 on DraftKings, or play it a bit safer with the 6+ alternate line at -138.

## Wendell Carter Jr. Over 22.5 Points & Rebounds (-114)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Brandon Kravitz](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/brandon-kravitz)
I absolutely love the way Wendell Carter Jr. is playing this year; he seems way more aggressive, and in my view, he is playing harder than anyone else on the Orlando roster right now.
I fully expect the [Magic](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/orlando-magic) to smash in this game, and this profiles perfectly as a Wendell Carter Jr. takeover spot.
If you look at his game logs, you really don't need a master's degree to figure out his production trends. When he goes up against elite bigs that can impact the game on both ends—like Karl-Anthony Towns or Anthony Davis—he gets worn out, falls into foul trouble, and struggles to produce.
However, when he goes up against teams that lack those skilled bigs on the interior, he absolutely feasts. He has already gone over this 22.5 line in five of his nine games this season, including cashing his PR prop in his last two outings.
Tonight, Sacramento has very little size to contend with him in the paint, a problem that will be amplified if Domantas Sabonis misses the game.
The Kings are already vulnerable when it comes to paint points allowed and rebound percentage, and Carter is built to exploit those weaknesses.
I debated playing his [double-double](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/offensive-props) prop, but isolating the combined points & rebounds is my favorite way to back a guy who should physically dominate the restricted area tonight.
**Pick:[Wendell Carter Jr. Over 22.5 Points & Rebounds](https://playbookbot.com/books/draftkings/passthrough?deeplinkId\[0\]=ML3189648984&context=action-articles) (-114)**

## Shai Gilgeous-Alexander Over 32.5 Points (-114)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Andrew O'Connor-Watts](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/andrew-oconnor-watts)
Shai Gilgeous-Alexander has actually stayed under this line in his last two games, but he is still quietly averaging nearly 30 points per night, and this specific matchup is simply too good to ignore.
SGA is the undisputed master of the mid-range jumper, and the [Trail Blazers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/portland-trail-blazers) consistently concede a high volume of looks from that area.
When you look at the games where SGA has cleared this total this season, he hasn't just barely sneaked over—he has obliterated the number, dropping 42 and 43 against Cleveland, 34 against the [Clippers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/los-angeles-clippers), and 34 against the [Spurs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/san-antonio-spurs).
The common denominator among all those opponents is that they structurally give up mid-range looks.
Portland's defense is predicated on drop coverage, particularly when Donovan Clingan is anchoring the paint. Clingan is a tremendous defender, but sinking him deep into a drop coverage opens up the intermediate zone for SGA to operate.
We are also seeing a clear rotational trend from Portland with Robert Williams' minutes trending down while Clingan's are going up, likely forcing the Blazers to stay in drop coverage for longer stretches.
Not to mention, Portland tends to foul at a very high rate. While SGA's free-throw volume has dipped a bit this season compared to previous years, that lack of defensive discipline provides him with a secondary path to manufacture easy points.
This feels like a spot where we might see another 40-point game from the reigning [MVP](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/futures/nba-mvp-odds).
**Pick:[Shai Gilgeous-Alexander Over 32.5 Points](https://playbookbot.com/books/draftkings/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3189458348&context=action-articles) (-114)**
