## **NBA DFS Picks: DraftKings & FanDuel DFS Primer**

### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Jalen Johnson (PF – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-johnson-f-f.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $9,500**
  * **FanDuel: $9,800**


Jalen Johnson is the most expensive option on the board today, but it’s perfectly OK to splurge on a small slate. He is coming off a huge game against the Miami Heat — 12 points, 11 assists and nine rebounds, just missing his first triple-double of the season.
Johnson hasn’t shot the ball particularly well this season — 42.6% from the field and only 15.8% from downtown. But he is averaging 21.5 points, 9.8 rebounds and 8.3 assists through five games. You’re paying a high price, but you get what you pay for — a lot of production.
#### **[Domantas Sabonis (C – SAC)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/domantas-sabonis.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $9,200**
  * **FanDuel: $9,200**


Domantas Sabonis is a strong play, and he is pulling down rebounds at a feverish pace. He has 14+ rebounds in each of his four games and at least eight assists in three of those outings. He’s also averaging 19 points per game.
Sabonis’ production — 19 points, 16.5 rebounds and 7.3 assists per game — offers amazing value at his price tag, especially on a short slate. The Pelicans have allowed at least 120 points in three out of four games, so Sabonis is poised to go off again.
#### **[Donovan Mitchell (SG – CLE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/donovan-mitchell.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $8,600**
  * **FanDuel: $8,600**


Donovan Mitchell and the Cavaliers are playing their second road game of the season. In the first game last Thursday in Philadelphia, Mitchell went for 32 points on 11-of-19 shooting, while adding 10 rebounds, three assists, two steals and a block, and he has hit at least two three-pointers in all four outings.
Mitchell will face a Hawks team with an opponent rank of 20, so look for the guard to shine in this road test in Atlanta.

### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[Ja Morant (PG, SG – POR)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/ja-morant.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $6,400**
  * **FanDuel: $6,600**


It’s still strange to list ‘POR’ for Ja Morant’s team, since it seems like he’s been with Memphis forever. And, as luck would have it, the Trail Blazers host the Grizzlies on Thursday night, so Morant gets to face his former team.
Morant had his best game of the season on the road against the Los Angeles Lakers on Tuesday, scoring 23 points on 9-of-13 shooting in 31 minutes in a 136-129 win, as the Trail Blazers remain unbeaten through three games. Facing his former team, you have to roster him, especially on a short night and at a very reasonable price.
#### **[Dyson Daniels (PG, SF – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/dyson-daniels.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $6,500**
  * **FanDuel: $6,500**


Also, at a mid-tier price, Dyson Daniels is a strong option against a giving Cavaliers team. He has struggled in the past two games, which has sent his DFS salary nosediving. Daniels is shooting just 33.3% (5-of-15) from the field, averaging 5.5 points, 5.5 assists, two rebounds and two steals in the past two outings.
But we know what Daniels can do. In his first three games, Daniels averaged 16.4 points, seven rebounds, 3.7 assists and 4.3 steals per game. If he can come close to replicating those numbers, he’ll be a steal at this price point.
#### **[Derik Queen (PF, C – NOP)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/derik-queen.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $6,300**
  * **FanDuel: $5,900**


Derik Queen will see a bump in run and usage against the Sacramento Kings, with [Yves Missi](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/yves-missi.php) (knee) sidelined with a left knee bone bruise.
Queen had 11 points and 12 rebounds in 25 minutes against the Denver Nuggets at the Smoothie King Center in the second game of a home-and-home. He has scored at least 10 points and grabbed seven or more rebounds in all four games, whether he starts or comes off the bench. Queen has shot 50% from the field in three of his four outings.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[Jarrett Allen (C – CLE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jarrett-allen.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $6,000**
  * **FanDuel: $6,200**


Jarrett Allen posted his lowest point total of the season last time out against Washington, going 3-for-6 for just eight points. But he grabbed 12 rebounds, with three blocks, two assists and a steal in 30 minutes.
In his first three games, Allen averaged 15 points, eight rebounds, and two blocks. Allen faces a Hawks team with an opponent rank of 26. He has averaged 31.6 fantasy points per game, which makes him a steal at this price.
#### **[CJ McCollum (PG, SG – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/cj-mccollum.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $5,600**
  * **FanDuel: $5,400**


CJ McCollum will face a Cavaliers defense with an opponent rank of 27. Cleveland has allowed at least 111 points in all four games this season.
McCollum is in the bargain bin, but he still has upside. The 35-year-old is in a bit of a rebirth, scoring 13+ points in all five games and hitting at least one three-pointer in each outing, averaging 2.4 threes per game. He doesn’t contribute much in other counting stats, but his scoring is enough.
#### **[Aaron Wiggins (SG – ATL)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/aaron-wiggins.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $4,900**
  * **FanDuel: $4,600**


Aaron Wiggins is a cheap bench producer for the Hawks and a solid DFS option.
The former Maryland guard has logged at least 10 points in all five of his games, averaging 14.6 points, and he is good for 3.4 rebounds, 1.8 assists and 1.2 steals per game. He has knocked down at least two three-pointers in all five of his outings, too, averaging 2.8 threes per game. That’s great production at this price.

### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Jerami Grant (SF, PF – MEM)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jerami-grant.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $4,400**
  * **FanDuel: $4,000**


Jerami Grant can put up a solid scoring performance in favorable matchups, but he can also be a little erratic. That’s the rub, but at this price point, it’s worth a roll of the dice. He scored 13 points on three shots from beyond the arc last time out, and he has at least one blocked shot in all four outings.
#### **[Meleek Thomas (SG – CLE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/meleek-thomas.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $3,800**
  * **FanDuel: $3,700**


Meleek Thomas might not be the first player people think of when discussing the Cavaliers, but he could exceed DFS salary expectations. The 20-year-old rookie has appeared in two games, and he is shooting 58.8% (10-of-17) from the field, averaging 13 points, three rebounds and one steal.
#### **[DeAndre Jordan (C – NOP)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/deandre-jordan.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $3,800**
  * **FanDuel: $4,100**


The veteran pivot is still kicking around down in the Crescent City. DeAndre Jordan has averaged 16.5 minutes per game, both coming in a home-and-home with the Nuggets. He had nine points, nine rebounds, two assists and a block on Saturday in Denver, so he could surprise at this low price.
