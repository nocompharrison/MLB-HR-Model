## NBA Best Bets, Player Props & Picks for Saturday, Oct. 31
## Philadelphia 76ers vs Charlotte Hornets: [Brandon Miller](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/brandon-miller-5896) over 26.5 points, -127 @ Betr
Unsurprisingly, Miller has emerged as Charlotte's primary offensive option early in the 2026-27 season following the offseason trade that sent [LaMelo Ball](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/lamelo-ball-5151) to the Minnesota Timberwolves. He's averaging 24.5 points through six appearances, and although that figure sits below Saturday's 26.5-point threshold, his recent performances suggest considerable upside. The forward has surpassed the 30-point mark in three of his last four appearances and scored a team-high 19 points in an NBA Cup 95-79 loss to the Wizards on Friday,
His shooting volume is particularly encouraging. Miller has attempted at least 24 field goals in four straight contests and at least 20 in all but one of his six games. He's also attempted at least 10 three-pointers in every game, so the shooting upside is there. 
The matchup is tough, as the 76ers have been very good defensively to open the season, but Miller should have enough volume to hover around this line. Charlotte still relies heavily on him even when his shots aren't falling. Another high-volume performance could push him beyond 26.5 points against Philadelphia.
## Atlanta Hawks vs. New Orleans Pelicans: [Jalen Johnson](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/jalen-johnson-5329) over 41.5 points + rebounds + assists, -120 @ DraftKings
Johnson's versatility makes him an intriguing candidate to exceed 41.5 combined points, rebounds and assists against New Orleans. Even though his season-long averages are below this threshold, as Johnson enters this game averaging a combined 37.0 P+R+A with 19.2 points, 9.3 rebounds and 8.5 assists per game, the matchup offers encouragement.
That's because New Orleans has allowed opponents to shoot 49.7 percent from the field through five games, one of the league's weakest defensive marks. Atlanta, meanwhile, leads the NBA with 32.5 assists per game, creating a favorable environment for Johnson's playmaking.
The primary concern is consistency: Johnson has exceeded 41.5 PRA only once in six games. However, his substantial offensive responsibilities and ability to contribute without relying exclusively on scoring provide a credible path to the over.
## Sacramento Kings vs. Memphis Grizzlies: [Darius Acuff](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/darius-acuff-6931) over 2.5 three-pointers, -122 @ FanDuel
Acuff has quickly established himself as an important perimeter option for Sacramento, and his recent performances suggest the rookie is trending in the right direction. Acuff has connected on three three-pointers in each of his last two appearances, against San Antonio and New Orleans, after making just four combined triples across his first three games. He's coming off the best performance of his career with a 34-point, 14-assist outing against the Pelicans on Oct. 29.
The matchup against Memphis offers an opportunity to maintain that momentum. The Grizzlies have allowed opponents to make an average of 13.9 three-pointers per game, so the Kings should have enough room to let it fly, but Memphis has limited opponents to just 30.3 percent from deep, the fourth-lowest mark in the NBA.
Acuff made only two three-pointers against Memphis on October 25, despite playing 36 minutes and has cleared this line in just two of his five games, but given that he's been trending in the right direction of late, I'm backing the rookie to build on his career-best 34-point effort against New Orleans to surpass this line once again. He's drained at least three threes in his last two outings.
