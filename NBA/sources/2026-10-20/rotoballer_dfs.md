## **NBA DFS Core Play**
**[Karl-Anthony Towns](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001431/Karl-Anthony+Towns), NYK - PF/C**
  * **DraftKings: $7.8K**
  * **FanDuel: $7.7K**


The easy click tonight among the star players is KAT. While a lot of players are underpriced on this opening slate, he's at least $1,000 cheaper than he will be most of the season.
While his scoring has gone down in New York, his rebounding has gone up as he moved back to center from power forward. He also was featured as a hub on offense quite extensively last year in the playoffs and this preseason.
[Mitchell Robinson](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002016/Mitchell+Robinson) is now in Boston, and I think the Knicks have given up on the big lineups where they move KAT to the four to play alongside another big man, like Robinson's replacement [Andre Drummond](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000810/Andre+Drummond).
His rebounding and passing give him a high floor tonight, and he was really good against [Joel Embiid](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000726/Joel+Embiid) last year in the playoffs.
**_Also consider:[Jayson Tatum](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001829/Jayson+Tatum), BOS - PF (not on FD main slate)_**
## **NBA DFS Value Play**
**[Dylan Harper](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003445/Dylan+Harper), SAS - PG/SG**
  * **DraftKings: $5.1K**
  * **FanDuel: $5.2K**


This price is just silly for Harper, who now slides into the starting lineup for the Spurs this season. We were all hoping it would happen, and it didn't take San Antonio long to make the decision to get their five best players on the court together. His performance in the playoffs was eye-opening, and you could argue he's easily their second-most important player on the roster after [Victor Wembanyama](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003031/Victor+Wembanyama).
The three-guard lineup could be an experiment, but I am willing to bet they stick with it, which means Harper is looking at 30 minutes a night. He's going to crush this price tag with those minutes.
On a really small slate, he'll get extremely chalky. But he's the good chalk that we want in all our lineups tonight.
**_Also consider:[Paul Reed](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002584/Paul+Reed), DET - C_**
