## Jalen Smith Rebounds Escalator

Jalen Smith 9+ Rebounds (-125) | 10+ Rebounds (+126) | 11+ Rebounds (+192)
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Jalen Smith is playing like much more than a fill-in starter.
The [Chicago Bulls](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/chicago-bulls) big is largely viewed as a stand-in for [Nic Claxton](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/nic-claxton-109927-c) following a preseason hamstring injury, but his excellent play has kept him in the first unit. He's hauling in a career and team-high 8.1 rebounds per game in 2026-27. His interior presence is even more important with [Caleb Wilson](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/caleb-wilson-337991-pf) sidelined.
Smith has logged at least 30 minutes in four straight contests and has racked up 23 rebounds in the two games Wilson has missed this season.
Claxton has eclipsed 20 minutes only once this season in four outings, and his workload is being managed carefully. His recovery window will give Smith plenty of opportunities, and he is averaging 11.7 rebounds per 36 minutes for a second consecutive season.
Smith has already hit the figure at the top of this ladder five times in 12 games this campaign.
This ladder is more attainable than some of my previous options. One unit on the first leg and at least half a unit on each of the remaining ones seems reasonable.

## Nickeil Alexander-Walker Points Escalator
[![Hawks Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/atl.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/atlanta-hawks)
Wednesday, Nov 18

[![Thunder Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/okc.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/oklahoma-city-thunder)
Nickeil Alexander-Walker 22+ Points (-112) | 25+ Points (+150) | 30+ Points (+383)
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
[Nickeil Alexander-Walker](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/nickeil-alexander-walker-109968-pg) could be in for a battle against his cousin on Wednesday.
[Kingston Flemings](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/kingston-flemings-337995-pg) and [CJ McCollum](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/cj-mccollum-289-pg) have been ruled out for the [Atlanta Hawks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/atlanta-hawks) meeting with the [Oklahoma City Thunder](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/oklahoma-city-thunder). Alexander-Walker will likely carry a heavy offensive workload with those two out.
Alexander-Walker is already averaging career highs in points (21.4), field goal attempts (17.0), and three-point attempts per game (10.4) this season.
I expect him to exceed all three of those figures against OKC. He averaged 27.0 points per game in the two contests he appeared in without Flemings this season, and McCollum adds another layer to the Hawks' lack of backcourt depth.
The Thunder still have a respectable defense without [Jalen Williams](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/jalen-williams-198201-pf) and [Chet Holmgren](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/chet-holmgren-198219-c). However, Alexander-Walker has scored at least 30 points in two of his six career starts against [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/shai-gilgeous-alexander-87378-pg).
Atlanta will need a lift, and Alexander-Walker can provide one by scoring more than 29 points for the third time this season.
