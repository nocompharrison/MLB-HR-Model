## Cade Cunningham Under 39.5 Points, Rebounds & Assists (-120)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Michael Fiddle](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/michael-fiddle)
Initially, I was looking at fading Cade Cunningham’s [double-double](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/offensive-props) prop here, because I simply don’t think he’s right physically. He’s dealing with knee soreness, and on film, he doesn't look like he’s generating his usual separation.
I dug into the beat writer quotes from Detroit’s last game, and while there isn't a ton of official detail out there, I do expect Cade to play—just at less than 100%.
However, looking at his last box score against the [Hawks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/atlanta-hawks) actually made me pivot away from the double-double fade. Cade finished with just five [points](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/points) in that game, yet he still racked up 15 [assists](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/assists).
Because he isn't moving at full strength, he’s clearly leaning into his playmaking. That gives him a high enough assist ceiling that fading a double-double becomes risky.
Instead, that exact dynamic makes the Under 38.5 Points, Rebounds, and Assists a smarter play. When Cade is operating more as a distributor than a scorer, he only picks up one tally on the stat sheet for an assist rather than two or three points for scoring the basket himself.
There are some 37.5s and 38.5s out there in the market that have been bet up to 39.5, so I am fading market directionality here. Still, I love Cade Under 39.5 PRA.
This projects to be a low-scoring, physical game against Orlando, and with Cade playing sub-100% through a knee issue, I’m fading his combined production.

## Neemias Queta Over 1.5 Assists (-135)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Bryan Fonseca](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/bryan-fonseca)
First, look at the matchup: the [Wizards](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/washington-wizards) allow the most assists per 100 possessions in the entire NBA at 29. Second, look at the recent form. Queta has cleared 1.5 assists in nine of his 16 games this season, and going back to November 8—roughly the last two weeks—he’s gone over this mark in six of his last seven games.
Queta even has multiple four-assist games on his resume this year, and he’s clearly outplaying Mitchell Robinson for his spot in the rotation.
It’s not just fluke numbers, either—it’s how Boston is utilizing him. Queta is a very willing passer for a big man, and there are multiple avenues for him to rack up assists in this offense.
We saw it against Denver: he’ll catch the ball in the short roll and hit a cutter like Hugo Gonzalez going to the basket, or he’ll get two feet in the paint and kick it out to an open shooter like Payton Pritchard for three.
With how much Boston loves to spray corner threes, plus the dribble handoffs he runs with guys like Derrick White pulling up in the midrange, the opportunities are going to be there.
I’m not going to ladder it, but I wouldn't be surprised at all if he finishes with three or four assists against this Wizards defense tonight.

## P.J. Washington Over 1.5 Three-Pointers Made (-150)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Matt Moore](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/matt-moore)
Old reliable: the [Nuggets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/denver-nuggets) allow a ton of corner threes, and PJ Washington makes a ton of corner threes. End of cap.
Seriously, it is remarkable how consistent this angle has been over the last two seasons. I just bet Matas Buzelis—who isn't exactly a lights out shooter—in the Nuggets' last game for this exact reason, simply because he’s the [Bulls](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/chicago-bulls)' primary corner shooter.
I’m usually hesitant to play alternate lines because I tend to whiff on them in magnificent fashion, but if you’re ever going to ladder a prop, targeting corner shooters against Denver is as easy as it gets.
The reason this continues to be profitable is schematic, and it’s such a narrow slice of data that sportsbooks just aren't weighing it heavily enough.
Denver is back to playing high at the level of the screen this season, putting Nikola Jokic up top and putting two defenders on the ball. The problem is that when Aaron Gordon, Jamal Murray—who has actually been really good defensively—and Christian Braun fight over the top of that screen, they can't close the gap fast enough.
That leaves two easy reads for the offense. Either the ball-handler hits the short roll, which forces Denver’s weak-side corner defender to come over and tag the roller so he doesn't get a free dunk, leaving the corner shooter wide open. Or, a ball-handler like Cooper Flagg simply throws an overhead pass straight to the corner out of the blitz for a clean catch-and-shoot look.
This has maintained its efficiency for two straight years. So, I'll back PJ Washington to hit at least two threes against Denver tonight.
#### **Pick:[P.J. Washington Over 1.5 Three-Pointers Made](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3243742929&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-150)**

## Javonte Green Over 5.5 Points (-110)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Bryan Fonseca](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/bryan-fonseca)
The [Pistons](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/detroit-pistons) come into this one having lost two straight games, and while Cade Cunningham is probable—though he didn't look great against Atlanta—they are still missing Jalen Duren, Taurean Prince, and Duncan Robinson.
With all those guys out, somebody has to shoot from the corners and the wings for Detroit.
Orlando’s defense is going to be locked in on Cade Cunningham and John Collins, which means they may just let Javonte Green knock himself out and put up six, seven, or eight shots.
The way Detroit is structured right now around these injuries, they need Green to give them 18 to 20 competent minutes.
The shot profile is right where we want it. Green has 60 three-point attempts on the season, averaging roughly four per game, with approximately 75% of his total shot attempts coming from beyond the arc.
On top of that, he sneakily gets to the free-throw line by attacking closeouts—he’s averaging almost two free-throw attempts per game and knocking them down at a 92% clip.
So, he doesn't even need to catch fire from deep; a three-pointer, a drive, and a couple of free throws gets us home.
Green has scored over 5.5 points in four straight games and finished with five points in each of the two games prior to that, meaning he’s over in six of his last eight and right on the doorstep in the two unders.
I’m riding with Green here.
#### **Pick:[Javonte Green Over 5.5 Points](https://playbookbot.com/books/bet365/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3242798559&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-110)**
