### **NBA DFS Core Plays**
#### **[Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (PG – OKC)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/shai-gilgeous-alexander.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $9,900**
  * **FanDuel: $10,200**


We saw SGA and the Thunder in action already, taking down the San Antonio Spurs on the road Tuesday, 116-104. Gilgeous-Alexander was good for 32 points on 13-of-27 shooting, while hitting three triples. He also added six rebounds, four assists, and three steals with three blocks in his 36 minutes.
We haven’t seen the Nuggets in action yet, but we know what we’re going to get. [Nikola Jokic](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/nikola-jokic.php) gums up the paint, so your best bet to take down the Nuggets is perimeter shooting, and no one does it better than SGA these days.
#### **[Tyrese Maxey (PG – PHI)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/tyrese-maxey.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $8,000**
  * **FanDuel: $8,500**


If you’re in a salary crunch, and just can’t squeeze SGA in your lineup, Maxey is a tremendous backup option. Of course, there is way to fit both of them into your lineup, too, on this light schedule night with just two games on tap.
Maxey was fabulous in a 106-100 win at New York against the defending champs on Tuesday, going for 28 points, five assists, four rebounds, three blocks, and three steals, while hitting a pair of 3-pointers. He was just 9-of-19 from the field, and 2-of-9 from downtown. If he tightens up his shot, he could be even more dangerous.
#### **[Joel Embiid (C – PHI)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/joel-embiid.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $7,700**
  * **FanDuel: $8,300**


Embiid is healthy, for now, and he has a tremendous supporting cast around him. He had a bit of an off-shooting night on Tuesday at Madison Square Garden, going 4-of-15 from the field to finish with 13 points, 11 rebounds, six assists, three blocks, and two steals on 33 minutes.
When he is in there, especially with an All-Star caliber starting five around him, Embiid has the potential to put up a double-double every night as long as his back and knee hold up.

### **NBA DFS Cash Game Targets**
#### **[LeBron James (PF – PHI)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/lebron-james.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $7,000**
  * **FanDuel: $7,800**


You’re going to have to pick some other players not on the 76ers, but, come on. You HAVE to take LeBron in an ESPN game against his original team, the Cavaliers, and the team he spurned in free agency to sign with Philadelphia, right?
LeBron made his Sixers debut Tuesday at MSG, going for 14 points, nine rebounds, and seven assists with a steal and a block in 34 minutes. As long as his sciatica doesn’t start acting up, LeBron has the potential to put up some tremendous stat lines as long as his supporting cast stays healthy around him, and LeBron doesn’t have to shoulder more of the load.
#### **[Jalen Williams (SF – OKC)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jalen-williams.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $6,400**
  * **FanDuel: $6,800**


Williams is a great play with a nice price tag in Tuesday’s matchup against the Nuggets, who are making their season debut as a team.
Williams misfired on 11 of his 16 field-goal attempts, so that wasn’t great for DFS players on Tuesday in San Antonio. However, Williams was good for 12 points, seven rebounds, and two assists with a block and a steal in his 30 minutes. And, just as a reminder, don’t mix him up with [Jaylin Williams](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jaylin-williams.php), who comes off the bench, and is a much lesser player in terms of overall production.
#### **[Chet Holmgren (PF – OKC)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/chet-holmgren.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $5,800**
  * **FanDuel: $7,000**


Between the two platforms, there is a giant salary disparity, so choose wisely. Holmgren is an especially good value on DraftKings.
Holmgren opened the season with 14 points on 3-of-8 shooting in San Antonio in the 116-104 victory Tuesday in the opener. He was able to grab seven rebounds with four blocks and two assists while turning it over just one time. He managed to swat four shots despite working against [Victor Wembanyama](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/victor-wembanyama.php) of the Spurs, so don’t be dissuaded with Holmgren working down low against The Joker.

### **NBA DFS GPP Targets**
#### **[VJ Edgecombe (PG/SG – PHI)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/vj-edgecombe.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $5,800**
  * **FanDuel: $5,400**


The second-year player Edgecombe really came into his own last season, and he’ll benefit from being surrounded by LeBron, Embiid, Maxey, and Brown in an All-Star lineup. It will be hard for the good habits and play not to rub off on Edgecombe.
Edgecombe was good for nine points on 4-of-10 shooting in 35 minutes, and he spent a little bit of time in foul trouble. He added four rebounds, one assist, and one steal while misfiring on all three of his triple attempts. His struggles the other night caused a dip in his salary, so the loss of DFS managers Tuesday is the gain of those on Thursday.
#### **[Peyton Watson (SG/SF – CLE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/peyton-watson.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $4,700**
  * **FanDuel: $5,300**


Watson makes his regular-season debut for the Cavaliers after an offseason acquisition from the Denver Nuggets. Once LeBron decided to go to Philadelphia, Watson become Plan B. And, honestly, he is a much better option for a young nucleus which is trying to build something long-term, not just win now, then blow things up.
The 24-year-old from UCLA went for 14.6 points, 4.9 rebounds, and 2.1 assists, which were all career highs across 40 starts and 54 games in Denver last season. He also shot 49.1% from the field, and 41.1% from behind the 3-point line. Look for Watson to hit the ground running in The Land.
#### **[Cason Wallace (SG/SF – OKC)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/cason-wallace.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $4,100**
  * **FanDuel: $4,200**


Wallace is quite a solid value play on a light night, and his inclusion will help you be able to squeeze in some of the top-priced options.
Wallace was in the starting five in San Antonio on Tuesday, going for six points with three rebounds and two assists while adding a steal. He misfired on his four 3-pointer tries, and he spent the night in a little bit of foul trouble, too. If he gets his three to fall, and he avoids the ref’s whistle, he could easily outproduce his projections.

### **NBA DFS Value Plays**
#### **[Anfernee Simons (PG/SG – PHI)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/anfernee-simons.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $3,500**
  * **FanDuel: $4,000**


Simons didn’t arrive in Philadelphia with quite the same kind of fanfare as Brown, LeBron, etc., but he had a pretty strong debut. In 25 minutes off the bench in New York, Simons hit 5-of-10 from the field, and 3-of-8 from the perimeter, posting 13 points off the bench. That would be great production at this price level.
#### **[Jared McCain (SG – OKC)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jared-mccain.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $3,800**
  * **FanDuel: $4,100**


In Tuesday’s win in San Antonio, McCain was one of just two players to go for 20+ points. He was extremely efficient, hitting 7-of-9 from the field, and 6-of-8 from behind the 3-point line, going for 20 points in 22 minutes off the bench.
#### **[Jaylon Tyson (SG/SF – CLE)](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jaylon-tyson.php)**
  * **DraftKings: $3,600**
  * **FanDuel: $4,100**


Tyson is worth a look, as he could slide into the starting lineup in place of [Jarrett Allen](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/players/jarrett-allen.php) (knee), who is a question make to play in the season opener due to a knee injury. If Tyson starts, he’ll be a fabulous value at this price point.
