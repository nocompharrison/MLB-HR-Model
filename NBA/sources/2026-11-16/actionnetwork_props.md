## Jamal Murray Points Escalator
[![Suns Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/875040_Suns.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/phoenix-suns)

[![Nuggets Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/725847_nba_den_150x150.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/denver-nuggets)
Jamal Murray 25+ Points | 30+ Points | 35+ Points
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
The Denver [Nuggets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/denver-nuggets) have a new leading scorer, and he could be primed for another huge night on Monday.
Jamal Murray will enter his matchup with the [Suns](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/phoenix-suns) averaging a team-high 25.9 points per game on ridiculous shooting splits. He's shooting 51.3% from the field and 49.4% from beyond the arc.
Denver has needed to call on him in big moments, as 2026-27 is shaping up to be a down year for Nikola Jokic. That's resulted in more than a couple of impressive stat lines for Murray.
Murray has eclipsed 34 points four times in 11 outings. Getting to the line was a major factor in three of those occasions, as he made 10 or more shots from the charity stripe in his first three games with at least 35 points.
Phoenix has struggled with foul discipline early on. The Suns are allowing 21.7 made free throws per game. Seven teams have been worse so far this campaign, but Denver is a borderline top-five team in free-throw attempts per game (26.5).
Murray will attack that weakness as Denver's leading free-throw shooter (5.7 attempts per contest). Especially with Dillon Brooks operating as the Suns' primary point-of-attack defender.
Brooks' quick temper and tendency to get into early foul trouble have helped Murray average 28.7 points per game against Phoenix (through three matchups) since he joined the team.

## Ayo Dosunmu Points Escalator
[![Trail Blazers Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/312105_Blazers.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/portland-trail-blazers)
Monday, Nov 16
8 p.m. ET
DAZN-MIN
[![Timberwolves Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/717650_min.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/minnesota-timberwolves)
Ayo Dosunmu 18+ Points | 20+ Points | 25+ Points
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Ayo Dosunmu is playing out of his mind once again. He played his way into an extension in 2025-26 when the [Timberwolves](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/minnesota-timberwolves) were shorthanded, and now he's proving that the money was well spent.
Anthony Edwards is out of commission with a knee injury, and LaMelo Ball is running the offense. However, Dosunmu has been Minnesota's leading scorer over its last nine games. The Illinois product has averaged 21.4 points per game while shooting 54.5% from the field and 45.2% from deep without Edwards.
Dosunmu has comfortably been the Timberwolves' most efficient and effective scorer — and his team will need his spark against a [Trail Blazers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/portland-trail-blazers) team that ranks third in team points per game (119.6).
Dosunmu has eclipsed 25 points three times in his previous nine outings, averaging 4.3 more minutes per game than Ball, who can be a liability because of foul trouble and costly [turnovers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/offensive-props).
Dosunmu will likely operate as Minnesota's lead guard for brief stretches. His shot volume (15.1 attempts per game) and production will make him hard to pass on here, even against one of the league's better defenses.
