## Bulls vs Cavaliers NBA Preview
[![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/chi.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/chicago-bulls)
### Chicago Bulls Betting Preview
I haven't voluntarily watched a ton of [Bulls](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/chicago-bulls) basketball this year, but their statistical profile is glaring.
The Bulls are currently number one in the NBA in possessions per game. They play fast, reckless basketball, and they have no problem pushing the pace even if they are down by 20 points.
With [Norman Powell](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/norman-powell-146-pg) back in the lineup and [Josh Giddey](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/josh-giddey-171025-pg) starting to play better, they have some offensive rhythm early in games. Defensively, however, they are a disaster, ranking in the bottom eight in defensive efficiency.
I don't trust Chicago to hold up for a full 48 minutes here, but the Bulls will come out of the gates sprinting no matter what.
* * *
[![Header First Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/408598_cleveland1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/cleveland-cavaliers)
### Cleveland Cavaliers Betting Preview
The [Cavaliers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/cleveland-cavaliers) are also sitting in the bottom eight in defensive efficiency, a problem compounded by the absence of [Evan Mobley](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/evan-mobley-170983-c). Without him, their interior defense is very vulnerable.
This handicap requires a bit of amateur psychology. The Cavs are coming off a crazy game on Saturday night where [Tyrese Proctor](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/tyrese-proctor-307480-pg) went off for 24 points, 11 rebounds, and 10 assists.
When a team expends that much emotional energy, they typically come out flat and lethargic defensively in their next contest. I expect Cleveland to sleepwalk through the opening frame before eventually waking up to trade baskets.
* * *
## Bulls vs Cavaliers Pick, Betting Analysis
The betting market is notoriously stubborn and lazy when pricing derivatives; oddsmakers simply take the full-game total (around 241) and chop it into quarters without accounting for early-game lethargy, pace, or rotation patterns.
The Bulls are going to run from the opening tip, and the Cavaliers' defense will be flat without Mobley.
I expect the Bulls to easily clear 30 points in the first quarter, and the Cavs will score enough against Chicago's flawed defense to cash this over before the game eventually slows down in the second half.
I am playing the first quarter over 59.5 points tonight.
