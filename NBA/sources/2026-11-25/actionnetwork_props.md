Mikel Brown Jr. 15+ Points (-153) | 18+ Points (+131) | 20+ Points (+203) 
[![Fanatics Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/573837_FanaticsSportsbook20ALT.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanatics)
The Brooklyn [Nets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/brooklyn-nets) have added a potent piece to their starting lineup.
Rookie guard Mikel Brown Jr. made his first career start against the Minnesota [Timberwolves](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/minnesota-timberwolves) on Monday and tallied 17 [points](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/points) on a season-high 29 minutes. He'll be asked to take on more responsibility moving forward, now that Nolan Troare is out for eight to 10 weeks with a hamstring strain.
Brown has been especially good at shooting the long ball in November. He is shooting 40.9% from deep this month on 5.9 attempts per contest and averaged 10.4 field goal attempts per game through seven November outings before logging 29 minutes in a blowout loss in his latest appearance.
His volume will likely climb in closer matchups, and he enters Wednesday's game against the Milwaukee [Bucks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks), the league's third-worst scoring defense, averaging 19.1 points per 36 minutes.
Brown's shot profile makes him a strong option against a Bucks defense that often struggles with perimeter assignments. Only three teams have given up more made triples per game (14.8). He can set a new career high after scoring at least 17 points in five of his last 10 games.

## Victor Wembanyama Rebounds Ladder

[![Trail Blazers Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/312105_Blazers.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/portland-trail-blazers)
Victor Wembanyama 12+ Rebounds (-143) | 13+ Rebounds (+103) | 14+ Rebounds (+153)
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/128508_Primary1.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Victor Wembanyama is having his best rebounding year (12.3 [rebounds](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/rebounds) per game) yet in 2026-27. He can continue his hot November run on the glass against the Portland [Trail Blazers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/portland-trail-blazers) this Wednesday.
Wemby took a game off against the [Kings](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/sacramento-kings) on Monday, but will be fresh for his team's next matchup. Donovan Clingan is a tough matchup, but Dylan Harper's absence will likely affect the [Spurs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/san-antonio-spurs) star's rebounding. Harper is San Antonio's second-leading rebounder with 5.4 boards per game.
Wemby has been increasing his activity when the second-year guard isn't available. The reigning Defensive Player of the Year has tallied 14.5 rebounds per game through two appearances without Harper this year. He also averaged 12.4 rebounds per game in nine outings without Harper in 2025-26.
Wemby's been especially active on the defensive glass lately. That's fueled his rebounding average's climb to 13.2 through 11 games in November. He's yet to eclipse 10 rebounds against Clingan, but Julian Champagnie's perimeter-oriented game will likely call for more assertiveness from Wemby down low. He's notched 18 rebounds against two top-10 rebounding teams in his previous five games.
