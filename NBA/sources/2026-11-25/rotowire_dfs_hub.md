**Top NBA DFS Plays Today**
Three games tonight: Pistons at Magic, Celtics at Wizards, and Mavericks at Nuggets. The Nuggets game has the highest total on the board, and it comes with the slate's biggest salary at $11,700, [Nikola Jokic](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3612). He has the highest ceiling on the slate, and on a three-game slate he's a tough fade. If he goes off, anyone without him is chasing.
The problem is the price. There's a lot of star power around him: [Jayson Tatum](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4108), [Cooper Flagg](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6548), [Cade Cunningham](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5336) (listed probable), [Franz Wagner](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=5370), [Anthony Davis](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3297) and [Kyrie Irving](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=3186). Cheap value is thin, and fitting Jokic means finding a few cheap plays that actually produce. Building without him is reasonable if you'd rather spread your salary across those other studs. Check late lineup news either way, since PJ Washington is questionable for Dallas.
[**Cooper Flagg**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6548)**, SF, Mavericks, $9,500.** He plays about 33 minutes with a usage rate around 31%, as the main driver of Dallas' offense. The game has the highest total on the board, and a star-tier salary needs a big score to pay off, so his ceiling is what matters. He's a star-tier player who won't be the most popular one.
[**Kyshawn George**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=6269)**, SG, Wizards, $6,400.** He's one of the heavier-minute players on the slate at about 36 minutes, with usage around 22%. That's the kind of workload that gives a mid-tier player like George a shot at a 40-point night, which is the kind of game that can win you this slate. He's a mid-tier play who may not draw a crowd.
[**John Collins**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player.php?id=4159)**, PF, Pistons, $4,700.** He plays about 24 minutes with a usage rate around 18%, so it's a modest role, but that's also built into his price. He's cheap enough to save salary for a star, and a hot shooting night can push him well past 30 DK points, and put an 8-10x night within reach.
