### **Ty Jerome (Grizzlies vs. Raptors) Over Points + Assists**

Jerome has spearheaded Memphis’ aforementioned surprisingly strong start, as the veteran point guard has been putting up career-best numbers across the board. Jerome is averaging 31.9 points + assists per contest, while also shooting a blistering 50.3% overall. Jerome has had a couple of modest performances, but he’s eclipsed 20 points in six of eight contests while putting up 24.8 field-goal + free-throw attempts per contest overall.

Jerome is 4-4 to the Over on the 27.5 so far this season, but with two days of rest and a matchup that’s projected to yield a wire-to-wire (3.5-point spread) and relatively high-scoring (230.5-point projected total) outcome, the veteran could be in line for one of his higher-percentile performances. The matchup doesn’t necessarily hurt his chances of doing so, either, as the Raptors are ranked No. 18 in offensive efficiency rating surrendered to point guards (26.7), and Toronto has also conceded the fifth-most assists per game (29.7) overall in the last three contests.

**Original Line Publish:** 27.5

### **Victor Wembanyama (Spurs vs. Suns) Over Rebounds**

Wembanyama is already in the thick of another MVP-caliber season, averaging 29.1 points, 12.0 rebounds, 3.0 assists, 3.9 blocks and 1.2 steals per contest across 30.3 minutes per game. The 2023 first overall pick has at least 10 rebounds in seven of 10 games as well, and Wemby is helping prop up his production on the glass by averaging a career-high 2.9 offensive rebounds per contest.

The opposing Suns figure to help his case for the Over in a significant way Monday. Phoenix is surrendering the sixth-most rebounds per game to centers (15.9), and the third-most rebounds per contest overall (57.5). They’re also conceding an NBA-high 34.2% offensive rebounding rate, which dovetails well with Wemby’s career-best success on the offensive glass at the moment.

Finally, there should be plenty of opportunities for boards on the other side of the floor as well, considering San Antonio is forcing plenty of misfires while surrendering the sixth-lowest shooting percentage (43.6) in the league.

**Original Line Publish:**10.5

### **Zion Williamson (Pelicans vs. Warriors) Over Points + Rebounds**

Zion came into training camp in fantastic shape by all accounts, and he’s parlayed that commitment into a strong start to his seventh NBA season. Williamson is averaging 26.0 points + rebounds per contest while shooting a typically efficient 59.8%, and he’s scored at least 22 points in six of nine games. The big man’s usage remains elevated as well, as he’s averaging 19.4 field-goal + free-throw attempts per contest.

The Warriors are ranked No. 26 in offensive efficiency rating allowed to power forwards (30.1), also surrendering 50.5% shooting to the position. Golden State has also been much more generous on the glass at home than on the road, giving up 55.0 rebounds per home contest, including 56.7 over the last three contests.

Williamson also has a solid career track record against the Warriors, averaging 28.7 points + rebounds per game against Golden State and putting up between 29 and 39 points + rebounds tallies in four of the last five games of the series.

**Original Line Publish**:25.5

## **Best NBA Sunday Props**

*   Paul George Over 23.5 Points + Rebounds

### **Paul George (Celtics vs. Bucks) Over Points + Rebounds**

There are some questionable defenses taking the floor Sunday, with the Bucks being one. George just exploited Milwaukee’s vulnerabilities two games ago at TD Garden, when he went off for 25 points, five assists, three rebounds and four steals across 35 minutes. PG 13 is coming off a clunker against the Hornets on Friday, but prior to that game, he was averaging 26.9 points + rebounds across his first seven games of the season.

George has played at both shooting guard and power forward this season, and the matchup against the Bucks is appealing either way. Milwaukee has surrendered the sixth-highest offensive efficiency rating to fours (29.8) and fourth highest to twos (27.8), and the Bucks are also facilitating 48.2% shooting and 14.2 made threes per game at home. Then, the Bucks are surrendering an NBA-high 64.3 rebounds per game in the last three.

George has hit the Over on the 23.5 PR on four occasions already, and he finished on the hook with 23 in one other instance.

**Original Line Publish:** 23.5

### **Darius Acuff Jr. (Kings vs. Heat) Over Points + Rebounds + Assists**

Acuff has immediately put himself in the conversation as an elite candidate for Rookie of the Year honors by averaging 25.2 points, 7.1 assists and 3.7 rebounds across his first nine games. The dynamic Arkansas product has been even better over his last five contests, when he’s ratched his production up to 41.6 PRA per game while shooting a slightly improved 44.8%, including 37.7% from behind the arc. Acuff’s usage has been through the roof in that span, as he’s put up 27.2 field-goal + free-throw attempts per contest.

Acuff now gets a crack at the fast-paced Heat, which have yielded the second-highest offensive efficiency rating to point guards (33.8), as well as 50.7% shooting, including 42.3% from three-point range, to the position. Miami is also allowing the second-most points per game (33.1) to ones, and the fact the Heat is also averaging the third-most possessions per game (107.8) sets Acuff up for even more opportunities to pile up numbers.

**Original Line Publish**: 33.5

### **Bam Adebayo (Heat vs. Kings) Over Points + Rebounds**

Adebayo has unsurprisingly ceded his fair share of usage early in his first season working with Giannis Antetokounmpo, but the talented big man is still averaging 27.0 points + rebounds while shooting 53.8%. Adebayo is still averaging 18.4 field-goal + free-throw attempts per game, and he’ll head into Sunday’s favorable matchup against the Kings averaging 33.4 points + rebounds and having shot 62.5% across 34.2 minutes over his last five contests.

The Kings are certainly porous enough to help Adebayo to superior production on the scoreboard and the glass. Sacramento is allowing the second-highest offensive efficiency rating to centers (37.7), as well as NBA-high 64.0% shooting to the position. The Kings are also surrendering the fifth-most rebounds per home game (58.0) and fifth-most points in the paint per contest overall (52.4), while Adebayo has at least nine boards in five straight and is scoring 64.7% of his points in the painted area.

**Original Line Publish:**28.5
