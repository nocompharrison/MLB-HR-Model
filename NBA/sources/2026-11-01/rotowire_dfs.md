## NBA Best Bets and Predictions Today: Player Props & Picks for Sunday, Nov. 1
##  **Boston Celtics vs. Orlando Magic:**[**Jayson Tatum**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/jayson-tatum-4108)**over 26.5 points, -107 @ DraftKings**
Tatum has opened the 2026-27 campaign averaging 27.6 points across five appearances, putting him slightly above Sunday's proposed threshold. The Celtics star has exceeded 26.5 points twice, scoring 36 against the Knicks on October 23 and a season-high 40 against Brooklyn four days later. He also finished with 26 points against Chicago on Friday, narrowly missing the line.
His offensive workload supports another strong scoring performance. Tatum has attempted at least 20 field goals in four of his five games, averaging 21.2 attempts per contest. His 40-point outing against the Nets demonstrated his ability to generate substantial scoring production even without exceptional three-point shooting, as he made just two of seven attempts from beyond the arc.
The main concern is efficiency, but Tatum has scored at least 23 points in every game since Opening Night. With 48.2 percent shooting over his last four games, the over looks like an attractive choice.

##  **Brooklyn Nets vs. Indiana Pacers:**[**Andrew Nembhard**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/andrew-nembhard-5679)**under 9.5 assists, -135 @ FanDuel**
Nembhard has taken on considerable playmaking responsibility for Indiana following the absence of [Tyrese Haliburton](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/tyrese-haliburton-5114) (hamstring), racking up 23 assists in his last two games while averaging 8.0 per game so far. However, that production remains below Sunday's 9.5-assist threshold, and he has finished with nine or fewer assists in four of his five games thus far.
His most recent outing provides a useful example, as he registered nine assists across 30 minutes against Miami on Friday. Although Nembhard delivered a season-high 14 assists against Philadelphia on October 28, that remains his only double-digit assist performance this season. Haliburton's absence should increase Nembhard's usage, but will it be enough to clear this line?
The strongest argument for the under comes from his previous meeting with Brooklyn, as Nembhard finished with just three assists in 30 minutes against the Nets on October 25. Nembhard can still produce double-digit assists, but his 80 percent under rate and the recent head-to-head record suggest staying below the 9.5-assist threshold here is the safest option.
##  **Toronto Raptors vs. Los Angeles Lakers:**[**Luka Doncic**](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/player/luka-doncic-4396)**over 48.5 points + assists + rebounds, -115 @ BetMGM**
Doncic has been one of the NBA's most productive offensive players early in the season, averaging 36.2 points, 6.8 rebounds and 7.6 assists through five appearances. Those numbers combine for 50.6 PRA per game, comfortably above Sunday's proposed line.
The Lakers star has exceeded 48.5 PRA in each of his last three outings, including consecutive performances of 70 P+R+A against Utah and 55 P+R+A against Portland. His 52-point explosion against the Jazz on October 25 was particularly impressive, as he also contributed eight rebounds and 10 assists.
Doncic followed that performance with 37 points, seven rebounds and five assists against Golden State on Friday, finishing with 49 PRA despite Los Angeles suffering a 112-95 defeat. Although Toronto possesses versatile defensive personnel, Doncic's exceptional scoring responsibility and ability to contribute across multiple categories make the over very attractive here.
