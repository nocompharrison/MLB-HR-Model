## Julian Champagnie Over 2.5 Three-Pointers Made (-135)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
By [Bryan Fonseca](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/bryan-fonseca)
Julian Champagnie has been great from beyond the arc lately, hitting four threes in his last game against Detroit and clearing this 2.5 line in four of his last six contests.
Without Dylan Harper eating up usage tonight, Champagnie's offensive role and shot volume will naturally increase. He is integral to what the [Spurs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/san-antonio-spurs) do offensively, which ensures consistent minutes for him regardless of whether he starts or comes off the bench.
While the [Clippers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/los-angeles-clippers) do not surrender a high volume of three-point attempts, they give up high-quality looks, allowing opponents to shoot 37.5% from deep.
Champagnie will do the vast majority of his damage from the corners, an area where Los Angeles is susceptible to breakdowns. He has attempted 95 threes this season compared to just 17 two-pointers, so his entire offensive output is tied directly to the perimeter.
Even in a potential blowout scenario, he should log at least 25 minutes, affording him six to eight clean looks from downtown. He consistently knocks down these opportunities, making 2.5 an attackable number.
#### **Pick:[Julian Champagnie Over 2.5 Three-Pointers Made](https://playbookbot.com/books/draftkings/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3220356816&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-135) **

## Landry Shamet Over 1.5 Three-Pointers Made (-140)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Bryan Fonseca](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/bryan-fonseca)
The [Knicks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/new-york-knicks) just handled the [Raptors](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/toronto-raptors) for the 14th and 15th consecutive time up north earlier this week, improving to 8-6 in an unbalanced title defense through a month — but to be fair, it's only been a month.
The [Timberwolves](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/minnesota-timberwolves), meanwhile, have the league's No. 1 defense and entered Wednesday night ranked third in net rating behind only the Spurs and the [Celtics](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/boston-celtics). Not to mention, they've won seven of their last eight games and have been on a tear despite losing Anthony Edwards.
Landry Shamet has been a top-five player on the Knicks this year and has gone over 1.5 made threes in 10 of 14 games, while taking 73 threes and 30 twos.
Shamet is averaging 25.7 minutes per game and is crucial to this Knicks team, logging minutes with mostly starters — and closing on occasion — while attempting over five threes per contest.

## Cameron Boozer Over 31.5 Points, Rebounds & Assists (-114)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Joe Dellera](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/joe-dellera)
In just the second game of the season, Cameron Boozer torched the [Warriors](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/golden-state-warriors) for a season-high 44 PRA, and he appears poised for a repeat performance tonight.
The [Grizzlies](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/memphis-grizzlies) are dealing with key absences, most notably Zach Edey and Ty Jerome. Edey's absence opens up additional interior rebounding opportunities for Boozer, while missing Jerome's facilitation forces Boozer into a primary scoring and playmaking role.
Operating on a back-to-back adds a slight element of fatigue, but Golden State's defensive structure is currently in shambles. The Warriors are very weak on the interior, and opposing centers and bigs have absolutely crushed them all season long.
Boozer boasts a high floor because he stuffs the stat sheet across all three categories. I expect this line to settle around 33.5, and given the lack of rim protection from Golden State and the sheer volume of usage heading Boozer's way, he is in a premier spot to dominate once again.
#### **Pick:[Cameron Boozer Over 31.5 Points, Rebounds & Assists](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3220760029&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-114)**

## Stephon Castle Over 12.5 Rebounds & Assists (-104)
[![Header Trailing Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Joe Dellera](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/joe-dellera)
The key injury news for San Antonio is that Dylan Harper isn't going to play due to a hip injury, and Tobias Harris remains sidelined. That opens up significantly more spacing and opportunity for Stephon Castle on the perimeter.
When Castle played without Harper alongside Victor Wembanyama and De'Aaron Fox last season, he hit this combined line in six of seven games, averaging 15.3 [rebounds](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/rebounds) and [assists](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/assists).
Even with a significant blowout risk against a Clippers team that is poised to get completely dismantled, I think the game script works in Castle's favor early on.
The Spurs are surprisingly undersized with their current lineups, and Julian Champagnie likely slots into the starting five. None of those surrounding role players are prolific rebounders, but Castle is a tremendous ball hawk who aggressively attacks the glass.
Without Harper—a player who consistently creates for himself—Castle will be tasked with facilitating more of the offense. With floor spacers like Champagnie and Devin Vassell out there, Castle should find plenty of drive-and-kick opportunities to rack up assists.
On the glass, the Clippers are going to miss a ton of shots in this mismatch, likely creating a surplus of defensive rebounding chances. I'm banking on Castle clearing 12.5 rebounds and assists regardless of a potential blowout.
#### **Pick:[Stephon Castle Over 12.5 Rebounds & Assists](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3220493125&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-104)**
