## Wizards vs. Pacers

Wizards +3.5 (-115)
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
### Wizards +3.5
The [Wizards](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/washington-wizards)’ numbers are impacted considerably by their 40-point blowout over OKC.
The [Pacers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/indiana-pacers) are not OKC. The Pacers are a below .500 team who has struggled on both ends of the floor this season, before and after Tyrese Haliburton’s injury, and their defense is just as bad as their offense, so the Haliburton injury doesn’t really cut it.
The market power rating line (built off relative spreads against other teams) is Pacers -3.5. I make this line Pacers -1.7 using my preseason priors, but if we just used this season’s play-type data, I would have the Pacers as 4-point underdogs.
With the Wizards, I’m always concerned about three-point separation — Washington has the third-lowest 3-point rate in the league — but the Pacers are only 16th in 3-point rate and 24th in percentage.
If the Pacers have a good night from three-point range, this bet is cooked. But the Wizards have a size and depth advantage, and they’ll be better in the halfcourt.
The Wizards struggle in transition, which could doom them in a matchup against Indiana, but they also allow a below-league-average transition rate via Synergy Sports.
At its core, this is a bet on the notion that I believe the Wizards and Pacers are relatively even teams, and homecourt does not account for 4.5 [points](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/points) of difference.
I’ll grab the points with Washington tonight.
**Pick:**[Wizards +3.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3192226581&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-115)****

## Nets vs. Pelicans
[![Brooklyn Nets Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2023/07/brooklyn-nets-logo-updated-2023.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/brooklyn-nets)

[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
### Nets +4.5
If you’re not willing to back the [Nets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/brooklyn-nets) on the road as a less-than-two-possession underdog, are you even really alive?
The market power-rated line on this game is only [Pelicans](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/new-orleans-pelicans) -3.1, which sounds a lot closer to correct.
I like the Pelicans; I think some things are working, and I believe they’ll be better than the Nets by more than this number by the end of the season. But the Pelicans probably aren't good enough to be favored by more than a possession over anyone in the league who isn’t Chicago or Sacramento.
The Nets are surprisingly decent at protecting the rim, but they give up a lot of threes. The Pelicans, however, are dead last in three-point rate and 26th in percentage.
The Pelicans don’t take the threes that the Nets will give them, and they don’t make them when they do take them. New Orleans wants to attack the rim where Brooklyn is actually good at defending.
Transition and [turnovers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/props/offensive-props) are a problem; the Nets are 27th in turnover rate and the Pelicans are third in fast-break points per 100 possessions. But I think Michael Porter Jr. and Julius Randle will be able to create enough offense to keep the Nets within striking distance.
The Nets just got clobbered by the [Mavericks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/dallas-mavericks) by over 30 points. Underdogs coming off a loss by more than 20 points are 12-9 ATS this season.

### Matisse Thybulle Over 5.5 Points
I initially wanted to back Matisse Thybulle to hit a few three-pointers in tonight's game, but over 1.5 three-pointers (six points) is currently at +114, and his regular scoring prop gives you more outs.
The [Bucks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/milwaukee-bucks) give up the fifth-most corner three-pointers in the league; their pick-and-roll coverage gets lost and they routinely over-help for seemingly no reason.
Thybulle is shooting 44.8 percent from the corners and takes the most corner three-point attempts for the [Lakers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/los-angeles-lakers).
The Bucks are playing on a back-to-back tonight, traveling back from the East Coast, and they should get smashed, but this is a lot of points for a Lakers team rolling on an East road trip, and I don’t like the obvious spots.
Based on in-season numbers only, though, I make the spread for this game Lakers -20, and I’m not exaggerating. The Bucks are truly awful. Even if this game turns into a blowout, Thybulle should still get plenty of run.
I’ll pay the [juice](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/juice) to get the possibility of a three-pointer, a layup, and free throws for the over, but if you want to get greedy and play over 1.5 three-pointers, I don’t blame you.

## Nuggets vs. Grizzlies
[![Denver Nuggets Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/725847_nba_den_150x150.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/denver-nuggets)
Wednesday, Nov. 11
8 p.m. ET
NBA League Pass
[![Memphis Grizzlies Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/nba/100/mem.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/memphis-grizzlies)
Jerami Grant Over 1.5 Three-Pointers Made (-118)
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
### Grizzlies +4.5
The [Nuggets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/denver-nuggets) just haven’t done anything to prove they deserve this kind of respect here. This line originally opened +4.5 and it has already fallen to +3.5.
Denver’s wins this season have come against the Pelicans twice, the [Suns](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/phoenix-suns), the [Raptors](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/toronto-raptors) without Kawhi Leonard, and the Pacers. That’s it. They’ve lost to every team over .500 they’ve faced, and Memphis is over .500.
Memphis presents all the same problems that Denver struggles with: a quick, crafty scoring guard in Ty Jerome. A strong, dominant interior presence who controls the glass in Zach Edey. Spacing. Athleticism. Speed.
I make this line [Grizzlies](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/memphis-grizzlies) +2.3. The Nuggets are coming off a loss to Houston where their sleep schedule was messed up after travel delays over the weekend. They likely still haven’t adjusted.
Denver is a good team, but Memphis has been better so far, and if we flip this 4.5 points for homecourt — being light on Denver’s altitude advantage — this would be Nuggets -7.5? Does that sound right?
No way. I’ll take Memphis and the points.
#### **Pick:[Grizzlies +4.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3192139182&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-118)**
### Jerami Grant Over 1.5 Three-Pointers Made
This is a prop I will consistently look to target based on Denver's specific defensive scheme. The Nuggets surrender a ton of corner three-pointers. It is a known structural flaw in their system; they simply do not execute great close-outs on the perimeter.
Because their defensive scheme is predicated on Nikola Jokic playing at the level of the screen, the back-line rotations are constantly forced into long, scrambling closeouts. As a result, Denver allows corner threes at one of the highest rates in the entire NBA, currently sitting third in the league in that category behind only Oklahoma City and Boston.
Despite playing at a relatively slow, methodical pace, the Nuggets still bleed these specific perimeter opportunities, and that is where Jerami Grant thrives.
Grant is currently shooting the basketball at an elite level, connecting on over 45 percent of his looks from deep and hitting an absurd 51 percent specifically from the corners. He averages roughly one made corner three per game, meaning we are already two-thirds of the way home on this prop just from his baseline corner production alone.
I also expect the Grizzlies to control the overall flow of this game. Denver is stepping into this matchup following an absolute nightmare of a travel schedule. They played in Mexico City, headed to the airport, got sent back to the hotel, slept for a few hours, returned to the airport, and finally landed in Houston at 5:00 a.m.
In a baffling decision, the Nuggets then proceeded to play all of their starters for 40 minutes the very next day—and still lost the game.
Between the heavy legs from the travel logistics and the Nuggets' inability to defend the corners, Grant should get a steady diet of clean looks from the outside tonight.
#### **Pick:[Jerami Grant Over 1.5 Three-Pointers Made](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3192377268&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball) (-118)**

### Rockets -5.5
I will cop to this particular bet being boosted by a certain sense of stubbornness. The [Warriors](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/golden-state-warriors) are a team I was down on in preseason, and the [Rockets](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/houston-rockets) are a team I was high on. So, my confirmation bias is screaming at this game right now, where Houston is a less-than-two-possession favorite over Golden State.
But also, Golden State can’t keep getting away with this! The Warriors’ surprise player is rookie Yaxel Lendenborg, who the team is 20 points better without this season in on/off splits.
The Warriors are a small-ball team dominating offensive rebounding. They are living and dying by the 37-year-old touched-by-the-basketball-gods shooter and some insane defensive scheming.
The Warriors are good, and this is an opportunity to really stake their claim to being a potential playoff team. However, I just think Houston is better by a good margin.
I make this line Rockets-9.6 based on preseason power rating, and Houston’s early performance has been much more sound than people realize. The Rockets have had a harder schedule and held a stronger point differential after it.
The Warriors have consistently caught teams in bad spots, like the Wolves in their first game without Anthony Edwards and the [Heat](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/odds/miami-heat) on a back-to-back.
Houston is down two centers, which matters on the rebounding side, but the Rockets still have so many athletes who can hit the glass. Plus, Brandon Williams is out for the Warriors, and he’s given them good minutes.
I gave this out as a one-star play on the [Buckets podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6l-GpcjBZow) because I know I’m walking into a trap here. But Houston has been the better team, and been better enough to be favored by more than this at home.
