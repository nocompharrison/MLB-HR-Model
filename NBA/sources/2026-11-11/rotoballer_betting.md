## NBA Player Prop Bets
We have a bit of a theme today (not planned, I promise) with a lot of big men popping in great rebounding spots!
**[Karl-Anthony Towns](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20001431/Karl-Anthony+Towns) OVER 12.5 Rebounds (-111 Novig)**
Towns leads all qualified players in the NBA with a 33.4% defensive rebounding rate. He's averaging 13.8 boards per game (in 31 minutes per contest) and facing a Cavs team that is allowing the second-most rebounds to opposing centers this season. Of the four games where he's gone under 12.5 boards, three of them have been on the hook right at 12, and several of those were games where he played low minutes due to foul trouble or a blowout.
The Knicks big man just faced Cleveland a few games ago and snagged 13 boards in that one. His elite rebounding rate, paired with his potential to play his full share of minutes tonight in what should be a close game (you better show up, Cavs!) as Cleveland is only a 4-point underdog on the road in New York.
**[Walker Kessler](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20002889/Walker+Kessler) OVER 10.5 Rebounds (-130 Novig)**
Towns has the second-best matchup for boards tonight, while Kessler has the absolute smash spot for bigs. We saw Towns clobber the Bucks last night for 16 rebounds in only 27 minutes! Milwaukee is dead last in rebounds allowed to centers, and also is allowing the most offensive rebounds to opponents in the league.
Enter Kessler, who averages 3.5 offensive rebounds per game, putting him inside the top ten in the NBA. He's been feasting on the glass lately, going for 11 or more boards in five of his last seven games.
We have a slight blowout risk here as the Lakers are 15-point favorites and the Bucks are playing their second game in as many nights, but I am not sure that is going to matter for Kessler as we saw Towns go nuts without having to close the game.
**[Anthony Davis](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000468/Anthony+Davis) OVER 22.5 Points (-115 Novig)**
AD has been in excellent form this year when healthy and in the lineup. He's giving the Wizards exactly what they had hoped for when they made the trade for him last season. His usage rate is sitting right around 30%, and he's gone over this points total in six of the nine games in which he's appeared so far this year. He's averaging 25.7 points on the season and coming off a massive 44-point outing against a good defensive team in Detroit.
This game looks juicy for props, as it carries a 227.5-point total and only a 2.5-point spread. The Pacers' defense has been rough this year, and we saw them allow both [Kyrie Irving](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20000574/Kyrie+Irving) and [Cooper Flagg](https://www.rotoballer.com/nba/player/20003444/Cooper+Flagg) to have huge nights on Monday. Look for AD to crush this prop, and I'd consider laddering him up to 30 points tonight.

I might lose some of you here. I mean, how can we trust Sharpe to get enough minutes tonight when his playing time has been inconsistent, and he keeps getting in foul trouble?
I will tell you why! The Pelicans are too soft a matchup to pass up! They're bleeding production to centers, allowing the sixth-most rebounds and third-most assists per game to opposing fives.
Sharpe's game log is all over the map, I know. But he's been dishing out dimes at an elite rate lately with 27 assists over his last three games. If we set the bar at 24 minutes, Sharpe has gone over this number in three of the four games that he's played that much this season, with 11 right on the nose against Charlotte.
He's a stat-stuffing machine, and I am taking the leap of faith on his ability to stay on the court tonight. If he does, he'll smash this number.

The Pacers are getting drubbed by opposing centers on the boards. They are allowing the fifth-most rebounds to centers, but have been particularly bad of late.
