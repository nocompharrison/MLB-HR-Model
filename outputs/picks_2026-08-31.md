🎯 PitchMix wOBA	Batter wOBA weighted by THIS pitcher's actual pitch distribution. The Barrel Boyz 'PitchMix AVG' signal (e.g. Schwarber .265 vs Vasquez mix). Computed as: sum(pitcher_usage_pct × batter_wOBA_vs_pitch_type). Requires ≥30% pitch coverage for reliable estimate.						
📊 wRC+ vs hand	Batter wRC+ specifically vs RHP or LHP (Barrel Boyz 'wRHP%/wLHP%' metric). Index where 100 = league average. 147 = 47% better than average vs that hand. Source: FanGraphs batting splits. ≥140 = elite (+2 conv), ≥120 = good (+1). ≤75 = struggles vs this hand (-1.5 conv). Different from wOBA — this is context-adjusted.						
🍖 Meatball rate (pitcher)	% of pitches the pitcher leaves in the heart of the zone (league avg ~8-9%). Source: Baseball Savant pitcher leaderboard. Directly measures 'HR-friendly contact' — Propfinder methodology uses this alongside barrel%, HH%, and HR/FB% to rank pitchers by HR risk. ≥12% = elite meatball rate, pitcher frequently over the plate. Contributes to pitcher_vuln_score and surfaces as 🍖 flag in picks tables.						
🎯 Pitch correlation	Two-sided pitch matchup check from Propfinder methodology: pitcher is weak on their primary pitch type (high wOBA/barrel% allowed on that pitch) AND batter specifically crushes that same pitch type. The 'direct correlation between pitcher weakness and hitter strength'. Fires as a note in detailed breakdown and flag in picks table. More precise than one-sided batter wOBA edge alone — both sides must confirm.						
🎯 N BZM-SZN (PT): zones	Barrel Zone Match (BZM) — a hyperlocal pitch-location edge. Fires when (1) the pitcher locates ≥12% of in-zone pitches of type PT in a specific strike zone cell (season data, normalized to in-zone pitches only), AND (2) the batter's season xwOBA in that same cell is ≥.400 (solidly above average). N = number of matching zones; PT = pitch type (FF, SI, SL, etc.); zones are labeled up/in, up, up/out, mid-in, middle, mid-out, low-in, low, low/out. Example: '🎯 2 BZM-SZN (FF): up/in, middle' = pitcher locates his four-seamer to the up/in and middle zones most often, and the batter has elite xwOBA in both cells. Higher N = more overlapping hot-spot edges on this exact pitch type. Both pitcher and batter use season data for stable zone coverage (200-400+ events). Conviction boost: +2 for 1 zone match, +4 for ≥2 zone matches (from Jun 15 2026). Only fires inside the pitch-correlation block — both sides of the matchup must already be confirmed before BZM is evaluated. Jun 15 2026 first live slate: 4/7 HR (57%), 5/7 hit (71%) across all BZM picks.
