## Iowa vs. Xavier
[![Iowa Hawkeyes Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/iowd.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/iowa-hawkeyes)
Friday, Nov. 20
7:30 p.m. ET
TNT
[![Xavier Musketeers Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/xav.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/xavier-musketeers)
Xavier -3.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
Iowa has dropped two straight games at neutral sites to teams worse than Xavier, and the Hawkeyes have their hands full at the Cintas Center on Friday.
The personnel on this team is lackluster, and the star presence that was Bennett Stirtz is actively missed. Drake transfers Kael Combs and Isaia Howard have stepped into bigger roles with mixed results.
Tate Sage was expected to take a step forward, but he is making just 20% of his triples and was benched after 13 scoreless minutes against Creighton.
Most importantly, transfer center Andrew McKeever, a 7-foot-3, 300-pound analytical darling from Saint Mary's, has not lived up to expectations.
In many ways, Xavier feels designed in a lab to frustrate this Iowa team. Iowa's Stirtz-less offense has a turnover problem, and Xavier ranks 24th nationally in forcing turnovers.
The Hawkeyes had 29 turnovers to just 18 assists in their two losses, and the Musketeers may be better suited to expose that weakness than either of those teams were.
Xavier has also been incredible at avoiding fouls on defense, ranking 44th in the country in defensive free-throw rate. For an Iowa team with a 59.5% FTA/FGA, it's highly likely that its offense will sputter if it can't get to the line.
Take the Musketeers to create some separation at home.

## Kentucky vs. Indiana
[![Kentucky Wildcats Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/kend.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kentucky-wildcats)
Friday, Nov. 20
8 p.m. ET
CBS
[![Indiana Hoosiers Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/iu.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/indiana-hoosiers)
Indiana -2.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
Kentucky has already dropped 31 spots in the national rankings on KenPom, opening the season at 22nd but currently sitting at 53rd overall. The Wildcats have been one of the most disappointing sides in all of college hoops, and pressure is on for Mark Pope.
They have their hands full in Indianapolis against the Hoosiers, as Indiana's bigs project as the perfect personnel group to frustrate Malachi Moreno. They dominated Arkansas on the boards last game, outrebounding the Razorbacks by 11 and pulling down 15 offensive rebounds.
Arkansas has been a better rebounding team than Kentucky so far this season, and the Hoosiers should have a straightforward path to replicate that success and generate more shot opportunities in this game.
The Hoosiers' guards should also like their matchups against a Kentucky perimeter defense that lacks the ability to pressure aggressively. Markus Burton and Bryce Lindsay are both shot-hunters, and if given the ability to step into pull-up jumpers, they can take over a game.
When things start to go wrong for a Mark Pope team, it can snowball fast. This has that potential for the Hoosiers to capitalize.
**Pick:[ Indiana -2.5 (Play to -6.5)](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3222085148&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

## Eastern Illinois vs. Illinois
[![Eastern Illinois Panthers Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/assets/upload/254056_eiupanthers.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/eastern-illinois-panthers)
Friday, Nov. 20
9 p.m. ET
Big Ten Network
[![Illinois Fighting Illini Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ill.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/illinois-fighting-illini)
Eastern Illinois +51.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
Is it fun to bet against this Illinois team? Not at all. I’m not exactly a thrill-seeker and I would never step in front of a moving train.
But the only thing powerful enough to de-rail this high-speed Illinois attack is the flu, and if you have followed the Illini for long enough, you know that this program is never immune to a good non-conference holiday sickness.
Brad Underwood informed the media in Champaign yesterday that star freshman Quentin Coleman, starting wing Lincoln Williams and backup center Zvonimir Ivisic all did not practice on Wednesday while multiple other players are “on the tail end of the flu."
Availability is a legitimate concern right now, and remember, this team you just watched punk Duke was already down one National Player of the Year candidate (David Mirkovic) and another starter (Jake Davis).
I will make no argument for Eastern Illinois holding a candle in this game, but given the spot, with Illinois coming off the greatest regular season win in program history and the availability concerns, I am willing to fire on the underdog at a number this large.
Illinois just wants to get out of here without any more sniffles.

## Sam Houston vs. Boise State
[![Sam Houston Bearkats Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/shs.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/sam-houston-state-bearkats)
Friday, Nov. 20
9 p.m. ET
YouTube
[![Boise State Broncos Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/boise.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/boise-state-broncos)
Boise State -11.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Boise State has had five days to stew on its ugly loss to Nebraska last weekend, and it’s a bounce back spot for a team that has looked comfortable at home.
The Broncos have scored 100, 111 and 132 points in their three home wins this season, all blowouts (including one 19-point win over Butler).
Game flow should be conducive to another high-scoring affair, as both teams play top-50 tempos and Sam Houston has played two games with 80 or more possessions already.
High-possession games always favor the team with more talent, but especially when they get extra possessions, which Boise State should get here.
Boise ranks 34th nationally in Forced Turnover Rate and Sam Houston has been turnover-prone against quality opponents (three games with 14 or more turnovers).
The spot and the stylistic outlook both seem like perfect conditions for a Boise State statement win.
Take the Broncos to win comfortably at home, covering in the process.

### Iowa Basketball

Following a 2-0 start with wins over [Lipscomb](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/lipscomb-bisons) and [Eastern Illinois](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/eastern-illinois-panthers), the Hawkeyes have now dropped back-to-back contests on neutral courts against [Virginia Tech](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/virginia-tech-hokies) and [Creighton](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/creighton-bluejays).
Both the Hokies and the Bluejays rank lower nationally than [Xavier](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/xavier-musketeers), and the undefeated Musketeers will play host to Iowa here, so it's safe to say this is its toughest game yet. It will be interesting to see how the Hawkeyes handle that challenge.
Some things look the same — as they did during Iowa's run to the Elite Eight last season. The Hawkeyes try to grind games to a halt, and they usually succeed. They sit at 363rd nationally in adjusted tempo through four games.
They also grade out incredibly well in 2-point percentage, making 64% of their shots as a team inside the arc. McCollum's offenses always generate good shots at the rim, and this one will continue to do so.
Everything else is a work in progress. The personnel on this team is lackluster, and the star presence that was Stirtz is actively missed. [Drake](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/drake-bulldogs) transfers [Kael Combs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/kael-combs-256693-g) and [Isaia Howard](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/isaia-howard-294963-g) have stepped into bigger roles — with mixed results.

Most importantly, transfer center [Andrew McKeever](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/andrew-mckeever-260779-c), a 7-foot-3, 300-pound analytical darling from [Saint Mary](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/saint-marys-gaels)'s, has not lived up to expectations. ## UCF vs Vanderbilt College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ucf.png)
### UCF Basketball
I tear apart the Knights below if you want to get into it right away.
UCF has been scarcely underwhelming out of the gate this season while playing a tougher schedule than the Commodores, but the Knights haven't looked all that impressive.
Through five games, the Knights are already outside the top 100 in defensive turnover percentage and offensive rebounding. On top of that, they rank 233rd in 3-point percentage defense, 256th in defensive rebounding and 243rd in far 2-point percentage defense.
If these early issues continue, the Knights will have a tough time maintaining their winning record once conference play rolls around.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/van.png)
### Vanderbilt Basketball
Coming off a 46-point dismantling of [Eastern Illinois](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/eastern-illinois-panthers) on Tuesday, Vanderbilt has a pretty tough schedule on the horizon, with three top-100 teams coming up over their next four games.
The Knights kick off that stretch before Vanderbilt heads to Columbus to take on the Buckeyes. The Commodores then host [Southern](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/southern-university-jaguars) before hitting the road to face the most lethal 3-point shooting team in the nation, the [Notre Dame](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/notre-dame-fighting-irish) Fighting Irish, in South Bend.
After that stretch, Vanderbilt faces [California](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/california-bears) before getting four bottom-feeders. If the Commodores want to build their resume, they need to keep their foot on the gas over the next few weeks.
The Commodores already looked unbelievable against [Wake Forest](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wake-forest-demon-deacons) and [Memphis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/memphis-tigers), and they should be able to take down this Knights team as it leaves their home gym for the first time all season.
I'll be looking forward to keeping my eye on Vanderbilt over the coming weeks.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ucf.png)
![Header Second Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/van.png)
## UCF vs. Vanderbilt Pick, Betting Analysis
How are we supposed to be able to trust UCF? Seriously? The Knights have been very underwhelming this season.
They kicked off opening night needing overtime to beat Florida Gulf Coast as a 22.5-point favorite, and then beat Yale by 12 to cover the 9.5-point spread.
After that, they failed to cover double-digit spreads against South Alabama and Georgia Southern before losing to Mississippi State as a short home favorite earlier this week.
Now, the Knights head out on the road for the first time to take on their first top-25 opponent, one that has been humming. The Commodores have pummeled everyone in their way, beating Wake Forest and Memphis by 23-plus points.
There should be no reason for Mark Byington's boys to take their foot off the gas, especially with only three more top-100 opponents on the schedule before they can coast into conference play on Jan. 2.
Vanderbilt should easily be able to take advantage of UCF's struggles on the glass and defending the perimeter. The Commodores rank inside the top five in both offensive and defensive rebounding percentage.
On top of that, they're inside the top 40 in 3-point percentage without being overly reliant on the deep shot offensively.
In this writer's eyes, we're forced to back the clearly better team in this spot.

## Kentucky vs Indiana Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/kend.png)
### Kentucky Basketball
It's a make-or-break season for Mark Pope at [Kentucky](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kentucky-wildcats), and it is off to a rocky start. Pope's third edition of the Wildcats has already dropped 31 spots in the national rankings on KenPom, opening the season at 22nd but currently sitting at 53rd overall.
Tensions are high — as they should be.
Not only was Kentucky thoroughly outplayed in its only game against a high-major opponent (a 20-point rout at the hands of [Kansas](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-jayhawks) and former priority recruit [Tyran Stokes](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/tyran-stokes-334700-g)), but the Wildcats narrowly escaped a fireable loss to [Northern Arizona](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northern-arizona-lumberjacks) last week at Rupp Arena, with [Alex Wilkins](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/alex-wilkins-320690-g) playing the role of momentary savior.
There is nothing this roster can hang its hat on at the moment. Its offensive profile is better than the rest it has to offer, but the areas it grades out as strong in (shooting efficiency, turnover rate, assist percentage) all vanished into thin air against a serious opponent.
Kansas' positional size bothered Kentucky's shooters into a 4-of-21 shooting night, and the Wildcats turned it over 15 times in that loss to just 13 assists.
With that data point, it is hard to take Kentucky's positive offensive profile seriously.
On the other end of the floor, the Wildcats are a disaster. They rank 103rd in adjusted defensive efficiency and are dropping fast. Their guards can't stay in front of anyone, their ball pressure is mediocre and their wings are slow and struggle with physicality.
[Malachi Moreno](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/malachi-moreno-320038-c)'s shot-blocking presence has been the only resistance on that end, but he is prone to foul trouble. Twice this season, he has finished with four fouls and had to play through early foul issues.
Indiana's bigs project as the perfect personnel group to frustrate Moreno.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/iu.png)
### Indiana Basketball
[Indiana](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/indiana-hoosiers)'s trajectory feels headed in the opposite direction of Kentucky's. Darian DeVries was one of the biggest winners of the college basketball offseason, landing four awesome guards and two monstrous bigs in the transfer portal. The early returns are inspiring.
Indiana is up six spots on KenPom since the season opened, currently holding the 41st spot nationally, but even that feels too low for my liking. The Hoosiers just earned one huge win on a neutral court, beating [Arkansas](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/arkansas-razorbacks) at Madison Square Garden with some heroics from [Bryce Lindsay](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/bryce-lindsay-256411-g) late.
They dominated the Razorbacks on the boards in that game, outrebounding them by 11 and pulling down 15 offensive rebounds.
Arkansas has been a better rebounding team than Kentucky so far this season, and the Hoosiers should have a straightforward path here to replicate that success and generate more shot opportunities.
In the frontcourt, the names to watch are [Samet Yigitoglu](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/samet-yi-ito-lu-294552-c) and [Aiden Sherrell](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/aiden-sherrell-283098-f). The 7-foot-2, 270-pound Yigitoglu will be an imposing presence for Moreno to deal with, but Sherrell is a flat-out mismatch for Kentucky's fours. [Ousmane N'Diaye](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/ousmane-n-diaye-334277-f) isn't strong enough to hang there, and [Justin McBride](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/justin-mcbride-250216-f) is too small.
The Hoosier guards should also like their matchups here against a Kentucky perimeter defense that lacks the ability to pressure aggressively. [Markus Burton](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/markus-burton-249284-g) and [Bryce Lindsay](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/bryce-lindsay-256411-g) are both shot-hunters, and if given the ability to step into pull-up jumpers, they can take over a game.
Indiana has another chance to build on an already impressive resume here.

## Kentucky vs. Indiana College Basketball Pick, Best Bet
This is a "neutral" site game in Indianapolis, but I'll be curious to see what the crowd looks like in this one.
While Kentucky fans travel everywhere, the support for their team is dwindling at the hands of Pope. Rupp Arena has had empty seats for a few games, and Indiana may have a home-crowd feel.
Even if they don't — they should love this matchup. Indiana has the better bigs and guards that won't be bothered by the limitations of this Kentucky defense.
The wheels are falling off for Pope, and at some point it's going to crash entirely.

## Georgia vs Georgia Tech College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/uga.png)
### Georgia Basketball
Two games removed from taking down the Tar Heels inside their own gym, the Georgia Bulldogs look to keep their road warrior ways alive as they attempt to take down little brother.
Aside from an eight-point win over Florida Gulf Coast days before heading to Chapel Hill, the Bulldogs have been humming.
Fourth-year guard Blue Cain has been the glue, leading the team in scoring at 15.8 points per game while snagging 6.2 rebounds, nearly leading the team in both categories. He has been a joy to watch this season and should have a monster game against this inexperienced Yellow Jackets defense.
Georgia does have to travel to Florida on Thanksgiving to take on [Cincinnati](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/cincinnati-bearcats), but with nearly a week between games, the Bulldogs should be fully focused on this matchup.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/gt.png)
### Georgia Tech Basketball
Quite possibly the most underwhelming 5-0 team in the country, the Yellow Jackets are looking forward to hosting their first opponent outside the bottom 140 teams in the country.
On top of that, Georgia Tech has not been dominating these games as they should. The Yellow Jackets beat Hampton by 20 in their opener, as expected. They followed that up with a 12-point win over Georgia Southern, a 14-point win over Eastern Kentucky, a one-point win over UNC Asheville and, most recently, a 14-point win over Norfolk State this past Tuesday.
To this writer, these are all tune-up games that the Yellow Jackets should be putting belt to backside in if they want to be fully prepared for the tougher opponents on the horizon.
Could this team be a late bloomer? Possibly. But it's still been a concerning start for a team sitting at 5-0.

## Georgia vs. Georgia Tech Pick, Betting Analysis
Get ready to learn about Cain, Yellow Jackets.
Friday night will mark the first top-50 opponent Georgia Tech has encountered this season, and it will be a tough task.
The Bulldogs looked very strong out of the gate, while the Yellow Jackets have been extremely underwhelming.
Georgia Tech is a perfect 5-0 straight up but just 1-4 against the spread. That lone cover came against Hampton, when the Yellow Jackets covered a 13.5-point spread, which also happened to be their shortest spread of the season.
That should be a major concern for any die-hard Georgia Tech fan, especially considering the Yellow Jackets have endured the eighth-easiest schedule, per Haslametrics.
Georgia, on the other hand, has faced a relatively easy path of its own, but its schedule still ranks 64 spots ahead of Georgia Tech's.
This game should be set to "Ludicrous Speed," with both teams ranking inside the top 35 nationally in pace. But will Georgia Tech be able to hold down the Bulldogs' top-25 offense? The best offense the hometown team has faced this season ranked 220 spots below Georgia.
With the Bulldogs not having anything else on the schedule until they face Cincinnati on Thanksgiving Day, they should be plenty focused on taking down little brother.
