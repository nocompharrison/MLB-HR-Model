## St. Thomas vs. LIU
[![St. Thomas Tommies Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2021/10/St-Thomas-Minn-Logo.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/st-thomas-tommies)
Monday, Nov. 16
7 p.m. ET
NEC Front Row
[![Long Island University Sharks Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/758194_1200px-LIU_Sharks_logo.svg_200x200.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/long-island-university-sharks)
LIU +1.5
[![Hard Rock Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2023/07/Hard-Rock.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/hard-rock-sportsbook)
There will be a consistent theme for me throughout Monday's best bets: Identifying shooting regression candidates in interesting spots.
As a program, LIU is on an upswing under head coach Rod Strickland, as he turned a 3-26 record in his first year to 24-11 and an NCAA Tournament appearance last season (his fourth year).
So far this season, the Sharks are 0-5, but that’s almost by design. They scheduled five consecutive road games to open the campaign — four of them against KenPom top-140 opponents — and fared competitively in losses to Seton Hall and Florida Atlantic.
Now they're home for the first time this season.
One could argue this may be a down season for St. Thomas — the Tommies' ranking on KenPom is down 30 spots since the season opened and they currently sit at 205th nationally, 90 spots lower than where they finished in 2025-26.
They're taking over half their shots from 3 and making 36% of them. That's scary since that hot shooting hasn’t yielded much in terms of results.
They shot 46% from deep and hit 11 triples in a loss last game to Army (343rd nationally).
Give me LIU at its best (and at home) to get a win. I’ll take an extra point at plus money (+102).
**Pick:[ LIU +1.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/hardrock/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3207958684&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

## Western Illinois vs. Minnesota
[![Western Illinois Leathernecks Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/wil.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/western-illinois-leathernecks)
Monday, Nov. 16
8 p.m. ET
Big Ten Network
[![Minnesota Golden Gophers Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/min.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/minnesota-golden-gophers)
Western Illinois +35.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
This is a huge number for the home favorite Gophers at The Barn.
As expected, Minnesota is up to 32nd nationally at KenPom and has handled business early.
However, the Gophers haven’t covered a number this big yet, and they stand out as a shooting regression candidate to me for a few reasons.
They returned a ton (13th in minutes continuity last season) from a team that was just average at making 3s (165th nationally). But this season, they're making 38% as a group through four games.
Add in that they're still missing their best shooter Cade Tyson, and it seems a bit too good to be true.
Western Illinois will run the Gophers off the line. The Leathernecks are first nationally at preventing 3-point attempts so far, and that's always a strength of a Chad Boudreau defense.
Boudreau’s teams usually play well in these spots. Western Illinois has faced three top-30 ranked Big Ten opponents in his last three seasons, and the Leathernecks have covered 35.5 each time, with an average loss of 24 points.
Also, Minnesota heads to the Charleston Classic later this week, meaning this is a good spot for Western Illinois to capitalize on what could be a sleepy Gophers side.
**Pick:[ Western Illinois +35.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/draftkings/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3207537115&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

## Montana vs. Kansas State
[![Montana Grizzlies Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/mont.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/montana-grizzlies)
Monday, Nov. 16
8 p.m. ET
ESPN+
[![Kansas State Wildcats Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/kstd.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-state-wildcats)
Montana +16.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
Stop me if you've read this before in this article, but this is a good spot against a shooting regression candidate.
Kansas State has been shooting the lights out so far, as Casey Alexander teams usually do (see Belmont’s shot profile for the last decade).
However, this has been a bit drastic through three games. The Wildcats are attempting 58.6% of their shots from outside, and so far, 39% of them are going in.
They've already shown what their floor looks like when they run cold, losing at home to South Dakota State on opening night when they made just 29% of their triples.
Now, they've had six days to hear about how great their win over Creighton was leading into this game.
Montana head coach Travis DeCuire prioritizes defending the 3-point line. His teams have ranked inside the top-100 nationally in limiting 3-point attempts in nine of the last 11 seasons. So far this season, opponents are shooting just 34% of their shots from deep against Montana.
The Grizzlies frustrated Oregon in that area, keeping the Ducks to just 11 attempts and five made 3s on opening night. That game got away from the Grizzlies late, but they were down just two points with 10 minutes left to play.
It feels like the right time — and the right opponent — to fade the hot shooting Wildcats.
**Pick:[ Montana +16.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3207544108&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

## Long Beach State vs. UCLA
[![Long Beach State Beach Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/lbsu.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/long-beach-state-49ers)
Monday, Nov. 16
10 p.m. ET
Big Ten Network
[![UCLA Bruins Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2023/07/ucla-bruins-logo-updated-2023.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ucla-bruins)
Long Beach State +34.5
[![Caesars Sportsbook Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/256064_caesars_800x200.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/caesars-sportsbook)
UCLA has been nonsensically good to start the season, and in the longterm, consider me a Bruins believer.
But their spot in the market has swung a bit too drastically for my liking following a 69-point win over a diabolically awful Arkansas-Pine Bluff side last Wednesday.
Various analytics providers agree — KenPom has this line at 25, Bart Torvik has it at 27 and EvanMiya marks it at 31.
Long Beach State is no walkover, as the Beach are currently ranked 219th nationally, roughly 100 spots better than UCLA’s last two opponents. The Beach have hung tough with a power opponent already, taking Utah down to the final possession on the road on opening night.
UCLA has been missing its two projected best bigs — Xavier Booker and Filip Jovic — and it hasn’t mattered at all yet. There have been no updates, but without them, this line is inflated, and even if they return, they'll be working back into game speed.
Long Beach won’t run up the score against itself like the Bruins’ last opponent. The Beach have dominated the turnover and free-throw attempt battles through three games. That should continue, so I’ll take the points.
**Pick:[ Long Beach State +34.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3207583333&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**
