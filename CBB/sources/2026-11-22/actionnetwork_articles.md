## Minnesota vs Utah College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/min.png)
### Minnesota Basketball
The Golden Gophers are off to a great start to head coach Nico Medved’s second year at the helm. Medved overhauled the majority of this roster through the portal, with the team being centered around a three-starter core that has resulted in an impressive 6-0 start.
Minnesota has rolled through its first six opponents by an average 22-point scoring margin, even securing an 85-70 victory over Oklahoma State on Friday.
The Golden Gophers fortunately retained three double-figure scorers from last season, including 6-foot-8 senior forward Jaylen Crocker-Johnson, 6-foot-3 junior guard Isaac Asuma and 6-foot-7 wing Bobby Durkin.
After losing star guard Chansey Willis Jr. last season to injury, Medved was forced to heavily rely on wing Cade Tyson creating his own shot, grinding out longer possessions resulting in the offense operating at a far slower pace.
Medved had a mission this offseason to secure agile two-ways guards who can stretch the floor, and create easy transition-scoring opportunities.
That is precisely what the Gophers head coach did in acquiring former Tar Heel guard Kyan Evans, a perfect complement to Asuma, who is scoring over 17 points per game on the season.
The North Carolina product boasts exceptional versatility, racking up at least 15 points and 10 assists in each of his last two contests.
Minnesota’s backcourt now features two primary ball-handlers who can push the tempo off misses, trigger quicker ball movement in half-court sets and collectively operate the Gophers' offense at a far quicker and more efficient pace than before.
Medved also made major improvement to the Gophers’ defensive frontcourt that was atrocious last season, ranking near the bottom of the Big Ten in points allowed inside the paint.
Minnesota added both former Michigan 7-foot-2 center out of Germany Malick Kordel and freshman 6-foot-10 forward Chadrack Mpoyi.
While the Gophers previously lacked a true shot-blocking center, the interior presence in itself has resulted in an elevated defensive efficiency on the perimeter, with guards no longer needing to collapse aggressively in the lane to contest opposing players driving to the basket.
Medved made some extremely impactful moves this offseason, resulting in the Gophers playing far better basketball at both ends of the floor, which has been quite evident following six straight victories.

### Utah Basketball
After a promising 3-0 start, [Utah](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/utah-utes) has dropped back-to-back games, falling to [Eastern Washington](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/eastern-washington-eagles) before suffering a 17-point loss to [Dayton](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/dayton-flyers) on Friday.
Head coach Alex Jensen significantly overhauled the roster this past offseason following a disappointing 10-22 campaign in his first year at the helm. After losing last season’s leading scorer, Terrence Brown, Jensen secured several key pieces via the transfer portal.
[Trey Campbell](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/trey-campbell-218835-g) leads an impressive portal class, averaging 14.6 points per game over his first five outings. The senior guard offers elite versatility on both ends of the floor, averaging more than four rebounds and five assists so far.
Jensen also added [Jackson Holcombe](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/jackson-holcombe-257079-g), who brings plenty of size to the backcourt and ranks third on the team at 12 points per game, while [TJ Burch](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/tj-burch-283407-g) provides additional guard depth.
Freshman forward [Alec Anigbata](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/alec-anigbata-348149-f) was brought in to bolster a frontcourt depleted by offseason departures. The German has made an immediate offensive impact, ranking second on the team at 12.2 points per game.
Anigbata rounds out a frontcourt featuring plenty of international talent, alongside returning [Babacar Faye](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/babacar-faye-176710-f).

Despite building an early double-digit lead, the Cowboys allowed the Golden Gophers to rally back and seal an 85-70 victory.
With a 3-2 record, Oklahoma State has dropped both of its games to top-50 KenPom programs. In both defeats, the Cowboys struggled to defend opposing guards and failed to connect on enough 3-pointers to remain competitive.
Under head coach Steve Lutz, Oklahoma State relies heavily on perimeter shooting. The Cowboys rank 127th in 3-point attempt volume and 34th in 3-point accuracy, knocking down nearly 40% of their shots from deep.
Additionally, they excel on the glass, boasting a top-20 ranking in offensive rebounding.
To hand the undefeated Hokies their first loss, the Cowboys will need to regain their stroke from beyond the arc.

## Oklahoma State vs. Virginia Tech Pick, Betting Analysis
The Hokies rank 10th nationally in 3-point shooting accuracy, connecting at just over 42% from deep as a team.
They were exceptionally hot in their recent victory against Northwestern, converting over 50% of their attempts from beyond the arc.

### Northwestern Basketball
[Northwestern](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northwestern-wildcats) is absolutely reeling right now. The Wildcats lost three straight games by 20+ points to Northwestern, [Virginia Tech](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/virginia-tech-hokies) and [DePaul](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/depaul-blue-demons).
The Wildcats are bereft of Big Ten talent. The only top-tier player on the roster is sophomore guard Jake West, who leads the team in scoring with 18.7 points and 6.5 assists per game while shooting close to 50% from the field.
And when West doesn’t score well, Northwestern is in a tough spot. The Wildcats scored just 53 points against [Columbia](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/columbia-lions) in West’s worst game of the season.
Offensively, the Wildcats are 148th in efficiency, per KenPom. Their only standout number is a 15% turnover rate. They struggle to find good looks, ranking 209th in effective field goal percentage (49%) and shooting 33% from 3 (153rd).
It’s easy to pinpoint the holes of almost every Northwestern starter. At the four, Chris Collins relies on Jack Karasinski, an offensive weapon who struggles defensively and collects just 2.2 rebounds per game.
Next to him is Okku Federiko, who averages just 2.5 points per game on 25% shooting. Federiko is a total non-shooter, as is Angelo Ciaravino, who starts at the three.
Collins will need a big effort from West and LA Pratt to beat [Dayton](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/dayton-flyers).
On the other end, Northwestern is fairly mediocre, ranking 134th in defensive efficiency. It would be wise for the Wildcats to push the pace and force turnovers, as they boast a 19% turnover rate.
If they allow teams to take shots, it won’t work well for them — they rank 142nd in 2-point defense and 157th in 3-point defense.
The biggest concern is they give up offensive rebounds 35% of the time.

### Dayton Basketball
Dayton desperately needed to beat [Utah](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/utah-utes) in the first round of the Charleston Classic, and the Flyers had that one locked in by halftime.
The Flyers win didn’t come without a loss, with Landen Joseph suffering a serious-looking injury that required crutches. Zaide Lowery (ankle) also went down but came back after Joseph got hurt.
There are reasons to feel optimistic about Dayton shaking off the two early losses to [Siena](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/siena-saints) and [Northern Illinois](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northern-illinois-huskies).
The biggest one is the Flyers’ rim dominance — they shoot 62% on 2s (18th) and hold teams to 47% on 2s (89th). Plus, the Flyers not only defend the rim well, but they do so emphatically with a 16% block rate.
Anthony Grant will task the trio of Amael L’Etang, Tristian Ford and Aidan Derkack with the scoring. Derkack has developed into an immediate star, leading the team with 17.7 points, 8.3 rebounds and 1.8 steals per game.
L’Etang feels poised for a big game against a lousy Northwestern interior, as his 7-foot-1 frame is much larger than Federiko and the other Cats’ bigs.
If the Flyers can iron out their turnover issues (20% turnover rate), their offense should trend in the right direction.
Defensively, the biggest issue is rebounding, which feels fixable, too. Very few Flyers grab rebounds outside of Derkack, L’Etang and Grant Randall, so if the guards and other reserves can help on the glass, that should make Dayton’s defense more reliable.

### Butler Basketball
In Year 1 of the Ronald Nored era, the brand-new [Bulldogs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/butler-bulldogs) have looked fine out of the gate this season.
The Dogs have lost their first two matchups against top-100 teams, falling to [Boise State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/boise-state-broncos) and Pitt, and now are eyeing the No. 12 team in Bart Torvik's rankings.
This will be a very tough matchup for them, but maybe it's a little easier with the game being played in Chicago rather than Lincoln.
There are a few issues for Butler out of the gate, as the Bulldogs have been getting its shots blocked at a very high rate (19th-worst nationally in shots blocked). They're also struggling to defend the mid-range while failing to force turnovers, ranking in the mid-200s nationally in both categories.
Another concern is Butler's inability to get to the free-throw line. The Bulldogs rank in the bottom 100 nationally in free-throw rate, which creates a tough matchup against a [Nebraska](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/nebraska-cornhuskers) team that ranks in the top 20 in free-throw rate defense.
The Cornhuskers aren't exactly going to make life easier for Butler in that department.
If Butler is unable to improve in these areas, Sunday is going to be a struggle.

### Nebraska Basketball
The Cornhuskers were one of my [preseason teams to keep an eye on](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/2026-27-college-basketball-betting-3-overrated-underrated-teams), and they should be just as good as they were during last season's massive milestone campaign.
Through five games, that statement has held true. Now, have they faced a fairly easy schedule? Sure, but they've still taken down two top-100 teams out of the gate, beating [Providence](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/providence-friars) by nine and Boise State by 27.
The Cornhuskers are eyeing their second straight win over a Big East opponent in the Windy City, where there should be an equal amount of die-hard corn heads and Butler fans.
There isn't much of a lookahead spot for Nebraska, as the Cornhuskers host [Southern](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/southern-university-jaguars) after this matchup on Wednesday. Their focus should be right where it needs to be on Sunday.

## Butler vs. Nebraska Pick, Betting Analysis
The first noticeable thing about Butler's game is that the Bulldogs rank No. 1 nationally in 3-point rate early this season. Are they lethal from deep? They're top-150, per Bart Torvik.
However, they've endured a pretty easy schedule up to this point, ranking 262nd in schedule strength, per Haslametrics, which is pretty similar to Nebraska's.
So, how good have the six teams the Bulldogs have faced been at defending the 3-ball?
Well, Butler has looked tremendous from deep against anyone outside the top 100. But when the Bulldogs faced Boise State (234th in 3-point percentage defense) and Pitt (101st), they shot a combined 15-for-70 (21.4%) from beyond the arc.
That doesn't give this writer much confidence in their ability to sustain that offense, especially when their opponent has one of the best shooters in the country in Pryce Sandfort. The Cornhuskers can make it rain from deep at a much more efficient rate.
Plus, Nebraska may allow plenty of 3-point attempts, but the Cornhuskers still rank 29th nationally in 3-point percentage defense.
They've done a great job of holding down teams that rely heavily on the 3-ball this season, and I see them having no issues in Chicago when it comes to holding down the Dawgs from the perimeter.
**Pick: Nebraska -11.5**
