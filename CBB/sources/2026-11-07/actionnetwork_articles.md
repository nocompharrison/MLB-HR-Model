Dutcher, being the defensive tactician that he is, I expect them to bounce back.

### Colorado Basketball
[Colorado](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/colorado-buffaloes) began its year by crushing [Abilene Christian](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/abilene-christian-wildcats), 94-59. That’s a great sight for veteran HC Tad Boyle, as most of last year's team hit the transfer portal.
The one key cog who stayed is Barrington Hargress, a 6-1 guard who averaged 14.5 ppg last year. Hargress had just 11 points in his season debut. He'll need to put on his Superman cape and score more in this matchup.
Of the other four starters, three are returning players, but none started in 2025. The best of the bunch is Jalin Holland, who scored 18 points and six rebounds in his season debut. Ian Inman is the best shooter of the trio, and Josiah Sanders scored 14 against Abilene Christian.
Big-man play is my biggest question for Colorado. The Buffs are starting NDSU transfer Noah Feddersen, who averaged nine ppg and five rpg for the Bison. He's not a reliable shooter, and he'll struggle against SDSU's physical bigs. Feddersen is Boyle's best option, but Eric Jacobsen and Goc Malual will likely fill bench minutes.

## Maryland vs Georgetown College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/mar.png)
### Maryland Basketball
Year two of Buzz Williams is underway, and coming off a 12-21 season, the [Terrapins](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/maryland-terrapins) are looking to take a massive step forward. Williams snagged the No. 1 recruit, Baba Oladotun, who is drawing comparisons to Kevin Durant. Maryland also returns five players while losing seven this offseason. The Terps retain just one starter, Andre Mills, who averaged 12.2 PPG and 4.1 RPG a season ago.
Both Williams and assistant coach Devin Johnson believe they have plenty of talent this season, with a lot of guys clawing for minutes.
In their 96-67 win over [Bucknell](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/bucknell-bison), Mills put up 22 points and snagged 11 rebounds. Wildly enough, the team combined for just 11 assists. I'm sure we'll see much more ball movement against a better opponent.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/gtown.png)
### Georgetown Basketball
Will year four of Ed Cooley be the year this program finally takes a step toward becoming relevant again? The Hoyas haven't been relevant since we were all buying songs on iTunes for 99 cents.
However, there was some hope last season, as Georgetown opened the year 5-0, beating the very team it's playing Saturday, along with Clemson, before injuries struck and things fell apart. Now, heading into 2026-27, Georgetown returns seven players, most notably Caleb Williams, the Hoya, not the Bear. Williams is a returning starter who averaged 8.8 PPG and 5.1 RPG a year ago.
In Game 1, second-year starter Vince Iwuchukwu put up 21 points while snagging nine rebounds against Loyola Maryland. This team should take a massive step toward getting back to the Big Dance, but if not, Cooley might have to hop on LinkedIn soon.

![Header Second Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/gtown.png)
## Maryland vs. Georgetown Pick, Betting Analysis
Georgetown did play some solid competition in the preseason, facing Wake Forest, George Washington and Loyola Maryland. However, Maryland faced some solid competition of its own, beating Georgia Tech, West Virginia and Bucknell.
The one game that stood out to me was Maryland's 90-81 win over West Virginia. Ross Hodge runs a tight ship over there, leaning heavily on defense while playing at a turtle-like pace. The Terrapins putting up 90 points, even in an exhibition, is a ton.
Maryland has plenty of offensive firepower, and I expect it to show out in this matchup.
