There's no reason for this Fighting Illini team to be looking ahead to Tuesday's matchup against [Texas Tech](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-tech-red-raiders). Not only is that too large of a gap, but it's also too early in the season to start looking ahead and taking your foot off the gas.

Illinois already has real game experience under its belt and has played at an incredibly high level out of the gate. Meanwhile, Penn has spent the past few months scrimmaging against itself.
This one could be a slap in the mouth for the Quakers.

### Wake Forest Basketball
Put simply, [Wake Forest](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wake-forest-demon-deacons) could use a big season. Steve Forbes is in his seventh campaign, and the Demon Deacons haven't made the Big Dance during his tenure.
I do think the Deacons are underrated this year. In the season-opening win, Wake Forest posted a 25% turnover rate and held [Maryland Eastern Shore](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/maryland-eastern-shore-hawks) to 0.88 points per possession.
Of course, it's just the Hawks, but Wake Forest also forced 22 [Georgetown](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/georgetown-hoyas) turnovers in an exhibition game. I could see Forbes leaning into that style with [Kevair Kennedy](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/kevair-kennedy-322068-g)'s experience at [Merrimack](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/merrimack-warriors) and having two 6-foot-11 players in the frontcourt.
When you aren't super talented, you have to find a way to win on margins. Wake can do that by forcing turnovers.
The top two scorers for Wake appear to be Kennedy, who scored 18 in his Wake debut, and [Gavin Placide](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/gavin-placide-348265-f), a 6-foot-11 freshman who dropped 20 in his first college game. Placide also pitched in three blocks and two steals, while Kennedy had four steals. The two can be vital two-way options for Forbes.
That's why it makes sense for Wake to play a full-court game. It has rangy players who can push the tempo, but shooting is a concern. So, if Wake Forest decides to muck things up, it suits the Deacs well.
I'd argue that [Justin Ray](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/justin-ray-283079-g) is the most important player on the team. He's the one real shooter that Wake has, as he drilled 42% of his 3s at [Monmouth](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/monmouth-hawks) last season.
Can Wake Forest finish in the bottom five in the ACC as KenPom projects? Sure, but I'd bet against it.

### Duke Basketball
After a 35-3 campaign that ended in the Elite Eight with UConn's Braylon Mullins drilling a 35-footer, it's safe to say that Duke head coach Jon Scheyer is out for revenge in his fifth year at the helm.
While Duke is typically accustomed to massive roster turnover in the transfer portal era, the Blue Devils return a great deal of last season's production and boasts one of the best rotations I've seen in quite some time.
Despite losing the likes of [Isaiah Evans](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/isaiah-evans-338076-sg) and [Maliq Brown](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/players/maliq-brown-338087-pf) to the NBA draft, Scheyer fortunately retained [Patrick Ngongba II](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/patrick-ngongba-283541-c), Dame Sarr, Caleb Foster and [Cayden Boozer](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/cayden-boozer-320752-g).

## Penn vs Illinois College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/pen.png)
### Penn Basketball
Year 2 of the Fran McCaffery experience at [Penn](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/pennsylvania-quakers) should be another successful season, as he returns four starters and five reserves from 2025-26.
McCaffery is building something special, losing only Ethan Roberts from last season. The swingman averaged nearly 17 points and grabbed close to four rebounds per game for the Quakers.
Returning sophomore [Dalton Scantlebury](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/dalton-scantlebury-320801-f/c) is a guy to look out for this season. The 6-foot-9 forward averaged 5.9 points and 3.9 rebounds per game while shooting over 51% from the floor.
[Augustus Gerhart](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/augustus-gerhart-256479-f) is another player Penn is hoping will break out this season. The senior forward posted similar numbers to Scantlebury last season.
The Quakers also bring in junior guard Sir Mohammed, who spent his first two collegiate seasons at [Notre Dame](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/notre-dame-fighting-irish).
With the number of players McCaffery is bringing back, this could be a lethal bunch in the Ivy League.

### Illinois Basketball
If you didn't already know, I'm in love with this year's [Fighting Illini](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/illinois-fighting-illini) team. I wrote in my [Big Ten preview](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/big-ten-basketball-betting-preview-2026-27-odds-picks-predictions) that this team is well worth backing to win it all this season.
Brad Underwood kept many key players from last year's Final Four run. The head coach knows how talented this group can be on offense.
Last season, Illinois finished second to [Purdue](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/purdue-boilermakers) in adjusted offensive efficiency, per KenPom. That's why Underwood's main focus this offseason was improving the defense, stating, "We have to get better."
Similar to Penn, bringing back that many players speaks volumes about how attractive the system and culture are to big-name talents, making Illinois an extremely dangerous team. The Illini showed that on opening night, beating [Fairleigh Dickinson](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/fairleigh-dickinson-knights) 120-45 as a 40-point favorite.
Now, for this matchup, Illinois likely won't win by that much, as Penn is 232 spots ahead of Fairleigh Dickinson in Bart Torvik's ratings. However, the Illini should still steamroll.

## Penn vs. Illinois Pick, Betting Analysis
There's no reason for this Fighting Illini team to be looking ahead to Tuesday's matchup against [Texas Tech](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-tech-red-raiders). Not only is that too large of a gap to overlook, but it's also too early in the season to start looking ahead and taking your foot off the gas.
They're going to clean up the flaws and use Penn as a get-ready game for next Tuesday.
Penn has yet to see any real game action this season. All the Quakers have had to prepare for Illinois is their annual Red and Blue Scrimmage. Yikes.
Illinois already has real game experience under its belt and has played at an incredibly high level out of the gate. Meanwhile, Penn has spent the past few months scrimmaging against itself.
This one could be a slap in the mouth for the Quakers.

### Cal Poly Basketball
Mike DeGeorge has a knack for upsetting high-majors teams. Two years ago, the Mustangs beat [Arizona State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/arizona-state-sun-devils), and last year they beat [Stanford](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/stanford-cardinal).
Can the ‘Stangs steal one in Tucson? Unlikely, but Cal Poly is a fun team.
It began the season with a tune-up win against Cal State San Marcos — a non-D-1 program — meaning this will be the first real action for Cal Poly.
Every member of Cal Poly’s starting five was on the roster last year, and three started: Hamad Mousa led the team with 18 ppg, Cayden Ward had 14.5 ppg, and sharpshooter Guzman Vasilic poured in 7.6 and shot 37% from 3.
It doesn’t matter who Cal Poly plays; it won’t deter them from [push](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/push)ing the pace. It led the country in adjusted tempo last year and took 3s on 52% of its field-goal attempts.
That’s why DeGeorge arms his roster with four guards 6-3+ since all of them can rebound and push the pace and shoot, and a versatile five-man like Jess Esso Essis.
I view Esso Essis as the key in this one. He offers a versatile skill set at 6-11, and Cal Poly needs him on the court, but Arizona will try to get him in foul trouble.
However, doing business at a rampant pace comes at the cost of turnover. Cal Poly turned it over 25 times against Cal State San Marcos. Arizona will bury Cal Poly in transition if the Mustangs' turnover woes carry into this one.

### Arizona Basketball
Arizona had a tough showing to open the year, losing outright as 5.5-point favorites against [UCLA](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ucla-bruins) on a neutral court. Expectations have grown so high under Tommy Lloyd that even the slightest slip-up causes panic. 
There’s reason for worry but plenty more for optimism. 
The concerns? Arizona went from the steady Jaden [Bradley](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/bradley-braves) to question marks at point guard. JJ Mandaquit tallied six assists and zero turnovers in 32 minutes but scored just three points. The other two guards, five-star freshman Caleb Holt and Derek Dixon, scored 7 points on 2-7 shooting and 18 points on 5-17 shooting, respectively. 
It was just one game, but Lloyd usually plays elite guard. That trio has to improve as time rolls on. 
The Wildcats frontcourt is one of the best in the country. Sophomore forward Ivan Kharchenkov kicked off his breakout sophomore campaign with 38 points and 10 rebounds. 
The one who’s supposed to be Arizona’s best player, Motiejus Krivas, a 7-2 defensive enforcer and reliable scorer, struggled against UCLA.
I wouldn’t count on Krivas’s struggles to last. He should destroy this matchup.
