The [Ohio State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ohio-state-buckeyes) Buckeyes lost their star leader, Bruce Thornton. Ohio State has a lot of young talent, but they don’t really have any leadership on the team. ### North Alabama Basketball
North Alabama is coming off a brutal 2025-26 campaign, but Tony Pujol has the necessary pieces to flip things this season.
The Lions have enjoyed a solid start, sitting at 2-1 with a true road win over [UC Davis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/uc-davis-aggies) and a narrow nine-point loss to [Oklahoma](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/oklahoma-sooners).
One area that separates North Alabama from other mid-major counterparts is its length — the Lions rank 99th in average height, and they have just one rotation player below 6-foot-4.
Usually, Pujol isn’t a coach who likes his teams shooting a bunch of 3s. In the past four years, the Lions have taken around 35% of their shots from downtown. So far in 2026, North Alabama has taken over half of its shots from 3.
I don’t expect them to sustain such a high volume, but it might need to in this matchup.
The well-traveled Mike Evbagharu has been the go-to guy for North Alabama. He leads the team with 19 points and 7.3 rebounds per game. The only other double-digit scorer is sixth man, Quan Lax.
Lax is probably the only true point guard on the roster, which makes the Lions’ robust 24% turnover rate a real concern. He plays 20 minutes per game and leads the team with 1.7 assists. Plus, only 38% of their made shots come via an assist.

### Miami Basketball
Trying to beat Miami with one-on-one 3s is a formula to get crushed.
Miami was able to release frustration, beating [Wagner](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wagner-seahawks) by 34 and [North Florida](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/north-florida-ospreys) by 38 after losing to [Florida](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-gators) on opening night.
The Hurricanes have a chance to be one of the top defensive units in the country, ranking 11th in defensive efficiency. Their rim deterrence has been huge, limiting teams to 36% shooting on 2s.
Four of the five starters for the Canes are 6-foot-6 or taller, and Shelton Henderson is the 6-foot-6 player who plays like he’s 6-foot-10. Bringing in Somto Cyril was huge for Miami. The uber-athletic big is one of the top shot-blockers and lob threats in the ACC.
Now, we encounter the one issue with Miami: Shooting.
The Canes are hitting a woeful 22% from downtown this season. It's not surprising that Miami is a poor shooting team, but 22% is a different level of bad. Henderson is 0-for-6 from 3 this year, and Acaden Lewis is shooting 16% from deep. Those two were supposed to level up from beyond the arc, but it hasn’t happened.
At least Caleb Gaskins is shooting 37% from deep and Malik Reneau is at 50% so far.

## Georgetown vs Clemson College Basketball Preview

### Georgetown Basketball
[Georgetown](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/georgetown-hoyas) is off to a stellar start in head coach Ed Cooley’s third season at the helm.
The Hoyas had quite the offseason, with a massive overhaul of their roster, but fortunately, they returned a few key players from last season's team who should provide Cooley’s bunch with critical experience.
Georgetown lost its entire backcourt, which featured last season’s leading scorers, guards Malik Mack and KJ Lewis.
In response to this, Cooley got an improvement with former [Kentucky](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kentucky-wildcats) guard Jaland Lowe, who's off to a phenomenal start to his first year in D.C. Lowe is already averaging 18.3 points per game this season, along with 8.3 assists and over three rebounds.
Cooley also acquired former [Kansas](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-jayhawks) guard Elmarko Jackson. The 6-foot-3 senior is averaging over 12 points per game, but he was primarily brought in as a defensive stopper after he proved himself as an exceptional on-ball defender in his two years in Lawrence.
They also added FAU transfer Josiah Parker to add some further depth to a loaded frontcourt that already features returnee and leading scorer in 7-foot-1 senior center Vince Iwuchukwu.
The Nigerian is off to a very impressive start to his final collegiate season, as he's already averaging 20.3 points and 8.3 rebounds per game.
Cooley was also able to retain 6-foot-4 junior guard Kayvaun Mulready, who boasts impressive versatility at both ends of the floor.
After a 2025-26 campaign that saw Georgetown finish with a 16-18 overall record and score just 73.6 points per game, the Hoyas have looked far superior offensively in their victories over [Maryland](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/maryland-terrapins), Loyola MD and [Delaware State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/delaware-state-hornets).
That said, the Hoyas have a very difficult non-conference schedule ahead, starting with their first road game on Friday.
The average age on this Georgetown roster is over 20 years old, which is undoubtedly a massive strength as the Hoyas navigate a tough strength of schedule over the next several weeks.

### Clemson Basketball
It was quite the offseason for [Clemson](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/clemson-tigers) head coach Brad Brownell, who's in his 17th year at the helm.
Despite Brownell having to start from scratch after losing three of his top four scorers from last season — RJ Godfrey, Jestin Porter and Nick Davidson — this program boasts a phenomenal portal class that has got off to a scorching start to the season thus far.
Clemson acquired some elite international talent in 6-foot-7 Lithuanian forward Liutauras Lelevicius and 6-foot-9 Austrian forward David Fuchs, both of whom are off to a great start this season.
Lelevicius has already drastically improved the Tigers’ perimeter shooting, with the former [TCU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tcu-horned-frogs) forward averaging 17 points this season while connecting at a remarkable 64.3% clip from beyond the arc.
Former [Notre Dame](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/notre-dame-fighting-irish) 6-foot-5 guard Cole Certa was also brought in to the same effect, boasting an impressive 3-point stroke of his own.
Brownell also added a ton of size to bolster the Tigers’ frontcourt and overall interior presence, landing the likes of 6-foot-10 former [Samford](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/samford-bulldogs) forward Dylan Faulkner and 6-foot-9 Austrian David Fuchs, who transferred from [San Francisco](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/san-francisco-dons).
Faulkner has already put his impressive versatility on full display, averaging 13.3 points, 7.7 rebounds and 5.0 assists over the Tigers’ first three games.
The former Bulldog has also made an immediate impact on the defensive end, recording 1.3 steals and 1.7 blocks per game.
Clemson guard Ace Buckner has emerged as the team’s leading scorer, with the 6-foot-3 sophomore already averaging a spectacular 19.7 points per game.
Both Buckner and 6-foot-8 forward Chase Thompson are key returnees for the Tigers this season and are both off to a fantastic start.
While the Tigers successfully overhauled most of their rotation, Brownell’s bunch has unfortunately dealt with some injury concerns to start the year, with guard Zac Foster re-tearing his ACL.
Carter Welling, who led the team in rebounding last season, suffered the exact same fate with an ACL tear that's expected to keep him off the court until late December.
Despite the Tigers dealing with several injuries, Brownell’s bunch is off to a phenomenal start with its new-look lineup.

## Georgetown vs Clemson Pick, Betting Analysis
We're in for a very closely contested matchup on Friday evening, featuring two power foes that underwent massive roster overhauls this past offseason, yet still boast a ton of experience throughout their respective lineups.
While I do feel the Hoyas are due for regression in their first road game of the season, I actually think Georgetown matches up very well with Clemson, given its massive interior size at both ends of the floor.
Buckner is in the midst of a breakout campaign for the Tigers, but he faces a very tough defensive matchup against Georgetown, which boasts two elite two-way guards.
Though Clemson’s Lelevicius and Faulkner have thrived offensively in Brownell’s frontcourt, they will be up against Iwuchukwu, who I expect to make it very difficult for the two to find open looks near the basket.
While Clemson has been lighting it up from beyond the arc, Buckner’s and Lelevicius’ respective 3-point field goal percentages of 47.1% and 64.3% on the season are largely unsustainable — and I expect some regression to inevitably hit.
Georgetown was very underrated heading into this season, even with its massive interior size, significantly improved guard play and very experienced overall lineup.
Though far from a high-confidence spot, I lean Georgetown to cover the 3.5-point spread.

## Pitt vs Butler College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/pit.png)
### Pitt Basketball
[Pitt](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/pittsburgh-panthers) is a team that has been rebuilt from the ground up, and the early returns have come with a physical edge. Jeff Capel's roster is almost entirely new after a 13-20 season, and the pieces that matter on the glass are in place.
Baye Ndongo, a senior forward who came over from [Georgia Tech](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/georgia-tech-yellow-jackets), is averaging 11.7 rebounds, and 14 of his 35 boards have been on the offensive end, while freshman forward Chase Foster adds 7.0 a game next to him.
The Panthers are out-rebounding opponents by 7.7 a game, 40.3 to 32.7, and they have 40 offensive rebounds through three games to 27 for their opponents.
The rest of the profile fits a team that wins by making games ugly. Pitt is forcing 16.7 turnovers a game while committing only 10.0, and opponents are shooting just 36.1% from the field against it.
That has led to wins of 95-57 over [Rider](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/rider-broncs), 80-61 over [Buffalo](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/buffalo-bulls), and an 82-76 win over [Penn State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/penn-state-nittany-lions) at the Palestra on Sunday, a game where A'lahn Sumler scored 26 points, and Jonathan Powell had five steals even though [Penn](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/pennsylvania-quakers) State won the rebounding battle 37-28.
That Penn State game is the one blemish on the glass, and it is a reminder that the Panthers' numbers are partly the result of a soft opening schedule.
Now Pitt goes to Hinkle Fieldhouse for its first road game of the season.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/butb.png)
### Butler Basketball
[Butler](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/butler-bulldogs) is a very different team than the one that lost Finley Bizjack, Michael Ajayi, and three other top scorers. First-year coach Ronald Nored has rebuilt the roster with transfers and international additions, and the early results have been loud at home.
The Bulldogs beat [Lafayette](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/lafayette-leopards) 96-60 and [Fairleigh Dickinson](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/fairleigh-dickinson-knights) 99-65 at Hinkle, shooting 36% from three against Lafayette and holding a 52-29 halftime lead over the Knights. Jalen Jackson (17.7 points a game), Asim Djulovic (17.3) and Eduardo Klafke (16.0) are the scoring core, and Butler is out-rebounding opponents by 13.3 a game behind forward Treyson Anderson, who averages 8.3 rebounds, and 6-foot-10 center Drayton Jones, who has seven blocks.
The more telling game came on the road. Butler lost 100-81 at [Boise State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/boise-state-broncos) on Sunday, allowing 100 points in a game that finished with 181 combined. The Bulldogs are turning the ball over 15.3 times a game and allowing 75.0 points a game, and their lopsided home wins came against Lafayette and Fairleigh Dickinson.
Pitt is a better test than either.

## Pitt vs Butler Pick, Betting Analysis
The "Rebound Unders" System is built for a game like this one:
The system targets non-conference games with an elevated total where the road team is a strong rebounder and the home team is not an overwhelming underdog, and each of those boxes is checked.
Pitt is the road team and is out-rebounding opponents by nearly eight a game, Butler is a 3-point home favorite rather than a long shot, and the total has climbed from an opener of 155.5 to 160 and 160.5 at some books.
The logic is that a rebounding road team limits the other side's second chances and extends its own possessions, which cuts down on the transition opportunities that drive high totals, and that unfamiliar opponents in November tend to play with discipline rather than pace. Pitt's offensive rebounding gives it a way to turn missed shots into possessions rather than into fast breaks the other way.
The market backs up the idea that the number has been pushed up more than the games have supported. Pitt's three games have totaled 152, 141, and 158 points, all below 160, and the moneyline has barely moved, from Pitt +138 to +146, with Butler a 3-point favorite.
The total has gone up about five points, which we read as a reaction to Butler's 92.0 points a game and the 181-point game at Boise State, but Butler's roster is brand new, and the sample is three games, two of them against opponents that lost by 36 and 34.
A new coach and a new group at Hinkle against a physical opponent is a different test, and we expect Pitt to try to take the game to the glass and shorten it. Pitt's defense gives it a way to do that, holding opponents to 64.7 points a game on 36% shooting.
Fade the recency bias inflating the total tonight.

## Northern Iowa vs Michigan State College Basketball Preview

### Northern Iowa Basketball
Kyle Green began his road to becoming the head man at [Northern Iowa](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northern-iowa-panthers) in 2001. This is his third tenure at UNI and his first season as head coach.
Northern Iowa faces tough competition to start the season, including a road game against a team coming off a statement win. The Panthers also have a road game at [Kansas](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-jayhawks) and neutral-site games vs. [Florida](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-gators) and [Davidson](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/davidson-wildcats).
This roster is heavily influenced by the NIL portal, but most of its contributors came from lower-division schools like [North Dakota](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/north-dakota-fighting-hawks) and [Tulsa](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tulsa-golden-hurricane).
The biggest issue we've seen so far in the early action has been their lack of defense. They haven't played offensive juggernauts, but this team has already given up at least 70 points in every game, including 98 to [Furman](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/furman-paladins).

### Michigan State Basketball
After two wins against inferior opponents in [Quinnipiac](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/quinnipiac-bobcats) and [Toledo](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/toledo-rockets), a couple of question marks remained. However, the Spartans answered many of those questions with a 79-73 win over [Duke](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/duke-blue-devils).
This Michigan State roster is also built differently than most big-time programs in this era. Their best player is [Jeremy Fears Jr.](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/jeremy-fears-257832-g), a four-year player who's considered an All-American.
He's joined by [Coen Carr](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/coen-carr-257836-f), [Cam Ward](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/cam-ward-320123-f) and [Jordan Scott](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/jordan-scott-320092-f), all notable returning players from a year ago.
That's without mentioning a pretty solid freshman class led by four-star recruit [Jasiah Jervis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/jasiah-jervis-349945-g).

## Northern Iowa vs Michigan State Pick, Betting Analysis
My first thought on this game was to take the points. The Panthers are catching the Spartans off a big win, and they get a near 30-point cushion.
But I couldn’t bring myself to pull the trigger on either side.
I think this game will see more tempo than most would expect, and if the Spartans get out to a decent lead early, we can expect even more pace from the Panthers.
I like this game to go over the total of 155.5.
