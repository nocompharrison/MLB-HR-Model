## Fairleigh Dickinson vs. Butler
[![Fairleigh Dickinson Knights Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/fdu.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/fairleigh-dickinson-knights)
Wednesday, Nov. 4
7 p.m. ET
ESPN+
[![Butler Bulldogs Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/butb.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/butler-bulldogs)
Under 153.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
By [Alex Kolodziej](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/alex-kolodziej)
Butler head coach Ronald Nored knows a thing or two about magic, as he was a part of two Final Four teams in Indianapolis.
Well, he's going to need all the magic possible if Butler wants to make noise in the Big East this season. The Bulldogs were picked to finish in last place in the conference's preseason poll in Nored's debut campaign.
Butler started things off in a big way, dropping 96 points in a win over Lafayette. But according to our [Action PRO signals](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/sharp-report), that might be an anomaly.
Our power ratings say that this total is off by over four points, creating one of the bigger over/under edges on the board. The line currently sits at 153.5 (at the time of writing), and our PRO model has this number at a smidge over 149.
There's some [sharp money](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/sharp-sports-betting) coming in on the over, but this model projection signal is a bit stronger, creating a more compelling edge.

MidcoSportsPlus
[![South Dakota Coyotes Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/sd.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/south-dakota-coyotes)
Under 161.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Evan Abrams](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/evan-abrams)
Both of these teams enter this game at 1-0, but their wins weren't equivalent.
Northern Kentucky beat UTEP during the 24-hour opening day marathon, thanks to massive outings from Willie Foster (24 points, 10 rebounds) and Noah Pagotto (19 points, 12 rebounds). Meanwhile, South Dakota topped Mount Marty, a non-D-I team.
Even on the road, doesn't that mean we should be licking our chops with the Norse -2.5?
My [Bet Labs Sports Insights](https://www.sportsinsights.com/get-bet-labs/) system — "Rebound Unders" — actually says the best play is on the under.
This system owns a solid 5% return on investment (ROI) all-time, including a strong 55% hit rate (545-451-5).

## North Dakota State vs. Saint Mary's
[![North Dakota State Bison Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ndakst.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/north-dakota-state-bison)
Wednesday, Nov. 4
10 p.m. ET
ESPN+
[![Saint Mary's Gaels Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/smc.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/saint-marys-gaels)
North Dakota State +13.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Carmine Carcieri](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/carmine-carcieri)
For the first time since 2001, Saint Mary's has a new head coach roaming the sidelines. Randy Bennett is off to Tempe while Mickey McConnell is taking over in Moraga.
And it's evident it's going to take the Gaels time to adjust to a new system, roster and direction.
Saint Mary's nearly lost to Illinois State in its opener, rallying from down six at the half to win by five in overtime.
Now, Illinois State is projected to finish second in the Missouri Valley Conference, so it's not exactly a slouch. However, neither is the Gaels' opponent on Wednesday, North Dakota State.
In fact, the Bison are expected to win their conference behind Preseason All-Summit League First Teamer Andy Stefonowicz.
North Dakota State doesn't have the massive frontcourt bodies to attack Saint Mary's where the Gaels are their weakest, but the Bison have continuity under coach David Richman and the junior Stefonowicz.
While I expect the Gaels to win at home, this number is too large. Saint Mary's has a lot to figure out under McConnell and it's coming off an overtime game two days ago.
Most importantly, two [Bet Labs systems](https://www.sportsinsights.com/get-bet-labs/) with positive all-time ROI's — "After Score 80, Fade on Quick Turn" and "Fade Hot Shooting" — like this pick, making me feel more confident.
**Pick:[ North Dakota State +13.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3164462232&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**
