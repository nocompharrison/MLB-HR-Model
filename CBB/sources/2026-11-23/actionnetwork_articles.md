[![Tulane Green Wave Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2023/07/Tulane-Green-Wave-Logo-Updated-2023.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tulane-green-wave)

PassThaBallLive.com
[![Georgia State Panthers Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/gst.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/georgia-state-panthers)
Tulane +1.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
[Tulane](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tulane-green-wave) holds a clear advantage in this matchup, sitting 17 spots ahead of [Georgia State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/georgia-state-panthers) on KenPom, which projects an outright win for the Green Wave.
Georgia State's resume is weak, with just two wins on the season — one against a non-Division I team and the other over 335th-ranked [Norfolk State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/norfolk-state-spartans).
Furthermore, Georgia State has dropped games to teams ranked 180th and 186th on KenPom, right in the same range as Tulane (177th).
A key factor will be Tulane's ability to draw fouls, as the Green Wave rank in the top 12 nationally in offensive free-throw rate.
Given Georgia State's defensive struggles and high foul rate (259th in defensive free-throw rate), Tulane should consistently get to the charity stripe. The Green Wave score almost 30% of their points from the line, so the fact that Georgia State fouls a lot will play right into Tulane’s biggest offensive strength.
The line favors the wrong team here, making Tulane +1.5 an outstanding value.

## Washington vs. BYU
[![Washington Huskies Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/was.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/washington-huskies)
Monday, Nov. 23
5 p.m. ET
ESPN2
[![BYU Cougars Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/byu.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/byu-cougars)
BYU -12.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
[BYU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/byu-cougars) enters this matchup firing on all cylinders on the offensive end. The Cougars boast the nation's seventh-ranked offensive efficiency, knocking down nearly 42% of their shots from deep and nearly 67% from inside the arc.
With a potent offensive core of Robert Wright III, Collin Chandler, Bruce Branch III and Jaxon Kohler, they present an extremely tough assignment for [Washington](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/washington-huskies).
Defensively, Washington ranks just 120th nationally, which is a major red flag considering the Huskies haven't tested themselves against a single opponent ranked inside KenPom's top 230.
Their flawless record feels a bit misleading, as they have yet to secure a victory over a high-major program.
While the Huskies rank among the top teams in 3-point attempt rate and excel on the glass with a top-20 offensive rebounding mark, BYU's top-five defensive rebounding efficiency should effectively erase that second-chance threat.
Washington simply can't afford to get drawn into a perimeter shooting contest against one of the country's elite 3-point shooting teams.
Look for BYU's high-powered offense to overwhelm Washington in Maui, so back the Cougars to cover the spread.

## Illinois State vs. Santa Clara
[![Illinois State Redbirds Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ils.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/illinois-state-redbirds)
Monday, Nov. 23
7 p.m. ET
FloHoops
[![Santa Clara Broncos Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/961996_santaclara.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/santa-clara-broncos)
Santa Clara -4.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
Illinois State faces a cross-country trip for this matchup, traveling to Palm Springs, California, to face [Santa Clara](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/santa-clara-broncos).
Although this is hosted at a neutral venue, playing in California gives a slight edge to the in-state Broncos.
From a stylistic standpoint, the Redbirds rely heavily on perimeter shooting — ranking in the top 20 nationally in 3-point volume — yet they struggle to convert efficiently, making only 31% of their attempts.
Santa Clara excels at forcing poor shooters into bad looks, holding opposing teams to under 27% from beyond the arc.
Conversely, [Illinois State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/illinois-state-redbirds)'s defense allows opponents to hit nearly 40% of their 3-pointers, perfectly aligning with Santa Clara's season average.
Illinois will attempt to slow this game down, but that is easier said than done against a Santa Clara team that has great guard play and versatile wings.

## VCU vs. Providence
[![VCU Rams Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/vcu.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/virginia-commonwealth-rams)
Monday, Nov. 23
9 p.m. ET
ESPNU
[![Providence Friars Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/prov.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/providence-friars)
Providence -1.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
Expect a fast-paced, high-chaos contest when [Providence](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/providence-friars) meets [VCU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/virginia-commonwealth-rams) in Maui, but Providence holds the upper hand.
The Friars rank 20th in national defensive efficiency and excel at forcing turnovers, ranking third overall.
That defensive prowess poses a significant challenge for a VCU squad likely battling fatigue. After overcoming an 18-point second-half deficit to beat Santa Clara in overtime on Thursday, VCU had to make the long journey to Maui for Monday's matchup.
Providence, conversely, has rested since its victory over [Sacred Heart](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/sacred-heart-pioneers) on Tuesday.
Beyond defense and turnovers, the glass will prove pivotal.
A cornerstone of Providence's offensive approach relies on firing from deep and aggressively crashing the offensive glass. Because defensive rebounding is a vulnerability for VCU (218th nationally in defensive rebounding percentage), fresh Friar legs attacking the glass should dominate the interior.

### Ole Miss Basketball
Ole Miss went with a cupcake schedule before the Maui Invitational, playing just one team better than 296th in KenPom.
To the Rebels' credit, they won all four of their games by 30+ points.
This is a classic Chris Beard team with Mark Adams calling the defensive shots. The Rebels boast the 10th-most efficient defense in the country. They generate turnovers 30% of the time (best in the sport) and hold teams to 40% on 2s (14%).
It may be an easy schedule so far, but those numbers point towards this defense being sustainable.
I’m less sold on Ole Miss scoring in the half-court. The Rebels will look to score on the interior and in the mid-range with the shifty Adam “Budd” Clark at the controls. The 5-foot-10 guard from Philly leads the team with 15.5 points and 4.8 assists per game, while shooting 53% from the field.
They rank 67th in offensive efficiency thanks to shooting 57% on 2s (64th) and turning the ball over just 12% of the time (11th).
If Clemson forces the Rebels to shoot 3s and pack the paint, it’ll be tough for Ole Miss to respond. The Rebels attempts 3s on just 30% of their field goal attempts and hit 32% of them.
The only player shooting well is Patton Pinkins, who’s 9-for-12 (75%) from 3. Nobody else with more than five attempts is in the 30% or better range.

## Washington vs BYU College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/was.png)
### Washington Basketball
There was not a lot of love coming into the season for Washington, as many believed this team will finish in the middle of the Big Ten.
Despite the very easy schedule to open the 2026-27 campaign, the Huskies have shown some bright spots.
Fifth-year guard Steele Venters, who has endured an injury-riddled college career, is leading the team in scoring while grabbing 4.3 rebounds per game. Senior guard and four-time transfer LeJuan Watts is second in scoring, stepping up for his new program.
It will be interesting to see how these guys gel once their schedule heats up. However, ranking 115th in adjusted defensive efficiency while enduring this easy schedule could be concerning.
The Huskies aren't defending the perimeter well, are sending opponents to the line a bit too often and aren't attacking the rim at all.
This matchup could be a wake-up call for Danny Sprinkle and his boys.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/byu.png)
### BYU Basketball
There is so much talent on this BYU team that, once it starts gelling, it could be a very hard team to stop. That's especially true if Robert Wright III and big man Jaxon Kohler are humming.
Wright, in particular, is not only leading the team in scoring at nearly 25 points per game, but he also leads in assists and steals.
Aside from those two studs, the team, as a whole, has been cruising, beating everyone in its way by an average of 25.4 points.
There are a few concerns, though. The Cougars have struggled to defend the interior (316th) and the perimeter (235th in 3-point percentage defense).
However, they don't foul often and block shots at a high rate, all while Bart Torvik ranks them 42nd in adjusted defensive efficiency.

## Washington vs. BYU Pick, Betting Analysis
It is hard to want to back the 'dog on a neutral-site court in lovely Hawaii when said 'dog has yet to leave its home this season.
Meanwhile, the heavy favorite kicked off its season on the road, taking down [Ohio State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ohio-state-buckeyes) by 11.
BYU should be able to manage the less battle-tested Huskies, as the 'dogs have only faced teams ranked outside the top 145 this season.
The highest-ranked team Washington has played was [Colgate](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/colgate-raiders) on opening night. Since then, the Huskies have faced Mississippi Valley State (362nd), Simon Fraser (non-Division I) and Utah Tech (294th).
Haslametrics has Washington enduring the ninth-easiest schedule in the nation, while BYU sits 154 spots ahead of the Huskies at 203rd.
The Huskies have struggled to force turnovers, get to the line and defend the perimeter, ranking in the bottom 130 nationally in all three categories.
On top of that, this unit isn't attacking the rim, ranking 360th in close 2-point share, according to Bart Torvik. Washington is very 3-point-reliant, owning the fourth-highest 3-point rate in the nation.
Guarding the outside shot has been an issue for BYU, but Kevin Young should have a game plan in place to shut down the perimeter and force the 'dogs to go inside.
If the Cougars can keep the Huskies from draining shots from beyond the arc, they should be able to come out alive behind their top-10 offense.

### VCU Basketball
In a transfer portal era — which is largely defined by extensive roster turnover — [VCU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/virginia-commonwealth-rams) second-year head coach Phil Martelli Jr. retained seven players from last season's team who accounted for nearly 60% of its offensive production.
The Rams retained three players from their starting lineup, including 6-foot-11 senior forward Lazar Djokovic, 6-foot-1 sophomore guard Nyk Lewis and 6-foot-4 junior wing Brandon Jennings Jr.
Both Djokovic and Lewis received Preseason First Team All-A-10 honors, and the duo is off to a phenomenal start to the season, recording 15 and 13 points per game, respectively.
Despite Martelli’s impressive roster continuity, he had to replace several elite outside shooters who combined for 143 triples last season.
VCU’s biggest loss this past offseason was the team’s premier scorer in 6-foot-2 guard Terrence Hill Jr., who ultimately chose to play for Tennessee.
Fortunately, Martelli secured former [Creighton](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/creighton-bluejays) guard Blake Harper to replace Hill, a move that's reaped significant rewards thus far, with the 6-foot-7 junior currently leading the Rams in scoring with 18 points per game.
VCU boasts arguably the deepest frontcourt in the Atlantic 10 with Djokovic and [Middle](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/middle)[Tennessee](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tennessee-volunteers) transfer Torey Alston.
While the Rams’ defense looked quite vulnerable in an 89-88 loss to [Memphis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/memphis-tigers), Martelli’s bunch defeated its other three opponents by an extremely impressive 18-point margin on average.
Though wins over [Little Rock](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/arkansas-little-rock-trojans) and [NJIT](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/nj.i.t.-highlanders) don't necessarily impress, VCU enters the Maui Invitational fresh off a six-point victory over a very talented [Santa Clara](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/santa-clara-broncos) squad.
Martelli’s coaching style emphasizes relentless full-court defensive pressure, high-tempo transition offense and physicality in the paint.

### Providence Basketball
[Providence](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/providence-friars) is off to a great start under first-year head coach Bryan Hodgson, winning four of its first five contests, instilling some confidence into a Friars squad that had a rough few years under Kim English.
The former South Florida head coach is coming off an impressive 25-9 campaign, winning [American](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/american-university-eagles) Conference Coach of the Year on his way to leading the Bulls to their first NCAA Tournament berth since 2012.
Hodgson brought a new coaching staff along with him to Friartown, one that's largely focusing on a fast-paced offense and aggressive perimeter defense.
The Friars lost several key contributors from last season's core, with Jaylin Sellers signing with the Bulls and freshman duo Stefan Vaaks and Jamier Jones entering the transfer portal.
6-foot-7 forward Ryan Mela is the sole returnee from last season's roster.
Despite it being just his first season with the program, Hodgson made an immediate impact, quickly assembling a top-10 portal class.
Hodgson acquired former Mountain West Defensive Player of the Year Miles Byrd, adding some much-needed experience to the Friars’ roster.
Providence boasts elite depth at the guard position, with its first-year head coach also landing the likes of [Georgetown](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/georgetown-hoyas)’s Malik Mack and [Florida Atlantic](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-atlantic-owls)’s Devin Vanterpool, among a couple of others.
Vanterpool has been phenomenal thus far, leading the Friars in scoring with 16 points per game, but he's played in just two of Providence’s five matchups this season.
Hodgson bolstered a Friars frontcourt that struggled mightily on the defensive end of the floor last season, landing an elite rim protector in 6-foot-11 senior forward Arrinten Page, in addition to 6-foot-9 junior forward Jacob Bannarbie.
Forward Elijah Saunders was another stellar acquisition for Hodgson, with the former Terp granted eligibility right before the season started.
While the Friars struggled in their sole loss of the season to [Nebraska](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/nebraska-cornhuskers), Hodgson’s bunch defeated its other four opponents by an average victory margin exceeding 20 points.

## VCU vs. Providence Pick, Betting Analysis
The Maui Invitational kicks off on Monday night with the Rams and Friars set to duke it out in a sharp contrast of playing styles that I expect to result in a very closely contested matchup.
VCU boasts a high-octane offense, scoring over 85 points per game on the season, while the Friars bring in a very physical defense, allowing opponents less than 60 points on average.
The Friars' defense is among the nation’s best at generating turnovers, ranking ninth nationally with 12.2 steals per game, largely thanks to Byrd’s 4.8 per game.
While Providence relies heavily on forcing turnovers on the defensive end to win games, the Rams rank outside the top 330 in college basketball in steals per game, which should make VCU’s ball security a massive factor on Monday.
Though Martelli’s roster continuity undoubtedly gives VCU an experience edge, Hodgson’s bunch boasts a ton of experience, as well, given its massive influx of talent via the portal.
I expect Hodgson’s lockdown defense and tremendous rotational depth to be the ultimate difference-makers on Monday night, so I'll be taking a shot on Providence moneyline.
