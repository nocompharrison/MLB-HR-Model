## Michigan State vs. Duke
[![Michigan State Spartans Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/msud.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-state-spartans)
Tuesday, Nov. 10
7 p.m. ET
ESPN
[![Duke Blue Devils Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/dukd.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/duke-blue-devils)
Under 149.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
By [Mike Calabrese](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/mike-calabrese/page/1)
It’s too early in the season to really glean anything useful from advanced stats. Duke laid waste to Army and Coastal Carolina by a combined score of 197 to 102.
Army has never made the NCAA Tournament and that won’t change this year. The Cadets are unlikely to break .500 in the Patriot League and provided little resistance to Duke in their opener, mainly due to their undersized front line.
Army doesn’t start a single player over 6-foot-9, while Duke threw waves of NBA-ready bigs at the Black Knights for 40 minutes. The result was a defensive masterclass (22 stocks), and utter dominance on the offensive glass (14 rebounds).
It was a copy-and-paste situation against Coastal Carolina in the following game.
These were tin cans; Michigan State is anything but.
The Spartans thumped a pair of teams in paycheck games so far, dropping Quinnipiac and Toledo by 37 and 30, respectively. This Spartans team wasn’t constructed to be a Duke Killer, but it does have that potential.
It starts with elite, unflappable point guard play. Jeremy Fears Jr. was a consensus preseason All-American, and with good reason. He sets the table for Tom Izzo as one of the best facilitators in the sport.
Everything Sparty wants to do begins and ends with him.
His surehandedness also prevents Duke from breaking out in transition, which is why I love the under in this matchup.
Duke’s half-court offense is still a work in progress as the Blue Devils figure out their post-Boozer identity. Cam Boozer was their go-to offensive engine out of the post, and while they’re supremely talented in the frontcourt, there are a lot of mouths to feed.
If you limit your turnovers and make them beat you in the half court, the game begins to slant towards an under.
The other element that Michigan State brings to the table is its defensive rebounding chops. The Spartans led the nation in defensive rebounding percentage last season, and the combination of Jordan Scott and Anton Bonke should be enough to limit Duke to single-digit offensive rebounds.
There will be highlight reel moments; look out for Michigan State’s All-World dunker Coen Carr, but for the most part this will be a half-court grind and a lower-scoring affair.

[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
By [Mike Calabrese](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/mike-calabrese/page/1)
In the nightcap, I don’t believe this Kentucky team is ready for primetime just yet. James Madison just threw a scare into the Wildcats at Rupp Arena, drawing even with Kentucky seven minutes into the second half.
The reason for that scare was Kentucky’s inability to turn James Madison over or keep the Dukes off the offensive glass.
Kansas is likely to struggle against aggressive, turnover-minded defenses in Big 12 play. But Kentucky isn’t on that level, and from a rebounding standpoint, Kansas should be able to generate extra scoring opportunities against this softer Kentucky front line.
Tyran Stokes, Kansas’ mega-talented freshman, will either feast on Milan Momcilovic or drag a big man out to the perimeter. Stokes will impact this game on every level as a facilitator, rebounder and pure half-court scorer.
In the end, Kentucky won’t have a counter for his production, and the Jayhawks will put them away in the second half.

## Kansas vs. Kentucky
[![Kansas Jayhawks Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/kan.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-jayhawks)
Tuesday, Nov. 10
9:30 p.m. ET
ESPN
[![Kentucky Wildcats Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/kend.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kentucky-wildcats)
Milan Momcilovic Over 2.5 3PM (-120)
[![bet365 Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/859377_Bet365@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/bet365)
By [Charlie DiSturco](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/charlie-disturco)
This opening number for Milan Momcilovic is too low.
This is one of the best 3-point shooters in the entire country, as evidenced by his 48.6% rate a year ago. He was brought over to Lexington for a reason, and he’s already let it fly in Kentucky’s opening two games, taking 17 combined 3s.
I wouldn’t look too much into that 1-of-8 showing against James Madison. The important thing is that he continued to fire.
That mentality will remain true in the Champions Classic against a familiar Kansas squad.
In the Jayhawks’ opening two games, they have allowed 3-point attempts on 43.8% of attempts. They currently rank first in 2-point defense (24.7%), though have struggled to secure offensive rebounds (33% rate allowed).
Those second-chance opportunities are going to be a plus for Momcilovic, who is one of the best catch-and-shoot players in the sport. Malachi Moreno should provide a steady presence down low and work a two-man game with his counterpart Momcilovic.
As a whole last season, Momcilovic went over this 2.5 number in 23-of-37 games (62%). In games where he played 30+ minutes, he was over this number in 19-of-25 (76%).
While he isn’t a ball dominant player, he is great off-ball and should have plenty of opportunities to attack. The limitless range and consistency at which he can score is a huge plus here.
Barring foul trouble, I’d be shocked if Momcilovic does not finish with at least three 3s. I’m happy to grab this number in what should be a fun, high-scoring bout.

[![BetMGM Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/779359_BetMGM800x200@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/betmgm)
By [Charlie DiSturco](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/charlie-disturco)
I'm going to fade the public here and take the home underdog.
While Illinois has looked like a well-oiled machine to start the season, the offense is in the hands of a freshman (Quentin Coleman) facing his first true road test.
Texas Tech's defense is going to be aggressive, and the Illini have already shown some issues taking care of the basketball, turning it over 19 times against Penn.
Even without JT Toppin, I think Texas Tech has enough firepower to hang with one of the best teams in the country. It's also hard to believe these veteran shooters will continue to struggle from beyond the arc. At some point, the shots will start falling, making this a perfect buy-low opportunity.
Illinois has been excellent at getting downhill, attacking the rim and crashing the offensive glass. But that's exactly what Texas Tech's no-middle defense is designed to take away.
I expect the Red Raiders to create some chaos defensively, while their offense finally starts to find its rhythm. In their biggest home game of the non-conference schedule, give me Texas Tech.

The thing is, he got there despite not shooting particularly well. In those three meetings, he shot 38.7% from the field and 23% from beyond the arc. Similarly, he has gotten off to a slow start in Durham, as he has scored 18 points in total and shot 40% from the field in his first two games this season.
However, with two blowouts to start the season, the Blue Devils have not needed Blackwell for heavy minutes, and he did score 15 points in his last game.
Additionally, he has averaged 17.4 points per game on 44/36/84 shooting splits over the last two seasons.
If he hits that average on Tuesday, he will score 15 points.

## Duke vs Michigan State NCAAB Preview
**Duke Basketball**
Duke had the socials buzzing before the season even started after dismantling [Tennessee](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tennessee-volunteers), 103-64. Its third exhibition game came against Kansas, where the Blue Devils picked up a 90-84 win inside Allen Fieldhouse.
However, when I took them in their first regular season game against [Army](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/army-black-knights), they fell short of covering the massive spread. The Blue Devils followed that up by putting 113 points on the board against [Coastal Carolina](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/coastal-carolina-chanticleers), winning by 61.
Jon Scheyer's boys are loaded and, much like Michigan State, have their eyes set on Detroit.
**Michigan State Basketball**
Delivering the belt to their first two opponents of the college basketball season comes as no surprise, as the [Michigan State Spartans](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-state-spartans) look to make yet another deep run under head coach Tom Izzo. With [Jeremy Fears Jr.](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/jeremy-fears-257832-g) leading the way, the ceiling is the roof, per Michael Jordan.
Sparty has taken down both [Quinnipiac](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/quinnipiac-bobcats) and [Toledo](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/toledo-rockets) by 30-plus points in its first two games. Now, the Spartans face their first true test of the season against the [Duke Blue Devils](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/duke-blue-devils), the No. 1-rated team on Bart Torvik.
Izzo's boys should be more than ready for this one, as they faced UConn and [Marquette](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/marquette-golden-eagles) in exhibition games before the season started, losing both. The Spartans followed those defeats with back-to-back blowout wins, and you can bet Izzo made sure they corrected those issues.

## Duke vs. Michigan State Pick, Betting Analysis
I love both teams in this matchup. I do think 6.5 is a lot for the Blue Devils to cover on a neutral-site court, but they are certainly talented enough to do it.
However, with both teams boasting exceptional defenses, the under is the play for me. I locked in 148.5 on Monday afternoon.
The pace of this game should have absolutely zero urgency, as both teams rank in the 200s in tempo, according to Haslametrics.
Michigan State hasn't been particularly efficient at the free-throw line or from mid-range. Duke, meanwhile, has been excellent at limiting fouls, ranking 37th in free throw rate defense. That should help keep scoring at a premium.
One concern is that the Spartans haven't been keeping their hands to themselves, currently ranking 281st in free throw rate defense. If they expect to keep this game close, they'll have to clean things up defensively.
The game of the week should be a defensive brawl. I want no part of picking a side, although I do lean with ole Sparty. I'll stick to rooting for bricks.

## Florida vs Florida State NCAAB Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/fla.png)
### Florida Basketball
Do we expect the Gators to show any ounce of mercy in this ball game? They have nothing in front of them; after this matchup, Florida hosts [Merrimack](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/merrimack-warriors).
They don't play a legit opponent for a week, when they play [Notre Dame](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/notre-dame-fighting-irish) in Las Vegas next Tuesday.
Entering Tuesday, the Gators have dominated everyone in their way, taking down [Miami](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/miami-fl-hurricanes) in their first game 77-61 before beating Jacksonville, 88-57.
With all the returning talent and guys familiar with head coach Todd Golden, this team should have no issues with taking care of the Seminoles on the road.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/fsu.png)
### Florida State Basketball
Florida State has no returning starters — just two players returned from a year ago — and those two have had minimal playing time and experience.
Despite the lack of chemistry, the Seminoles played an impressive preseason schedule, beating LSU and Auburn back in October.
Florida State then kicked off the season by blowing out Florida A&M and Troy. Now the Noles draw one of the most complete teams in the country.
Luckily, they get to play this massive game in front of their home crowd.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/fla.png)
![Header Second Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/fsu.png)
## Florida vs. Florida State Pick, Betting Analysis
In this spot, backing Florida to cover the 9.5 is the only option. The Gators have nothing to look ahead to for another week, so their main focus should be destroying their in-state rival.
On top of that, early in the season, having 11 guys on the roster who already have chemistry within Golden's system and one another is far more lethal than a team returning two players, one with just 13 career minutes and the other recovering from an ACL tear.
If the number worries you, take Florida's team total over instead. Both teams rank inside the top 50 in pace and love to zip up and down the floor.

## Illinois vs Texas Tech College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ill.png)
### Illinois Basketball
Falling just short of its National Championship aspirations, Illinois once again enters the season as one of the best teams in the country.
While Keaton Wagler has departed for the NBA, Illinois reloaded with plenty of talented freshmen. Chief among them is No. 24 overall recruit Quentin Coleman, who steps in as Wagler's replacement. Through two games, Coleman is averaging 16 points, 6.5 rebounds and 4.0 assists in just 21.5 minutes per contest.
Granted, those numbers have come against two lower-level opponents that Illinois has dominated. Still, it's an encouraging sign that the Fighting Illini haven't missed a beat early in the season, especially with David Mirkovic still working his way back from injury.
The 6-foot-9 sophomore underwent offseason foot surgery, and a recent emergency appendectomy further delayed his return. He's now expected back toward the end of November.
In the meantime, Illinois will lean on Andrej Stojakovic and the Ivisic brothers to carry much of the load.
In his second season with the Illini, Stojakovic has the potential to emerge as the No. 1 option in this offense. He broke out during the NCAA Tournament and has carried that momentum into year two.
Stojakovic has accounted for 34.2% of the team's shots while on the floor and has scored at least 20 points in each game despite averaging just 21 minutes.
The Ivisic brothers, meanwhile, provide plenty of rim protection thanks to their length. Illinois ranks 10th nationally in average height, with much of that advantage coming from its two 7-footers.
It'll be interesting to see how Illinois performs against legitimate competition, particularly without Mirkovic. The Illini enter Tuesday's matchup with the No. 1 offense in the country in adjusted efficiency and an impressive 51.4% offensive rebounding rate.
Of course, [Fairleigh Dickinson](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/fairleigh-dickinson-knights) and [Penn](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/pennsylvania-quakers) aren't exactly equipped to handle Illinois' size and physicality.
Stefan Vaaks has also been an early bright spot. The [Providence](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/providence-friars) transfer is 5-for-11 from beyond the arc and could become a major X-factor in these top-25 matchups.
Defensively, Illinois hasn't generated many turnovers this season, ranking 314th nationally. Instead, the Illini have focused on staying disciplined and avoiding fouls, forcing opponents to settle for perimeter and mid-range looks.
That approach has helped produce back-to-back wins by 40-plus points.

### Texas Tech Basketball
Texas Tech boasts one of the most experienced rosters in college basketball, with National Championship aspirations firmly at the forefront for Grant McCasland and his staff.
Similar to Illinois, this will be the Red Raiders' first real test of the season. Texas Tech opened with a pair of convincing wins against lesser opponents, holding both [Jackson State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/jackson-state-tigers) and Bethune-Cookman under 60 points.
Offensively, this is a physical group that has prioritized its rim-and-3 rate. Texas Tech has taken 53.6% of its shots from beyond the arc, though the Red Raiders have struggled to the tune of a 26.7% clip.
That number should eventually climb. Donovan Atwell, who ranked 10th nationally in 3-point shooting last season at 45.8%, is connecting on just 18.8% of his attempts.
Even veteran transfers Dra Gibbs-Lawhorn ([UNLV](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/unlv-rebels)) and Cruz Davis ([Hofstra](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/hofstra-pride)) are shooting below 30%, down more than 10 percentage points from last season.
Darrion Williams' return to Lubbock is another major addition for McCasland and his staff. After leaving Texas Tech for [NC State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/north-carolina-state-wolfpack) last season, Williams saw his 3-point percentage climb back above 40%. He was the focal point of the offense, accounting for 28.6% of the team's shots during conference play.
On the opposite end of the floor, there are plenty of positives to look toward. Texas Tech ranks second nationally in turnover rate at 30% and has been excellent on the defensive glass — a major factor against a physical team like Illinois.
With JT Toppin still rehabbing from the ACL injury he suffered in February, McCasland has turned to Josiah Moseley and 6-foot-10 freshman Bassala Bagayoko. The latter will be particularly important in providing rim protection Tuesday night, as Illinois' size can overwhelm opponents.
For all its experience and physicality, this isn't an especially tall team. Bagayoko is the only rotational player taller than 6-foot-8, and he ranks seventh nationally in block rate at 21.8%. Staying out of foul trouble will be critical.
It should be important to note that Jamichael Stillwell was granted a temporary restraining order and is expected to play for Texas Tech on Tuesday. He's a 6-foot-8 forward who averaged 8.2 rebounds at [UCF](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ucf-knights) last season.
McCasland's no-middle defensive scheme helps compensate for the lack of size. Texas Tech is a versatile, athletic group that works to deny penetration through the middle of the floor. With constant switches and aggressive ball pressure, opponents are often forced to settle for perimeter shots.
Early in the season, opponents have taken 54.3% of their shots from beyond the arc. Texas Tech ranks 10th nationally in average 2-point attempt distance allowed (8.6 feet). That approach will be especially important against an Illinois team that does much of its damage around the rim.

## Illinois vs. Texas Tech Pick, Betting Analysis
I'm going to fade the public here and take the home underdog.
While Illinois has looked like a well-oiled machine to start the season, the offense is in the hands of a freshman facing his first true road test. Texas Tech's defense is going to be aggressive, and the Illini have already shown some issues taking care of the basketball, turning it over 19 times against Penn.
Even without Toppin, I think Texas Tech has enough firepower to hang with one of the best teams in the country. It's also hard to believe these veteran shooters will continue to struggle from beyond the arc. At some point, the shots will start falling, making this a perfect buy-low opportunity.
Illinois has been excellent at getting downhill, attacking the rim and crashing the offensive glass. But that's exactly what Texas Tech's no-middle defense is designed to take away.
I expect the Red Raiders to create some chaos defensively, while their offense finally starts to find its rhythm. In their biggest home game of the non-conference schedule, give me Texas Tech.
