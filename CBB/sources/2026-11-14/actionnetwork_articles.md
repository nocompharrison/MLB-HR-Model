## LSU vs. Gonzaga

[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Colby Marchio](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/cmaricho)
Technically, this is a neutral-site game, but [Gonzaga](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/gonzaga-bulldogs) is just a six-minute drive from Spokane Arena. This is a home game, especially when the opposing team is coming from the other side of the country.
With this being [LSU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/lsu-tigers)'s first true road test against competent competition, I fully expect the Tigers to struggle. The Bulldogs are still waking up in their own beds, aren't changing their routine at all and will have their die-hard fans backing them just a few miles off campus.
Gonzaga should also be able to exploit a few issues LSU has shown against much weaker competition, namely its tendency to foul at a high rate and struggles shooting from beyond the arc.
Those are two areas where Mark Few's boys excel, getting to the free-throw line and defending the perimeter.
I'm not a trends guy, but I've mentioned Few's ability to get his squad ready for early-season marquee games. Excluding this season, Few is 22-16 ATS in November since the 2020-21 season, including an astounding 12-4 mark over the last two years.
**Pick:[ Gonzaga -6.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3199270993&context=action-articles)**

## New Mexico vs. Santa Clara
[![New Mexico Lobos Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/nm.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/new-mexico-lobos)
Saturday, Nov. 14
10 p.m. ET
ESPN+
[![Santa Clara Broncos Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/961996_santaclara.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/santa-clara-broncos)
Under 164.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
By [Carmine Carcieri](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/carmine-carcieri)
This game may not get national attention — especially on a college football Saturday — but the battle between [New Mexico](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/new-mexico-lobos) and [Santa Clara](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/santa-clara-broncos) is one of the most underrated early-season college basketball matchups on the board.
The Lobos are projected to be one of the best teams in the Mountain West this season, and they've backed that up through three games.
Even though the competition hasn't exactly been stiff, they're averaging 96 points per game and Jake Hall — the Mountain West Preseason Player of the Year — is putting up 30.7 points on his own.
On the flip side, Santa Clara reached its first NCAA Tournament since 1996 last season, and with Gonzaga off to the Pac-12 and [Saint Mary's](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/saint-marys-gaels) breaking in a new head coach, Herb Sendek's squad is considered the frontrunner in the WCC.
The Broncos are off to a 4-0 start to this season, including a road win at [North Texas](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/north-texas-mean-green).
With this game essentially being a coin flip, I'm staying away from the spread. Instead, I'm going to focus my attention on the total and back the under.
You might be saying: Well, how can you take an under in a New Mexico game, especially with Santa Clara's early-season pace?
Even though the Broncos are running at one of the fastest tempos in the nation (according to Bart Torvik), this type of, potentially, gritty game will incentivize a slower pace than they've shown so far this season.
Also, our "Rebound Unders" system — which says strong rebounding teams in non-conference settings can quietly suppress scoring more than the market anticipates — backs my thoughts on the play.
Specifically, the Broncos are in the top 100 in defensive rebounding, meaning they limit second-chance opportunities and force scoring in the half-court.
I expect a much different game than these two have played to start their seasons.
**Pick:[ Under 164.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/draftkings/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3199418494&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

## LSU vs Gonzaga College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/lsu.png)
### LSU Basketball
The [LSU Tigers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/lsu-tigers) enter Saturday with a perfect 3-0 straight up and ATS record while enduring the 26th-easiest schedule in the country, per Haslametrics. All three of their early-season non-conference opponents rank among the bottom 125 teams nationally.
The return of Will Wade will be an interesting one. With several players already ineligible, will the roster Wade has available be enough to keep this team competitive?
So far this season, the Tigers are led in scoring by 7-foot freshman [Michael Ruzic](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/michael-ruzic-351838-c). [Donovan Dent](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/donovan-dent-220374-g) has been all over the floor, averaging 13.7 points and 8.3 assists per game. Dent leads the team in assists, blocks and steals per game.
As LSU heads to Spokane for its first true test of the season, will these two be able to rise to the occasion?
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/gonz.png)
### Gonzaga Basketball
With a week to prepare for their matchup against LSU, the [Gonzaga Bulldogs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/gonzaga-bulldogs) enter the weekend refreshed after a 41-point win over [New Mexico State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/new-mexico-state-aggies).
Before that, they earned a six-point win over [Purdue](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/purdue-boilermakers) on opening night.
It's tough to gauge just how good that win over the Boilermakers really is, considering Purdue followed it up with a double-overtime home loss to [Valparaiso](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/valparaiso-crusaders).
However, much like the reason I liked Gonzaga on opening night, Mark Few always does a tremendous job of having his boys ready for early-season marquee games.
Early on, [Davis Fogle](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/davis-fogle-324904-g), sadly no relation to McLovin, has been a huge contributor, scoring 22 points in back-to-back games. On top of that, he's averaging 4.5 rebounds and dishing out four assists per game.
Senior guard [Xaivian Lee](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/xaivian-lee-223323-g) has also been an assist machine, dishing out 10 assists against New Mexico State while scoring six points in the massive win.
If these guys are gelling, this team should be just fine heading into LSU with a week of rest.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/lsu.png)
![Header Second Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/gonz.png)
## LSU vs Gonzaga Pick, Betting Analysis
Technically, this is a neutral-site game, but Gonzaga is just a six-minute drive from Spokane Arena. This is a home game, especially when the opposing team is coming from the other side of the country.
With this being LSU's first true road test against competent competition, I fully expect the Tigers to struggle. The Bulldogs are still waking up in their own beds, aren't changing their routine at all and will have their die-hard fans backing them just a few miles off campus.
Gonzaga should also be able to exploit a few issues LSU has shown against much weaker competition, namely its tendency to foul at a high rate and struggles shooting from beyond the arc.
Those are two areas where Few's boys excel, getting to the free-throw line and defending the perimeter.
I'm not a trends guy, but I've mentioned Few's ability to get his squad ready for early-season marquee games. Excluding this season, Few is 22-16 ATS in November since the 2020-21 season, including an astounding 12-4 mark over the last two years.
**Pick:[ Gonzaga -6.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3199270993&context=action-articles)**

## Colorado vs Providence College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/col.png)
### Colorado Basketball
It’s been a downward trajectory for [Colorado](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/colorado-buffaloes) over the past two seasons. The Buffaloes won 14 and 17 games and are picked near the bottom of the Big 12 this season.
The key for Colorado so far? Shooting. The Buffaloes are taking more 3s, attempting them on 45% of their field goals and hitting 42% of them (21st).
I’m not sure I buy into the 3-point percentage, though. Only two Buffs are strong shooters: [Barrington Hargress](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/barrington-hargress-224044-g) and [Ian Inman](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/ian-inman-320164-g/f). Hargress leads the team with 20 points per game but six of his 3s this season came in one game; the same applies to Inman, who drilled eight 3s in Colorado’s last game.
Defense has been the calling card for Colorado in the early going, ranking 69th in efficiency on that end of the floor. The Buffs are holding opponents to just 46% shooting on 2s, while collecting 75.2% of missed shots.
I’ll be monitoring how much of those are matchup-related, as none of Colorado’s three opponents are better than 70th in offensive efficiency, per KenPom.
Aussie point guard Alex Dickeson is Colorado’s X-factor. Dickeson leads Colorado in rebounding (7.0), while pitching in 3.4 assists per game. His ability to play on the ball allows Hargress to get some looks off the ball.
One concern to me is Colorado’s interior. Starting at the five is [Noah Feddersen](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/noah-feddersen-219677-f), a 6-foot-10 big man who averages 7.0 points and 5.3 rebounds per game. He’s not much of a floor stretcher and can struggle to defend more physical opponents.
It hasn’t stung Colorado yet, but it could.

### Providence Basketball
[Providence](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/providence-friars) is 2-1 so far this season and lost to [Nebraska](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/nebraska-cornhuskers) in its only high-major matchup this season. That was an ugly game for the Friars, as they went 7-for-38 from downtown. Shooting below 20% from 3 against a KenPom top-15 team isn’t exactly a winning formula.
Shooting will determine how good Providence is in Bryan Hodgson’s first season. Each of his three prior teams (Arkansas State and USF) attempted 3s at least 42% of the time. So, the Friars will let them fly, but can they make them? I think they will eventually.
The Friars big three should consist of [Miles Byrd](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/miles-byrd-220379-g), [Devin Vanterpool](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/devin-vanterpool-257490-g) and [Malik Mack](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/malik-mack-256486-g). Byrd is streaky; he’s a capable shooter but made just 30% from 3 the last two seasons. Meanwhile, Mack hit 35% at Georgetown two seasons ago and Vanterpool hit 35% at FAU last season.
Those three joining a more offensive-friendly system should lead to them shooting better. Just look at their prior stops — San Diego State ran an archaic offense when Byrd was there, Mack was on a bad Georgetown team and Vanterpool struggled with shot selection.
I’ll buy stock in the Friars shooting better than 26% from 3.
The Friars' defense looks strong thus far — they rank 29th in defensive efficiency and rank second in the country with a 29.6% turnover rate. Plus, they’re 30th in defensive rebound rate.
On the downside, teams are shooting a strong 54% on 2s against them.
Bigger teams can exploit Providence’s lack of rim deterrence, but I’m not sure Colorado can.

## Colorado vs Providence Pick, Betting Analysis
I don’t view this Colorado team highly. It’s a long road trip to the Northeast, and I think both teams are due for shooting regression — positive on the Friars side, and negative on Colorado’s side.
Moreover, Colorado is turning the ball over 18% of the time, which should allow the Friars to pressure and get running in transition.
If this is a full-court game, Colorado won’t stand a chance.
Lay the points with the home team.

## Cincinnati vs Dayton College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/cind.png)
### Cincinnati Basketball
Jerrod Calhoun earned the Cincinnati job after two pretty remarkable years at [Utah State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/utah-state-aggies). He led the Aggies to back-to-back tournament appearances, and his roster makeup for his first season at [Cincinnati](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/cincinnati-bearcats) is similar.
This team returned virtually no production from a season ago.
The Bearcats opened the season with two convincing wins against the Patriot League’s American and the Horizon’s [Green Bay](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/green-bay-phoenix). Then they lost on a buzzer-beating 3-point shot against [Minnesota](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/minnesota-golden-gophers).
Through three games, it’s clear that their focus will be defense, as they are currently ranked top-15 in defensive efficiency.
Their best portal addition was [Myles Colvin](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/myles-colvin-256213-g), but the transfer from [Wake Forest](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wake-forest-demon-deacons) is averaging just 12 points per game.
The Bearcats are projected to finish outside the top eight in the Big 12, but Calhoun has taught us not to undervalue his team.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/day.png)
### Dayton Basketball
[Dayton](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/dayton-flyers) opened the preseason ranked 64th in KenPom, but after two straight losses to [Northern Illinois](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northern-illinois-huskies) and [Siena](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/siena-saints), both at home, the Flyers have fallen to 114th.
Unlike Cincinnati, it’s their offense that's their identity. They scored 89 points in their lone win, followed by 76 and 89, respectively, in their losses.
Surprisingly, the team's leader is the freshman Aidan Derkac, who's averaging 22 points and 10.3 rebounds per game.
This Dayton team has four players averaging double digits in scoring, but the Flyers' lack of defensive efficiency is worrisome. They allow teams to beat them with speed offensively.
Dayton needs every win it can get, especially with its non-conference slate only getting tougher as November wears on.

## Cincinnati vs Dayton Pick, Betting Analysis
Here’s my biggest issue with taking points with Dayton, even on its home court: Cincinnati's defense. Dayton likes to play at a fast tempo offensively, ranking near the top of the sport in possession length.
Cincinnati also plays comparably offensively. But the big difference here is that the Bearcats apply so much pressure that they make every shot tougher.
Cincinnati is near the top in terms of defensive possession length, meaning poor shots tend to stack up for the opponent. And Dayton thrives on open looks, something the Flyers won't get much against the Bearcats.
I hate laying points, especially on the road, but I think Cincinnati wins by a margin, although I wouldn’t necessarily lay a lot more than the current line.
