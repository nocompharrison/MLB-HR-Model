## Maryland Eastern Shore vs. Rhode Island
[![Maryland Eastern Shore Hawks Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/umes.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/maryland-eastern-shore-hawks)

[![Rhode Island Rams Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ril.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/rhode-island-rams)
Under 143.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
_By[Alex Kolodziej](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/alex-kolodziej)_
The top-graded play on the board is the under in Maryland-Eastern Shore vs. [Rhode Island](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/rhode-island-rams). PRO projects this one for just 137.15 total points, more than six points below the posted 143.5. That gap earns it an A+ edge rating.
The projections come from [Tanner McGrath](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/tanner-mcgrath)’s data-driven models, which have beaten the market early this season. His over/under picks opened 76-47 (61.8%) through the first 10 days.
McGrath’s model starts with adjusted efficiency and pace metrics, similar to KenPom or BartTorvik, and then adds two things. It folds in shot-quality metrics to account for likely shooting regression. It also scales the underlying numbers to sit closer to the betting market, which makes the projections more accurate in that context and makes mispriced lines easier to spot.
Here, the model sees a slower, lower-scoring game than the market is pricing. That’s enough to back the under at 143.5.

## Oklahoma State vs. Texas A&M
[![Oklahoma State Cowboys Logo](https://images.actionnetwork.com/blog/2022/09/oklahoma-state-cowboys-updated-2022-logo-100w-100h.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/oklahoma-state-cowboys)
Thursday, Nov. 12
8 p.m ET
SECN+
[![Texas A&M Aggies Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/txam.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-am-aggies)
Over 178.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
_By:[Sean Paul](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/sean-paul-smock-ii)_
The total is about as high as you'll see in college basketball, sitting at 177.5. It's fair since both teams are top-15 in adjusted tempo.
I'm rolling with the over here. The two teams are plenty comfortable playing in the 90-point range, and if one of them cracks 100, it wouldn't shock me.
KenPom has Texas A&M and [Oklahoma State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/oklahoma-state-cowboys) sitting in the top 40 in defensive efficiency, and I'm not sure I buy that. The Aggies should have a solid defense, but they lack a shot-stopper if the Oklahoma State guards beat the press.
I also don't see the vision of Oklahoma State being even a top-50 defense. Some of the pieces, like Grbovic and Burks, aren't the caliber of defenders that should help the Cowboys' defense be top-40 level.
Give me the over. I expect a lot of 3s and possessions.
**Pick:[Over 178.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/draftkings/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3194673422&context=action-articles) (Play to 179.5)**

### Oklahoma State Basketball
The [Oklahoma State Cowboys](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/oklahoma-state-cowboys) are 2-0 to start the season with a 31-point win over [Incarnate Word](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/incarnate-word-cardinals) and a 50-point win against a strong [Queens](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/queens-nc-royals) team.
Cowboys head coach Steve Lutz runs an exciting brand of basketball — they play fast (12th in adjusted tempo). Plus, the Cowboys are shooting more 3s, up from 35% of their shots last season to 40% this campaign.
They don't have a bona fide "star," but Oklahoma State is deep. The Cowboys can play 12 reliable players, and the current leading scorer is 6-foot-11 forward [Andrija Grbovic](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/andrija-grbovic-324184-f), at 13.5 points per game.
Lutz opted to play a jumbo shooter lineup, pairing Grbovic with wing [Jordan Burks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/jordan-burks-255899-g). Both can shoot close to 40% from 3, which will help spread the floor for big man [Julius Halaifonua](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/julius-halaifonua-294261-c).
In Lutz's first season, Oklahoma State was 148th in offensive efficiency and 72nd in defensive efficiency. Last season, it jumped to 48th in offensive efficiency and fell to 158th in defensive efficiency.
Can Lutz even things out in 2026-27? I don't think this team has more offensive talent, but it has more willing passers and more roster balance.
Two of the key cogs in that balance are [Kashie Natt](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/kashie-natt-228288-g/f) and [Jacob Walker](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/jacob-walker-321836-g), two [Sam Houston](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/sam-houston-state-bearkats) imports. Natt is one of the most athletic players in the country and posted 8.2 rebounds per game from the guard spot last season. Walker has struggled to find his scoring touch, but he's contributing to winning with his 8.5 assists per game.

### Texas A&M Basketball
Bucky McMillan was a home-run hire for [Texas A&M](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-am-aggies), and it paid off immediately. The Aggies made the NCAA Tournament and won a game in the event in his first season, and the 2026-27 roster blows last season's out of the water from a talent standpoint.
McMillan's fun style is coined "Bucky Ball." That style includes full-court pressure, leading to the Aggies playing at one of the fastest tempos and taking 3s on over 45% of their shots last season.
It takes a lot of buy-in to play Bucky Ball. Pressuring for a whole game isn't for everyone, but I thought he brought in the right players to fit his system.
The biggest add was [P.J. Haggerty](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/pj-haggerty-219106-g), a former All-American during his time at [Memphis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/memphis-tigers). He averaged 21+ points per game over the last three seasons at three different schools ([Tulsa](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tulsa-golden-hurricane), Memphis, [Kansas State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-state-wildcats)). He'll likely make it a fourth playing in such an offense-friendly system.
Haggerty was awesome at Memphis, which played a similar style to Texas A&M.

The total is about as high as you'll see in college basketball, sitting at 177.5. It's fair since both teams are top-15 in adjusted tempo.

KenPom has Texas A&M and Oklahoma State sitting in the top 40 in defensive efficiency, and I'm not sure I buy that. The Aggies should have a solid defense, but they lack a shot-stopper if the Oklahoma State guards beat the press.
