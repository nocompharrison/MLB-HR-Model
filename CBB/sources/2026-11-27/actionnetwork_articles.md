## Cincinnati vs. Boston College
[![Cincinnati Bearcats Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/cind.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/cincinnati-bearcats)
Friday, Nov. 27
4:30 p.m. ET
ACC Network
[![Boston College Eagles Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/bc.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/boston-college-eagles)
Boston College +9.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
The undefeated Eagles, guided by new head coach Luke Murray, match up well against [Cincinnati](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/cincinnati-bearcats) by combining elite work on the glass with a methodical pace.
[Boston College](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/boston-college-eagles) ranks fourth in offensive rebounding percentage and 356th in tempo nationally.
While Cincinnati relies on high-turnover defense, the Eagles' veteran guards are well-positioned to protect the ball, dictate pace and limit possessions.
On defense, Boston College's primary weakness is its tendency to commit fouls. However, this issue becomes far less critical in this matchup, given that Cincinnati struggles to get to the free-throw line (ranking 329th in free-throw rate).
Also, Cincinnati generates over 50% of its scoring inside the arc, which aligns with Boston College's main strength defensively. The Eagles feature a top-20 2-point field goal defense, holding opponents to just 43% shooting from that range.
Expect Boston College to stay competitive.

## Marquette vs. Texas A&M
[![Marquette Golden Eagles Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/marq.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/marquette-golden-eagles)
Friday, Nov. 27
5 p.m. ET
SEC Network
[![Texas A&M Aggies Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/txam.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-am-aggies)
Texas A&M -2.5
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
[Marquette](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/marquette-golden-eagles) has underwhelmed significantly early this season. After a disappointing 12-20 campaign in 2025-26, the Golden Eagles held high hopes for a turnaround behind sophomore Nigel James Jr.
However, James has been a shadow of his former self, seemingly losing both his confidence and a degree of trust from his coaching staff.
In turn, Marquette has struggled, dropping every matchup against teams ranked inside the KenPom top 170.
While the Golden Eagles excel at forcing turnovers and pushing the tempo, that style plays right into the hands of Texas A&M.
Head coach Bucky McMillan’s signature fast-paced "Bucky Ball" thrives in high-tempo environments, making the Aggies comfortable in a track meet.
Looking to bounce back from a loss to [Xavier](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/xavier-musketeers), Texas A&M holds a clear edge, as it's difficult to see Marquette matching the Aggies' scoring efficiency or pace.
Also, Marquette relies heavily on interior scoring, which poses a severe matchup issue against an Aggies defense that ranks ninth nationally in 2-point percentage allowed.
Expect Texas A&M to secure the win and cover.
**Pick:[ Texas A&M -2.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/draftkings/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3256711395&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

## West Virginia vs. North Carolina
[![West Virginia Mountaineers Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/wvu.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/west-virginia-mountaineers)
Friday, Nov. 27
7 p.m. ET
ESPN
[![North Carolina Tar Heels Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/nc.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/north-carolina-tar-heels)
West Virginia -1.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
[West Virginia](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/west-virginia-mountaineers) enters this matchup with a 4-2 record, having made a strong impression early in the season following a solid performance in the Players Era 8.
Led by standout freshman point guard Miles Sadler, the Mountaineers earned an impressive victory over [Auburn](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/auburn-tigers) and proved competitive against both [Kansas](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-jayhawks) and [Florida](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-gators).
On the other side, UNC has seen mixed results in the early going under new head coach Michael Malone. The Tar Heels have played just one opponent ranked in the KenPom top 50, resulting in a home loss to [Georgia](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/georgia-bulldogs).
Rebounding has been a major vulnerability for UNC, as the Heels rank outside the top 250 in both offensive and defensive glass work.
Meanwhile, West Virginia ranks in the top 100 in both categories.
Given their clear advantage on the boards, the Mountaineers look like the stronger side to take in this game.
**Pick:[ West Virginia -1.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3256651389&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

## Wichita State vs. Kentucky
[![Wichita State Shockers Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/wich.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wichita-state-shockers)
Friday, Nov. 27
9 p.m ET
ESPN2
[![Kentucky Wildcats Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/kend.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kentucky-wildcats)
Wichita State +3.5
[![DraftKings Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
This [Kentucky](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kentucky-wildcats) team has been uninspiring all season. Since the start of the new campaign, the Wildcats have dropped 40 spots on KenPom, all the way down to 64th nationally.
Maybe even more shockingly, they are down to 130th nationally in defensive efficiency.
The analytics are bad with this Kentucky team, and so is the eye test.
This team is disconnected, and the seat of head coach Mark Pope seems to get hotter and hotter by the day. Kentucky has yet to beat a top 100 team on KenPom this season (0-2 with both losses by double digits), while [Wichita State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wichita-state-shockers) is 1-1 with a blowout victory over [New Mexico](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/new-mexico-lobos) and a close loss to [USC](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/usc-trojans).
While Kentucky has fallen in rankings, Wichita State is up 12 spots on KenPom.
This is a game between two teams trending in opposite directions.
Nothing I have seen from Kentucky this season convinces me that it's a team that is going to figure it out or have the ability to beat good opponents.
I will take the Shockers with the points.
**Pick:[ Wichita State +3.5](https://playbookbot.com/books/draftkings/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3256759362&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

## West Virginia vs UNC Preview
**West Virginia Basketball**
If you are new to my writing, hello, and thank you for being here — however, if you've been here before, you know I love me some Ross Hodge. His teams are an absolute joy to watch. They play tough defense and consistently make smart plays.
Now in his second year at [West Virginia](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/west-virginia-mountaineers), Hodge has finally acquired some shooters, allowing the team to spread the floor.
Bart Torvik has the Mountaineers ranked 44th in adjusted offensive efficiency, while ranking them in the top 35 in both offensive and defensive turnover rate.
After the gauntlet this team went through last week, West Virginia has had time to clean things up. We should expect a very polished Mountaineers team in Charlotte.
**UNC Basketball**
Again, for those who keep up with my writing, you know I'm a strength-of-schedule die-hard.
Through the first few weeks of the season, the Tar Heels haven't faced many tests.
Sure they beat [Wyoming](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wyoming-cowboys) before falling to [Georgia](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/georgia-bulldogs) in their next game; however, [North Carolina](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/north-carolina-tar-heels) has yet to leave the comfort of its own bed.

Yes. However, against top-100 teams, North Carolina has struggled to shoot from outside the paint, get after rebounds and defend the perimeter.
According to Bart Torvik, the Tar Heels rank 269th in offensive effective field goal percentage (eFG%) against top-100 teams. That's a two-game sample size, but it's worth noting, especially considering North Carolina ranks 136th in the category for the season.

## Alabama vs Miami Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/bama.png)
### Alabama Basketball
Alabama is and always will be "3-point or bust" under Nate Oats. He wants an analytically-minded shot diet, featuring 3s and layups. The Tide attempt 3s on 53% of their field goals, but Oats built this roster to give his team a chance on an inevitable poor shooting night.
It worked against [Baylor](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/baylor-bears). Alabama shot 23% from 3 and lost by five with a chance to tie at the buzzer. Rim protection is the biggest shift Alabama has made this season, holding teams to 40% on 2-pointers (7th).
Alabama played two high-major teams — Seton Hall and Baylor – and held both below 45% shooting from inside the arc.
Oats brought in some added size with Brandon Garrison and Drew Fielder to surround Amari Allen and London Jemison. All four of them are 6-foot-6 or taller, leaving the speedy sharpshooter Aden Holloway as the lone sub-6-foot-6 player in the Crimson Tide's lineup.
Shooting-wise, the Crimson Tide have been solid, but Oats would like to be better than 35%. Holloway, Jemison and Allen are each hitting 50%. On the flip side, Fielder, Cole Cloer and Qayden Samuels are shooting below 30% on more than 20 attempts.
The biggest knock issue for Alabama thus far is defending the glass — the Tide give up offensive boards at a 38% rate. Baylor grabbed 18 of them against Alabama.

### Miami Basketball
Miami finally found its long-range stroke against [Michigan](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-wolverines). Entering that game, the Hurricanes were shooting 29% from 3, but Dante Allen and Nick Dorn each drilled a 3 against the Wolverines.
Allen drew the start after freshman Caleb Gaskins was injured against [TCU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tcu-horned-frogs). Gaskins’ status for this one is to be determined.
I'm glad to see Miami can make 3s, but the pressing issue is four of the five starters can't shoot. Shelton Henderson exploited Michigan for 26 points and made one 3 (that 3-pointer was only his second make this season).
Point guard Acaden Lewis is just 3-for-28 from deep, and Somto Cyril is a non-shooter.
With so many shooting concerns, it's imperative that Miami is elite on the defensive end. So far, the Hurricanes have been one of the better defensive teams in college basketball, ranking 12th in defensive efficiency while holding opponents to just 39% shooting on 2-pointers (fourth nationally).
Moreover, Miami is dominant on the glass. The Canes can maximize their offense using their 39% offensive rebound rate, and they also limit teams to a 24% offensive rebound rate. Cyril is the Hurricanes' leader with a 14% offensive rebound rate.

## Louisville vs Tennessee College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/lou.png)
### Louisville Basketball
Louisville got a taste of revenge in the Rick Pitino reunion. In the last two games, the Cardinals held [Texas Tech](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-tech-red-raiders) to 6-for-27 from 3, and St. John’s couldn't buy a triple for most of the game.
Some of that is luck-related, as most 3-point defensive numbers are, but it helped get Louisville here.
Seth Trimble and Karter Knox have been saviors for Louisville. Trimble scored 20+ points in the first two games of the Players Era 16, and Knox scored 15+ in both. I didn't fashion those two as Louisville's top two options, but they've played that way in Vegas.
The Cardinals have mauled teams on the glass so far, securing offensive boards 44% of the time while holding opponents to a 24% offensive rebound rate. That makes up for Louisville shooting just 29% from deep.
If the shooting comes around, Louisville becomes a legitimate force.
My biggest Louisville question is: how good are the Cardinals defensively? They rank 36th in defensive efficiency, but they’re also outside the top 100 in 2-point and 3-point field goal defense.
A team will eventually shoot well from 3 against them, and will their interior defense be good enough to withstand a good shooting night? We'll see.

### Tennessee Basketball
Remember when Tennessee added a bunch of offensive-first portal players, and then the world (including me) panicked after a few bad exhibitions?
Well, the constant is Rick Barnes, and he'll lead one of the best defenses in the country.
The Vols own one of the best resumes in college basketball, too, beating [Michigan State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-state-spartans), [Iowa State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/iowa-hawkeyes), and [Maryland](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/maryland-terrapins). In each of those games, Tennessee held its opponent below 0.95 points per possession.
Those efforts helped give Tennessee the third-most efficient defense nationally, per KenPom.
Scoring inside on Tennessee is an arduous task. The Vols hold opponents to 32% shooting on 2s, which leads the country.
Barnes went to a double-big lineup with Miles Rubin and DeWayne Brown II, and it's totally changed the team. Rubin is a lethal shot-stopper, swatting 2.3 shots per game. Meanwhile, Brown is 6-foot-8, 248 pounds, and is built of granite. He's a cinderblock wall that's tough to move off his spot.
The other end of the floor remains a work in progress for the Vols, as they rank 55th in offensive efficiency. Two areas have held Tennessee back: a bloated 20% turnover rate and shooting 32% from 3.
We'll see if the turnovers subside, but the shooting should. Dai Dai Ames has found his groove in Vegas, scoring 19 and 24 points in the two games.
The key is the trio of Juke Harris, Terrence Hill Jr., and Tyler Lundblade shooting to their career averages. Harris is at just 22%, Hill is at 30% and Lundblade is at 27%, all well below what they shot last season.
I still think Barnes has lineup construction things to iron out, but I feel better about Tennessee today than I did two weeks ago.

## Louisville vs. Tennessee Pick, Betting Analysis
Am I a little worried about Tennessee giving up offensive boards at a 32% rate against one of the best offensive rebounding teams in the country?
A little, but not enough to change my pick.
Tennessee will eventually shoot it better with the weapons it has, and Louisville can't hold teams to such poor shooting outputs forever.
The Volunteers' defense is just terrific, and Louisville has to shoot it very well from 3, which the Cardinals haven't done consistently this season.
