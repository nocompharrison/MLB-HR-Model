## Creighton vs. Michigan
[![Creighton Bluejays Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/crei.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/creighton-bluejays)

ESPN
[![Michigan Wolverines Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/mich.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-wolverines)
Michigan -4.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
By [Sean Paul](https://www.actionnetwork.com/article/author/sean-paul-smock-ii)
The Mike Boynton era in Ann Arbor is off to an inauspicious start, as the Wolverines are 3-2 and are coming off a difficult loss to [Villanova](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/villanova-wildcats) in Philly. The other loss was to [UConn](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/connecticut-huskies), which is clearly better than [Michigan](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-wolverines), so there's no shame in that.
Usually, if you grab 20 offensive boards, you aren't losing. Michigan gave up 18 to Villanova and went a dreadful 7-for-31 (26%) from deep.
That's the story for Michigan thus far: poor shooting. The Wolverines are 28% from deep, with over 45% of their shots coming from 3. Trey McKenney is Michigan's top shooter, connecting on 37% of his 8.6 attempts per game. Freshman forward Quinn Costello is also close to 40% from downtown.
Meanwhile, [Creighton](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/creighton-bluejays) is also bringing on a new coach — Alan Huss — who mirrors his offensive style after his mentor, Greg McDermott. The Jays want to push the tempo — not too fast, but an opportunistically quick pace — and shoot 3s close to half the time.
The Jays are attempting 3s on 50.2% of their shots under Huss and are hitting 34% of them. They lack a scoring big who the Creighton guards can dump the ball to on post-ups, so shooting is paramount.
Ultimately, though, I'm laying the points with Michigan here. I'll buy some Wolverines stock.
I don't expect them to shoot it well this season, but they have a stout defense and dominate the boards.
Creighton gives up a fair share of offensive boards, too.
This feels like a perfect get-right spot for Michigan.

Meanwhile, the Buckeyes enter this matchup with a 4-2 record, with their only two defeats coming away from home against top-30 KenPom teams in [BYU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/byu-cougars) and UConn.
Securing a home victory against an undefeated, top-five KenPom opponent would mark a signature non-conference win for head coach Jake Diebler and [Ohio State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ohio-state-buckeyes).

I anticipate a star-caliber performance from Tanner, carrying Vanderbilt to a victory and cover in this matchup.

### Michigan Basketball
The Mike Boynton era in Ann Arbor is off to an inauspicious start, as the Wolverines are 3-2 and are coming off a difficult loss to Villanova in Philly. The other loss was to UConn, which is clearly better than Michigan, so there's no shame in that.
Usually, if you grab 20 offensive boards, you aren't losing. Michigan gave up 18 to Villanova and went a dreadful 7-for-31 (26%) from deep.
That's the story for Michigan thus far: poor shooting. The Wolverines are 28% from deep, with over 45% of their shots coming from 3. Trey McKenney is Michigan's top shooter, connecting on 37% of his 8.6 attempts per game. Freshman forward Quinn Costello is also close to 40% from downtown.
The issue is everyone else. Elliot Cadeau is barely over 20% from deep. Neither Moustapha Thiam nor J.P. Estrella are big-time shooters, and Roddy Gayle Jr. is at 16% from downtown.
The Wolverines won't shoot 36% from 3 like they did last season, and that's especially the case without Yaxel Lendeborg. However, Cadeau is a better shooter than he's shown, so he'll improve.
Boynton has kept the core principles instilled by Dusty May: crashing the offensive glass (41% offensive rebound rate) and defending the rim (holding teams to 38% shooting on 2s).
That combo will give Michigan a chance to compete in most games. If the shooting comes around, things get exciting again in Ann Arbor.

### Creighton Basketball
Creighton is also bringing on a new coach — Alan Huss — who mirrors his offensive style after his mentor, Greg McDermott. The Jays want to push the tempo — not too fast, but an opportunistically quick pace — and shoot 3s close to half the time.
The Jays are attempting 3s on 50.2% of their shots under Huss and hitting 34% of them. They lack a scoring big who the Creighton guards can dump the ball to on post-ups, so shooting is paramount.
Two guards lead the way for Creighton: Wes Enis, a transfer from South Florida, and San Diego State transfer BJ Davis. Enis averages over 15 points per game, but shoots just 34% from the field and 32% from 3. That's a byproduct of Enis' freedom within Huss' offense and his poor shot selection.
Meanwhile, Davis is shooting a terrific 43% from 3 and averages 14 points per game.
The reliance on Davis and Enis shooting 3s is why Creighton is just 76th in offensive efficiency. It's nice to have two threats like them, but that's the only consistent offense the Bluejays have found this season.
Defensively, expect a healthy diet of pressure. Creighton rarely pressed under McDermott, but Huss has to let this speedy group flex its athletic muscles.
The Jays boast a 27% turnover rate, including forcing 15 turnovers against Iowa and 22 from Kansas State. Essentially, that number isn't bloated from buy games.
On the downside, Creighton is woeful at defending the rim, allowing opponents to score at a 54% clip on 2s (243rd).
If Michigan can evade the pressure and the Creighton guard defense, the Wolverines can attack the hoop and thrash a poor interior defense.

## Iowa State vs San Diego State Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/isu.png)
### Iowa State Basketball
The Cyclones are always a lethal bunch, year in and year out, especially with T.J. Otzelberger leading the way.
Despite falling to [Memphis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/memphis-tigers) this season, the Cyclones picked themselves back up and have yet to let another team score more than 57 points.
Were those teams in the bottom 150? Yes, but it's still impressive to allow just 49.6 points per game (excluding the Memphis game).
Now, the Cyclones draw their first top-100 opponent since that opening-night loss.
Iowa State does have some offensive concerns, even with the early cupcake schedule. The Cyclones rank third-worst in 3-point percentage, 283rd in free-throw percentage and 206th in offensive turnover rate.
This team could be in for yet another wake-up call.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/sdsu.png)
### San Diego State Basketball
Sitting at a perfect 5-0, the Aztecs look to bounce back from a down season. Entering this matchup, San Diego State ranks in the top 30 in both effective field goal percentage offense and defense, excelling at attacking the rim, generating second-chance points and getting to the line.
Led by junior guard Latrell Davis, who leads the team in scoring at 20 points per game while shooting over 57% from the floor, the Aztecs have plenty of firepower.
Senior forward Bear Cherry (incredible name) has been contributing a ton as well, averaging 12 points while grabbing more than four rebounds per game this season.
With Iowa State already falling to a top-100 team, the Aztecs have a chance to keep this one interesting.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/isu.png)
![Header Second Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/sdsu.png)
## Iowa State vs. San Diego State College Basketball Picks, Best Bet
Two extremely talented defensive teams go toe to toe on Tuesday in what should be an all-out fistfight. I initially leaned toward San Diego State when the line opened at +11.5, but with it dropping to +9.5, I shied away and landed on the under.
The tempo should be more moderate, even though Iowa State has become a quicker team over the last few years. The defensive tenacity in this ballgame should make every offensive possession feel like pulling teeth.
I am concerned about Iowa State's early success, considering it has played three straight bottom-150 teams following its season-opening loss to Memphis.
However, those concerns are more on the offensive end. The Cyclones' defense is currently ranked No. 2 in adjusted efficiency on that end of the floor, per Bart Torvik, while ranking in the top 20 in forcing turnovers, defending the interior and blocking shots.
The Aztecs have been very good in those exact categories as well, ranking inside the top 100 in all three while also sitting 28th in effective field goal percentage defense.
With the Cyclones enduring the 41st-easiest schedule in the country, the Aztecs should be able to make life hell for an offense ranked 228th in effective field goal percentage on Tuesday.

### Vanderbilt Basketball
Off to an unblemished start this season, Vanderbilt has been propelled by the stellar play of National Player of the Year candidate Tyler Tanner.
Averaging 18.7 points, 6.2 rebounds and 3.5 assists per game, Tanner stands out as one of the nation's fastest guards and a defensive force, snatching 2.5 steals per game.
Sharing the backcourt is Washington State transfer Ace Glass, who has delivered an outstanding start for the Commodores by contributing 16 points per game and shooting 40% from deep on eight attempts per contest.
In the frontcourt, transfers Berke Buyuktuncel and Sebastian Williams-Adams lead the team on the boards, pulling down seven and five rebounds per game, respectively.
Thanks largely to their control of the glass (top-seven nationally in both offensive and defensive rebounding), the Commodores currently sit in the top 15 of KenPom's offensive and defensive efficiency metrics.
Vanderbilt's schedule has been relatively manageable so far, playing all of its games at home and securing five of its six victories by at least 20 points.
However, this upcoming matchup presents both a tougher caliber of opponent and the Dores' first true road test.

### Louisville Basketball
The 23rd-ranked [Cardinals](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/louisville-cardinals) had an even busier offseason than their Tuesday night opponent, boasting the top portal class in all of college basketball after guards Mikel Brown Jr. and Ryan Conwell moved on to the NBA.
Louisville lost far more than just Brown and Conwell from last season's offensive core, though, with eight of the Cardinals’ top nine scorers leaving the team in the offseason.
Fortunately, Pat Kelsey secured the No. 1-ranked player in the portal in 6-foot-9 forward Flory Bidunga, who instantly added a strong interior presence.
In his first four games under Kelsey, the former Jayhawk has thrived, averaging 14.5 points and over nine rebounds per game.
Since Bidunga’s arrival, the Cardinals rank 11th in the nation in team rebounding, averaging over 46 per game, with former [Iowa](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/iowa-hawkeyes) 6-foot-11 center Alvaro Folgueiras making a huge impact as well.
Bidunga headlines an elite portal class also featuring the likes of [Oregon](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/oregon-ducks)’s Jackson Shelstad, [North Carolina](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/north-carolina-tar-heels)’s Seth Trimble and former Razorback Karter Knox.
Kelsey did a remarkably impressive job replacing last season's top-tier guard play with the three aforementioned portal acquisitions who are all posting double-figure scoring efforts over Louisville’s first four games this season.
Louisville was also able to retain guard Adrian Wooley, whose role in the offense has taken a massive leap from last season, with the 6-foot-4 junior ranking second on the team in scoring with 13.5 points per contest.
Kelsey’s bunch has gotten off to a stellar start to his third season at the helm, winning its first two games by a 32-point margin
The Cardinals took a massive leap in the wrong direction in their third game, losing to [Memphis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/memphis-tigers) by 20 points despite holding a double-digit lead at halftime.
While Louisville bounced back with a 14-point victory over [Stetson](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/stetson-hatters), Texas Tech will be the Cardinals’ toughest challenge to date by a considerable margin.

### Texas Tech Basketball
It was quite the hectic offseason for [Texas Tech](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-tech-red-raiders) head coach Grant McCasland, who had to overhaul most of his roster while navigating legal eligibility battles and key frontcourt recoveries.
Despite the legal red tape, McCasland ultimately secured 6-foot-6 forward Darrion Williams, who played for the Red Raiders two seasons ago.
While Texas Tech lost its starting backcourt in guards Christian Anderson (NBA) and Jaylen Petty ([UCLA](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ucla-bruins)), the Red Raiders' head coach went to work in the transfer portal, acquiring several talented players to replace those he lost from last season.
McCasland signed former [Hofstra](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/hofstra-pride) guard Cruz Davis, who is off to a phenomenal start to the season despite uncertainty about his level of play against far-superior Power-Four opponents.
The 6-foot-3 senior has had zero learning curve since transferring from a mid-major program, racking up a team-leading 16.4 points per game.
Davis boasts remarkable versatility at both ends of the floor and also averages over five rebounds and five assists.
The Red Raiders also acquired two elite guards from the Mountain West in the portal, adding [UNLV](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/unlv-rebels)’s Dra Gibbs-Lawhorn and 6-foot-3 [Wyoming](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wyoming-cowboys) product Damarion Dennis, both of whom are averaging over 14 points per game on the season.
The biggest storyline of Texas Tech’s offseason was forward JT Toppin, who missed the majority of last year with a torn ACL and is expected to return for the Red Raiders at some point this season.
McCasland bolstered the Red Raiders' frontcourt with fresh talent, as former [UCF](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ucf-knights) 6-foot-8 senior forward Jamichael Stillwell is currently averaging a double-double in Toppin’s absence.
Texas Tech also landed a few key incoming freshmen, with 6-foot-10 forward Bassala Bagayoko adding some key rim protection.
Despite Toppin still not having played a game this season, McCasland’s bunch has ripped through its first five opponents with an average victory margin of 37.2.
The Red Raiders took down one of the best teams in college basketball — Illinois — and I'm about as high on Tech as I am on any other team in the nation.
Prior to the season, I placed a futures wager on Texas Tech to cut down the nets at +2500, which I think is very feasible when Toppin returns to action.

## Louisville vs. Texas Tech College Basketball Picks, Best Bet
In one of the biggest matchups in all of Feast Week, I expect a closely contested matchup featuring two extremely well-rounded lineups that also boast a ton of depth in their respective rotations.
While the Cardinals have the best player in Tuesday night’s matchup in Bidunga, the Red Raiders love to attack the basket, getting opposing bigs into foul trouble.
The Red Raiders are averaging over 30 free throw attempts per game, which I expect will be very significant given how much Kelsey relies on Bidunga on the defensive end.
Though Louisville is quite impressive offensively, averaging nearly 90 points per game, the Cardinals were exposed by Memphis when both Bidunga and Folgueiras got into early foul trouble and had to ride the bench early in the second half.
I expect McCasland to approach Tuesday’s matchup in a very similar way, attacking the basket to get the Louisville frontcourt into early-foul trouble.
While I expect Kelsey’s Cardinals to hang around early, the Red Raiders are the far better team, and I feel confident taking a shot on Texas Tech to cover a -4.5 spread with -125 [juice](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/juice) in what should be a very thrilling matchup.
