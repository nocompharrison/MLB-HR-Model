## Louisville vs Memphis College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/lou.png)
### Louisville Basketball
[Louisville](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/louisville-cardinals) has opened the season with back-to-back wins and a nice number 10 next to its name.
Pat Kelsey is in his third season and currently only has one scholarship holdover, Adrian Wooley. He's the Cardinals' leading scorer so far this season, and they also have maybe the best portal class in college basketball.
Their best transfer get has to be Flory Bidunga, the excellent interior presence who came over from [Kansas](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-jayhawks). He was the Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year and provides excellent rim protection and rebounding.
But with a new roster, we have yet to see what this Louisville roster will be. The Cards have two wins, by 14 and 50, but those lopsided victories came against [Chattanooga](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/chattanooga-mocs) and Morehead State, respectively.
This will be their first test of the season.
![Header First Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/26320_memphis.png)
### Memphis Basketball
[Memphis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/memphis-tigers) will play its third game against a top-25 opponent this season. The Tigers have split the first two matchups, with a win over [Iowa State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/iowa-hawkeyes) and a loss to [Vanderbilt](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/vanderbilt-commodores).
Their star Jayden Reid is expected to play, and he leads a plethora of options for Memphis. The Tigers have four players averaging double digits.
For some reason, Penny Hardaway’s Memphis teams start the season out mostly undervalued. Last season was the only campaign in which he didn't lead the Tigers to at least 20 wins.
But overall, this is a rebuilt roster, thanks to a $9 million revenue-share budget. Hardaway brought in 9 NIL portal transfers, as well as three freshmen.
A lot of people have picked [Wichita State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wichita-state-shockers) to win the American, but I think Memphis has so much upside; the value lies with the Tigers.

## Niagara vs Iowa State Preview

### Niagara Basketball
[Niagara](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/niagara-purple-eagles) is off to a very tough start, checking in with a 0-3 record. The Purple Eagles' only similar competition to [Iowa State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/iowa-state-cyclones) was a road game against [West Virginia](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/west-virginia-mountaineers), which they lost by 45 points.
Neither end of the floor has been very encouraging if you’re a Niagara fan. It ranks 328th in offensive efficiency and 332nd in defensive efficiency, per KenPom.
Shooting has kept Niagara above water offensively, as it’s hitting 37% from deep and taking 3s at a 48% rate.
That’s a concern, though. If the only thing Niagara does well is shoot, it leaves the Purple Eagles susceptible to a clunker if that shot-making regresses.
That's particularly true since they turn the ball over 23% of the time, only collect 16% of their offensive boards and shoot just 42% on 2. All of those are bottom 40 nationally.
Greg Paulus only has one double-digit scorer on his roster: guard [Logan Woods](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/logan-woods-221287-g), a transfer from [Wright State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wright-state-raiders), at 13 points per game. Bastian Rieber has been another consistent source of scoring, as he’s shooting 50% from deep.
Lastly, this Niagara team will have to keep everything on the perimeter. I touched on the lack of interior scoring, which is a byproduct of starting just one player taller than 6-foot-5. This isn’t a team built to battle high majors.

### Iowa State Basketball
Iowa State checks in at 2-1 with a pair of dominant wins over [Southern Miss ](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/southern-miss-golden-eagles)and [Southern](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/southern-university-jaguars) after a stunning loss to Memphis on opening night.
The Cyclones' style of bludgeoning, physical full-court defense is built to wear down smaller mid-major teams. They held Southern Miss to 34% shooting on 2s and forced 18 turnovers, and a few days later forced an absurd 30 turnovers and held Southern to 40% shooting on 2s.
This is a classic T.J. Otzelberger team. The Cyclones boast the fifth-most efficient defense in the country, per KenPom. They’re forcing turnovers at a robust 27% clip (6th) and limit teams to just 38% on 2s.
The Cyclones' length should overwhelm Niagara. One of [Dom Pleta](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/dominykas-pleta-320066-f) or [Blake Buchanan](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/blake-buchanan-249297-2) will be on the court at all times, and I’m not sure how Niagara stops them. Pleta appears to be a strong breakout candidate, averaging 12 points per game on 81% shooting.
The biggest breakout guy on the Cyclones' roster is [Tre Singleton](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/tre-singleton-320098-f), a versatile 6-foot-8 forward who leads the team with 19.3 points and 7.7 rebounds per game. ### Nebraska Basketball
How could you not be impressed by the Fred Hoiberg era in Lincoln? He flipped one of the worst high-majors in the country to a consistent tournament threat. Last season, the [Huskers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/nebraska-cornhuskers) made the Sweet 16 and have their sights set higher in 2027.
Last season, it took until mid-January for the Huskers to crack 13th or better in KenPom. The analytics believe in this team more than last season.
I’m not sure I do, despite Nebraska winning each game by double digits thus far.
Nebraska is thriving on the defensive end, ranking 10th in defensive efficiency, per KenPom. I see some staying power there, given it forces turnovers 24% of the time (10th) and holds teams to 46% shooting on 2s (83rd). Those are the two stats that are major indicators of defensive success in the future.
One that is more luck-based is 3-point defense. Nebraska is “holding” teams to 23% shooting from deep, including limiting [Providence](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/providence-friars) to 7-for-38 from deep last Saturday. Teams will eventually make shots against Nebraska, and that number will level out to the 30% range — at best. That’s just college basketball.
Offensively, Hoiberg wants his team to spread the floor and launch from deep. The Huskers are attempting 3s over 50% of the time and hitting on just 33% of them. However, they’re shooting 67% from inside the arc, which allows them to be 24th in offensive efficiency.
Five Cornhuskers average 10+ points per game, led by [Pryce Sandfort](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/pryce-sandfort-256180-f)’s 22. I'm most concerned with a potential [Braden Frager](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/braden-frager-282452-f) sophomore slump — he’s shooting 32% from the field and 16% from deep.
How about the point guard situation? Sam Hoiberg was ultra steady last season. This season, Nebraska entrusted Trevan Leonhardt, who’s struggling so far. The Huskers are mostly relying on another former walk-on in [Cale Jacobsen](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/cale-jacobsen-218747-2) and big guard [Taj DeGourville](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/taj-degourville-284098-g).
I’d consider Nebraska’s point guard situation to be a question mark.

### Boise State Basketball
Who said old dogs can't learn new tricks? Leon Rice, at a spry 62 years old, has learned some new tricks. [Boise State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/boise-state-broncos) ran an archaic offense the past few seasons, but that's long gone.
Rice has been at Boise State since 2011, and it's only finished inside the top 100 in adjusted tempo twice: 2011 and 2017. Neither of those teams is playing faster than this season's, which sits 65th in adjusted tempo.
Not only are the Broncos playing fast, but they're also leading the country with a 10.7% turnover rate and shooting 43% from 3, with 49% of their shots coming from downtown.
The catalyst is point guard [Ag Neto](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/aginaldo-neto-320672-g), who's averaging 20 points and six assists per game this season. He scored 25 points in his last two games and boasts a 54% shooting percentage this season.
Neto has paired nicely with [Damari Wheeler-Thomas](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/damari-wheeler-thomas-219676-g), who checks in with 17 points and 4.3 assists per game while shooting 46% from deep. That guard duo can carry the Broncos in the Pac-12. Don't sleep on [Pearson Carmichael](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/pearson-carmichael-283412-f), [Spencer Ahrens](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/spencer-ahrens-320700-f), and [Dovydas Butka](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/dovydas-butka-284076-f), either — each of them averages 11+ points this season.
Defensively, Boise State is solid but not to Nebraska's territory. The Broncos rank 42nd in defensive efficiency, but opponents are shooting 58% on 2s against them.
That'll have to be better against Nebraska.

## Missouri vs Marquette College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/miz.png)
### Missouri Basketball
Missouri is going to be a ton of fun this season, and I think the Tigers are a sleeper team to watch in the SEC.
There will certainly be some growing pains with an offense led by five-star freshman Crowe — as we saw against [Saint Louis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/saint-louis-billikens) — but this roster is a balanced mix of savvy veterans and high-upside freshmen.
The biggest status to monitor ahead of Sunday's matchup is fifth-year forward Mitchell. He suffered a head injury in the opening minute of Missouri's most recent game against SIUE and didn't return.
Getting Mitchell back would be a major boost for an offense that has been clicking on all cylinders. When the Tigers stalled against Saint Louis, Mitchell was often the one tasked with creating a much-needed bucket. He finished with 21 points in the six-point loss.
Offense is the name of the game for Missouri. This roster is loaded with scoring talent, putting up at least 84 points in all three games this season. The Tigers rank sixth nationally in adjusted offensive efficiency and have been scorching from beyond the arc, shooting 43.6%.
That number will likely come back down to earth over the coming weeks. Bryson Tiller, who shot just 26.9% from 3 last season, is connecting on 50% of his attempts so far. [BYU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/byu-cougars) transfer Kennard Davis Jr. is another example, shooting 50% from deep after connecting on just 32.1% last season.
Even with some expected shooting regression, this is an extremely well-rounded Tigers team. Missouri ranks second nationally in average height, with no rotational player checking in below 6-foot-4. The Tigers are athletic and can attack downhill.
The Tigers are also strong on the glass and use their length to funnel opponents away from the 3-point line. The problem? They've struggled mightily on the defensive end.
Opponents are averaging 2-point attempts from just 5.5 feet away, ranking Missouri 219th nationally in average 2-point attempt distance. Despite consistently having two 6-foot-10-or-taller big men on the floor, the Tigers still rank outside the top 100 in 2-point defense.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/marq.png)
### Marquette Basketball
After a down couple of years under Shaka Smart, Marquette is looking to get back on track with a deep, experienced roster.
The Golden Eagles dominated their first two games, but both came against bottom-tier opponents ranked outside the KenPom top 300.
Their third game was quite the opposite, as they struggled against [Michigan](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-wolverines), their first test against legitimate competition. In fact, the offense actually shot better from the perimeter (36.4%) than inside the arc (34.2%).
Nigel James Jr. has been at the forefront of those struggles. After being the most-used player in Big East play a season ago, he failed to score a single point on 11 attempts against Michigan. He has yet to reach double figures this season.
It's been difficult to get a true read on Marquette, but there are some encouraging signs on the defensive end. The Golden Eagles rank 16th in adjusted defensive efficiency and sit inside the top 25 in both turnover rate and average 2-point attempt distance.
The biggest bright spot against Michigan was the 15 turnovers Marquette forced. Defensive pressure and the ability to get out and run in transition are essential to this team's success. The problem has been converting those opportunities into points.
Similar to last season, Marquette remains heavily reliant on the 3-point shot despite struggling to convert. The Golden Eagles take nearly 45% of their attempts from beyond the arc but are connecting at just a 30% clip. The struggles inside the arc have continued as well, with Marquette ranking 139th nationally at 54.8%.
Royce Parham has been the primary engine of the Marquette offense. While he's struggled from 3-point range, the 6-foot-9 junior has grabbed at least four offensive rebounds in each of the last two games. He leads the team in scoring at 12 points per game and is one of three Golden Eagles averaging double figures.
There's still plenty of uncertainty surrounding this Marquette team, but it should be considerably better than last season's group.
[Louisville](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/louisville-cardinals) transfer Sananda Fru brings a much-needed presence around the rim and on the glass — two areas where the Golden Eagles struggled a year ago.
It's still early, but Marquette's 40% offensive rebounding rate is already up more than 10 percentage points from last season.

## Missouri vs Marquette Pick, Betting Analysis
Can Crowe take on the Marquette ball pressure in stride? I think so.
This Golden Eagles team loves to be aggressive, and they force opponents to often attack in the mid-range or from the perimeter. For someone like Crowe, if he can handle the intensity, there should be plenty of potential assists available.
Whether it’s a catch-and-shoot 3 or through the pick-and-roll, expect Missouri to utilize its big men effectively. If Mitchell is a go as well, this offense should be able to pick apart Marquette and take advantage of second-chance points.
As mentioned earlier, the key is whether Mitchell suits up. He is still being evaluated ahead of Sunday’s contest with no concrete update on his status.
If he plays, I would take the Tigers. In my opinion, this team should be favorites, holding the advantage at just about every position.
If Mitchell is out, I’d focus more toward Crowe’s assists. Mizzou will ask a lot out of its freshman playmaker. The offense will go as he does.

### High Point Basketball
After winning its first NCAA Tournament game in program history last season, [High Point](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/high-point-panthers) has picked up right where it left off, dispatching its first four opponents in impressive fashion.
Despite head coach Flynn Clayman losing most of his offensive production from last season’s 31-5 campaign, the team had quite an offseason, landing 11 new players to overhaul its lineup.
The Panthers have posted a 15-point margin over their first four games, though each win came on their home floor, which will undoubtedly be another factor to consider before this mid-major clash.
Senior forward [Cam’Ron Fletcher](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/cam'ron-fletcher-149303-g) is off to a great start to his second season under Clayman, leading the team in both scoring and rebounding with 12.8 points per game, along with eight rebounds.
Fletcher’s impact at both ends of the floor will be vital on Sunday, especially considering how poorly High Point has crashed the glass over its first four games, ranking outside the top 200 in rebounding.
He's far and away the best player on this roster and was voted Preseason Big South Player of the Year.
[South Florida](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/south-florida-bulls) transfer guard [CJ Brown](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/cj-brown-282445-g) has also started strongly, recording 12.8 points per game over the Panthers’ four victories.
Brown leads an experienced backcourt that also features Jason Rivera-Torres, another acquisition via the transfer portal this past offseason.
These guards lead a High Point portal class that includes six other players. Clayman also recruited three incoming freshmen to help replace its four leading scorers from last season.
While the head coach evidently focused on acquiring long, physical athletes who also excel at crashing the glass, that hasn't been the case so far for High Point, which has struggled to generate second-chance scoring opportunities over its first four games.
Despite how well this new-look lineup has played so far, the Billikens are considerably more talented offensively than all four of the Panthers' previous foes.

### Saint Louis Basketball
It's been quite a hectic first two weeks of the season for [Saint Louis](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/saint-louis-billikens), which lost out on former star forward Robbie Avila (just acquired by [TCU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tcu-horned-frogs) after the court granted him an additional year of collegiate eligibility).
Despite the complications surrounding the Avila saga, the Billikens have looked phenomenal over their first four games, with head coach Josh Schertz and his team averaging a whopping 31-point margin over their first three opponents.
The Billikens are coming off an impressive, hard-fought win over an extremely talented [Missouri](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/missouri-tigers) squad, followed by a remarkable 125-point performance in their latest win over [Northern Kentucky](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northern-kentucky-norse).
While Saint Louis has quickly established itself as one of the top mid-major programs in college basketball, the same rings true for a very talented High Point team coming off its first-ever NCAA tournament victory last season.
Schertz returned the likes of [Trey Green](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/trey-green-256704-2), [Kellen Thames](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/kellen-thames-219862-g), [Quentin Jones](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/quentin-jones-257063-2), [Amari McCottry](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/amari-mccottry-283519-g) and [Ishan Sharma](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/ishan-sharma-283587-f) from last seaon's team, boasting some experience that will be vital to the team’s success as it navigates a tricky non-conference schedule that also features another talented mid-major in [Saint Mary's](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/saint-marys-gaels).
Green has been impressive in his second season under Schertz, leading Saint Louis in scoring with 16 points over the team’s first three contests.
The former [Xavier](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/xavier-musketeers) guard is lighting it up from 3-point land. He has already knocked down 12 triples at an exceptional 57.1% clip, leading a Billikens squad that ranked third in all of college basketball last season in 3-point field-goal percentage.
Saint Louis boasts some of the best guard play in the country, with all four returning guards averaging more than 13 points per game so far.
McCottry has also been phenomenal at both ends of the floor, scoring 15 points per game with a 77.3% field goal percentage that ranks 13th in college basketball. The 6-foot-6 player is also averaging five rebounds and over three assists.
While Saint Louis has excelled offensively, it has been far less efficient on defense, but can attribute much of that to its offense playing at a very up-tempo pace.
Given how well the Bills played against a Mizzou team with insane athleticism, I expect them to be up to the task here.

## High Point vs Saint Louis Pick, Betting Analysis
While Sunday afternoon’s game has all the makings of a closely contested matchup on the surface, with both teams ranked as the top two mid-major programs before the season started, I expect a far more one-sided contest than the betting markets indicate.
Though the Panthers were exceptional last season, with a ton of experience and offensive playmakers, this is a completely different team with glaring holes throughout its rotation that I expect to be exposed by a Billiken squad with legitimate Final Four upside.
I absolutely love the Billikens, and will be backing them to cover both their first-half and full-game spreads in what I expect to be a double-digit point victory.

## Creighton vs Iowa College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/crei.png)
### Creighton Basketball
Creighton was in a tough spot heading to [Kansas State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-state-wildcats) in its last game, and the Bluejays allowed 89 points to the Wildcats. Now they head to Iowa to face a team that's scored 76 or more in all three games so far this season.
I pegged BJ Davis as the difference maker for this team, but he hadn’t had a good offensive game until the Kansas State defeat. That may be good news for the Bluejays, as I think that might help take pressure off Will Enis moving forward.
This will be their second straight game on the road, and I expect them to be a little more ready for this matchup.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/iowd.png)
### Iowa Basketball
Iowa is in a similar position to Creighton. The Hawkeyes won their first two games, and then fell against their first true test of the season. They have yet to play a game on the road, and that will be the case again as they play host.
Ben McCollum has prioritized offense for this Iowa bunch, as the Hawkeyes are 19th in effective field goal percentage to start the season and are also playing at the 342nd-slowest pace overall.
They take a lot of mid-range shots and rarely get to the free-throw line, which is something that Creighton also does.

## Creighton vs Iowa Pick, Betting Analysis
I faded Creighton in its last game, and I’m going to do so again.
These teams are very similar in how they approach the game, but early indications suggest Iowa can shoot the ball at a much higher clip and create more. The Hawkeyes also do a better job at rebounding and creating second chances.
Iowa has had the benefit of not playing a true road game, so the Hawkeyes are comfortable on their home court to start the season.
I'm going to try to shop for the cheapest line I can get, but I'm comfortable betting Iowa to win by more than three points.
