## Houston vs. Florida
[![Houston Cougars Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/hou.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/houston-cougars)
Wednesday, Nov. 18
4:30 p.m ET
ESPN
[![Florida Gators Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/fla.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-gators)
Houston +2.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
This matchup serves as a rematch of the championship game from two years ago, and it promises to be an exceptionally competitive clash. It is hard to find two teams in college basketball as physical as these two. Each program builds its identity on controlling the glass and imposing its physical dominance to wear down opponents.
[Florida](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-gators) currently sits at number 1 and boasts a solid resume of quality victories. However, their reliance on out-muscling opponents will face a severe challenge against a tough [Houston](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/houston-cougars) squad. Florida's depth in the backcourt is also a concern, as starting guard Boogie Fland exited their previous contest with an injury and remains questionable.
Urban Klavzar performed admirably filling in, but Houston holds the clear edge in guard play, powered by stellar point guard Dedan Thomas Jr.
To make matters worse for Florida, ball security has been a persistent flaw. The Gators rank just 243rd nationally in turnover percentage, whereas Houston boasts the top-rated defense in the country and ranks in the top 45 at forcing mistakes.
In what expects to be a grinding, physical battle, maximizing every possession is critical—and Florida has simply failed to protect the ball. Houston could certainly win this game outright so I will gladly take the points.

## Northern Illinois vs. Marquette
[![Northern Illinois Huskies Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/niu.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northern-illinois-huskies)
Wednesday, Nov. 18
7:30 p.m ET
ESPN+
[![Marquette Golden Eagles Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/marq.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/marquette-golden-eagles)
Northern Illinois +29.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Following a 2-0 start to the season, [Marquette](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/marquette-golden-eagles) has fallen in consecutive matchups to [Michigan](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-wolverines) and Missouri. Up next, the Golden Eagles face a [Northern Illinois](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northern-illinois-huskies) squad riding a three-game winning streak—a matchup featuring two programs heading in contrasting directions.
While Marquette has moved up just one spot from its preseason KenPom ranking, Northern Illinois has climbed 46 spots.
From a matchup perspective, Northern Illinois relies heavily on the long ball, ranking among the top 15 teams nationally in three-point attempt rate while shooting 38% from beyond the arc.
Meanwhile, Marquette’s defense struggled against quality competition, allowing both Michigan and Missouri to shoot over 41% from deep. Given that track record, Northern Illinois should generate and convert enough perimeter shots to keep this game competitive against the Golden Eagles.
In addition to their perimeter threat, Northern Illinois excels at protecting the defensive glass and minimizing turnovers—neutralizing two primary areas where Marquette usually capitalizes on opponents. The Huskies are led by senior point guard and Wisconsin native Max Weisbrod, who brings extra motivation playing in his home state. Weisbrod puts up 15.6 points per game while maintaining an impressive assist-to-turnover ratio near 7:2.
I like Northern Illinois to cover this one.

They come into this game averaging 70 points a game and in a game where Arizona State should be able to score 80. Arizona State has scored at least 85 points in their last three games versus similar competition.

## UConn vs. Arizona
[![Connecticut Huskies Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/uconn.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/connecticut-huskies)
Wednesday, Nov. 18
9:00 p.m ET
Fox Sport 1
[![Arizona Wildcats Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ari.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/arizona-wildcats)
UConn +3.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
The former back-to-back national champions look fully primed for another championship run this season. They are entering a brutal stretch of their schedule, with five of their next eight matchups coming against teams ranked in the KenPom top 12, beginning with a road game against the [Arizona Wildcats](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/arizona-wildcats) at the McKale Center.
While this represents a tough challenge for the Huskies, their defensive strengths match up exceptionally well against Arizona. The Wildcats heavily avoid three-pointers, ranking 315th nationally in three-point attempt rate, and convert just 30% of those shots. Facing a top-five defense in [UConn](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/connecticut-huskies), an offense this one-dimensional will face immense difficulty scoring.
Silas Demary and Braylon Mullins stand out as arguably the nation's premier backcourt duo, complemented by wing player Colben Landrew, a potential lottery pick. Their exceptional versatility presents numerous ways for the Huskies to break down and exploit the Arizona defense.
UConn's chances of securing a win heavily rely on Najai Hines staying out of foul trouble against Big 12 Preseason Player of the Year Mo Krivas, as Hines's physical presence on the court makes the Huskies look like genuine title contenders.
Additionally, while starter Nik Khamenia will be sidelined for the near future, Colben Landrew and Jayden Ross will step up to fill the void. Landrew and Ross should make life difficult on Arizona’s Ivan Kharchenkov.
Given that UConn has a strong chance to secure an outright victory, taking the +3.5 points is a compelling choice.
**Pick:[ UConn +3.5 (Play to +2.5)](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3215829584&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

### Jackson State Basketball
[Jackson State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/jackson-state-tigers) underwent a major transition this offseason after former head coach Mo Williams left to join Mark Pope’s staff at [Kentucky](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kentucky-wildcats). New head coach Trey Johnson was fortunate to retain much of last season's offensive core, though the Tigers have dropped three of their first five games.
Sophomore guard [Mike Williams](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/mike-williams-321319-g) has been phenomenal in the backcourt, posting 18.4 points per game. Alongside him, junior guard [Dorian McMillian](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/dorian-mcmillian-293257-g) has recorded just shy of 15 points a night while pulling down over four rebounds per contest.
Despite the strong guard play, Jackson State has a glaring frontcourt issue, with only one forward scoring over five points a night.
Senior forward [Nikk Williams](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/nikk-williams-349957-f) was brought in via the portal after playing at Division II New Mexico Highlands, but he has struggled to make an offensive impact against tougher competition.
Defensively, the Tigers haven't fared much better, ranking 348th nationally in team rebounding at less than 30 per game.
While they showed promise in wins over [Tulane](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tulane-green-wave) and [Winthrop](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/winthrop-eagles), they will have their hands full against an athletic, physical Baylor squad.

### Baylor Basketball
[Baylor](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/baylor-bears) is off to a promising start in head coach Scott Drew’s 24th year at the helm, winning three of its first four contests after a narrow three-point loss to fifth-ranked [Texas](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-longhorns) on Sunday.
Despite that defeat, the Bears still boast a +26 scoring margin after dismantling their first three opponents by over 36 points on average.
It was a busy offseason in Waco, as Drew had to overhaul the majority of his roster following an uncharacteristic sub-.500 campaign that saw the program miss the 2026 NCAA Tournament.
After losing top scorers Cameron Carr and Tounde Yessoufo to the NBA draft, he reloaded with a mix of key returnees, elite incoming freshmen and a high-impact portal class.
Leading the charge is an elite guard tandem: the Mingo brothers. Sophomore guard [Kayden Mingo](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/kayden-mingo-320033-g) paces the team with 17.0 points, five rebounds and over three assists per game, quickly establishing himself as the go-to scoring option.
His younger brother, freshman guard [Dylan Mingo](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/dylan-mingo-348084-g), has also excelled in his first four collegiate games, averaging 12.8 points, 5.8 rebounds and 3.3 assists per game. He's a true two-way threat, thriving defensively with over three steals per game.
Drew rounded out his portal class with guard [Brett Decker](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/brett-decker-283430-g) Jr., forward [Isaac Celiscar](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/isaac-celiscar-294762-f) and senior forward [Evan Chatman](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/evan-chatman-320296-f).
Decker, a [Liberty](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/liberty-flames) transfer, has made an immediate two-way impact by posting 13 points per game, while the [Yale](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/yale-bulldogs) and [UAB](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/uab-blazers) products add crucial frontcourt depth.
After struggling mightily on defense a season ago — a surprise given Drew’s status as one of the sport's premier defensive minds — Baylor appears back on track.
Wednesday afternoon offers a prime bounce-back spot against Jackson State, and I expect a motivated Bears squad to run up the score after Sunday's heartbreak.

## Notre Dame vs Rutgers College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/nd.png)
### Notre Dame Basketball
The Fighting Irish are coming off covering a hefty spread against one of the more complete teams in the country. With Notre Dame facing only [Villanova](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/villanova-wildcats) on the other side of the pond and a few bottom feeders, the Irish held their own and looked very impressive.
Were the Gators cruising to get to [Houston](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/houston-cougars)? Possibly, but good teams win, and great teams cover.
Now, Notre Dame draws Rutgers, another top-80 squad, according to Bart Torvik's advanced analytics.
Will the Irish be able to replicate their success on back-to-back days against top talent, or did they use up all their energy against one of the best teams in the country and come up short?
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/rut.png)
### Rutgers Basketball
The [Scarlet Knights](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/rutgers-scarlet-knights) are coming off their toughest test yet as they took on Houston on Tuesday night. Rutgers ultimately fell 85-73 but comfortably covered the 17.5-point spread.
Despite being abysmal from deep (3-for-14 from beyond the arc), Rutgers looked solid against its first top-five opponent.
The defense struggled to rebound and committed a few too many fouls. However, the Scarlet Knights did a great job getting to the free-throw line themselves while remaining very efficient around the basket.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/nd.png)
![Header Second Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/rut.png)
## Notre Dame vs. Rutgers Pick, Betting Analysis
The Scarlet Knights will face a very talented and lethal 3-point shooting team. The Fighting Irish are heavily reliant on the outside shot, ranking 46th in 3-point rate while shooting the second-best percentage from beyond the arc in the country.
If Rutgers can limit those quality looks from deep and force the Fighting Irish to shy away from the 3-point shot, the Scarlet Knights can come out alive in this one. Rutgers currently ranks 40th in 3-point percentage defense.
Against Houston, the Scarlet Knights held the Cougars to just 13 attempts from beyond the arc, with only five finding the bottom of the net. Plus, considering how efficient Notre Dame was from deep against the Gators, I don't expect the Irish to shoot nearly 40% from 3 in back-to-back games.
Two things the Scarlet Knights need to do against this solid Fighting Irish defense are attack the offensive glass and protect the basketball.
If Rutgers can accomplish both, the Scarlet Knights should be in a good position to cover the +2.5.

## Florida vs Houston College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/fla.png)
### Florida Basketball
The [Gators](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-gators) beat [Notre Dame](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/notre-dame-fighting-irish), 86-78. If you told me Florida scored 1.27 points per possession, I would assume they covered the 15.5-point spread. However, Notre Dame exploited the Gators' point-of-attack defense with Boogie Fland [injured](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/injury-report) and scored 1.15 points per possession itself.
Without Fland, the Gators had to shift their play style a bit. They couldn't be as aggressive on the ball because they don't have a guard who defends like Fland. Plus, losing their point guard pushed Denzel Aberdeen into more ball-handling duties.
Florida has played very well this season on both ends. The Gators lead the country in offensive efficiency with a 126 adjusted efficiency margin. They use their size to dominate the glass, securing 42% of their misses, and also get to the foul line at the 14th-best rate in college basketball.
The secret sauce to being the best offense is improving their shooting from 31% last season to 39% so far in 2026-27.
Thomas Haugh is shooting the long ball better, and Aberdeen is a much better marksman than Xaivian Lee. Plus, Urban Klavzar, who'll likely start if Fland is out, is a big-time shooter. He also scored 24 points against Notre Dame.
The Gators win the shot-volume battle better than anyone. They hold opponents to a 20% offensive rebound rate, but Houston grabs 42% of its misses. Something has to give.

### Houston Basketball
The [Cougars](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/houston-cougars) lost the second half against [Rutgers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/rutgers-scarlet-knights) after heading into the break up 15. Houston ended up winning by 12.
Despite not covering the spread, Houston never had to sweat. The Cougars led by 21 at one point in the second half.
This feels like one of the most Kelvin Sampson teams Houston has had. What do I mean by that? Well, the Cougars lead the country in defensive efficiency, force turnovers at a 22% clip (45th) and grab offensive rebounds 42% of the time.
Plus, the Cougars' offense is more about getting inside and drawing fouls than in past seasons. Houston is 55th in free-throw rate — by far the best mark in Sampson's time there.
Sampson can thank Dedan Thomas Jr. for the surge in free throws. He ranks 50th in fouls drawn per 40 minutes and has already taken 32 foul shots this season.
With Thomas operating more in ball screens than other Houston guards of recent times, he loves getting into the lane. He doesn't shoot as much as Kingston Flemings, for instance, but he's a big-time player.
As a result, the Cougars are 364th in 3-point percentage at 27%. We'll see if they can be a top-five level squad with shooting so few 3s.
The big-man duo is strong, too. Delrecco Gillespie and JoJo Tugler are both double-double machines who'll welcome the matchup against these Florida bigs.
The X-factor, though, is Chendall Weaver, who's eligible via lawsuit. He shifted the game in the first half against Rutgers and should be a defensive pest against the Florida guards, especially if Fland is out.

## West Virginia vs Kansas College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/896106_wvu2.png)
### West Virginia Basketball
Never doubt Ross Hodge! Despite having one of the easiest schedules to kick off the 2026-27 season, Hodge and the [Mountaineers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/west-virginia-mountaineers) took down the [Auburn Tigers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/auburn-tigers) on Tuesday night, 76-70, as a short neutral-site underdog. Now comes their toughest task yet, taking on Bill Self and his national title-hungry [Jayhawks](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/kansas-jayhawks).
Against Auburn, West Virginia allowed the Tigers to do whatever they wanted inside, shooting 21-of-31 from two-point range. If the Mountaineers are unable to protect the interior, Hodge and his boys could be in for a very long evening.
Kansas currently ranks in the top 10 in two-point percentage, close two-point percentage and far two-point percentage, per Bart Torvik. The Jayhawks haven't been reliant on the deep ball, either, making this a massive test for the Mountaineers tonight.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/kan.png)
### Kansas Basketball
One of the most impressive teams in the country has easily been the Kansas Jayhawks. They're kicking off the season a perfect 4-0 straight up and against the spread, with Bill Self's boys surpassing just about every expectation out of the gate.
Despite enduring one of the toughest schedules in the country, Kansas has faced exclusively top-160 opponents, taking down and covering against two teams ranked inside the top 60. The lowest-ranked team the Jayhawks have beaten is [Middle Tennessee](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/middle-tennessee-blue-raiders), which currently sits at No. 152 in Bart Torvik's rankings.
Self had every reason to want to run it back for one more year with this group because, folks, this team is STACKED!

## West Virginia vs. Kansas Pick, Betting Analysis
Ross Hodge is easily one of my favorite coaches in college basketball. He does an unbelievable job of getting the right guys for his slow, precise and defense-led teams. However, Bill Self's boys may be too much for this Mountaineers squad.
For starters, Auburn may be a top-50 team, but the Tigers have fallen short in back-to-back games against top-40 opponents.
With Auburn being West Virginia's first top-100 opponent last night, the Mountaineers now draw a Kansas team that has endured the 52nd-toughest schedule in the country, per Haslametrics, without missing a single beat.
It's still early in the season, but the Jayhawks currently own a top-five offense and defense, per Bart Torvik's metrics. Their lone issue at the moment? They haven't been efficient at the free-throw line, shooting just 61.4%.
If they can clean that up, we might need to start shopping for national championship odds.
