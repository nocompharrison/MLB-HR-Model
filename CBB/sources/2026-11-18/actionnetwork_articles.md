## Houston vs. Florida
[![Houston Cougars Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/hou.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/houston-cougars)
Wednesday, Nov. 18
4:30 p.m ET
ESPN
[![Florida Gators Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/fla.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-gators)
Houston +2.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
This matchup serves as a rematch of the championship game from two years ago, and it promises to be an exceptionally competitive clash.
It is hard to find two teams in college basketball as physical as these two. Each program builds its identity on controlling the glass and imposing its physical dominance to wear down opponents.
[Florida](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-gators) currently sits at number 1 and boasts a solid resume of quality victories. However, their reliance on out-muscling opponents will face a severe challenge against a tough [Houston](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/houston-cougars) squad.
Florida's depth in the backcourt is also a concern, as starting guard Boogie Fland exited their previous contest with an injury and remains questionable.
Urban Klavzar performed admirably filling in, but Houston holds the clear edge in guard play, powered by stellar point guard Dedan Thomas Jr.
To make matters worse for Florida, ball security has been a persistent flaw. The Gators rank just 243rd nationally in turnover percentage, whereas Houston boasts the top-rated defense in the country and ranks in the top 45 at forcing mistakes.
In what expects to be a grinding, physical battle, maximizing every possession is critical, and Florida has simply failed to protect the ball. Houston could certainly win this game outright so I will gladly take the points.

## Northern Illinois vs. Marquette
[![Northern Illinois Huskies Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/niu.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northern-illinois-huskies)
Wednesday, Nov. 18
7:30 p.m ET
ESPN+
[![Marquette Golden Eagles Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/marq.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/marquette-golden-eagles)
Northern Illinois +29.5
[![DraftKings  Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/477013_DraftKings@1x.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/draftkings)
Following a 2-0 start to the season, [Marquette](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/marquette-golden-eagles) has fallen in consecutive matchups to [Michigan](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-wolverines) and Missouri. Up next, the Golden Eagles face a [Northern Illinois](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/northern-illinois-huskies) squad riding a three-game winning streak, a matchup featuring two programs heading in contrasting directions.
While Marquette has moved up just one spot from its preseason KenPom ranking, Northern Illinois has climbed 46 spots.
From a matchup perspective, Northern Illinois relies heavily on the long ball, ranking among the top 15 teams nationally in 3-point attempt rate while shooting 38% from beyond the arc.
Meanwhile, Marquette’s defense struggled against quality competition, allowing both Michigan and Missouri to shoot over 41% from deep. Given that track record, Northern Illinois should generate and convert enough perimeter shots to keep this game competitive against the Golden Eagles.
In addition to their perimeter threat, Northern Illinois excels at protecting the defensive glass and minimizing turnovers, neutralizing two primary areas where Marquette usually capitalizes on opponents.
The Huskies are led by senior point guard and Wisconsin native Max Weisbrod, who brings extra motivation playing in his home state. Weisbrod puts up 15.6 points per game while maintaining an impressive assist-to-turnover ratio near 7:2.
I like Northern Illinois to cover this one.

In five of their last six games the Golden Grizzlies have given up at least 80 points, including two games where they gave up 90.
This total has gone over in every game that [Arizona State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/arizona-state-sun-devils) has had against competition outside of 200 on KenPom.
Oakland has been tested, though they haven't been successful, as they have played five top 100 teams on KenPom already. They struggle mighty on defense but offensively they grade out as a middle of the pack team in the country.
They come into this game averaging 70 points a game and in a game where Arizona State should be able to score 80. Arizona State has scored at least 85 points in their last three games versus similar competition.

## UConn vs. Arizona
[![Connecticut Huskies Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/uconn.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/connecticut-huskies)
Wednesday, Nov. 18
9:00 p.m ET
Fox Sport 1
[![Arizona Wildcats Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ari.png)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/arizona-wildcats)
UConn +3.5
[![FanDuel Logo](https://assets.actionnetwork.com/122955_FanDuelSportsBook@1x.jpg)](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/fanduel)
The former back-to-back national champions look fully primed for another championship run this season.
They are entering a brutal stretch of their schedule, with five of their next eight matchups coming against teams ranked in the KenPom top 12, beginning with a road game against the [Arizona Wildcats](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/arizona-wildcats) at the McKale Center.
While this represents a tough challenge for the Huskies, their defensive strengths match up exceptionally well against Arizona.
The Wildcats heavily avoid three-pointers, ranking 315th nationally in three-point attempt rate, and convert just 30% of those shots. Facing a top-five defense in [UConn](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/connecticut-huskies), an offense this one-dimensional will face immense difficulty scoring.
Silas Demary and Braylon Mullins stand out as arguably the nation's premier backcourt duo, complemented by wing player Colben Landrew, a potential lottery pick. Their exceptional versatility presents numerous ways for the Huskies to break down and exploit the Arizona defense.
UConn's chances of securing a win heavily rely on Najai Hines staying out of foul trouble against Big 12 Preseason Player of the Year Mo Krivas, as Hines's physical presence on the court makes the Huskies look like genuine title contenders.
Additionally, while starter Nik Khamenia will be sidelined for the near future, Colben Landrew and Jayden Ross will step up to fill the void. Landrew and Ross should make life difficult on Arizona’s Ivan Kharchenkov.
Given that UConn has a strong chance to secure an outright victory, taking the +3.5 points is a compelling choice.
**Pick:[ UConn +3.5 (Play to +2.5)](https://playbookbot.com/books/fanduel/passthrough?deeplinkId%5B0%5D=ML3215829584&context=playbook-slack-quickslip&sport=basketball)**

### Florida Basketball
The [Gators](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/florida-gators) beat [Notre Dame](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/notre-dame-fighting-irish), 86-78. If you told me Florida scored 1.27 points per possession, I would assume they covered the 15.5-point spread. However, Notre Dame exploited the Gators' point-of-attack defense with Boogie Fland [injured](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/injury-report) and scored 1.15 points per possession itself.
Without Fland, the Gators had to shift their play style a bit. They couldn't be as aggressive on the ball because they don't have a guard who defends like Fland. Plus, losing their point guard pushed Denzel Aberdeen into more ball-handling duties.
Florida has played very well this season on both ends. The Gators lead the country in offensive efficiency with a 126 adjusted efficiency margin. They use their size to dominate the glass, securing 42% of their misses, and also get to the foul line at the 14th-best rate in college basketball.
The secret sauce to being the best offense is improving their shooting from 31% last season to 39% so far in 2026-27.
Thomas Haugh is shooting the long ball better, and Aberdeen is a much better marksman than Xaivian Lee. Plus, Urban Klavzar, who'll likely start if Fland is out, is a big-time shooter. He also scored 24 points against Notre Dame.
The Gators win the shot-volume battle better than anyone. They hold opponents to a 20% offensive rebound rate, but Houston grabs 42% of its misses. Something has to give.

### Houston Basketball
The [Cougars](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/houston-cougars) lost the second half against [Rutgers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/rutgers-scarlet-knights) after heading into the break up 15. Houston ended up winning by 12.
Despite not covering the spread, Houston never had to sweat. The Cougars led by 21 at one point in the second half.
This feels like one of the most Kelvin Sampson teams Houston has had. What do I mean by that? Well, the Cougars lead the country in defensive efficiency, force turnovers at a 22% clip (45th) and grab offensive rebounds 42% of the time.
Plus, the Cougars' offense is more about getting inside and drawing fouls than in past seasons. Houston is 55th in free-throw rate — by far the best mark in Sampson's time there.
Sampson can thank Dedan Thomas Jr. for the surge in free throws. He ranks 50th in fouls drawn per 40 minutes and has already taken 32 foul shots this season.
With Thomas operating more in ball screens than other Houston guards of recent times, he loves getting into the lane. He doesn't shoot as much as Kingston Flemings, for instance, but he's a big-time player.
As a result, the Cougars are 364th in 3-point percentage at 27%. We'll see if they can be a top-five level squad with shooting so few 3s.
The big-man duo is strong, too. Delrecco Gillespie and JoJo Tugler are both double-double machines who'll welcome the matchup against these Florida bigs.
The X-factor, though, is Chendall Weaver, who's eligible via lawsuit. He shifted the game in the first half against Rutgers and should be a defensive pest against the Florida guards, especially if Fland is out.

### UConn Basketball
Even without Solo Ball, this UConn team looks like one of the most complete teams in the country. The Huskies are coming off a Friday win over [Ohio State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ohio-state-buckeyes), steamrolling them by 16 points.
Dan Hurley's boys don't look like they're taking their foot off the gas pedal anytime soon. UConn has won by an average of 26.25 points this season, with its closest game coming against [Michigan](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/michigan-wolverines) on Nov. 6, when the Huskies won by seven.
Now, heading into Tucson for their first true road game and test of the season. It'll be interesting to see how well they perform in a hostile environment against a top-ranked team.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/ari.png)
### Arizona Basketball
Will the Wildcats get their much-needed get-back win over a top-ranked team following their opening-night loss to [UCLA](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/ucla-bruins)? Only time will tell, but they'll have the luxury of their passionate hometown crowd backing them in this Wednesday night thriller.
Since that loss to UCLA, the Wildcats have taken care of business against everyone they've faced. Granted, the only victory worth mentioning is their road win over [Auburn](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/auburn-tigers), but they're demolishing the teams they should be.
The loss to the Bruins could be exactly what this team needed out of the gate, and something head coach Tommy Lloyd can hold over his boys' heads for the rest of the season.

## UConn vs. Arizona 2026 Expert College Basketball Pick, Best Bet
It hurts fading Hurley & Co. for a second straight game, especially as a massive Hurley guy. However, I don't hold the Huskies' home win over Ohio State that highly — even with me backing the Buckeyes in that matchup — or their most obvious revenge spot against Michigan.
Arizona went to Auburn and won by 11, and then lost to a very talented UCLA team on a neutral court on opening night. Haslametrics gives Arizona an 80-spot advantage in strength of schedule.
The one area of concern for the Wildcats, and something Lloyd should be hyperfocused on in this matchup, is defending the perimeter. Arizona has struggled to guard beyond the arc, ranking 304th in 3-point percentage defense and 320th in 3-point rate allowed.
The Wildcats have simply allowed teams to chuck it from deep and knock down shots at a high rate. UConn takes its fair share of 3-pointers and ranks inside the top 100 in 3-point percentage early in the season.
If the Wildcats can contain the 3-ball, they should be able to come out alive at home and cover the number.
