### West Virginia Basketball
If there's two words to describe West Virginia, they would be pure scrap.
We're five games in and Ross Hodge has his Mountaineers well prepped and motivated to take on any task at hand. They led for over 23 minutes against Kansas on Wednesday, with their biggest lead getting up to 11.
The Jayhawks looked like they were going to need overtime to pull off the win, before they snuck out to a four point lead at the end.

## Auburn vs Notre Dame College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/aub.png)
### Auburn Basketball
About time the [Auburn Tigers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/auburn-tigers) secured their first win over a team ranked inside the top 110 after dropping games to Arizona and West Virginia. Prior to those two matchups, the Tigers had only beaten Southeastern Louisiana and App State.
On Wednesday evening, Auburn took down the UNLV Runnin' Rebels, 86-71, leading by as many as 15 points at one point. That was despite shooting just 41% from the charity stripe. The team's struggles at the free-throw line need to improve, or the Tigers are going to lose a lot of close games.
This evening, Auburn draws its fourth straight top-110 opponent. Luckily for the Tigers, they have the upper hand over [Notre Dame](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/notre-dame-fighting-irish) in strength of schedule. According to Haslametrics, Auburn ranks 111 spots ahead of Notre Dame in that category.
Despite Auburn's struggles against top-100 teams, its defense should be solid enough to keep the Tigers alive in this ballgame.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/nd.png)
### Notre Dame Basketball
The 2026-27 Fighting Irish are going to be so much fun to watch if they can stay this lethal from deep. Don't expect them to shoot nearly 50% every night, although the team currently ranks second in the country in 3-point percentage, shooting 46.3% through its first six games.
There were some doubts about how good this team was coming into Players Era 8, given that it hadn't played anybody since returning from Rome. However, the Irish looked very strong against Florida. They nearly fell to Rutgers before turning on the burners in overtime Wednesday afternoon.
If Notre Dame can get through Auburn and end the week on a high note, it would be a massive week for Micah Shrewsberry and his team.

## Auburn vs. Notre Dame Pick, Betting Analysis
On Wednesday, I wrote that one of the keys to beating Notre Dame was limiting the Fighting Irish from beyond the arc. If Rutgers could keep them from getting comfortable and knocking down 3-pointers, the Scarlet Knights had a chance to pull off the upset.
Unfortunately, they couldn't. Notre Dame shot 11-for-23 from deep, following a 10-for-26 performance against Florida the day prior. Although Rutgers nearly pulled it out in the end, going into overtime is where all [dogs](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds) go to die. The Irish are a lethal threat from beyond the arc, and Auburn will need to make sure they don't get the same looks.
Now, they face an Auburn Tigers team that should be able to limit the Irish from getting quality looks from deep. Through five games, the Tigers rank 79th in 3-point percentage defense and 118th in 3-point rate defense.
Aside from limiting arguably the best 3-point shooting team in the country from doing what it does best, Auburn will need to take advantage of Notre Dame's fouling issues. Currently ranked 219th in free-throw rate defense, the Irish have a hard time keeping their hands to themselves.
However, making shots at the line has been an issue for the Tigers, who are shooting just a knuckle hair below 60% this season. If they can get to the line, make their shots and defend beyond the arc, [Auburn should come out alive](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/picks).

### Murray State Basketball
The [Murray State Racers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/murray-state-racers) are off to a phenomenal start to their season thus far, entering their first-round matchup at the Baha Mar Championship at 5-0.
Despite significant departures from last season’s roster, the Racers retained key pieces and reshaped their lineup via an impressive transfer portal class and an influx of international talent.
6-foot-5 senior guard Brayden Shorter (20.5 PPG) has been a massive breakout piece for head coach Ryan Miller.
6-foot-9 French sophomore Roman Domon is in scorching form, as he's also averaging over 20 points per game. Domon boasts tremendous versatility at both ends of the floor, as he's grabbing 5.5 rebounds and 3.8 assists per game.
Miller brought in experienced, high-upside additions to his rotation, including former [Auburn](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/auburn-tigers) guard Kaden Magwood and 6-foot-9 ex-[Belmont](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/belmont-bruins) forward Brigham Rogers.
Although Murray State’s lineup is fairly top-heavy — with Shorter and Domon dominating statistically relative to the rest of the roster — the Racers have looked exceptional to start Miller’s second season.

### NC State Basketball
[NC State](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/north-carolina-tar-heels) underwent a massive transitional period this past offseason, as former head coach Will Wade returned to his old post at [LSU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/lsu-tigers) following just one season with the Wolfpack.
After Wade’s departure, NC State hired former Wolfpack point guard Justin Gainey as its next head coach.
The Wolfpack had to overhaul their roster after the coaching change, as several players from last season's core either graduated or entered the transfer portal.
Fortunately, among the few scholarship returnees is 6-foot-6 sharpshooting guard Paul McNeil Jr., who decided to return to Raleigh to play under Gainey after initially entering the portal.
The Wolfpack junior is off to a great start in his third season with the program, posting 13.8 points and 7.5 rebounds per game.
NC State also returned 6-foot-9 senior forward Ven-Allen Lubin, who's in the midst of a strong campaign in his second year with the Wolfpack. Lubin leads the team in both scoring (15.3 PPG) and rebounding (9.3 RPG) through four games.
Those two returnees are the team’s top two scorers, and their decisions to stay in Raleigh have significantly shaped Gainey’s first year at the helm.
Gainey also secured an elite portal class, landing guards Preston Edmead ([Hofstra](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/hofstra-pride)), Christian Hammond ([Santa Clara](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/santa-clara-broncos)) and Darius Adams ([Maryland](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/maryland-terrapins)) to bolster the team’s backcourt depth.
NC State boasts impressive balance, as all four of those guards — alongside Lubin — are currently averaging double figures.
That said, former Wolfpack forward Darrion Williams’ decision to return to [Texas Tech](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-tech-red-raiders) could impact the ceiling of an athletic rotation with versatility at both ends of the floor.
NC State lacks interior size in its starting lineup, and it remains to be seen whether Gainey’s "small-ball" lineup will hold up against opponents with larger forwards.

## Texas A&M vs TCU College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/txam.png)
### Texas A&M Basketball
Five games into Bucky McMillan's second season as [Texas A&M](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/texas-am-aggies)'s head coach, and his offense is on pace to be the best he has coached in his career.
The Aggies are playing at the fastest tempo and with the highest offensive efficiency of any team he has coached, ranking fifth in college basketball in tempo and 11th in offensive efficiency.
What makes this team so hard to guard is its high-level efficiency while playing fast, shooting 41% from 3 and 62% from 2.
The playmaking of [PJ Haggerty](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/pj-haggerty-219106-g) has been there his whole career and is even more apparent in this fast-paced system. Of his 34 made shots this year, 27 have been around the paint.
Though Haggerty is averaging fewer than 20 points per game for the first time since his freshman year in 2022-23, he is shooting career-best percentages from the field (60%) and from 3 (42%) while averaging 19 points in 24 minutes per game.
With his ability to put stress on opposing defenses, the perimeter will continue to open up for A&M's elite shooters like [Jordan Pope](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/jordan-pope-218890-g) and [Rylan Griffen](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/rylan-griffen-218984-g).

### TCU Basketball
[TCU](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/tcu-horned-frogs) has had a strong offensive start to its campaign, averaging 95 points per game through the first four games of the season. To be fair, though, the best team the Horned Frogs have faced to start the season has been [Milwaukee](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/milwaukee-panthers), which ranks 222nd on KenPom. TCU scored a season-low 76 points against it.
Offensively, the Horned Frogs have done a good job this season getting to the free throw line and knocking down triples (38%), but an area of their game that they have struggled with against (inferior) teams has been shooting 2-pointers, where they rank 299th in 2-point field goal percentage (45%).
[Brock Harding](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/brock-harding-256171-g) leads the team in 3-point shooting at 50%, with [Micah Robinson](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/players/micah-robinson-282483-f) right behind him at 44%.
Last season, TCU had a top-25 defense and was able to make teams turn the ball over.
With Texas A&M's play style, TCU will have to continue to protect the 3-point line (opponents shoot 29%) and cause chaos by jumping passing lanes like they have done so far this season.
As a team, TCU averages 13 steals per game, led by Robinson and Harding.

## Texas A&M vs. TCU Pick, Betting Analysis
Texas A&M has the fastest average possession length in the country at 12.7 seconds. Can the Aggies continue that elite pace while taking care of the basketball against a defense that likes to play aggressively and, at times, gamble? I believe so.
Bucky Ball is built to take care of the ball while playing fast, and even if the Aggies start slow, they have the scorers to overcome an early deficit on the road.
This is the first strong test for TCU. Luckily for them, it's at home, but with their inefficiencies scoring around the rim and shooting low-percentage twos, I like A&M to take care of business on the road.

## Seton Hall vs Wisconsin College Basketball Preview
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/hall.png)
### Seton Hall Basketball
The [Seton Hall Pirates](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/seton-hall-pirates), otherwise known as the team that held [Alabama](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/alabama-crimson-tide) 20 points below its season average, now face another team that loves to run: the [Wisconsin Badgers](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/wisconsin-badgers).
The Crimson Tide rank fifth nationally in tempo, while Wisconsin sits 97th, per Bart Torvik. The Pirates rank outside the top 60 in pace this season, which is right in line with how Shaheen Holloway likes his teams to play: slow things down and play hard-nosed defense.
If the Pirates can dictate the pace and keep this game in the half court, they should have a chance to make things interesting. However, one issue Holloway's teams have dealt with over the last few years is scoring (important, I know).
They've struggled to generate offense inside, which puts even more pressure on their defense to keep opponents in check.
Seton Hall did take a step forward offensively last season, but this is still a team that can struggle to put points on the board.
The key will be whether the Pirates can turn this into the kind of rock fight Holloway wants to play. If Wisconsin gets comfortable running, Seton Hall could have a long night.
![Header First Logo](https://static.sprtactn.co/teamlogos/ncaab/100/wis.png)
### Wisconsin Basketball
The Badgers' first true test of the season awaits as they take on their first top-75 opponent, per Bart Torvik's rankings. Prior to Thursday's matchup, Wisconsin has only faced teams ranked in the upper 200s. The best team it has taken on so far was [Coastal Carolina](https://www.actionnetwork.com/ncaab/odds/coastal-carolina-chanticleers), which it defeated 89-81 on Sunday.
This is going to be a very interesting matchup for the Badgers, who will want to push the pace in this neutral-site game in the Bahamas.
Seton Hall, meanwhile, is going to do everything it can to force this game to be played as fast as your parents run when you want to show them a toy commercial before it goes away. In other words, at a snail's pace.
If the Badgers are unable to get out and run, it'll be interesting to see whether they can find a way through the Pirates.

## Seton Hall vs. Wisconsin Pick, Betting Analysis
With nearly a week having passed since the Pirates covered the 18.5-point spread against the Crimson Tide, Seton Hall should be more than prepared to hold down the Badgers.
The elite-level defense the Pirates possess was good enough to hold Alabama, which ranks eighth in adjusted offensive efficiency, to its lowest point total of the season.
Now, Seton Hall draws the 68th-ranked offense, which has yet to face a defense ranked inside the top 191.
If the Pirates can dictate the pace of play — which they should be able to do with this being a neutral-site game — it should work in their favor.
Their offense just needs to find quality looks and draw fouls, something they do at a pretty high rate, while knocking down their free throws at an even higher rate.
Their far tougher strength of schedule should also work to their advantage.
If this breakdown scares you, take the under and root for bricks.
